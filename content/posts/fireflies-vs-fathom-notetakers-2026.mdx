Fireflies and Fathom both join your calls, transcribe them and write the summary. The difference that matters is what the free plan gives you and how the paid plans charge. Fathom's free plan offers unlimited recordings and transcriptions. Fireflies' free plan limits storage to 400 minutes per team. As of November 2026, that gap shapes the choice for most small teams. Last tested: 27 November 2026. Fathom figures are from fathom.video/pricing. Fireflies prices come from a third party review, [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/fireflies-ai-pricing), which says it read Fireflies' pricing page on 28 October 2026, because the live page we fetched showed feature names without numbers.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free notetaker for everyday use | Fathom | Unlimited recordings and transcriptions, instant call summaries |
| Cheapest paid seat | Fireflies | Pro is $10 a seat a month billed annually, against Fathom Team at $15 |
| Sales teams with CRM sync | Fathom Business | CRM field sync, Deal View and coaching scorecards |
| Heavy archive of past meetings | Fireflies | Business and Enterprise have unlimited storage |
| Conversation analytics | Fireflies Business | Conversation intelligence, team analytics and sentiment tools |
| Refund safety | Fathom | Paid team plans list a 90 day guarantee |

Our arithmetic on the Dupple figures: a Fireflies Pro seat is $120 a year on annual billing and $216 if you pay monthly. A five person team on Fathom Team on annual billing pays 5 x $15 = $75 a month, while five Fireflies Pro seats cost $50. Business at $19 is $95 for five seats.

## What the free plans give you

Fathom Free lists unlimited recordings and transcriptions, instant AI call summaries, clips, playlists and a choice of bot-free capture (in beta) or bot capture. Fireflies Free lists unlimited transcription with auto-join, limited AI summaries, the AskFred assistant, uploads and API access, but only 400 minutes of storage per team and 20 AI credits, per Dupple.

If your goal is to record every call without paying, the Fathom page gives you the better start. If you want to try Fireflies' analytics, you need at least Business.

## What the paid plans add

Fathom Premium adds advanced call summaries, AI action items, a meeting assistant and MCP connections to the LLM of your choice. Team adds a shared workspace with folders, comments, mentions and playlists, team trackers, HIPAA compliance with a standard BAA and unlimited MCP connected users. Business adds CRM field sync, Deal View and coaching metrics.

Fireflies Pro adds unlimited AI summaries, video recording, downloads, AI Skills and personal and email assistants. Business adds multi-language mode, conversation intelligence, team analytics and public meeting access. Enterprise adds SSO, SCIM, audit logs and HIPAA.

## Seats, guests and sharing

A Fireflies seat is any teammate you add to the workspace, and paid plans bill per seat. People outside the workspace can still open a shared meeting recap link at no charge, per Dupple, which suits teams where only a few people run the calls. Fathom's Team and Business plans bill per user with a two user minimum, and its shared workspace adds folders, comments, mentions and playlists for those users.

## Integrations and AI assistants

Fathom lists MCP integrations with the LLM of your choice on Premium, and unlimited MCP connected users on Team. Fireflies lists unlimited integrations on Pro and personal and email assistants, and its Enterprise tier adds a rules engine. If your stack already includes a CRM, check which plan unlocks your connector before you count seats, since Fathom puts CRM sync on Business and Fireflies puts apps and CRM integrations on the paid tiers.

## Where each one fails

Fireflies fails on credits and storage. Free allows 400 minutes of storage per team, and Pro allows 8,000 minutes per seat, so a team that records daily will fill it. AI credits are small pools of 20 to 50 a month. Fathom fails on team minimums: Team and Business plans require 2 users, and its bot-free capture is still labeled beta.

## Who should skip which

Skip Fathom if you need long term unlimited storage and analytics for managers. Skip Fireflies if you want a free plan you can use daily for every call. Pick Fathom if you want a free notetaker and a clean team plan at $15 a seat annually. Pick Fireflies Pro at $10 a seat for the lowest paid price, or Business at $19 if you need unlimited storage and conversation analytics. Prices were read on 27 November 2026 and may differ by region.
