## What Is AI-Powered Marketing Automation?

AI-powered marketing automation involves using artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms to automate and optimize marketing processes. This includes tasks such as lead scoring, email marketing, and social media management. AI-powered marketing automation platforms can analyze customer data, behavior, and preferences to personalize marketing messages and improve campaign effectiveness.

### HubSpot Marketing Hub

HubSpot Marketing Hub is a comprehensive marketing platform that includes AI-powered features such as predictive lead scoring and automated email marketing. The Pro plan costs $2,400/year, which includes 5,000 contacts, but offers a free trial for new customers.

### Pardot Automation Cloud

Pardot Automation Cloud is an AI-powered marketing automation platform designed for B2B businesses. It offers real-time AI-powered lead scoring and advanced email personalization. The price starts at $1,000/month, but offers a free trial for new customers.

## Who Should Use This?

B2B businesses with complex marketing campaigns and a large customer database should use AI-powered marketing automation platforms like Marketo Engage or Pardot Automation Cloud. These platforms offer robust AI-powered features and real-time lead scoring, making it easy to personalize marketing messages and improve campaign effectiveness.

## Who Should Skip This?

Small B2B businesses or those with simple marketing campaigns may not need AI-powered marketing automation platforms. Instead, they can use more affordable options like Zoho CRM or Infusionsoft, which offer basic marketing features and a user-friendly interface.
