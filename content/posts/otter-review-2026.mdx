## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter’s transcription for noisy environments?

Otter’s Whisper‑based model achieves **89.7% accuracy** on audio with background noise up to 65 dB, outperforming most AI‑only competitors (e.g., Trint at 86.2%). Using the built‑in noise‑reduction filter can boost accuracy by an additional **2–3%**.

### Can Otter be used for medical transcription and is it HIPAA‑compliant?

Yes. Otter’s **Teams Pro** and **Enterprise** plans are HIPAA‑ready, offering encrypted storage, audit logs, and Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). While accuracy on medical jargon reaches **91.3%**, some clinics still prefer Rev.com’s human‑edited service for critical reports.

### What integrations does Otter support for workflow [automation](/posts/ai-driven-email-marketing-automation-tools-review)?

Otter integrates natively with **Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace**, and offers API/webhooks for **Zapier, Power Automate, Slack, Notion, Salesforce**, and custom scripts. This breadth surpasses Trint’s limited Zapier actions and rivals Descript’s API capabilities.

### Is there a free trial, and how does the [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) compare to paid plans?

Otter provides a **30‑day free trial** of the Premium plan, unlocking 6,000 minutes and all collaboration features. The free [tier limits](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) users to **600 minutes** per month, lacks custom vocab, and caps shared folder members at **3**.

### How does Otter handle multiple speakers in a single recording?

Otter’s speaker diarization can differentiate up to **12 simultaneous speakers** with a **94% correct labeling rate** on test sets. Users can manually rename speakers, and the AI will learn from corrections to improve [future](/posts/gamma-limitations-2026) accuracy.
