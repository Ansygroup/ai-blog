## Top AI Tools for Automated Customer Segmentation

### Segmi

Segmi offers advanced AI-driven segmentation, integrating seamlessly with Shopify. Its features include:

* **Behavioral segmentation**: categorize customers based on purchase history and browsing behavior
* **Demographic segmentation**: segment customers by age, location, and income
* **Predictive [analytics](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-marketing-teams-2026)**: forecast customer behavior and preferences

Segmi offers a **14-day free trial**, with pricing starting at **$49/month**.

### Zendaya

Zendaya provides AI-driven segmentation, with features [like](/posts/best-ai-tools-grammarly-for-accountants-2026):

* **Integration with popular marketing tools**: connect with [tools like](/posts/best-ai-tools-grammarly-for-accountants-2026) Mailchimp and Klaviyo

Zendaya starts at **$29/month**, with a **30-day money-back guarantee**.
