## What Is a Cursor AI Tool for Teachers?

A cursor AI tool is a software overlay that enhances the on‑screen pointer with intelligent features such as auto‑highlighting, real‑time diagram generation, and voice‑to‑cursor commands. In the classroom, these tools let teachers:

1. **Highlight key concepts** instantly—type “highlight definition” and the AI draws a colored box around the selected text.
2. **Generate visual aids on the fly**—say “show a Venn diagram of mammals vs. reptiles” and the AI draws it in seconds.
3. **Sync annotations across platforms**—students see the same cursor actions in Google Slides, PowerPoint, or Canvas.

The term “cursor” here goes beyond the classic arrow; it’s a dynamic, AI‑powered interaction point that adapts to the teacher’s intent. In 2026, the most popular tools are web‑based SaaS platforms that work on any device, from interactive whiteboards to iPads.
