## What Is Writesonic Free Tier?

Writesonic is an AI‑powered writing platform that offers everything from short ad copy to long‑form blog articles. In 2026 the company still maintains a **free tier** aimed at hobbyists, students, and small‑business owners who are curious about AI‑generated content. The free tier grants users a set number of **credits** each month; each credit can be exchanged for a predefined amount of generated text, typically around **150 words**.  

The free plan also limits access to the **template library**. While basic tools like **Blog Idea Generator**, **Product Description**, and **Social Media Caption** are available, premium modules—such as the **SEO Content Optimizer**, **AI Image Generator**, and **Long‑Form Assistant**—remain locked behind a paid subscription. This design encourages users to upgrade once they hit the credit ceiling or need advanced features.

In practice, a writer on the free tier can produce roughly **10 short ads**, **5 product descriptions**, or **2‑3 short blog posts** before the monthly credit pool is exhausted. The experience is smooth, with a clean UI and real‑time suggestions, but the **hard caps** are the most important factor for anyone planning a content strategy around Writesonic.

## FAQ

### How many words can I generate per month on Writesonic free tier 2026?  
The free tier grants **25 credits**, each roughly equal to **150 words**, giving you about **3,750 words** of generated content each month.

### Does Writesonic free tier include AI image generation?  
No. The AI Image Generator is a premium feature only available on paid plans (Starter and above).

### Can I use the SEO Content Optimizer on the free tier?  
The SEO Content Optimizer is locked behind the paid subscription. Free users only have access to basic copy‑generation templates.

### What happens when I exceed the credit limit?  
When you run out of credits, the platform blocks further generation until the **1st of the next month** when the credit pool resets. You can also choose to upgrade instantly to continue working.

### Is the free tier still available in 2026, or has Writesonic moved to a trial model?  
Yes, as of October 2026 Writesonic still offers a free tier with the 25‑credit limit. The company discontinued its previous 7‑day trial in favor of this credit‑based model.
