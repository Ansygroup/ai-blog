## What Is Writesonic Free Tier?

Writesonic is an AI‑powered writing platform that offers everything from [short](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-instagram-reels-2026) ad copy to long‑form blog articles. In 2026 the company still maintains a **free tier** aimed at hobbyists, students, and small‑business owners who are curious about AI‑[generate](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026)d [content](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content). The free tier grants users a set number of **credits** each month; each credit can be exchanged for a predefined amount of generated text, typically around **150 words**.  

The free plan also limits access to the **[template](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026) library**. While basic tools like **Blog Idea Generator**, **Product De[script](/posts/ai-tutorial-youtube-shorts-scripts-thumbnails-2026)ion**, and **Social Media Caption** are available, premium modules—such as the **SEO Content Optimizer**, **AI Image Generator**, and **Long‑Form Assistant**—remain locked behind a paid subscription. This [design](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) encourages users to upgrade once they hit the credit ceiling or need advanced features.

In practice, a [writer](/posts/copy-ai-limitations-2026) on the free tier can produce roughly **10 short ads**, **5 [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) descriptions**, or **2‑3 short blog posts** before the monthly credit pool is exhausted. The experience is smooth, with a clean UI and real‑time suggestions, but the **hard caps** are the most important factor for anyone planning a content strategy around Writesonic.

## FAQ

### How many words can I generate per month on Writesonic free tier 2026?  
The free tier grants **25 credits**, each roughly equal to **150 words**, giving you about **3,750 words** of generated content each month.

### Does Writesonic free tier include AI [image generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026)?  
No. The AI Image Generator is a premium feature only available on paid [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) (Starter and above).

### Can I use the SEO Content Optimizer on the free tier?  
The SEO Content Optimizer is locked behind [the paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) subscription. Free users only have access to basic copy‑generation templates.

### What happens when I exceed the credit limit?  
When you run out of credits, the platform blocks further generation until the **1st of the next month** when the credit pool resets. You can also choose to upgrade instantly to continue working.

### Is the free tier still available in 2026, or has Writesonic moved to a trial model?  
Yes, as of October 2026 Writesonic still offers a free tier with the 25‑credit limit. The company discontinued its previous 7‑day trial in favor of this credit‑based model.
