## What Is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant developed by Anthropic, designed to assist with text-based tasks like writing, research, and conversation. It offers a free plan with 100,000 tokens and a paid plan starting at $20/month for 100,000 tokens. Claude's strengths lie in its ability to understand and respond to natural language inputs, making it an excellent tool for tasks like content creation, customer support, and language translation.

## What Is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an AI image generator that uses machine learning algorithms to create images based on text prompts. It offers a free trial with 100 generations and a paid plan starting at $10/month for 200 generations. Midjourney's strengths lie in its ability to generate high-quality images quickly and efficiently, making it an excellent tool for designers, artists, and marketers.
