## What Is Loom Limitations 2026?

Loom’s core promise—“record, share, and get feedback in seconds”—has been diluted by a [series](/posts/mike-flanagans-carrie-release-date-2026-full-guide) of technical and [policy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) constraints that surfaced throughout 2025 and solidified in 2026. The most visible limitation is the **recording length cap**: free users can only create videos up to 30 [minutes](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026), and even paid Business users are throttled to 1,000 minutes per month. This is a sharp contrast to Vidyard’s “unlimited recordings” policy [intro](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026)duced in 2024.

Storage is another choke point. Loom now offers **5 GB** of [cloud](/posts/n8n-pricing-breakdown-2026) storage on the [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) and **100 GB** on Business. For a typical remote‑work team that archives [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) walkthroughs, that translates to roughly **150–200 videos** before hitting the ceiling, prompting frequent manual deletions.

Beyond raw numbers, Loom’s **[editing](/posts/ai-youtube-chapter-timestamp-generators-2026) suite** has lagged. While competitors rolled out timeline‑based trimming, multi‑track [audio](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026), and AI‑generated [subtitles](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) in early 2025, Loom still provides only basic cut‑and‑trim tools and static captions that must be added manually. The result is a clunky post‑production workflow that can add **15–30 minutes** of extra work per video.

Security and [compliance](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) have also become a sticking point. Loom’s standard Business plan does not include SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certifications, which many regulated industries (finance, healthcare) now demand. Only the Enterprise tier—priced on a quote basis—offers those audits, [push](/posts/best-ai-powered-push-notification-ab-testing-platforms-2026)ing smaller firms toward alternatives that provide out‑of‑the‑box compliance.

These limitations collectively affect user [experience](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026), team productivity, and cost‑effectiveness, especially as remote‑first work cultures mature.

## Editing Features & Collaboration Gaps

Loom’s editing toolbox still centers on **trim‑to‑clip** and **static captions**. Advanced users who [need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) to splice multiple takes, add background music, or embed call‑out graphics must export the MP4 and rely on third‑party [editors](/posts/best-descript-tools-2026) like Adobe Premiere Rush or CapCut. This adds at least **10 minutes** per video to the workflow.

Collaboration suffers as well. While Loom allows [comments](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026) and emoji reactions, it lacks **real‑time co‑editing** or **version control**. Vidyard introduced **multi‑user annotation** and **AI‑driven highlight reels** in Q1 2025, cutting post‑production time by **40 %** for sales teams. CloudApp’s **shared libraries** let multiple creators edit a single video without overwriting each other’s work.

The absence of **[auto](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026)‑generated subtitles** is another pain point. Loom only offers manual caption upload, whereas Vidyard’s AI subtitle engine produces accurate captions in **over 30 languages** within seconds, improving accessibility and SEO.

## Integration & Export Restrictions

Loom integrates with a respectable list of SaaS tools—Slack, Notion, Gmail, and Google Workspace—but the depth of those [integrations](/posts/best-n8n-tools-2026) is shallow. For example, the Slack app posts a link but cannot embed the video inline; users must click through to Loom’s web player. Vidyard’s **native embed** works directly inside Slack messages, Teams [chat](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026)s, and even PowerPoint slides.

Export [options](/posts/langchain-pricing-breakdown-2026) are limited to **MP4** at a fixed 1080p resolution. Users needing **GIFs**, **MOV**, or **lower‑bandwidth WebM** files must convert them externally, adding extra steps and potential [quality](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026) loss. CloudApp, by contrast, offers **one‑click export** to MP4, GIF, and even **transparent PNG overlays**, streamlining the content repurposing process.

## Security & Compliance Shortcomings

Security is non‑negotiable for many [enterprise](/posts/zapier-for-teams-2026-review)s. Loom’s Business tier provides **AES‑256 encryption at rest** and **TLS 1.2 in transit**, which is standard. However, **SOC 2 Type II** and **ISO 27001** certifications are only available on the Enterprise tier, and the audit reports are provided **upon request** rather than publicly. This opacity can delay procurement cycles.

Vidyard Pro includes **SOC 2 Type II**, **ISO 27001**, and **GDPR** compliance as part of its standard package, with certificates posted on its compliance portal. CloudApp also offers **SOC 2 Type II** for all paid [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) and **HIPAA** add‑on for healthcare [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial)ers.

For teams handling **PII** or **PHI**, Loom’s lack of built‑in compliance forces them to either upgrade to an expensive Enterprise contract or switch to a competitor that already meets [regulatory](/posts/top-ai-powered-compliance-platforms-finance-2026) standards out of the box.

## FAQ

### How many minutes can I record on Loom’s free plan in 2026?

The free plan caps each video at **30 minutes** and does not provide a monthly minute allowance, effectively limiting you to a handful of short clips before hitting the 5 GB storage ceiling.

### Does Loom support SOC 2 compliance for [business](/posts/best-ai-tools-chatgpt-for-small-business-owners-2026) users?

SOC 2 Type II compliance is only available on Loom’s **Enterprise** tier, which requires a custom quote. Business‑level subscribers do not receive this certification.

### Can I export Loom videos as GIFs or MOV files?

No. Loom only allows **MP4** export. To create GIFs or MOV files you must use a third‑party converter, adding extra steps and potential quality loss.

### How does Loom’s pricing compare to Vidyard and CloudApp in 2026?

Loom Business is **$12 /mo** per user, Vidyard Pro is **$15 /mo**, and CloudApp Business is **$9 /mo**. While Loom is cheapest, Vidyard offers unlimited recordings and compliance, and CloudApp adds offline mobile capture and GIF export for a similar price.

### Is there a limit to how many videos I can store on Loom’s Business plan?

Yes. Business users receive **100 GB** of cloud storage, which translates to roughly **150–200 standard 5‑minute videos** before [you need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) to delete or upgrade.
