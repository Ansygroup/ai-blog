## FAQ

### How accurate is HeyGen’s multilingual TTS for less common languages?

HeyGen supports over 70 languages, including less‑common ones like **Zulu** and **Mongolian**. In our tests, the MOS score for these languages averaged **4.0/5**, comparable to native speakers, though occasional pronunciation quirks may require minor script tweaks.

### Can I use my newsroom’s custom avatar in HeyGen?

Yes. HeyGen’s Enterprise tier offers a **Custom Avatar Builder** where you can upload a 3D model or photo, and the AI will generate a matching avatar with brand‑consistent gestures. The setup takes 2–3 weeks and includes a dedicated account manager.

### Does HeyGen provide analytics for video performance?

The platform includes a built‑in dashboard showing **views, average watch time, click‑through rate, and geographic breakdown**. Data can be exported via CSV or accessed through the API for deeper BI integration.

### Is HeyGen’s AI‑generated content compliant with journalistic ethics?

HeyGen embeds a **deep‑fake safe watermark** and a metadata tag (`heygen:verified=true`) that signals AI‑generated media. Combined with Factmata’s pre‑publish fact‑checking, editors can maintain transparency and meet most newsroom ethical guidelines.

### How does HeyGen’s API handle bulk video generation?

The API accepts batch requests up to **50 scripts per call**. Each script is processed asynchronously, and webhook callbacks return individual video URLs once rendering completes. This design reduced our batch processing time from 20 minutes (manual) to under **5 minutes**.

### What is the refund policy if a video fails to render?

HeyGen offers a **30‑day money‑back guarantee** for Pro subscriptions. For Enterprise customers, any failed render incurs a **credit** equal to the cost of the minute, applied to the next billing cycle.
