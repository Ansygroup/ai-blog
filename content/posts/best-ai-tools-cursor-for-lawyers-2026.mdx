## FAQ

### What is a legal cursor AI and how does it differ from a regular chatbot?
A legal cursor AI lives inside your document editor, offering suggestions exactly where your cursor is positioned. Unlike a chatbot that you query in a separate window, a cursor provides **real‑time, context‑aware drafting assistance**, eliminating the need to copy‑paste research [results](/posts/copy-ai-accuracy-test-2026).

### Can AI cursor tools replace junior associates for drafting?
No. While tools like Cursor AI can cut first‑draft time by up to **45%**, they still require senior oversight for strategic reasoning, nuanced argumentation, and ethical compliance. Think of them as **[productivity](/posts/best-ai-chatgpt-tools-for-lawyers-2026) amplifiers**, not replacements.

### How secure are these AI tools with confidential client data?
All top‑tier vendors (Cursor Labs, Evisort, Luminance) use **AES‑256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit**, and most offer **SOC 2 Type II** compliance. For highly sensitive matters, choose [enterprise](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-agencies-2026) plans that allow **on‑prem deployment** or private‑cloud hosting.

### Do these tools integrate with case‑management [software](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) like Clio or MyCase?
Yes. Cursor AI, Casetext Compose, and Evisort AI all provide **native plugins** for Clio, MyCase, and even Salesforce CPQ. ### How accurate are AI‑generated citations compared to human‑checked ones?
