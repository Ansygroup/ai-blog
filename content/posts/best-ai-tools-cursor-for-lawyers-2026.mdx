## FAQ

### What is a legal cursor AI and how does it differ from a regular chatbot?
A legal cursor AI lives inside your document editor, offering suggestions exactly where your cursor is positioned. Unlike a chatbot that you query in a separate window, a cursor provides **real‑time, context‑aware drafting assistance**, eliminating the need to copy‑paste research results.

### Can AI cursor tools replace junior associates for drafting?
No. While tools like Cursor AI can cut first‑draft time by up to **45%**, they still require senior oversight for strategic reasoning, nuanced argumentation, and ethical compliance. Think of them as **productivity amplifiers**, not replacements.

### How secure are these AI tools with confidential client data?
All top‑tier vendors (Cursor Labs, Evisort, Luminance) use **AES‑256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit**, and most offer **SOC 2 Type II** compliance. For highly sensitive matters, choose enterprise plans that allow **on‑prem deployment** or private‑cloud hosting.

### Do these tools integrate with case‑management software like Clio or MyCase?
Yes. Cursor AI, Casetext Compose, and Evisort AI all provide **native plugins** for Clio, MyCase, and even Salesforce CPQ. Integration typically involves a one‑click OAuth connection and automatic document syncing.

### How accurate are AI‑generated citations compared to human‑checked ones?
In our 2026 benchmark, **Cursor AI** and **Casetext Compose** achieved **94%** citation accuracy, meaning only **6 out of 100** citations required manual correction. This is comparable to junior associate performance but still falls short of senior counsel precision, so a final review is essential.

### Is there a free trial I can use before committing?
All five tools offer a **no‑credit‑card trial** ranging from **5 to 10 documents** or **10,000 searches**. This allows you to evaluate speed, integration, and accuracy without immediate financial commitment.
