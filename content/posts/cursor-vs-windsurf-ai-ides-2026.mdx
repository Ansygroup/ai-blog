Neither tool is sold the way you remember. As of 27 November 2026, Cursor is part of "The X Subscription", one plan that bundles Grok, Cursor and X. Windsurf's pricing page is titled "Devin Plans and Pricing" and puts Devin cloud agents on its paid tiers. If your mental model is "Cursor Pro at $20 against Windsurf Pro at $20", it is out of date on one side and only half true on the other.

This matters because the choice between them is now mostly a billing and ecosystem choice. Both are editors with agents, and both let you pick strong models. What differs is what you pay for, what is included, and what happens when you go past the included usage.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lowest paid entry price for an AI editor | Cursor | Plus is $8 a month, but only Grok models are available on it |
| A flat $20 plan with frontier models included | Windsurf | Pro is $20 a month with quotas covering OpenAI, Claude and Gemini models |
| Using third-party models on a budget | Windsurf | Cursor's new plans bill them separately at API list price from prepaid credit |
| Existing Cursor Pro customer | Cursor | Legacy plans keep working unchanged, with their two monthly usage pools |
| Heavy daily agent use | Cursor | Super at $100 a month lists 15x Plus usage; Ultra at $200 lists 50x |
| Free tier with unlimited completions | Windsurf | Free includes unlimited Tab completions and unlimited inline edits |
| Team seats and pooled controls | Both | Cursor Teams seats run $30, $100 or $200; Windsurf Teams lists $80 a month |

## What Cursor sells now

Cursor's documentation says it is sold as part of The X Subscription, with a weekly pool of usage for Cursor models shared across Grok and Cursor. The plan table is below.

| Plan | Price | Included usage | Third-party models |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Limited | Not available |
| Plus | $8 a month | Base | Not available |
| Premium | $30 a month | 4x Plus | API list price, from prepaid credit |
| Super | $100 a month | 15x Plus | API list price, from prepaid credit |
| Ultra | $200 a month | 50x Plus | API list price, from prepaid credit |

Annual billing is $80 a year for Plus, $300 for Premium, $1,000 for Super and $2,000 for Ultra. The pool refreshes weekly, so one heavy day does not wipe out a month.

The trap is in the third column. On the new plans the included pool covers Cursor's own models, listed as Grok 4.7, Grok 4.6, Grok 4.5 and Composer 2.5. Models from other vendors are not included. On Premium and above you can use them at API list price from prepaid credit. Plus, the $8 plan, only gets Grok models. If your habit is to pick a Claude or OpenAI model in the dropdown and forget about it, your effective cost is the plan price plus API charges.

Cursor says each new paid plan includes at least twice as much coding usage with Cursor models as the legacy plan it replaces. Legacy plans are Pro at $20, Pro+ at $60, Ultra at $200 and Teams at $40 or $120 a seat. They keep working, with two monthly usage pools, one for Cursor models and one for third-party models drawn at API price. Switching to the new subscription is optional and permanent, so do not click through it without comparing.

## What Windsurf sells now

Windsurf's page lists four plans.

| Plan | Price | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Light quota with agents, limited models, unlimited inline edits and Tab completions |
| Pro | $20 a month | Increased quotas with frontier models from OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and SpaceXAI, free use of SWE-2, access to Devin Cloud agents |
| Max | $200 a month | Everything in Pro, significantly higher quotas, access to Devin Bots |
| Teams | $80 a month | Up to 100 users, SSO, centralized billing, cost controls with 3 usage tiers |

Pro lets you buy extra usage at API pricing, and Pro, Max and Teams all list a "Use your ChatGPT plan" option. That last line is the quiet bargain: if you already pay for ChatGPT, you may be able to run it inside the editor instead of paying twice. We did not test the sign-in flow, so check its limits before counting on it.

Windsurf's own FAQ gives one piece of advice that is worth repeating: control the size of your prompts, be precise with instructions and remove unnecessary context. That is a billing tip as much as a quality one, because quotas drain faster on long context.

## Where each one fails

Cursor fails on surprise. The brand you knew now lives inside a Grok and X subscription, billing sits at grok.com, and a developer who never wanted X benefits still buys them. Third-party model usage is the second surprise, since it is billed apart from the plan price.

Windsurf fails on clarity of limits. The page describes quotas as "increased" and "significantly higher" without a number, so you cannot compare the $20 Pro plan to Cursor's 4x Plus pool on paper. The Teams line shows $80 a month but the page does not say whether that is per user, so confirm that before you quote it to a finance team.

## Who should skip which

Skip Cursor's new plans if you rely on non-Grok models all day and want them included in the price. Skip Windsurf if you want a plan ladder with clear multipliers, because the public page gives you names, not numbers. If you are still choosing between editors and agents more broadly, [our guide to AI coding tools](/news/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-cursor-windsurf-claude-code-codex) puts both in context, and the [Cursor pricing breakdown](/posts/cursor-pricing-breakdown-2026) covers the older plan names.

## Which should you pick

If you are already on a legacy Cursor plan, stay put until you have compared both pools against your actual usage. If you are starting fresh and want frontier models without an API bill, try Windsurf Pro at $20 for a month. If you want the biggest included pool and are happy with Grok models, Cursor Super or Ultra are the only ones that publish a clear multiplier. Either way, track your first month of usage before you commit to an annual plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does Cursor cost now?

Cursor's docs list Free, Plus at $8 a month, Premium at $30, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200, sold as part of The X Subscription. Older plans such as Pro at $20 keep working as legacy plans.

### How much does Windsurf cost?

Windsurf lists Free at $0, Pro at $20 a month, Max at $200 a month and Teams at $80 a month. Pro includes frontier models from OpenAI, Claude and Gemini, and extra usage is bought at API pricing.

### Are third-party models included in Cursor?

Not on the new plans. Cursor says third-party models are not included, and on Premium and above they are billed at API list price from prepaid credit.

### Do I have to switch to The X Subscription?

No. Cursor says legacy plans keep working as they are, and that switching is optional and permanent, so compare both before changing.
