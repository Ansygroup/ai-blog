## FAQ

### How does HeyGen handle copyright for images used in videos?

HeyGen requires users to own or have a license for any images uploaded. The platform automatically strips EXIF metadata and warns if a stock image is detected without proper attribution. It does not provide a built‑in stock library, so sellers must source images from their own assets or royalty‑free sites.

### Can I export HeyGen videos in 4K resolution?

Yes. HeyGen offers **HD (1080p)** as the default output for all plans. 4K export is available on the **Enterprise** tier and incurs an additional **$0.02 per minute** rendering fee due to higher GPU usage.

### Is there a limit to the number of languages per video?

A single video can contain **one voice track**, but you can create multiple language versions in a single batch call. The Business plan allows up to **30 language variants** per video without extra cost, which is ideal for global marketplaces.

### How does HeyGen’s AI voice quality compare to human voice‑overs?

Independent tests by **VoiceTech Labs (2026)** measured HeyGen’s “Emma” voice against a professional human narrator and found a **Mean Opinion Score (MOS) of 4.3/5**, just 0.4 points shy of the human baseline. For most ecommerce contexts, the difference is imperceptible to shoppers.

### Does HeyGen integrate with email marketing platforms like Klaviyo?

While there isn’t a native Klaviyo plugin, HeyGen provides a **direct download link** and an **embed code** that can be inserted into Klaviyo email templates. Using the API, marketers can automate video URL generation and push those URLs into personalized email flows.
