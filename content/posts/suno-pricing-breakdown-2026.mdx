## What Is Suno Pricing Breakdown 2026?

Suno is an AI‑driven music generation platform that lets users create royalty‑free tracks in seconds. The 2026 pricing breakdown introduces three distinct tiers—Free, Pro, and Enterprise—each with its own minute allowance, licensing terms, and support level.  

- **Free tier**: 5 minutes of generated audio per month, watermarked previews, and a non‑commercial license.  
- **Pro tier**: 100 minutes, full commercial rights, higher‑quality rendering, and priority in the job queue.  
- **Enterprise tier**: Unlimited minutes, API access, custom model fine‑tuning, and a dedicated account manager.

These tiers reflect Suno’s shift from a “pay‑as‑you‑go” model (used in 2023) to a more predictable subscription structure, making budgeting easier for both indie musicians and large media houses.

## Suno Free Tier: What You Get

The Free tier is Suno’s entry point, aimed at hobbyists, students, and anyone curious about AI‑generated music.  

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly audio allowance | **5 minutes** (≈10‑15 short loops) |
| Export format | WAV 44.1 kHz, MP3 128 kbps |
| Licensing | Personal, non‑commercial only |

While the minute cap feels tight, the tier is generous enough for quick proof‑of‑concepts. The main limitation is the non‑commercial license, which prevents monetization on platforms like Spotify or Twitch. Users who need a commercial license must upgrade.

### Real‑World Cost Scenarios

- **YouTube creator**: Publishes 8 videos/month, each needing a 30‑second intro. That’s 4 minutes of audio. At $19/month, the cost per minute is **$0.19**, far cheaper than hiring a composer (~$150 per minute).  
- **Indie podcaster**: Uses 12 minutes/month for episode intros/outros. Effective cost per episode: **$1.58**.  

Suno’s Pro tier also includes a **commercial license** that covers worldwide distribution, a crucial advantage over free‑tier restrictions.

## FAQ

### How many minutes does Suno’s Free tier actually give you?

The Free tier provides **5 minutes** of generated audio per calendar month, which usually translates to 10‑15 short loops (30‑seconds each). Exceeding this limit requires an upgrade to Pro.

### Can I use Suno’s Pro music commercially on platforms like Spotify?

Yes. Suno’s Pro plan includes a **full commercial license** that covers worldwide distribution, including streaming services, podcasts, and ad placements. No additional royalties are required.

### Does Suno offer a discount for non‑profits or educational institutions?

Suno currently provides a **15 % discount** on the Pro plan for verified non‑profits and accredited schools. You must submit proof of status through Suno’s support portal to receive the reduced rate.

### How does Suno’s API pricing compare to Aiva’s API limits?

Suno’s Pro tier allows **2,000 API calls per month** at $19/mo, whereas Aiva’s comparable tier caps at **1,500 calls** for $25/mo. Enterprise customers on both platforms enjoy unlimited calls, but Suno’s lower base price makes it more attractive for mid‑size developers.

### Is there a hidden cost for exporting high‑resolution WAV files?

No. All export formats—MP3, WAV 48 kHz (Pro), and WAV 96 kHz (Enterprise)—are included in the subscription price. Only custom model fine‑tuning (Enterprise) may incur additional engineering fees.

### What happens if I exceed my minute allowance in the Pro plan?

When you surpass the 100 minute quota, Suno automatically **pauses generation** until the next billing cycle. You can purchase an **overage pack** (10 minutes for $2.99) or upgrade to Enterprise for unlimited usage.

## Final Verdict

**Suno’s Pro plan ($19 / month) delivers the best value for most creators, offering generous minute allowances, a full commercial license, and fast priority rendering.** For high‑volume studios, the Enterprise tier provides unbeatable unlimited generation and API flexibility at a price that undercuts Aiva and Soundraw. If you’re only dabbling, the Free tier is a solid entry point, but most serious users will quickly see the ROI of upgrading to Pro.
