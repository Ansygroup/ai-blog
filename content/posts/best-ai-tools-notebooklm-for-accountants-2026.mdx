### What is NotebookLM and how can accountants use it?
NotebookLM is Google's AI‑powered notebook that combines large language model capabilities with spreadsheet‑like organization. Accountants can use it to automatically extract data from financial documents, generate summaries of expense reports, create draft tax calculations, and ask natural‑language questions about their data without writing complex formulas.

### Which AI tools integrate best with NotebookLM for accounting tasks?
The top AI tools that integrate smoothly with NotebookLM in 2026 are:
1. **Zapier AI** – automates data import from accounting software into NotebookLM.

### Is NotebookLM secure enough for confidential financial data?
Yes. NotebookLM uses end‑to‑end encryption, role‑based access controls, and complies with SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR standards. For accountants handling sensitive client data, it is recommended to enable two‑factor authentication, restrict sharing links to specific domains, and store any personally identifiable information (PII) in encrypted fields.
