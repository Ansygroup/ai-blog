Descript edits video by editing a transcript. CapCut edits video on a timeline, with templates and a growing set of AI video models. They overlap on captions, filler word removal and AI voices, but they are built for different jobs. As of November 2026, Descript fits talking-head, podcast and interview work. CapCut fits short social video. Last tested: 27 November 2026, from both vendors' live pricing pages.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Podcasts and interviews | Descript | Text-based editing, Studio Sound and filler word removal are listed from Hobbyist up |
| Short social clips and templates | CapCut | Free templates, premium templates and a brand kit on Teams |
| Cheapest paid plan | Descript | Hobbyist is listed at $16 against CapCut Pro at $19.99 a month |
| AI video generation | CapCut | Pro lists Seedance 2.5 from $0.12 a second in the first month |
| 4K export | Descript Creator | 4K watermark-free export is listed on Creator at $24 |
| Team editing | Descript Business | Brand Studio, team access and dubbing in 30+ languages |

## Descript plans

| Plan | Listed price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Text-based editing, a trial of the AI tools, 1 media hour a month, 720p export |
| Hobbyist | $16 per person a month | 10 media hours, 400 AI credits, 1080p, Underlord, custom voice clones |
| Creator | $24 | 30 media hours, 800 AI credits, 4K export, video generation, stock library, team of 3 |
| Business | $50 | 40 media hours, 1,500 AI credits, Brand Studio, dubbing in 30+ languages, team of 5 |

The page has a monthly and annual toggle and says annual saves up to 35% over monthly. Our fetch did not show which setting the listed prices belong to, so check the toggle before you compare. Media hours count imported or recorded media you process in the month. Top ups for hours and credits are sold on Creator.

## CapCut plans

| Plan | Listed price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Free templates, basic editing tools |
| Pro | USD 19.99 a month | 1,200 monthly AI credits, premium templates, 1 TB storage |
| Ultra | USD 79 a month | 8,645 monthly AI credits, exclusive AI features |
| Teams | USD 12.49 listed | 1,200 AI credits per seat, brand kit, Pro features for all seats |

CapCut's page says displayed pricing is for reference only and that final prices, taxes and offers show after you sign in. It lists a 7 day free trial of Pro with no card needed, but notes availability may vary.

AI video credits: Pro lists Seedance 2.5 from $0.12 a second and Seedance 2.0 Mini at 480p from $0.007 a second, both marked first month. Ultra lists $0.18 and $0.009. Our arithmetic: a 30 second Seedance 2.5 clip is about $3.60 on Pro at the first month rate. The page does not say what the rate is after month one.

## What Descript's comparison table adds

Descript's feature table lists transcription in 25 languages, speaker detection for 8+ speakers, Studio Sound, Eye Contact, Green Screen, Remove Filler Words, Shorten Word Gaps and Remove Retakes. It also lists caption translation into 61 languages and native-sounding AI speakers in 14 languages. Remote recording hours rise from 2 on the first column to 5, 15 and 25 hours a month across the paid columns, with room for 10 participants on the upper plans. That makes it a recording tool as well as an editor, which CapCut's page does not claim.

## What to check before paying

Both pages tell you to look again at checkout. Descript has a billing toggle, so confirm monthly or annual first. CapCut says taxes and offers show after sign in, and that its AI video rates are first month prices. Write down the price you will pay in month two, not month one, and compare those.

## Where each one fails

Descript fails on speed for fast social work. A transcript editor is great for dialogue and clumsy for music driven cuts. Its media hours also cap how much footage you can process: 10 on Hobbyist.

CapCut fails on price clarity. The listed amounts are reference only, and the Teams price shows two different figures on the page. Its AI video rates are marked first month.

## Who should skip which

Skip Descript if you mostly cut music driven vertical video. Skip CapCut if you edit long interviews and want to cut by deleting words. ## Test it yourself

1. Record a three minute talking clip with a few filler words.
2. Edit it in Descript Free by deleting words in the transcript, then export.
3. Edit the same clip in CapCut Free with auto captions.
4. Compare the time taken and the export quality.
5. Repeat with a 15 second music clip and see which tool feels faster.

## Which should you pick

Pick Descript Hobbyist or Creator for podcasts, interviews and tutorials. Pick CapCut for social clips and when you want AI video generation inside the editor. Check the live prices at sign in, since both pages say theirs can differ.
