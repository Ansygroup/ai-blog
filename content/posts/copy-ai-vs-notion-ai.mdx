## What Is Notion AI?

Notion AI is an extension of the popular productivity and note-taking app Notion. It offers AI-powered writing assistance, task [management](/posts/ai-tools-for-project-management-2026), and content creation [capabilities](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026) within the Notion workspace.
