## What Is a Copilot Prompt?

A Copilot prompt is a clear, concise instruction that guides the AI model to generate accurate and relevant text. Effective prompts are the key to unlocking Copilot's full potential, and mastering the art of crafting them is essential for getting the most out of the tool.

Imagine you're researching a complex topic, and you need to summarize a 10-page document in 200 words. A good prompt would be: "Summarize the key points of the document, focusing on the main arguments and supporting evidence." This prompt is clear, concise, and provides specific guidance on what the AI model should focus on.

## Crafting Effective Prompts

Crafting effective prompts requires a combination of creativity, specificity, and practice. Here are some tips to get you started:

- **Use specific language**: Avoid vague terms and generic phrases. Instead, use specific words and phrases that accurately convey your intentions.
- **Keep it concise**: Aim for a prompt length of 10-20 words. Any longer, and the AI model may struggle to understand your instructions.
- **Use active voice**: Active voice is more engaging and easier to understand than passive voice.
- **Experiment with different formats**: Try out different prompt formats, such as questions, statements, and lists.
