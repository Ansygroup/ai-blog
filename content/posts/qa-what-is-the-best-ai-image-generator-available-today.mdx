2. **Gather audience signals** – Pull the top three search queries that drive traffic to the product (use Ahrefs or Google Search Console). Note the primary pain point each query reveals.  
3. **Create a structured prompt template** – Jasper works best with a clear hierarchy. Use the following skeleton:  

   ```
   Write a [type of copy] for [product] aimed at [audience segment] that addresses [pain point] and includes a call‑to‑action that emphasizes [unique benefit]. Keep the tone [desired tone] and stay under [character limit] characters.
   ```

   ```
   Write a 150‑character Instagram ad for the XYZ smartwatch aimed at tech‑savvy runners that addresses battery‑life anxiety and includes a CTA that emphasizes “track every mile without recharging”.    ```  
   ```  

4. **Feed Jasper the template plus data** – Paste the filled template into Jasper’s “Custom Prompt” field. Attach the three search queries as bullet points under “Additional context” so Jasper can weave relevant keywords natural](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026)ly.
