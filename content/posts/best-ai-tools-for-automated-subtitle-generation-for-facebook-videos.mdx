## How Automated Subtitle Generation Works
Automated subtitle generation uses artificial [intelligence](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-data-visualization-for-ecommerce-business-intelligence) to transcribe spoken words into text. This process involves speech recognition technology, natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing, and machine learning algorithms. The AI tool analyzes the [audio](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026) track of the video, identifies the spoken words, and generates subtitles in real-time. For example, **GoTranscript** uses a combination of AI and human transcriptionists to achieve 99% [accuracy](/posts/claude-best-settings-2026).
