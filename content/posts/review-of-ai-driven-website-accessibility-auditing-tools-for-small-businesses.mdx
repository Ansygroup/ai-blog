## What Is AI-Driven Website Accessibility Auditing?

AI-driven website accessibility auditing uses artificial intelligence to scan websites for accessibility issues. These tools analyze website elements, such as images, videos, and [code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026), to ensure compliance with accessibility standards like WCAG 2.1. For example, AccessiBe uses AI-powered algorithms to identify and fix accessibility issues, such as missing alt text and color contrast errors.

While AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools offer many benefits, there are also some challenges to consider:
