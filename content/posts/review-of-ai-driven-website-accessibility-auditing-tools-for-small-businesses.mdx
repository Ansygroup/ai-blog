## What Is AI-Driven Website Accessibility Auditing?

AI-driven website accessibility auditing uses artificial intelligence to scan websites for accessibility issues. These tools analyze website elements, such as images, videos, and code, to ensure compliance with accessibility standards like WCAG 2.1. For example, **AccessiBe** uses AI-powered algorithms to identify and fix accessibility issues, such as missing alt text and color contrast errors.

## Common Challenges and Limitations of AI-Driven Website Accessibility Auditing Tools

While AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools offer many benefits, there are also some challenges and limitations to consider:

- **Accuracy and reliability of AI-powered scans**: AI-powered scans may not always be 100% accurate, and human review may be necessary to ensure compliance.
- **Potential for false positives or negatives**: AI-powered tools may generate false positives or negatives, requiring manual review and verification.
