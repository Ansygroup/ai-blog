## What Is AI-Driven Website Accessibility Auditing?
AI-driven website accessibility auditing uses [artificial](/posts/gpt-5-release-date-and-features-2026) [inte](/posts/chatgpt-api-guide)lligence to scan websites for accessibility issues.
