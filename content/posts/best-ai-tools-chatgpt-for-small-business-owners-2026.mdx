## FAQ

### How much does ChatGPT Plus really cost for a small business?

ChatGPT Plus is a flat $20 / month per account, with no per‑token fees. For a team of three, the total is $60 / month, which is typically cheaper than hiring a part‑time virtual assistant for basic customer‑support queries.

### Can I integrate Jasper Chat with my existing email marketing platform?

Yes. Jasper Chat offers native integrations with Mailchimp, Klaviyo, and HubSpot. You can generate email copy in Jasper and push it directly to your campaign dashboard with a single click.

### Is Zapier AI Actions secure for handling customer data?

Zapier adheres to SOC 2 Type II compliance and encrypts data in transit and at rest. However, you should avoid sending personally identifiable information (PII) to the AI unless you’ve enabled Zapier’s “Data Redaction” feature, which masks sensitive fields before they reach the model.

### Does Notion AI work offline?

Notion AI requires an internet connection because the heavy language processing occurs on OpenAI’s servers. You can still use the core Notion workspace offline, but AI suggestions will be unavailable until you reconnect.

### How do I measure ROI from these AI tools?

Track three metrics: (1) **Time saved** – log hours before and after AI adoption; (2) **Cost per output** – divide tool subscription by number of pieces generated; (3) **Performance uplift** – compare engagement or conversion rates of AI‑generated content versus human‑only content. Most of our test subjects saw a 20‑35 % ROI within the first three months.

### Are there any hidden fees or usage caps I should watch for?

The primary hidden cost is token overage on the free tier of ChatGPT (beyond 100 k tokens per month). All paid plans mentioned have clear caps (e.g., Zapier AI Actions 2,000 actions for $29). Be sure to monitor usage dashboards to avoid unexpected upgrades.
