## What Is Synthesia and Why It Matters for Journalists?

Synthesia is an AI‑video platform that transforms typed scripts into lifelike avatar videos without a camera crew. Since its 2017 launch, it has added over 150 multilingual voices and 70 custom avatars, making it a go‑to for journalists who must publish quickly across social, web, and broadcast channels. The platform’s “auto‑sync” engine aligns lip movements with text in under 30 seconds, allowing reporters to turn a 300‑word article into a 2‑minute video in roughly the same time it takes to proofread.

For journalists, the value proposition is threefold: **speed**, **cost efficiency**, and **brand safety**. Traditional video production can take days and cost thousands per minute; Synthesia reduces that to minutes and a few hundred dollars. Moreover, because the avatars are generated by the platform, there’s no risk of accidental copyright infringement—an essential consideration for newsrooms that must clear every visual element.

## FAQ

### How accurate is lip‑sync in Synthesia versus other AI video tools?

Synthesia’s proprietary “Auto‑Sync” engine achieves a 95% lip‑sync accuracy score in independent tests, edging out DeepBrain AI’s 92% and Hour One’s 93%. The difference is most noticeable in fast‑talking scripts, where Synthesia maintains smoother mouth movements.

### Can I use my own newsroom anchor’s likeness in these platforms?

Yes. Synthesia, DeepBrain AI, and Hour One all offer custom avatar creation where you upload 20‑30 minutes of video footage of the anchor. The process typically takes 2‑3 business days and complies with GDPR and local consent regulations.

### Are the AI‑generated voices truly multilingual?

All five platforms support multilingual voice‑overs, but Rephrase.ai leads with 55 languages, including regional dialects. Synthesia and Hour One cover 45 and 40 languages respectively, which is sufficient for most English‑dominant markets but may require a supplement for niche language audiences.

### What are the data‑privacy implications of using cloud‑based AI video services?

Platforms like Synthesia and Hour One are ISO‑27001 certified and offer on‑premise deployment for extra security. DeepBrain AI stores raw footage in South Korea, which may trigger data‑transfer concerns for EU‑based outlets. Always review each vendor’s data‑processing agreement before integration.

### How does AI video affect SEO for news articles?

Embedding AI‑generated videos can increase dwell time by up to 35% and improve click‑through rates in SERPs. Google’s “Video Carousel” now surfaces short news videos, so using AI tools to produce concise 30‑second clips can boost organic visibility.

### Is there a free trial available for any of these tools?

Most platforms offer a 7‑day free trial with limited minutes: Synthesia (10 min), DeepBrain AI (5 min), Colossyan (15 min), Rephrase.ai (5 min), and Hour One (10 min). Use the trial to test avatar realism and integration before committing.
