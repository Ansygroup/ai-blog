Jasper offers four main pricing plans: Creator, Teams, [Business](/posts/jasper-for-small-business), and Enterprise.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creator | $29 | $39 | Up to 20,000 words | Essential features, 50+ templates |
| Teams | $49 (save 20%) | $69 | Up to 50,000 words | Advanced features, 50+ templates, SEO mode |
| Business | $99 | $119 | Custom word limit | All advanced [features](/posts/gemini-api-guide), custom templates, priority support |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom features, dedicated support, custom onboarding |
