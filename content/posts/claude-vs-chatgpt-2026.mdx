## What Are AI Chatbots?

AI chatbots are software applications designed to simulate human-like conversations. They utilize natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to understand and respond to user queries. Claude and ChatGPT are two prominent AI chatbots that have gained popularity for their impressive capabilities.

Claude excels in conversational tasks, demonstrating a more natural and empathetic tone.
