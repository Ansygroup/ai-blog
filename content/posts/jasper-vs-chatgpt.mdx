### What is the difference between Jasper and [ChatGPT](/posts/chatgpt-pricing-explained)?

### Is Jasper or ChatGPT more expensive?
Jasper is more expensive than [ChatGPT](/posts/chatgpt-pricing-explained), with a starter plan at $29/month. ChatGPT, on the other hand, is free but with [limitations](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026).

### Can I use Jasper and ChatGPT for free?
Jasper offers a free trial, but you need to upgrade to a [paid](/posts/jasper-vs-free-version) plan to access more features. ChatGPT is free but with limitations, and you need to upgrade to a paid plan to access more features.
