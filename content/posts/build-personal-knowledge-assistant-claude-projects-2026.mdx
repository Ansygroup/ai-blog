By the end of this [tutorial](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) you will have a Claude Project that answers questions about your own documents: your notes, research, contracts, meeting records, or whatever pile of text you keep re-searching by hand. Ask it "what did we promise the client in March" and get an answer grounded in your files instead of your memory.

**Time required:** about 60 to 90 [minutes](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) for setup, plus an hour of [testing](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026).
**Cost:** Claude Pro at $20 a month (or $17 a month billed annually, $200 up front) as of November 2026. Projects is a [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) [feature](/posts/copy-ai-vs-chatgpt-2026); the [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) does not include it.

## Prerequisites

- A Claude account upgraded to Pro.
- Your source material gathered in one folder: PDFs, [docs](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026), markdown notes, or plain text. Ten to fifty documents is a good first corpus.
- A written paragraph describing what this [assistant](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-developers-2026) is for. You will use it as the project's [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) instructions.

## Step 1: Create the project

Open Claude on the web and find **Projects** in the left sidebar. Click **Create project**, [give](/posts/cursor-editor-deep-dive-2026) it a name that describes its job ("Client [work](/posts/best-ai-tools-otter-freelancers-2026) KB" beats "Project 1"), and add a one-line [description](/posts/best-ai-powered-resume-tailoring-ats-optimization-2026). The project is a container: every chat you start inside it shares the same knowledge base and instructions.

## Step 2: Load your knowledge

Click **Add [content](/posts/ai-powered-content-gap-analysis-niche-blog-topics-2026)** in the project's knowledge section. You can upload files directly (PDFs, Word docs, text and markdown files) or paste text. Practical guidance from use:

- Prefer clean text over scanned images of text. A scanned PDF that is really a [photo](/posts/top-ai-image-upscaling-restoration-tools-2026-photos) of pages will load but answers get unreliable.
- Name files [like](/posts/best-ai-tools-grammarly-for-accountants-2026) a librarian, not a downloader. `2026-03-acme-sow-v2.pdf` is queryable; `document (7).pdf` is not.
- Curate rather than dump. The forty documents [you actually](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) ask about beat four hundred that dilute retrieval. Large projects can hit knowledge limits, and bloated projects answer worse long before that.

## Step 3: Write the custom instructions

Open the project's [settings](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings) or instructions field and write how you want it to behave. Three lines carry most of the value:

1. Scope: "Answer only from the project knowledge. If the answer is not in the documents, say so."
2. Citation habit: "After each claim, name the source file it came from."
3. Voice: any [style](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026) rules you want for drafts it produces.

That first instruction is the single most important setting in this tutorial. It converts Claude from a confident improviser into a grounded assistant that says "I don't have that" instead of inventing it.

## Step 4: Test with questions you already know the answer to

Start a chat inside the project and run five to ten questions whose answers you know cold. You are testing retrieval, not the [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial). Check that answers cite the right file, that "not in the documents" questions get a refusal rather than a guess, and that date-sensitive questions (latest version, most recent meeting) pick the newest document. Fix problems by renaming files, removing stale versions, and tightening the instructions, in that order.

## Step 5: Build the habit

The assistant only stays useful if the corpus stays current. Pick a trigger: every Friday, drop the week's meeting notes and signed documents in, and delete superseded versions. A knowledge assistant that knows everything except the last three months is worse than none, because you stop checking.

## Step 6: Tighten the instructions after real use

After a week of real questions, you will have a list of misfires: answers that were technically sourced but missed the point, refusals on questions the documents actually answer, drafts in the [wrong](/posts/jasper-limitations-2026-what-ai-writers-get-wrong) register. Convert each misfire into one instruction line. "When I ask about deadlines, always check the newest document first." "Draft emails in under 120 words." "Distinguish between signed [terms](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) and proposed terms." The instructions you write from real failures outperform any [template](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026), because they describe your corpus and your habits. Revisit them monthly; five precise lines beat a page of generic rules the model skims past.

## What can go wrong

- **Hallucination under pressure.** Even with strict instructions, a hard question over a thin corpus can produce a plausible invented answer. The defense is the citation rule from step 3: no citation, no trust. Click the source.
- **Stale documents winning.** Two versions of a contract in the project means the model may quote either. Keep one canonical version.
- **Confidential [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial).** Project knowledge lives in your cloud account. Client-confidential or regulated material belongs here only if your plan's terms and your professional obligations allow it.
- **Expecting cross-project search.** Projects are silos by [design](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026). If you need one assistant over everything, that is one big project, curated hard, not five small ones it cannot join up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a paid Claude plan for Projects?

Yes. Projects is a Pro feature: $20 a month, or $17 a month billed annually ($200 up front) as of November 2026. The free tier does not include it.

### How many documents can a Claude Project hold?

Enough for most personal corpora, but large projects hit knowledge limits and bloated projects answer worse before that. Curate to the documents you actually query, and keep one canonical version of each.

### How do I stop the assistant from inventing answers?

Set custom instructions requiring answers only from project knowledge, with the source file named per claim. Then test with questions you already know the answers to and check every citation.

### Claude Projects or NotebookLM?

Both ground answers in your documents. Projects [wins](/posts/claude-vs-gpt-4-for-coding-2026-showdown-which-ai-wins) if you also draft and work inside Claude; NotebookLM wins for pure source-grounded Q&A inside Google's world.
