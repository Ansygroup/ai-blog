ChatGPT is an AI-powered chatbot developed by OpenAI. ## ChatGPT Features for Small Business

ChatGPT offers a range of [features](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) that can benefit small businesses, including:

* Content generation: ChatGPT can help generate blog posts, [social media](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) posts, and product descriptions.

## ChatGPT Pricing for Small Business

ChatGPT offers a free [plan](/posts/cursor-pricing-breakdown-2026), as well as a [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) plan, ChatGPT Plus. The free plan includes 100 messages per 3 hours, while [the paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) plan offers 400 messages per 3 hours, priority access, and faster response times.
