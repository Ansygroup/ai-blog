## FAQ

### How does Jasper ensure financial data accuracy?

Jasper’s Finance module cross‑references every generated figure against the uploaded CSV or ledger file. It flags discrepancies in real time and highlights any numbers that lack a source, prompting the accountant to verify before finalizing the report.

### Can Botkeeper replace my junior bookkeeper entirely?

Botkeeper automates up to **80%** of routine entries and reconciliations, but a human reviewer still validates exceptions and complex journal entries. It’s best viewed as a **partner**, not a complete replacement.

### Is Vic.ai compliant with international tax regulations?

Yes. Vic.ai’s engine includes rule sets for **US IRS**, **EU VAT**, **Canada CRA**, and **Australia ATO**. The platform receives quarterly updates from tax‑law experts to stay current across jurisdictions.

### What integrations are required for OneUp to work with my existing accounting software?

OneUp offers native syncs with **QuickBooks Online**, **Xero**, and **Sage Intacct** via secure APIs. Setup typically takes **30–45 minutes**, and data flows bidirectionally, ensuring invoices and payments stay in sync.

### How does AutoEntry handle multi‑currency invoices?

AutoEntry detects currency symbols automatically and converts amounts using the latest exchange rates from **Open Exchange Rates** API. The converted values are then mapped to the appropriate GL accounts in your accounting system.

### Will using these AI tools affect my firm’s data security or compliance posture?

All five tools are **ISO 27001** certified and employ end‑to‑end encryption. Botkeeper and Vic.ai also provide SOC 2 Type II reports. However, firms should still conduct internal risk assessments and ensure that data residency requirements align with local regulations.
