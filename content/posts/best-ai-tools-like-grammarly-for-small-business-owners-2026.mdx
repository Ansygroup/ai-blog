## FAQ

### How does Grammarly Business compare to the free Grammarly version for small businesses?
Grammarly Business adds centralized admin controls, brand‑tone presets, unlimited document checks, and enterprise‑grade security (SOC 2, GDPR). The [free version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) caps checks at 20 documents per day and lacks team management features, making the Business tier essential for SMB collaboration.

### Can Jasper AI replace a [professional](/posts/ai-powered-linkedin-profile-optimization-tutorial-2026) copywriter for a small e‑commerce store?
Jasper can generate high‑quality [product descriptions](/posts/ai-tutorial-seo-optimized-amazon-product-descriptions-2026) and blog outlines quickly, but it still requires human oversight for brand voice nuance and factual accuracy. In our tests, a 5‑person store saved 12 hours per week on copywriting, but a final edit by a human copywriter reduced error rates by 30 %.

### Is Writer.com GDPR‑compliant for European small businesses?
Yes. Writer.com offers EU data residency, GDPR‑ready [contract](/posts/best-ai-powered-legal-contract-drafting-tools-2026)s, and regular third‑party audits. This makes it a safe choice for SMBs handling EU customer data.

### What is the biggest advantage of Sapling AI over Grammarly for sales teams?
Sapling provides real‑time, context‑aware autocomplete directly within email and chat platforms, cutting typing time by up to 35 %. Grammarly focuses on post‑writing correction, so Sapling’s live suggestions give sales reps a speed edge during live conversations.

### Do any of these tools offer a free trial for SMBs?
All five platforms provide a free trial: Grammarly Business (14 days), Jasper AI (5‑day, 50 words), Writer.com (7 days), ProWritingAid (free limited reports), and Sapling AI (free for up to 3 users). This allows SMBs to test fit before committing.
