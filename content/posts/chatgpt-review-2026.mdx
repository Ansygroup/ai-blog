## Introduction to ChatGPT
ChatGPT is an AI chatbot developed by OpenAI, designed to understand and respond to human input in a [conversational](/posts/shopify-conversational-big-picture-design-control-2026-guide) manner. With its [release](/posts/mike-flanagans-carrie-release-date-2026-full-guide) in 2023, ChatGPT quickly gained popularity for its ability to [generate](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) human-like text based on the input it receives. In this review, we'll delve into the features, pricing, and performance of ChatGPT, comparing it to other notable AI chatbots on the market.

## Features and Capabilities
ChatGPT boasts an impressive array of features, including but not limited to:
