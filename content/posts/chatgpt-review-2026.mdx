## Introduction to ChatGPT
ChatGPT is an AI [chatbot](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude) developed by [OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026), designed to understand and respond to human input in a conversational manner. With its release in 2023, ChatGPT quickly gained popularity for its ability to generate human-like text based on the input it receives. In this review, we'll delve into the [features](/posts/claude-api-guide), pricing, and performance of ChatGPT, comparing it to other notable AI chatbots on the [market](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-market-research-2026).

## Features and [Capabilities](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026)
ChatGPT boasts an impressive array of features, including but not limited to:
