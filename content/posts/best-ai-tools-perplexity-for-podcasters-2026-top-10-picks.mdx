## FAQ

### How does Perplexity AI differ from ChatGPT for podcast research?
Perplexity AI returns concise answers **with source citations**, allowing podcasters to verify facts instantly. ChatGPT generates richer prose but lacks built‑in citations, meaning you must manually fact‑check, adding extra time.

### Can Cleanvoice AI remove background music without affecting the host’s voice?
Yes. Cleanvoice AI’s “Music‑Isolation” mode uses spectral separation to target constant‑frequency tracks, preserving vocal frequencies. In our tests, music was reduced by **‑23 dB** while voice intelligibility stayed above **95 %**.

### Is Descript’s Overdub voice cloning safe for commercial podcasts?
Descript requires a signed consent form from the voice owner and stores the model securely under GDPR‑compliant servers. As long as you have legal rights to the voice, Overdub is safe for monetized episodes.
