## What Are AI Video Generators?

## Runway

Runway generates high-quality videos and has a [user](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings)-friendly [interface](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-real-estate-agents-2026-top-picks). Its strengths include:

* High-quality video output (up to 4K)
* Easy-to-use interface
* Supports text-to-video and image input

However, Runway's pricing is higher, with [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) starting at $15/month.

## Pika

Pika excels in [customization](/posts/best-ai-powered-resume-tailoring-ats-optimization-2026) options and community features, making it ideal for collaborative projects. Its strengths include:

* Extensive customization options

* Affordable pricing (starting at $10/month)

## Kling AI

Kling AI has impressive text-to-video capabilities, making it a great [choice](/posts/kaggle-vs-zapier-2026) [for creating](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-creating-interactive-ebooks-and-digital-ma) videos from text [prompts](/posts/copy-ai-prompting-guide-2026). Its strengths include:

* Excellent text-to-video capabilities
* Affordable pricing (starting at $10/month)

## Hailuo

Hailuo focuses on ease of use and rapid rendering, making it a great [choice for](/posts/kaggle-vs-zapier-2026) those who want to create videos quickly. Its strengths include:

* Easy-to-use interface
* Rapid rendering

## Performance Comparison

In our tests, all four AI video generators produced high-quality videos, but with some differences:
