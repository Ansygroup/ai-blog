## What Are AI-Powered Customer Service Chatbots?

AI-powered customer service chatbots are software applications that use natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning to simulate human-like conversations with customers. In the financial sector, these chatbots help institutions provide 24/7 support, answer frequently asked questions, and route complex issues to human representatives.
