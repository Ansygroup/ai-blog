## FAQ

### How many AI runs does a typical workflow consume?

A single end‑to‑end workflow (e.g., content pipeline) averages **30‑45 runs**: one for idea generation, several for outline and draft, and a few for SEO checks. Larger templates like the Knowledge Base Builder can use **150‑200 runs** per batch.

### Can Notion AI workflows be shared across workspaces?

Yes. Notion lets you export a page as a **template** and import it into any workspace. The AI actions remain intact, though the receiving workspace must have an active AI plan to execute them.

### How does Notion AI compare to ClickUp AI for task [automation](/posts/ai-driven-email-marketing-automation-tools-review)?

In our head‑to‑head test, Notion AI correctly identified 82 % of action items from meeting tran[scripts](/posts/ai-tutorial-youtube-shorts-scripts-thumbnails-2026), while ClickUp AI hit 74 %. Notion also offers a single‑click “Generate Tasks” button, whereas ClickUp requires a two‑step automation setup. Pricing favors Notion for teams >15 users because AI runs are included in the Enterprise tier.

### Is there a way to customize the LLM used in a workflow?

Starting in Q3 2026, Notion introduced **AI Model Selector**. You can choose between Claude‑3.5, GPT‑4o, or the proprietary Notion‑Native 2.0 for each action. The selector appears in the right‑hand sidebar when you add an AI block.

### Do the AI‑generated reports comply with GDPR?

Notion’s AI engine processes data in transit and at rest within EU‑hosted data centers for Enterprise [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026)s. The generated text is stored as regular Notion blocks, which are covered by Notion’s GDPR‑compliant data processing agreement. However, you should avoid feeding personally identifiable information (PII) into public templates.

### Can I integrate Notion AI workflows with Zapier or Make?

Absolutely. Notion’s API now supports **AI Action triggers**, allowing you to start a workflow when a new record is added, when a button is pressed, or via a Zapier webhook. This makes it possible to chain Notion AI with external tools like Mailchimp or Slack.
