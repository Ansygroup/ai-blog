## What Are AI [Music [Generator](/posts/best-ai-music-generators-2026)s](/posts/best-ai-image-generators-2026)?

AI music generators use machine learning algorithms to create original [music](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026) based on user inputs. These tools can produce a wide range of music, from electronic tracks to orchestral compositions. With the rise of AI music generation, [creators](/posts/ai-tools-for-youtube-creators-2026) can now produce high-quality music quickly and efficiently.

## [Features](/posts/gemini-2-0-features-2026) and Capabilities

### Suno

Suno is known for its high-quality music generation and user-friendly interface. Key features include:

* [High](/posts/review-of-ai-driven-seo-audit-tools-for-ecommerce-websites-with-high-traffic)-quality music generation with realistic outputs
* User-friendly interface for easy music [creation](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation)
* Collaboration tools for working with others
* Pricing starts at $8/month for the "Creator" plan

### [Udio](/posts/suno-vs-udio-2026)

Udio offers robust features and commercial usage rights, making it a popular choice among [creators](/posts/ai-tools-for-youtube-creators-2026). Key features include:

* Robust features for music generation and [editing](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison)
* Commercial usage rights for monetizing creations
* Free plan available, with [paid](/posts/jasper-vs-free-version) plans starting at $19.99/month
* Collaboration tools for working with others

### MusicFX

MusicFX offers a unique [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) integration and fast generation times, making it a great option for those who prioritize speed. Key features include:

* Fast music generation times
* Google integration for seamless [workflow](/posts/ai-workflow-automation-2026-n8n-vs-zapier-vs-make)
* Free plan available, with paid plans starting at $4.99/month
* Simple interface for easy music creation
