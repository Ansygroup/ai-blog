## What Are AI Music Generators?

AI music generators use machine learning algorithms to create original music based on user inputs. These tools can produce a wide range of music, from electronic tracks to orchestral compositions. With the rise of AI music generation, creators can now produce high-quality music quickly and efficiently.

## Features and Capabilities

### Suno

Suno is known for its high-quality music generation and user-friendly interface. Key features include:

* High-quality music generation with realistic outputs
* User-friendly interface for easy music creation
* Collaboration tools for working with others
* Pricing starts at $8/month for the "Creator" plan

### Udio

Udio offers robust features and commercial usage rights, making it a popular choice among creators. Key features include:

* Robust features for music generation and editing
* Commercial usage rights for monetizing creations
* Free plan available, with paid plans starting at $19.99/month
* Collaboration tools for working with others

### MusicFX

MusicFX offers a unique Google integration and fast generation times, making it a great option for those who prioritize speed. Key features include:

* Fast music generation times
* Google integration for seamless workflow
* Free plan available, with paid plans starting at $4.99/month
* Simple interface for easy music creation
