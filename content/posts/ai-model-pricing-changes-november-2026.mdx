November 2026 was the busiest single month for AI price moves this year: flagship API prices halved, a new top tier of consumer subscriptions appeared, and one tracker counted 48 price changes across the month. If you set your model budget in October, parts of it are already wrong. Every item below is verified and dated.

## 1. OpenAI cut GPT-6 Sol and Luna API prices by 50 percent

OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna on November 22 with API prices 50 percent below their GPT-5.6 promotional pricing. Sol moved to $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output, from $4 and $20. Luna moved to $0.10 input and $0.50 output, from $0.20 and $1.20. Halving the everyday tier while raising capability is the clearest sign yet that the major labs are competing on cost per task, not just benchmarks. Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/), November 22, 2026.

## 2. Google priced Gemini 4 Neon aggressively at the top end

Gemini 4 Neon, released November 18, costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output, with cached input at $0.15 per million, a 95 percent discount, and a one-million-token context window. That cached rate is the story for production apps: heavy-context workloads that reuse documents or conversations get near-free input on repeat calls. Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/), November 18, 2026.

## 3. Gemini 4 Hydrogen undercut the fast-model tier

Google's efficiency entry, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, launched November 12 at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output, with Google claiming task completion 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash. Between Hydrogen and Luna, the sub-flagship tier got a two-sided price war in ten days. Source: [The Peak Content](https://www.thepeakcontent.in/posts/10-new-ai-tools-worth-trying-in-november-2026), November 19, 2026.

## 4. ChatGPT Pro became three plans, up to $500 a month

OpenAI restructured ChatGPT Pro into three monthly tiers in late November: Pro 100 at $100, Pro 200 at $200, and Pro 500 at $500. Pro 200 reopened to new subscribers with its previously announced usage reduction canceled, and Pro 500 adds Ultrafast variants of the top models. Consumer AI now has a price ladder from $8 to $500, which quietly redefines "expensive subscription." Source: [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers), updated November 25, 2026. Our [ChatGPT pricing breakdown](/posts/chatgpt-pricing-breakdown-2026) tracks the full consumer ladder.

## 5. OpenRouter prices swung hard in both directions

Price tracker Bertrande counted 48 headline price moves in November across 27 offers, with 26 cuts and 22 increases. The biggest cuts: Nemotron 3 Super output down 83 percent to $0.40 per million tokens, gpt-oss-120b input down 67 percent to $0.05, DeepSeek V4 Flash batch pricing down 56 percent, and GLM 5.3 Prime down 40 percent. The 22 increases are the part nobody announces: routed API bills can rise even in a "prices are falling" month, so check your actual provider mix. Source: [Bertrande](https://bertrande.com/en/reports/2026-11/), November 26, 2026.

## 6. ByteDance pushed voice pricing down with Seed TTS 3.0

ByteDance released Seed TTS 3.0 on November 24, with script-level direction controls, at roughly a quarter of ElevenLabs v4's price per character according to tracker news.the9.pro. Voice synthesis joins images and text in the race to the bottom, which matters if your product narrates at scale. Source: [news.the9.pro](https://news.the9.pro/week/2026-W48/), November 24, 2026. For where ElevenLabs still justifies its premium, see our [ElevenLabs deep dive](/posts/elevenlabs-deep-dive-2026).

## 7. Black Friday hit AI subscriptions directly

ElevenLabs is running up to 55 percent off annual plans until December 1, cutting Creator's first year to $99 from $220. Annual-lock discounts this deep are worth a second look at the renewal price, since year two bills at the full rate. Source: [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing), checked November 27, 2026.

## 8. OpenAI's fine-tuning wind-down set its final dates

The shutdown timeline for OpenAI's fine-tuning platform is now firm: closed to new users, existing users can create training jobs until January 6, 2027, and fine-tuned gpt-4.1 family models retire with their base models on January 14, 2027. Teams still running tuned models have weeks, not quarters, to plan their migration, and training prices listed this month are effectively going-out-of-business rates. Source: [OpenAI developers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/supervised-fine-tuning), checked November 27, 2026. Our guide to [fine-tuning versus prompt engineering](/posts/fine-tune-vs-prompt-engineering-when-2026) covers the migration decision.

## What to do with this

Recompute your cost per task this week, not your cost per token: with Sol and Hydrogen halving the everyday tier, workloads you parked on expensive models in October may have crossed into viability. Audit routed traffic for the quiet increases Bertrande counted. And treat any annual discount as a bet on your usage twelve months out, because the direction of these prices says waiting usually gets cheaper.
