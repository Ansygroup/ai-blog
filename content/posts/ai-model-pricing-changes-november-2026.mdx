November 2026 was the busiest single month for AI [price](/posts/suno-vs-chatgpt-2026) moves this year: flagship API [prices](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) halved, a new top [tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) of consumer subscriptions appeared, and one tracker counted 48 price changes across the month. If you set your [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) budget in October, parts of it are already wrong. Every [item](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) below is verified and dated.

## 1. OpenAI cut GPT-6 Sol and Luna API prices by 50 percent

OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna on November 22 with API prices 50 percent below their GPT-5.6 promotional pricing. Sol moved to $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output, from $4 and $20. Luna moved to $0.10 input and $0.50 output, from $0.20 and $1.20. Halving the everyday tier while raising capability is the clearest sign yet that the major labs are competing on [cost](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) per task, not just benchmarks. Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/), November 22, 2026.

## 2. Google priced Gemini 4 Neon [aggressively](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) at the top end

Gemini 4 Neon, released November 18, [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output, with cached input at $0.15 per million, a 95 percent discount, and a one-million-token context window. That cached rate is the story for [production](/posts/best-ai-tools-heygen-for-podcasters-2026) apps: heavy-context workloads that reuse documents or conversations get near-free input on repeat calls. Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/), November 18, 2026.

## 3. Gemini 4 Hydrogen undercut the fast-model tier

Google's efficiency entry, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, launched November 12 at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output, with Google claiming task completion 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash. Between Hydrogen and Luna, the sub-flagship tier got a two-sided price war in ten days. Source: [The Peak Content](https://www.thepeakcontent.in/posts/10-new-ai-tools-worth-trying-in-november-2026), November 19, 2026.

## 4. ChatGPT Pro became three [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026), up to $500 a month

OpenAI restructured ChatGPT Pro into three monthly tiers in late November: Pro 100 at $100, Pro 200 at $200, and Pro 500 at $500. Pro 200 reopened to new subscribers with its previously announced usage reduction canceled, and Pro 500 adds Ultrafast variants of the top models. Consumer AI now has a price ladder from $8 to $500, which quietly redefines "expensive subscription." Source: [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers), updated November 25, 2026. Our [ChatGPT pricing breakdown](/posts/chatgpt-pricing-breakdown-2026) tracks the full consumer ladder.

## 5. OpenRouter prices swung hard in both directions

Price tracker Bertrande counted 48 headline price moves in November across 27 offers, with 26 cuts and 22 increases. The biggest cuts: Nemotron 3 Super output down 83 percent to $0.40 per million tokens, gpt-oss-120b input down 67 percent to $0.05, DeepSeek V4 Flash batch pricing down 56 percent, and GLM 5.3 Prime down 40 percent. The 22 increases are the part nobody announces: routed API bills can rise even in a "prices are falling" month, so check your actual provider mix. Source: [Bertrande](https://bertrande.com/en/reports/2026-11/), November 26, 2026.

## 6. ByteDance pushed [voice](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) pricing down with Seed TTS 3.0

ByteDance released Seed TTS 3.0 on November 24, with script-level direction controls, at roughly a quarter of ElevenLabs v4's price per character according to tracker news.the9.pro. Voice [synthesis](/posts/best-ai-tools-elevenlabs-for-doctors-2026) joins images and text in the race to the bottom, which matters if your product narrates at scale. Source: [news.the9.pro](https://news.the9.pro/week/2026-W48/), November 24, 2026. For where ElevenLabs still justifies its premium, see our [ElevenLabs deep dive](/posts/elevenlabs-deep-dive-2026).

## 7. Black Friday hit AI subscriptions directly

ElevenLabs is running up to 55 percent off annual plans until December 1, cutting Creator's first year to $99 from $220. Annual-lock discounts this deep are worth a second look at the renewal price, since year two bills at the full rate. Source: [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing), checked November 27, 2026.

## 8. OpenAI's fine-tuning wind-down set its final dates

The shutdown timeline for OpenAI's fine-tuning [platform](/posts/hugging-face-review-2026) is now [firm](/posts/best-ai-tools-jasper-for-lawyers-2026): closed to new users, existing users can create training jobs until January 6, 2027, and fine-tuned gpt-4.1 family models retire with their base models on January 14, 2027. Teams still running tuned models have weeks, not quarters, to plan their migration, and training prices listed this month are effectively going-out-of-business rates. Source: [OpenAI developers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/supervised-fine-tuning), checked November 27, 2026. Our guide to [fine-tuning versus prompt engineering](/posts/fine-tune-vs-prompt-engineering-when-2026) covers the migration decision.

### How much does Gemini 4 Neon cost?

As of its November 18, 2026 launch: $3 per million input tokens, $15 per million output, and cached input at $0.15 per million, a 95 percent discount, with a one-million-token context window.

### What are the new ChatGPT Pro tiers?

## FAQ

### What are the new pricing tiers for AI models announced in November 2026?
In November 2026, major AI providers introduced three new pricing tiers: a [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) with limited usage, a standard tier that charges per 1,000 tokens at a reduced rate compared to 2025, and an [enterprise](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-agencies-2026) tier with volume discounts, dedicated support, and customizable SLAs. The exact rates vary by provider, but most have lowered the cost per token by 10‑15% to stay competitive.

### How will the November [2026 pricing](/posts/best-gemini-ai-tools-real-estate-agents-2026) changes affect my monthly AI usage costs?
The impact depends on your current usage pattern. If you stay within the free [tier limits](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026), you may see no cost. For standard users, the reduced per‑token price typically translates to a 12% savings on average. Enterprise customers benefit from volume discounts, which can lower costs by up to 30% for large‑scale deployments. It's recommended to run the providers' cost calculators with your actual token consumption to see the exact savings.

### Do the November 2026 AI pricing [updates](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) include any new hidden fees or usage caps?
No hidden fees were introduced with the November 2026 update. Providers clarified that all charges are transparent and based on token usage, API calls, or compute time. However, each tier has its own usage caps: the [free tier limits](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) daily token usage, the standard tier imposes a soft monthly cap with overage charges, and the enterprise tier offers configurable caps that can be adjusted in the [contract](/posts/best-ai-powered-legal-contract-drafting-tools-2026).

### Can I switch [back](/posts/cursor-editor-deep-dive-2026) to the old pricing plan if the new November 2026 rates don't work for me?
Most providers allow a grace period of 30‑60 days during which [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) revert to the previous pricing plan without penalty. After the grace period, you must commit to the new tier, but many providers offer a custom plan negotiation for high‑volume users who [need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) a tailored pricing structure.
