## What Is Notion AI?

Notion AI is the native artificial‑intelligence layer embedded inside Notion’s all‑in‑one workspace. Launched in 2023, it adds generative‑text capabilities—summarization, brainstorming, task generation, and even code snippets—directly to pages, databases, and wikis. By 2026, Notion AI has matured into a “token‑based” service: every AI request consumes a certain number of **kilo‑tokens** (1 kilo‑token ≈ 1 KB of text). This model lets Notion price usage precisely, similar to OpenAI’s API.

For example, a 300‑word blog outline typically costs 0.7 kilo‑tokens, while a 2,000‑word research summary may use 4.5 kilo‑tokens. The system tracks token consumption per user or per team pool, automatically throttling requests when limits are reached. Because the AI model runs on Notion’s own infrastructure, the experience feels native—no copy‑paste to external chat windows, no API keys to manage.

Notion AI also integrates with Notion’s existing features: you can generate a meeting agenda directly inside a shared page, ask the AI to “turn this table into a kanban board,” or have it draft a product spec from bullet points. The result is a seamless blend of knowledge‑base and generative assistant, which is why many productivity‑focused teams have adopted it as a core part of their workflow.

## Free Tier vs. Paid Plans: What You Get

The **Free tier** grants every registered Notion user 5 kilo‑tokens per month. That’s enough for roughly seven 500‑word documents or 30‑minute brainstorming sessions. Features include:

- Basic text generation (summaries, bullet points)
- Limited “AI Assist” button placement (only on personal pages)
- No priority support or SLA guarantees

While generous for hobbyists, the free tier caps at 10 requests per day, and any token overrun results in a “quota exceeded” error until the next billing cycle.

The **Personal Pro** plan, priced at $10 / month, expands the token pool to 100 kilo‑tokens and lifts daily request limits to 200. It also unlocks:

- Advanced prompts (tone, style, length controls)
- AI‑generated tables and databases
- Access to the “AI‑Powered Search” beta
- Email support with a 24‑hour response window

For freelancers who produce client deliverables, the Pro tier typically covers 1,200 words of high‑quality copy per month—far beyond the free tier’s capacity.

The **Team** plan starts at $20 / user / month. Tokens are pooled at 300 kilo‑tokens per seat, and admins gain:

- Centralized usage dashboards
- Role‑based AI permissions (e.g., “view‑only” vs. “edit‑and‑generate”)
- Shared prompt libraries for brand consistency
- Integration with Notion’s SSO and audit logs

Large squads benefit from the shared pool because token consumption averages 2 kilo‑tokens per user per day, leaving a comfortable buffer for spikes.

Finally, the **Enterprise** contract (minimum 50 users) is quoted at $35 / user / month. It offers:

- Unlimited tokens (subject to fair‑use policy)
- Dedicated account manager and 99.9 % uptime SLA
- Custom model fine‑tuning (e.g., industry‑specific jargon)
- Advanced compliance (SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA)

Enterprises typically use Notion AI for knowledge‑base generation, compliance documentation, and internal training—tasks that can quickly exceed 10,000 kilo‑tokens per month, making the unlimited tier essential.

## FAQ

### How many tokens does a typical Notion AI request consume?
A standard 150‑word paragraph uses about **0.5 kT**, while a 2,000‑word research summary can consume **4‑5 kT**. Complex table generation may cost up to **1 kT** per operation.

### Can I roll over unused tokens to the next month?
No. Notion AI’s token allowances reset at the start of each billing cycle. Unused tokens are forfeited, encouraging efficient usage.

### Is there a discount for non‑profits or educational institutions?
Notion offers a **15 % discount** on Team and Enterprise plans for verified non‑profits and accredited schools. You must submit proof of status through the Notion sales portal.

### How does Notion AI handle data privacy?
All AI‑generated content stays within Notion’s encrypted environment. Enterprise customers can opt for **on‑premise inference** (available in 2027) and have the ability to delete raw prompt logs on demand.

### What happens if I exceed my token limit mid‑session?
Requests will be throttled and you’ll receive a “quota exceeded” notification. You can either wait for the next cycle or purchase an **add‑on pack** (10 kT for $0.75) from the billing dashboard.

### Does Notion AI offer a free trial of the Pro plan?
Yes. New users receive a **30‑day trial** with 200 kT, allowing you to test advanced features before committing to a paid subscription.
