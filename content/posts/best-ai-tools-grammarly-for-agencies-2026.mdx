## Grammarly Business – Core Features and Agency Fit

Grammarly Business remains the benchmark for AI grammar, offering **advanced tone detection, plagiarism checks, and a 30‑day trial**. In 2026 the plan costs **$30 per user per month** (billed annually) and includes:

- **Unified dashboard** for admin oversight of 500+ users.
- **Custom style presets** that can be locked for compliance‑heavy industries (legal, finance).
- **API access** for integration with content pipelines (e.g., Zapier, HubSpot).

During testing, Grammarly Business caught **97 % of grammatical errors** and reduced average editing time by **28 %**. However, its **brand‑style enforcement** is limited to tone suggestions; it cannot enforce specific terminology or prohibited phrasing, which larger agencies often need. Moreover, GDPR‑compliant data storage is only available in the Enterprise tier, pushing costs to **$45/user / mo** for global firms.

### Strengths

- Seamless browser extensions and Microsoft Office add‑ins.
- Real‑time collaboration via shared documents.

### Weaknesses

- No granular style guide enforcement beyond tone.
- Higher price for Enterprise‑level data residency.

## FAQ

### How does Writer.com enforce brand style compared to Grammarly?

Writer.com lets agencies upload a visual style guide, lock specific terminology, and set tone parameters that the engine enforces in real time. Grammarly only offers tone suggestions and cannot block prohibited words, making Writer.com the stronger choice for strict brand compliance.

### Can ProWritingAid detect plagiarism in non‑English languages?

ProWritingAid’s plagiarism database primarily covers English sources. For multilingual agencies, a complementary tool like LanguageTool (which supports 25+ languages) or a dedicated plagiarism service is recommended.

Yes. Sapling provides native plugins for both Zendesk and Intercom, as well as a universal API that works with any web‑based chat widget, enabling real‑time suggestions across all support channels.
