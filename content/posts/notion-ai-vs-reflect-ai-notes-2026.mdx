The AI [notes](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-podcasters-2026) category quietly split into two camps with opposite answers to one question: where does the intelligence live? Notion says it lives in the vendor's cloud, bundled into a $20 per seat Business plan with [agents](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) that work across your whole workspace. Reflect, relaunched as an open-source app, says it lives wherever you point your own API key, while your notes stay in local Markdown files. With Mem officially winding down (notes go read-only December 18, 2026, full shutdown January 15, 2027), this is now the real contest for people who think in notes.

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team knowledge base with AI on top | Notion AI | Agents, [meeting](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) notes, and enterprise search across shared [docs](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026) |
| Private thinking, local files | Reflect | Markdown on your disk, sync via iCloud or Git |
| AI spend [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) cap precisely | Reflect | Your own API key, billed by your provider at [cost](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) |
| Meeting notes with zero [setup](/posts/copy-ai-setup-guide-2026-complete-installation-step-by-step) | Notion AI | Built-in AI Meeting Notes on Business |
| Avoiding vendor lock-in | Reflect | Open source, plain files, walk away anytime |
| Non-technical users | Notion AI | No keys, no [config](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings), no sync decisions |

## Two philosophies, honestly stated

Notion AI is a bet that notes get smarter when the AI sees everything: your wikis, projects, meeting notes, and, through enterprise search, connected tools. On the Business plan, Notion Agent and Custom Agents can act across that corpus, and admins buy extra AI credits when usage outgrows the included 1,500 Standard and 500 Select monthly credits. The cost of that intelligence is that your [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) lives in Notion's cloud, in Notion's format, behind Notion's pricing.

Reflect is a bet that your notes should outlive any vendor. Notes are local Markdown files, sync runs through your own iCloud Drive or Git repo, and the AI [assistant](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-developers-2026) connects to your own provider key, with requests going directly to the provider and billed separately by them. Notes marked private are excluded from AI access entirely. The app is free on Mac; the paid part is the iPhone and iPad app.

## Pricing in plain [terms](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026)

Notion, as of November 2026: Free at $0, Plus at $10 per seat a month (with only a trial of Notion AI), and Business at $20 per seat a month, which is where the real AI lives: Notion Agent, Custom Agents, AI Meeting Notes, and Enterprise Search. So the honest [price](/posts/suno-vs-chatgpt-2026) of "Notion AI" for an individual is $20 a month, and for a ten-person [team](/posts/descript-for-teams-2026-review) it is $200.

Reflect's core app [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) nothing on the desktop, and AI usage is billed by whichever provider you connect, at their API rates. For a moderate personal [workflow](/posts/best-ai-copy-tools-for-translators-2026) that usually lands in the low single-digit dollars per month, but it is your responsibility to watch, because there is no subscription ceiling to catch you.

## Daily use: where the philosophies bite

In daily use, Notion AI is better at work you share: turning meeting notes into tasks, [drafting](/posts/best-ai-powered-legal-contract-drafting-tools-2026) status docs from a project database, asking "what did we decide about the pricing change" and getting an answer with sources from across the workspace. Its failure mode is gravity: the more it does, the harder it is to imagine leaving, and databases full of AI-enriched [content](/posts/ai-powered-content-gap-analysis-niche-blog-topics-2026) do not export cleanly into anything else.

Reflect is better at thinking you keep: daily notes, backlinks forming a graph of ideas, quick capture from a Chrome extension, [voice](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026)-note [transcription](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) through your own transcription provider. Its failure mode is friction: you manage sync, you manage keys, and there is no team layer at all. Collaboration means sharing files, not a workspace.

## Where each one fails

Notion AI fails the [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026)-sensitive and the budget-sensitive. AI features are effectively gated behind the $20 tier, credits add cost at scale, and zero data retention with LLM providers is an Enterprise feature, not a default.

Reflect fails anyone who needs structure beyond notes: no databases, no permissions, no admin console, no [mobile](/posts/best-ai-powered-push-notification-ab-testing-platforms-2026) parity on Android. And "bring your own key" is a real barrier for people who do not know what an API key is.

## Which should you pick

If notes are how your team coordinates, pay the $20 and live in Notion. If notes are how you think, and the phrase "my files, my sync, my [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial)" appeals to you, Reflect is the rare tool that gets cheaper and more trustworthy at the same time. The wrong choice is picking by feature list; pick by where you want your thinking to live in five years.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Notion AI free?

Not fully. As of November 2026, the Free and Plus [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) include only a trial of Notion AI; the full AI features (Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, Enterprise Search) require the Business plan at $20 per seat a month.

### Is Reflect free?

The Reflect [desktop app](/posts/ollama-review-2026) is free on Mac with local Markdown files; the iPhone and iPad app is a paid subscription. AI features use your own provider API key, billed separately by the provider.

### What happened to Mem?

Mem is winding down: no new paid subscriptions, notes become read-only on December 18, 2026, and the [service](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) shuts down on January 15, 2027.

### Which is more private, Notion AI or Reflect?

Reflect. Notes stay in local files you sync yourself, private notes are excluded from AI access, and AI requests go directly to your provider. Notion's zero data retention with LLM providers is an Enterprise-tier feature.
