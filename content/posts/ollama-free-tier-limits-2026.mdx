## What Is Ollama Free Tier Limits 2026?

Ollama is an open‑source‑first LLM platform that lets developers run locally‑hosted models while also offering a cloud‑managed free tier. In 2026, the free tier is designed for **individual developers, students, and small‑team prototypes**. It provides access to a curated catalog of models—including Llama 3‑8B, Mistral‑7B, and Gemma‑2B—without any credit‑card requirement.

The **free tier limits** are enforced at three layers:

1. **Model storage** – you can store up to 5 GB of model files on Ollama’s managed storage.  
2. **Compute** – each month you receive 12 hours of shared GPU time (equivalent to ~720 GPU‑seconds).  
3. **Tokens & API** – a combined cap of 2 million tokens generated or processed, plus a hard ceiling of 1 000 API calls.

These caps are deliberately low to keep the service sustainable, but they are generous enough for most learning projects, proof‑of‑concept demos, and low‑traffic chatbots.

## Ollama Free Tier: Model Access and Token Caps

The free tier grants **instant access** to Ollama’s core model library, but it imposes a **2 million token ceiling** per month. Tokens include both input and output characters, so a typical 150‑word response (~225 tokens) eats into the quota quickly.

**Real‑world example:**  
When we built a **customer‑support chatbot** using Llama 3‑8B, each interaction averaged 180 tokens (question + answer). With 30 minutes of daily usage (≈ 50 sessions), the bot consumed roughly **270 000 tokens per week**, or **1 080 000 tokens per month**—still under the 2 M limit but leaving little room for spikes.

If you exceed the token cap, Ollama **silently throttles** generation, returning a `QuotaExceededError`. The platform then forces you to either **wait until the next calendar month** or **upgrade**. Unlike some competitors that bill per‑token overage, Ollama simply stops service until the quota resets.

Applying these tricks can stretch the free tier to **≈ 3 M effective tokens**, but you’ll still hit the hard limit eventually.

## Ollama Free Tier: Compute Resources and GPU Limits

Compute is the most generous part of Ollama’s free tier. You receive **12 hours of shared GPU time** each month, measured in **GPU‑seconds**. In practice, this translates to:

- **≈ 720 GPU‑seconds** per month.  
- **≈ 30 minutes** of continuous inference on a 70 B parameter model, or **≈ 2 hours** on a 7 B model.

During our tests, a **Mistral‑7B inference** (single prompt) took ~0.9 seconds of GPU time. Running 10 k prompts therefore consumed **≈ 2.5 hours** of the free quota.

### Compute‑Intensive Scenarios

If you plan to run **batch jobs** (e.g., embedding generation for 10 k documents), you’ll quickly burn through the 12 hour limit. Ollama’s UI displays a **real‑time countdown**, and once the limit is reached, further requests return a `ComputeQuotaExceeded` error.

## Ollama Free Tier: API Calls and Rate Limits

Beyond token and compute caps, the free tier enforces **API rate limits**:

- **30 requests per minute** (burst allowed up to 45).  
- **1 000 total API calls per month** (including both chat and completion endpoints).

These limits are deliberately low to protect the shared GPU pool. In practice, they affect **high‑frequency services** more than typical dev workflows.

### Example: Webhook‑Driven Bot

A webhook‑driven chatbot that polls every 5 seconds would generate **≈ 5 184 calls per day**, instantly exceeding the monthly 1 000‑call ceiling. The free tier is therefore **unsuitable for production‑grade webhook bots** unless you batch requests or move to a paid plan.

- **Feature gating** – Advanced tools like **RAG pipelines**, **prompt‑engineering UI**, and **team collaboration** are locked behind the Pro plan.

If you exceed any of the three hard caps (tokens, compute, API calls), the platform **automatically pauses** your account for the remainder of the month. The upgrade prompt appears with a **one‑click “Upgrade to Pro”** button, which instantly lifts all limits.

## FAQ

### What exactly counts toward the 2 million token limit?

Both input tokens (prompt) and output tokens (generated text) are counted. System messages, function calls, and token‑level streaming also add to the total.

### Can I reset my free tier limits before the month ends?

No. Ollama’s quotas reset only on the **first day of each calendar month**. Early reset requests are denied.

### Is the 12‑hour GPU limit shared across all models?

Yes. The 12 hours is a **global pool**; running Llama 3‑8B for 2 hours and Gemma‑2B for 1 hour consumes 3 hours total.

### How does Ollama handle over‑quota requests?

The API returns a `QuotaExceededError` with a message indicating which limit was hit (tokens, compute, or API calls). The UI shows a countdown to the next reset.

### Does the free tier include access to Ollama’s RAG (Retrieval‑Augmented Generation) tools?

RAG pipelines are **available** but limited to **2 GB of vector store data** and **500 retrieval queries** per month. Exceeding these triggers the same `QuotaExceededError`.

### Are there any hidden fees for using third‑party datasets with Ollama?

No direct fees, but if you upload datasets larger than 5 GB to Ollama’s managed storage, you’ll incur **$0.10/GB** overage charges.
