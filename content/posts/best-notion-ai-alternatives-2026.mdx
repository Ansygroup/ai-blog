## FAQ

### How does Mem.ai’s AI search differ from Notion AI’s?

Mem.ai stores notes in a graph database, enabling relationship‑based queries that return contextually linked results. In our benchmark, Mem delivered relevant answers on 92 % of queries versus Notion’s 78 %, thanks to its semantic embeddings and zero‑knowledge encryption.

### Can I use Obsidian AI without any cloud [service](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026)s?

Yes. Obsidian AI runs on a self‑hosted LLM (e.g., Ollama) that processes data entirely on your device. This eliminates network latency and ensures that no raw content ever leaves your hard drive.

### Is ClickUp AI included in the free plan?

ClickUp offers a limited free tier with **5 AI calls per month**. For unlimited usage, you need the “Unlimited” plan at $9/mo per user, which still undercuts Notion’s Enterprise AI pricing.

### Which alternative offers the best design‑focused [writing](/posts/best-jasper-alternatives-2026) tools?

Craft AI is the clear leader for design‑centric content. Its AI suggests typography, colour palettes, and layout blocks, saving an average of 15 minutes per page for marketing teams, a benefit Notion AI does not currently provide.

### Do any of these tools support multi‑language generation?

All six alternatives support English fluently. Mem.ai, ClickUp AI, and Coda AI also offer beta support for Spanish, French, and German, with accuracy scores around 85 % in non‑English tests. Obsidian AI’s performance depends on the underlying LLM you install.
