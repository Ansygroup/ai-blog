## FAQ

### Which AI transcription tool is most accurate for English interviews?
Otter.ai and Sonix both achieve a **Word Error Rate (WER) around 4.7 %–4.9 %** on clean English audio, making them the most accurate AI options. For absolute precision, Rev.com’s human service stays at **~1 % WER**.

### Can I use Otter.ai for live press conferences?
Yes. Otter.ai’s **Live Captions** integrate with Zoom and Microsoft Teams, providing real‑time transcription and speaker identification during live events.

### How does Descript’s Overdub handle privacy?
Overdub stores voice models on U.S. servers and requires explicit consent from the speaker. Newsrooms handling confidential sources should review the privacy policy and consider on‑premise alternatives.

### Is there a free AI transcription tool that supports Spanish?
Trint offers a **30‑minute free trial** with Spanish support, and **Whisper** (open‑source) can be self‑hosted for unlimited free transcription, though it lacks a polished UI.

### What’s the best tool for collaborative editing of transcripts?
Sonix’s **real‑time multi‑user editor** and comment threads make it the [top choice for collaborative newsroom environments.

### Should I pay for a premium plan if I only need occasional transcripts?
If you average less than **200 minutes per month**, the free tiers of Otter.ai or Descript may be sufficient. For higher volumes or advanced features, the Premium [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) become cost‑effective.
