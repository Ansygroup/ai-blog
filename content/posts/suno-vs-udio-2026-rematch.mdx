The Suno and Udio rematch is no longer close on one question: which of them lets you take a finished song out of the app. As of November 2026, Suno does, on [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026), with commercial rights. Udio does not, because downloads have been paused since 29 October 2025 and its [terms](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) limit output to personal, non-commercial use. Last tested: 27 November 2026.

A [note](/posts/top-notebooklm-alternatives-2026-best-ai-note-taking-tools) on sources. Suno's own pricing page ([suno](/posts/best-ai-suno-tools-marketing-teams-2026).com/pricing) loaded for us with its feature table, but the dollar amounts did not render. Udio's pricing page returned nothing. The prices below come from a third party [comparison](/posts/ai-youtube-chapter-timestamp-generators-2026), [Dupple's Suno vs Udio](https://dupple.com/learn/suno-vs-udio), which says it read both vendor pages on 4 November 2026. Check the live pages before you pay.

## What Suno gives you

From Suno's own plan table: the free plan uses the v6-mini [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial). Pro and Premier unlock v6 and v6-wild, a priority queue with 10 concurrent generations, saved Voices, add vocals, add instrumental, [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) models tuned on your own [audio](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) and uploads up to 30 minutes. Free uploads stop at 8 minutes. Suno Studio, the stem and layering workspace, is on Premier and above, not Pro. Extract Stems and advanced editing are on Pro.

Suno's 10 August 2026 terms, quoted by Dupple, say a paying subscriber has commercial rights to any song they download. Free trial downloads do not carry that right.

## What Udio gives you

Udio's latest model is v1.5 Allegro, with no public [update](/posts/ai-regulation-update-creators-november-2026) since November 2025 according to Dupple. It settled with Universal Music Group and Warner Music Group, and the rebuilt licensed platform it promised has not shipped as of the source's November update. The result is a service where you can [generate](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) and share by link, but not export a file.

## Where each one fails

Suno fails on cap anxiety. Pro allows 20 downloads a month, so 2,500 credits do not equal 2,500 usable files. Add-on credits help, but you still budget downloads. Udio fails on the thing most people want from a [music](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) tool: an exportable track. A song you cannot download cannot go on a [video](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools), a [podcast](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) or a store.

## The label [deals](/posts/perplexity-comet-browser-2026-review), in plain English

In 2024 Universal, Sony and Warner sued both companies over training [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial). The Los Angeles Times reported on 21 September 2026 that settlements followed. Universal settled with Udio in October 2025 and Warner settled with both companies in November 2025. In September 2026 Suno released its first models built on licensed music from Warner Music Group, BMG and Believe. Suno was valued at $5.4 billion in that report.

For a [creator](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) this matters in one way. Licensed models are meant to let artists opt in and be credited and paid, and they may change what is allowed later. Today, the terms you agreed to when you downloaded a song are what count, so save a copy of them with each release.

## Test it yourself in 20 minutes

1. Open both free [plans and](/posts/langchain-api-cost-comparison-2026) enter the same [prompt](/posts/fixing-undefined-errors-ai-apps): a 90 second instrumental in a named genre and a short lyric.
2. Generate three versions on each. Note how many credits each used.
3. Try to export [the best](/posts/best-ai-tools-accountants-bookkeeping-2026) one. On Suno Free you get a limited number of trial downloads. On Udio you will find a share link only.
4. Write down where the song could legally go: personal listening, a monetized video, a store. If the answer is "only personal" on both, the [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) is not enough for your plan.
5. If Suno [wins](/posts/claude-vs-gpt-4-for-coding-2026-showdown-which-ai-wins), buy Pro for one month at $10 rather than a year, then compare your monthly download count with the 20 file cap.

## Which should you pick

Pick Suno Pro at $10 if you will publish anything. Move to Premier only if you want Studio. Keep Udio on the free plan to [watch](/posts/watch-mayday-air-disasters-on-tubi-its-free-2026-guide) whether downloads return, because its licensing deals suggest it plans to relaunch, but no shipped [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) backs that up yet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use Suno songs commercially?

Paid subscribers hold commercial rights to songs they download, according to Suno's August 2026 terms as quoted by Dupple. Free plan downloads do not include commercial rights.

### Can I download songs from Udio?

Not currently. Downloads have been suspended since 29 October 2025 and Udio's terms limit output to personal, non-commercial use.

### Which is cheaper, Suno or Udio?

Suno Pro is $8 a month and Udio Standard is $10 a month, per a November 2026 third party comparison. Verify live prices before buying.
