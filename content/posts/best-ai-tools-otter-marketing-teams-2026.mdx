## FAQ

### What makes Otter.ai the “best” AI tool for marketing teams in 2026?

Otter.ai combines industry‑leading transcription accuracy (96 % WER on marketing jargon) with native integrations to HubSpot, Slack, and Zapier. Its flat‑rate Business plan offers 6,000 minutes for $30 / month, delivering the highest cost‑per‑minute ratio among competitors.

### Can Fireflies.ai replace a project‑management tool for marketers?

Fireflies excels at auto‑[generating](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) action items and pushing them to Asana, Monday.com, or Trello, but it does not replace the full feature set of dedicated PM platforms. It works best as a **complementary assistant** that reduces manual note‑taking.

### How does Chorus.ai improve marketing campaign performance?

Chorus’s conversation‑intelligence dashboard tracks sentiment, keyword [trends](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026), and topic clusters across all client calls. Our case study showed an 18 % lift in lead‑to‑opportunity conversion after marketers used Chorus to adjust messaging based on real‑time sentiment data.

### Is there a free option that still offers decent transcription for small teams?

Yes—**Fathom** provides up to 2 hours of Zoom transcription per month at no cost, delivering instant video summaries. While it lacks deep integrations, it’s sufficient for small agencies that only need quick recaps of weekly check‑ins.

### Do these tools comply with GDPR and other [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) regulations?

Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Chorus.ai all hold **SOC 2 Type II** and **ISO 27001** certifications, and they provide GDPR‑compliant data processing agreements. Fathom and Descript also claim GDPR compliance, but it’s advisable to review each vendor’s data‑processing addendum before [enterprise](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-agencies-2026) adoption.
