## What Is Synthesia Free Tier Limits 2026?

Synthesia is a cloud‑based platform that turns [text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review) into realistic video using AI [avatar](/posts/heygen-setup-guide-2026)s. The **free tier** is a no‑credit‑card, no‑commitment entry point aimed at hobbyists, educators, and small‑business owners [testing](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) the technology. In 2026, the free tier caps **total monthly video runtime at 10 minutes**, split across a maximum of **5 separate video exports** (each export can’t exceed 2 minutes). The resolution is locked at **720p**, and every video carries a **Synthesia watermark** in the lower‑right corner.

Beyond runtime, the free tier restricts avatar selection to a static library of five pre‑made characters (e.g., “John,” “Emma,” “Olivia”). You cannot upload [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) avatars, use brand‑specific backgrounds, or access the advanced [script](/posts/ai-tutorial-youtube-shorts-scripts-thumbnails-2026)‑to‑speech fine‑tuning that paid tiers provide. The platform still offers a full‑featured script editor, [multilingual](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) text‑to‑speech (over 60 languages), and a library of stock footage, but each of these features is subject to the same minute‑cap.

These limits matter because they directly affect [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026)ion workflow. For example, a 2‑minute [tutorial](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) on a new software feature will consume **20 % of your monthly quota**. If you need to create a series of five such tutorials, you’ll hit the ceiling after just two videos. Understanding these constraints helps you decide whether the free tier can serve a pilot project or if you need to upgrade early.

## FAQ

### How many videos can I create per month on Synthesia’s free tier in 2026?
You can produce up to **5 separate videos** per month, each limited to **2 minutes**, totaling **10 minutes** of runtime.

### Does the free tier allow me to download videos without a watermark?
No. All free‑tier exports include a **Synthesia watermark** in the lower‑right corner. Removing it requires upgrading to at least the Starter plan.

### Can I use my own background images or custom branding on the free tier?
Backgrounds are limited to the built‑in stock library. Custom branding (logos, colors, custom avatars) is only available on paid plans.

### What happens when I reach the 10‑minute limit?
The “Create New Video” button becomes disabled until the **first day of the next month**. You can still edit existing projects but cannot render new videos.

### How does Synthesia’s free tier compare to D‑ID’s free offering?
D‑ID offers **15 minutes/month** and a **3‑minute per‑export limit**, but only **3 preset avatars** and a smaller language set. Synthesia provides more avatars and broader language support, though with fewer free minutes.

### Is there a way to get more free minutes without paying?
Synthesia occasionally runs **promo [code](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026)s** for additional minutes, typically tied to webinars or educational partnerships. These are not guaranteed and must be claimed during the promotion period.
