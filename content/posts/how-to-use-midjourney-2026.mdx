Crafting effective prompts is crucial for achieving desired results with Midjourney 2026. Here are some [tips](/posts/claude-prompt-guide-2026):

* **Use Descriptive [Language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026)**: Incorporate sensory details like colors, textures, and emotions to enrich the output.
