## What Is the Midjourney Desktop App?

The Midjourney desktop app is a standalone version of the popular AI art generator, designed specifically for Windows and macOS. It offers a more streamlined and intuitive experience compared to its web-based counterpart. With the desktop app, users can generate stunning artwork, edit images, and even create animations.
