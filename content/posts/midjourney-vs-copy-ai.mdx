### Advanced [Features](/posts/claude-api-guide): Inpainting, Outpainting, and Character Consistency
Midjourney isn't just a one-shot generator; it offers powerful](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/jasper-use-cases) editing [capabilities](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/claude-honest-review-2026)](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026):
