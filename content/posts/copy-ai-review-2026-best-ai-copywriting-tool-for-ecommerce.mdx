## What Is Copy.ai?

Copy.ai is an AI-powered copywriting tool designed to help [businesses](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-content-localization-for-global-e-commerce-businesses) generate high-quality content quickly. Copy.ai uses natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to create engaging content.
