Zoom AI Companion no longer exists under that name. As of November 2026, Zoom calls its built-in [meeting](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) features "Zoom AI" and sells a separate assistant called ZoomMate. That matters for this comparison, because the question is now Otter against whatever Zoom already includes in your plan. Last tested: 27 November 2026. Otter prices are from otter.ai/pricing, and the Zoom details come from Zoom's ZoomMate page and a third party review, [Scribbl](https://scribbl.co/post/zoom-ai-companion), which says it checked Zoom's pricing pages on 7 October 2026.

## The [verdict](/posts/descript-vs-chatgpt-2026) first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You already pay for Zoom Workplace | Zoom AI | Summaries, questions and My Notes are included with [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) with no cap |
| Meetings across Zoom, Teams and Google Meet | Otter | Basic lists Zoom, MS Teams and Google Meet |
| Free [tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) you can use all month | Otter | 300 monthly [transcription](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) minutes, against 3 hosted meetings on Zoom's free plan |
| Long meetings | Otter Business | Up to 4 hours a meeting |
| Agent-style follow-up [work](/posts/best-ai-tools-otter-freelancers-2026) | ZoomMate | Schedules, updates records, drafts slides, connects Salesforce and Jira |
| Cheapest paid individual plan | Otter | Pro is listed at $8.33 on annual billing |

## What Zoom [actually](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) sells now

Zoom retired the AI Companion name in June 2026, according to Scribbl, and [split](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026) it in two. Zoom AI is the set of built-in features: meeting [summary](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026), in-meeting questions, My Notes and writing help. ZoomMate is the AI assistant launched on 1 June 2026.

| Zoom [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) | Price | AI included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic (free) | $0 | Summaries for 3 hosted meetings a month, 3 My Notes uses, 20 AI queries |
| Pro | $16.99 a month, or $14.16 billed annually | Unlimited summaries, questions and My Notes |
| Business | $21.99 a month, or $18.33 billed annually | Unlimited |
| ZoomMate | $20 a month, or $16.67 billed annually | 2,200 AI credits a month, workflows across [apps](/posts/best-notebooklm-tools-2026) |

Free Zoom meetings also end at 40 minutes, per the same review. The AI features do not apply to users with Basic permissions on paid accounts.

## What Otter sells

| Plan | Price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Free | 300 monthly transcription minutes, 3 lifetime file imports, [live](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026) transcription, AI Chat |
| Pro | $16.99 monthly, $8.33 on annual billing | 1,200 in-app recording minutes, 10 monthly imports, up to 90 minutes a meeting |
| Business | $30 monthly, $19.99 on annual billing | Unlimited meetings and imports, up to 4 hours a meeting, activity logs |
| Enterprise | Custom | SSO, SCIM, HIPAA add-on, API and webhooks, Sales Notetaker |

Otter's page shows 51% savings on Pro annual billing and 33% on Business, [plus](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) a Black Friday promo, so confirm the final number at checkout.

## What ZoomMate adds

Zoom's own ZoomMate page lists delegated tasks such as [building](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026) a meeting agenda, drafting a slide deck, [generating](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) a document and sending follow-ups. It connects to Salesforce, Jira, Google Drive and OneDrive, runs scheduled routines like daily briefings, and lets you set custom meeting note formats. Zoom Paper turns meetings into Word-ready documents. Those are vendor claims, and we did not test them. The free ZoomMate Basic version is limited to 10 workflow runs a month in Zoom apps only, per Scribbl.

## What Otter adds beyond transcripts

Otter's Basic plan already lists AI Chat within and across meetings, AI meeting workflows, speaker identification and an MCP server. Its Enterprise plan adds AI Chat across Gmail, Google Drive, Notion and Jira, plus [video](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) replay for Zoom and Google Meet. If you want one searchable archive of every call, whatever the platform, that cross-meeting search is the reason to pick Otter.

## Where each one fails

Zoom AI fails outside Zoom. Its summaries work from Zoom's own [speech](/posts/best-ai-tools-elevenlabs-for-translators-2026) to text, and the features live in Zoom Workplace, so a Teams call is not covered. Its free allowance is [small](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026) at 3 hosted meetings a month, and Zoom's own help pages still describe the features as paid only, so confirm that your account has them.

Otter fails on limits. Pro stops at 1,200 in-app recording minutes and 90 minutes a meeting, which a long workshop can exceed. Basic's 3 lifetime imports are easy to use up in a day.

## Who should skip which

Skip Otter if your whole company lives in Zoom and already pays for Workplace, since you pay twice for [notes](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-podcasters-2026). Skip Zoom AI if you join calls on other [platforms](/posts/best-ai-powered-push-notification-ab-testing-platforms-2026) or want a standalone notes library. For more reading, see [Otter tools for journalists](/posts/best-ai-otter-tools-journalists-2026) and [transcription across Zoom, Teams and Google Meet](/posts/ai-transcription-showdown-zoom-teams-google-meet-2026).

## Test it yourself

1. Run one real 30 minute meeting with Otter Basic and Zoom's free meeting summary at the same time.
2. Compare the action items against your own notes.
3. Check who gets the summary, and whether guests see it.
4. Look for a meeting you hosted on another platform and see which tool can handle it.
5. Count minutes used on Otter Basic out of 300 and Zoom summaries out of 3.

## Which should you pick

If you pay for Zoom Workplace, use Zoom AI first, since it [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) nothing extra. Add Otter Pro at $8.49 on annual billing if you meet on several platforms or [need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) a searchable archive. Look at ZoomMate at $16.67 on annual billing only when you want it to act on meetings, not just summarize them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happened to Zoom AI Companion?

Zoom retired the name in June 2026. The built-in features are now called Zoom AI, and Zoom sells a separate assistant called ZoomMate.

### Is Zoom AI free?

Partly. A free account gets summaries for 3 hosted meetings a month, 3 My Notes uses and 20 AI queries. Paid Workplace plans include unlimited use.

### How much is Otter Pro?

Otter's pricing page lists Pro at $16.99 monthly and $8.49 on annual billing, with 1,200 in-app recording minutes.
