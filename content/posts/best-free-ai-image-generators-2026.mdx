### Adobe Firefly (Free Tier) — Best for Commercial-Safe Images

Adobe Firefly's free tier gives you 25 generative](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/how-to-use-generative-ai-for-prototyping](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/how-to-use-generative-ai-for-prototyping-mobile-app-designs)-mobile-app-designs) credits per month, which translates to roughly 25 standard images. For businesses worried about copyright — and the ongoing lawsuits against Stable Diffusion and Midjourney — Firefly is the safest bet in 2026. The image quality is competitive with Bing on most prompts, though it struggles with stylized illustration and tends toward a "stock photo" aesthetic.

If you only need a handful of high-quality, legally-clean AI images per month, Firefly's free tier is worth the monthly reset. If you need volume, look elsewhere.

### Google ImageFX (Imagen 3) — Best for Speed

Google's ImageFX runs on Imagen 3 and offers the fastest generation speed of any tool we tested. Most images return in under 4 seconds, which is roughly twice as fast as Bing and three times as fast than Leonardo. The free tier gives you unlimited generations with no daily cap, though a soft rate limit kicks in after roughly 50 fast generations per day, after which you'll see a "try again later" message for a few hours.

Image quality is excellent on photorealism and landscapes but weaker on text and stylized illustration. Imagen 3 is also the only model in our test that consistently produced racially diverse and inclusive outputs without explicit prompting, which we appreciated.

The interface is minimal — just a text box and a "Generate" button — and there's no negative prompt, no seed control, and no model selection. It's a beginner-friendly tool optimized for speed over flexibility.
