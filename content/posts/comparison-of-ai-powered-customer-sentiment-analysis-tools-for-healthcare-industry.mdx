AI-powered sentiment analysis tools use natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to analyze patient feedback from various sources, including social media, surveys, and reviews. This analysis helps healthcare providers identify areas for improvement and develop targeted strategies to enhance patient satisfaction.
