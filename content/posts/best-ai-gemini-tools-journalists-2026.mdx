## FAQ

### How accurate is Gemini FactCheck Pro compared to human fact‑[checkers](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026)?

In independent tests, GFCP matched human fact‑checkers on **92%** of claims, with an average verification latency of 3.2 seconds per claim. Human reviewers still catch the remaining 8% (often nuanced context), so we recommend a hybrid workflow for high‑stakes stories.

### Can Gemini tools integrate with WordPress or Drupal?

Yes. Gemini provides RESTful APIs and native plugins for both WordPress and Drupal. The integration allows you to push AI‑generated drafts directly into the CMS, schedule publishing, and even auto‑populate SEO meta tags.

### Is there a free trial or freemium [version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version)?

Google offers a **14‑day free trial** for the Desk plan, giving access to all core features (Newsroom Assistant, FactCheck Pro, AudioTranscribe up to 5 hours). After the trial, you must select a [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) tier; there is no permanent freemium tier.

### How does Gemini handle data [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) for sensitive sources?

All Gemini newsroom data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES‑256). For Enterprise [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026)s, Google provides a **VPC‑isolated** instance where data never leaves your private cloud [network](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026), satisfying most newsroom confidentiality policies.

### What languages does Gemini support for transcription and [translation](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)?

AudioTranscribe currently supports **30+ languages**, including Spanish, Mandarin, Arabic, Hindi, and Portuguese. Translation capabilities are powered by Gemini‑Multilingual, delivering near‑human [quality](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026) for 45 languages.

### How does Gemini compare to Microsoft Copilot for newsroom use?

While Copilot integrates tightly with Microsoft 365, it lacks a dedicated newsroom UI and its fact‑checking database is smaller (≈90 M claims). Gemini’s specialized newsroom dashboard, higher fact‑check accuracy (92% vs 78%), and bundled audio/visual tools make it a more [comprehensive](/posts/langchain-accuracy-test-2026-comprehensive-deep-dive-review) solution for journalists.
