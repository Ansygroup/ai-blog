## Features and Limitations of Writesonic's Free Version

Writesonic's free version offers 10,000 words per month, making it a great option for [small](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026)-scale content creators. However, it has limitations, such as limited access to advanced features like AI article writer and content rewriter. The free version also includes:

* Access to basic writing tools like paragraph writer and sentence writer
* Limited SEO [optimization](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) capabilities
* Support for 5 languages

![Advanced Features of Writesonic's Paid Version](/images/illustrations/writesonic-vs-free-version-illo1.svg)
## Advanced Features of Writesonic's Paid Version

Writesonic's paid version offers advanced features like:

* AI article writer: generates high-quality articles with a single click
* Content rewriter: rewrites existing content to make it more engaging and readable
* SEO optimization: optimizes content for [search](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) engines to improve rankings
* Support for 15 languages

The paid version starts at $29/month for 20,000 words, making it a great option for large-scale content creators.

The main difference is the number of words per month and access to advanced features. The free version offers 10,000 words per month, while [the paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) version offers more words and advanced features like AI article writer and content rewriter.

Yes, [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) upgrade to the paid version at any time. Writesonic offers flexible pricing plans to suit your needs.

### Is Writesonic better than Jasper?
Both Writesonic and Jasper are high-quality AI writing tools. Writesonic offers a more user-friendly interface, while Jasper offers a more generous free plan.
