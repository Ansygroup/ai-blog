## What Is Writesonic?

Writesonic is an AI-powered writing tool that helps users generate high-quality content, including articles, blog posts, and [social](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) media posts. With its [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026)-friendly interface and advanced features, Writesonic has become a popular choice among [writers](/posts/writesonic-pricing-explained), marketers, and content creators.

## Features and Limitations of Writesonic's Free Version

Writesonic's free version offers 10,000 words per month, making it a great option for [small](/posts/copy-ai-for-small-business)-scale content creators. However, it has limitations, such as limited access to advanced features like AI article [writer](/posts/chatgpt-vs-jasper) and content rewriter. The free version also includes:

* Access to basic writing tools like paragraph writer and sentence writer
* Limited SEO optimization [capabilities](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026)
* Support for 5 languages

## Advanced Features of Writesonic's Paid Version

Writesonic's paid version offers advanced features like:

* AI article writer: generates high-quality articles with a single [click](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026)
* Content rewriter: rewrites existing content to make it more engaging and readable
* SEO optimization: optimizes content for [search](/posts/perplexity-vs-google-search-2026) engines to improve rankings
* Support for 15 languages

The paid version starts at $29/month for 20,000 words, making it a great option for large-scale content creators.
