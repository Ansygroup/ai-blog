## Introduction to ChatGPT Pricing
ChatGPT is an AI chatbot developed by OpenAI, offering various pricing plans to accommodate different user needs. The free plan is ideal for casual users, while the paid plan, ChatGPT Plus, is geared towards power users and businesses. ChatGPT Plus offers additional features like priority access and faster response times.

## Understanding ChatGPT Tokens
ChatGPT uses a token-based system to measure usage. Each token represents a unit of text, with the free plan limited to 10,000 tokens per month. This translates to approximately 5,000 to 10,000 words, depending on the complexity of the input. For users who exceed this limit, ChatGPT Plus offers 100,000 tokens per month.

## Comparing ChatGPT to Competitors
ChatGPT faces competition from other AI chatbots like Google's Bard and Meta's Llama. Bard offers a similar pricing structure, with a free plan and a paid plan starting at $10/month. Llama, on the other hand, offers a more affordable pricing model, with a free plan and a paid plan starting at $5/month.

For businesses, ChatGPT offers custom pricing plans tailored to their specific needs. These plans include additional features like dedicated support, advanced security, and priority access. Businesses can contact ChatGPT's sales team to discuss their requirements and receive a custom quote.
