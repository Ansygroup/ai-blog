## Introduction to ChatGPT Pricing
ChatGPT is an AI chatbot developed by OpenAI, offering various pricing [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) to accommodate different user needs. The free plan is ideal for casual users, while the paid plan, ChatGPT Plus, is geared towards power users and businesses. ChatGPT Plus offers additional features [like](/posts/best-ai-tools-grammarly-for-accountants-2026) priority access and faster response times.
![Understanding ChatGPT Tokens](/images/illustrations/chatgpt-pricing-explained-illo2.svg)
## Understanding ChatGPT Tokens
ChatGPT uses a token-based system to measure usage. Each token represents a unit of [text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review), with the free plan limited to 10,000 tokens per month. This translates to approximately 5,000 to 10,000 words, depending on the complexity of the input. For users who exceed this limit, ChatGPT Plus offers 100,000 tokens per month.

## Comparing ChatGPT to Competitors
ChatGPT faces [competition](/posts/kaggle-review-2026-top-data-science-competition-platform) from other AI chatbots like Google's Bard and Meta's Llama. Bard offers a similar pricing structure, with a free plan and a paid plan starting at $10/month. Llama, on the other hand, offers a more affordable pricing [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial), with a free plan and a paid plan starting at $5/month.
