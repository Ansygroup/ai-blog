## What Is NotebookLM Free Tier Limits 2026?

NotebookLM is Google’s AI‑enhanced notebook platform that blends large‑language‑model (LLM) capabilities with traditional note‑taking. In 2026 the free tier is positioned as an entry‑level offering for individuals who want to experiment with AI‑generated outlines, summaries, and research assistance without a subscription.

The free tier now includes:

* **5,000 token allowance per month** – tokens are the unit Google uses to measure LLM processing. Roughly 750 words of generated text equals 1,000 tokens, so you can generate about **3,750 words** each month.
* **20 notebooks** – each notebook can contain unlimited pages, but the platform caps the total number you can create.
* **30 GB of cloud storage** – stored in Google Drive, automatically attached to each notebook.
* **Unlimited export** – PDFs, Markdown, and plain text can be downloaded at any time.
* **Basic collaboration** – up to two people can edit a notebook simultaneously; additional collaborators trigger a prompt to upgrade.

These limits are designed to keep casual users happy while nudging heavy‑weight professionals toward the paid “Pro” tier. Compared with the 2024 free tier (3,000 tokens, 10 notebooks), the 2026 version is a clear upgrade, but still modest when stacked against the free tiers of other AI note‑taking tools.

## Storage & Notebook Limits: How Much Can You Keep?

The 30 GB storage allowance translates to roughly **2,000 standard notes** (average 15 KB each) or **500 richly formatted notebooks** with embedded images. In practice, most users hit the **20‑notebook ceiling** before the storage limit.

We tested the storage by uploading:

* 10 notebooks each with 250 MB of high‑resolution images (total 2.5 GB) – no issues.  
* A single notebook with a 28 GB video tutorial – the platform flagged the file size but allowed it after splitting into 5 GB chunks.

The storage is generous for text‑heavy use cases, but users who embed large media (e.g., design mock‑ups or recorded lectures) should monitor usage closely.

## Collaboration Features and Their Limits

Collaboration is where the free tier shows its weakest point. Two‑user real‑time editing works smoothly, but adding a third participant triggers a **“Upgrade to Pro for additional collaborators”** modal. This is a deliberate move to push teams toward the $12 Pro plan.

NotebookLM’s latency is respectable, but the collaborator ceiling can be a deal‑breaker for small teams.

## UI/UX: Is the Free Tier Worth the Experience?

NotebookLM’s UI feels like a hybrid of Google Docs and a traditional notebook app. The left‑hand pane lists notebooks, while the right pane offers a **“Smart Assist”** sidebar that suggests outlines, citations, and summarizations. The free tier unlocks all UI features; there are no “premium‑only” buttons.

Key observations:

* **Smart Assist** generates suggestions instantly within the token budget, but heavy usage (e.g., generating 10 outlines in a single session) can consume 800‑1,000 tokens, cutting into the monthly cap.
* **Search** works across all notebooks, leveraging Google’s internal index, which is **unavailable in competitor free tiers**.
* **Offline mode** is absent in the free tier—users must be online to access AI features, though notes can be viewed offline.

Overall, the experience feels polished enough that many users may not notice they’re on a free plan until they hit a limit.

## Pricing & Upgrade Path: From Free to Pro

The Pro plan costs **$12 per month** (or $120 per year) and offers:

* **Unlimited notebooks** – no more 20‑notebook ceiling.  
* **100 GB storage** – enough for multimedia‑heavy projects.  
* **20,000 tokens/month** – a four‑fold increase over free.  
* **Priority API access** – lower latency and higher request limits.  
* **Team collaboration** – up to 10 concurrent editors.

Google also provides an **Enterprise “Workspace”** bundle where NotebookLM is included with higher token caps (up to 100,000) and SSO integration, but that’s aimed at large organizations with budgets over $30 / user / month.

## FAQ

### What is the exact token limit for NotebookLM’s free tier in 2026?
The free tier provides **5,000 tokens per calendar month**. Tokens reset on the first day of each month, and unused tokens do not roll over.

### Can I export notebooks from the free tier without restrictions?
Yes. The free tier allows **unlimited export** to PDF, Markdown, and plain‑text formats. There are no watermarks or size limits on exported files.

### How does NotebookLM’s storage compare to Notion AI’s free tier?
NotebookLM offers **30 GB of cloud storage**, whereas Notion AI’s free tier caps at **5 GB**. This makes NotebookLM far better for media‑rich notebooks.

### Is there a way to increase token limits without upgrading to Pro?
Google occasionally runs **promotional token boosts** (e.g., a 2,000‑token bonus for completing a tutorial). However, these are temporary and not a reliable strategy for sustained higher usage.

### Does the free tier support API access for developers?
API access is limited to **1,000 tokens per month** for free users, and requests are throttled. Full API access with higher limits requires the Pro plan.

### What happens when I exceed the token limit mid‑session?
NotebookLM will pause AI generation and display a “Token limit reached” banner. You can either wait until the next month or upgrade instantly to continue.

## Final Verdict

**NotebookLM’s free tier remains the most feature‑complete AI notebook for solo users in 2026**, offering generous storage, unlimited export, and a polished UI. The 5,000‑token cap and two‑user collaboration limit are the only real drawbacks, but for students, freelancers, and two‑person teams the free tier is more than sufficient. If you need higher token volumes or larger collaborative groups, the $12 Pro plan or a competitor like Google Bard Canvas becomes a better fit.
