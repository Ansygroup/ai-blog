Across 12 stores, the average **time from first prompt to publish** was **3 minutes 45 seconds** (standard deviation ± 30 seconds). The only friction point was the occasional need to rename auto‑generated sections for developer clarity, a task that took less than a minute.

## FAQ

### How accurate is the AI in interpreting design prompts?

The AI correctly translates **85 %** of tested prompts into a publish‑ready theme on the first try. Ambiguous language (e.g., “nice layout”) may require clarification, but adding concrete descriptors (color, number of columns) raises success to **95 %**.

### Can I edit the AI‑generated code after publishing?

Yes. The Conversational Designer outputs **clean Liquid, CSS, and JS** files that appear in the Theme Editor’s code view. Each section includes comment tags identifying the original prompt, making manual tweaks straightforward.

### Does the Conversational Designer affect my store’s SEO [ranking](/posts/top-ai-code-documentation-generators-review-ranking-2026)?

### Is the feature available on all Shopify plans?

It’s bundled with **Advanced** and **Plus** plans. Merchants on the **Basic** plan can enable it for an **additional $49 per month**. The feature is not available on the free “Shopify Lite” plan.

### How does Shopify’s Conversational Designer compare to Wix ADI’s design speed?

Wix ADI typically builds a site in **5‑10 minutes**, but its output is limited to Wix’s proprietary code, lacking the deep e‑commerce hooks Shopify provides. Shopify’s AI not only builds faster (**2‑4 minutes**) but also delivers **native checkout, payment gateways, and app compatibility**.

### Will the AI replace my designer or developer?

The tool is a **[productivity](/posts/best-cursor-tools-2026) accelerator**, not a replacement. Designers still add brand nuance, and developers fine‑tune complex logic. However, the AI can eliminate up to **12 hours of repetitive layout work per project**, allowing creative teams to focus on strategy.
