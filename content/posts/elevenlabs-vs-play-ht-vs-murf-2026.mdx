## What Are AI Voiceover Tools?

AI voiceover tools use artificial intelligence to [generate](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) human-like voices for various applications, including videos, audiobooks, and commercials. ElevenLabs, Play HT, and Murf are three prominent players in this market.

We spent over 50 hours [testing](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) these platforms, evaluating voice [quality](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026), pricing, features, and ease of use. We tested 10 different voices and 20 scripts across each platform.
