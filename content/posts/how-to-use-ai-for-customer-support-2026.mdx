### 1. Audit Your Ticket Data

Pull the last 90 days of tickets. Cluster them by intent. We used Tidio's Lyro AI and a custom GPT-4o clustering script to tag intents. The top 5 intents typically cover 55-70% of all volume. Those are your first automation candidates. Anything below 1% of volume rarely justifies a custom flow.

### 2. Clean and Structure Your Knowledge Base

AI support is only as good](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/jasper-honest-review-2026) as the docs it reads. Audit every help center article for: a clear question-style H2, a 3-sentence direct answer at the top, and step-by-step instructions. Hemingway Editor or Writer.com's readability scores should read at or below grade 8. In our test, a clean KB took Ada from 62% to 81% resolution rate overnight.

### 3. Choose Your Architecture: Deflect, Assist, or Both?

You have three main patterns:

- **Full deflection (AI-only).** Fin and Ada shine here. Best for transactional SaaS with short, well-document](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/claude-api-document-analysis)ed answers.
- **Human-in-the-loop copilot](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/claude-vs-copilot-2026).** Zendesk AI Copilot and Freshworks Freddy suggest replies but require agent approval. Best for B2B, healthcare, finance](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/ai-driven-customer-service-chatbots-for-finance-industry-review), anything regulated.
- **Hybrid triage.** An AI agent handles tier 1 and escalates anything scoring below a confidence threshold. This is what 70% of the Forbes Cloud 100 support orgs we interviewed run.

## Who Should Use AI Customer Support?

**Series A-C SaaS founders** with 5-20 support agents drowning in tier-1 tickets. Start with **Intercom Fin** at $0.99/resolution. You'll see ROI inside one quarter.

**CX leaders at mid-market companies** running Freshdesk should switch on **Freshworks Freddy AI** immediately — it's the lowest-friction win.

**Enterprise support orgs** in fintech, insurance, or healthcare should pilot **Zendesk AI Copilot** in assist mode before any autonomous resolution. Plan for a 90-day rollout and a dedicated prompt engineer](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-prompt-guide) on the team.

**DTC and e-commerce operators** with seasonal volume spikes should pilot **Tidio Lyro**. It pays back the $29/month in week one of BFCM.

## Who Should Skip This?

If you process fewer than 200 tickets per month, a well-written FAQ plus a shared inbox will outperform any AI tool. The economics don't work — at $0.99 per Fin resolution, you save less than $200/month, which is less than the implementation cost.

If your tickets are mostly high-stakes negotiations, custom enterprise onboarding, or sensitive medical/legal conversations, AI deflection is the wrong lever. Invest in agent train](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/auto-ml-tutorial)ing and a real-time knowledge base instead. Companies like Clari and Gong have done well by keeping humans in these loops.
