## Top Copy.ai Cheaper Alternatives

### Writesonic

Writesonic is a popular AI content generation tool that offers a free plan with 10,000 words/month. Its [premium](/posts/jasper-for-students) plans start at $25/month for 50,000 words. Writesonic provides a range of features, including:

- 100+ content templates
- Support for 15 languages
- Integration with popular [platforms](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-online-course-platforms-for-education) like WordPress and [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Docs

### Rytr

Rytr is another affordable option, with a premium plan costing $29/month for 50,000 characters. Rytr offers:

- 30+ content templates
- Support for 5 languages
- Integration with Google Docs and [Microsoft](/posts/microsoft-project-polaris-copilot-2026) Word

### Jasper AI

Jasper AI charges $29/month for 20,000 words, making it a [potential](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings-2026) [budget](/posts/midjourney-cheaper-alternative)-friendly option. Jasper AI features:

- 50+ content templates
- Support for 10 languages
- Integration with popular platforms like Google Docs and Slack
