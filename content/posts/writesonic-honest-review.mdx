## What Is Writesonic?

## Pricing Overview

Writesonic offers two pricing plans: Starter ($15/month) and Pro ($45/month). The Starter plan includes basic features, such as content generation, article rewriting, and grammar checking. The Pro plan adds advanced features, such as AI-powered content suggestions, [keyword](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) research, and SEO [optimization](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial).
