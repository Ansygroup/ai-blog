Gamma and Beautiful.ai both make slide decks from a prompt, but they price and meter AI differently. Gamma gives you a free tier with credits and sells monthly credit pools. Beautiful.ai skips the free plan, offers a 14 day trial and sells unlimited AI generation on a flat plan. As of November 2026, that difference decides which one is cheaper for you, and it depends on how many decks you make. Last tested: 27 November 2026, from both vendors' live pricing pages.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trying AI slides for free | Gamma | Free plan gives 400 credits at signup, exports to PDF, PPTX, PNG and Google Slides |
| Heavy deck production on one plan | Beautiful.ai | Pro lists unlimited AI content generation |
| Cheapest paid plan | Gamma | Plus is 8 euros a month, against Beautiful.ai Pro at $14.50 |
| Small team of three | Beautiful.ai | Teams is $40 a month annual and includes 3 users |
| Websites and docs, not just decks | Gamma | Free lists presentations, docs, websites, social and images |
| Strict template layouts | Beautiful.ai | Over 300 Smart Slide layouts with auto-formatting |

## Gamma pricing

| Plan | Price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 400 credits at signup, up to 10 slides per prompt, import PDF and PPTX |
| Plus | 8 euros a month, 96 euros billed annually | 1,500 monthly credits, up to 100 slides per prompt, no Gamma branding |
| Pro | 216 euros billed annually | 4,000 monthly credits, premium image models, custom branding, analytics, up to 10 custom domains |
| Ultra | 77.33 euros a month, 928 euros billed annually | 20,000 monthly credits, the most advanced models, up to 100 custom domains |

Our arithmetic: 216 euros a year for Pro is 18 euros a month. The page showed the annual total, not the monthly figure, so we derived it. Credits drive cost. Gamma says the number used varies with the action, the AI model and the length of the generation, so we cannot say how many decks a pool covers. Gamma lists Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Flux, Recraft, Ideogram, Luma and Leonardo among its models.

## Beautiful.ai pricing

| Plan | Price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | $14.50 a month, billed annually | Unlimited AI generation, 300+ Smart Slide layouts, custom themes, AI image generation, translation |
| Teams | $40 a month annual, $50 monthly, includes 3 users | Live collaborative editing, version control, locked slides, shared asset library, analytics |
| Enterprise | Custom | SSO, SCIM, audit logs, brand guardrails, SOC 2 Type II |

There is a 14 day free trial of Pro. Beautiful.ai says live demos are best for enterprise teams.

## Features beyond the prompt

Gamma's paid tiers add branding and publishing controls: Plus removes Gamma branding, Pro adds custom branding, analytics, advanced sharing and up to 10 custom domains. The page says Gamma is SOC 2 Type II compliant and that taxes may apply by location.

Beautiful.ai's Pro lists a custom theme builder with colors, fonts, logos and footers, a pre-set theme library, dynamic data visualizations and slide transition animations. Teams adds version control with locked slides, live data linking to charts and tables, viewer and slide analytics, engagement alerts, and slide translation across 100+ languages. Enterprise adds SSO, SCIM, audit logs and configurable brand guardrails, and lists CCPA, PCI, SOC 2 Type II and GDPR certification.

## How to estimate your own cost

Count the decks you make in a month and how long they are. On Gamma, generate one typical deck and read the credit balance before and after. Divide the plan's monthly credits by that number to get decks per month. If the answer is less than your need, compare the next plan or buy credits. On Beautiful.ai there is no balance to read, so the check is simply whether you will use it enough to justify $14.50 each month.

## Where each one fails

Gamma fails on predictability. Credits vary by model and length, and the free plan's 400 credits are a one time signup grant, not a monthly allowance, so you can run out in a day. Plus caps a single prompt at 100 slides, which is plenty, but premium image models need Pro.

Beautiful.ai fails on openness. There is no free plan, and the Pro price shown is annual only. Its pricing page lists presentations features only, with no websites or docs, so check that it covers your formats.

## Who should skip which

Skip Gamma if you hate metered credits or need guaranteed unlimited generation. Skip Beautiful.ai if you want a permanent free tier or want to publish webpages. ## Test it yourself

1. Write a three line brief for a ten slide deck on a topic you know well.
2. Generate it in Gamma Free and in a Beautiful.ai trial.
3. Export both to PowerPoint and open them in PowerPoint or Google Slides.
4. Fix factual errors and count how many edits each needed.
5. Check how many Gamma credits that deck used, then multiply by the decks you make a month.

## Which should you pick

Pick Gamma Plus at 8 euros if you make a few decks a month and want a free start. Pick Beautiful.ai Pro at $14.50 if you build many decks and want tidy layouts without counting credits. For a small team, compare Beautiful.ai Teams at $40 with Gamma Pro at 18 euros per person. Prices were read on 27 November 2026.
