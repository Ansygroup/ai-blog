## FAQ

### How does Grammarly calculate character usage for billing?

Grammarly counts every UTF‑8 character sent to the API, including spaces and punctuation. The count resets monthly, and any overage is billed at the tier’s per‑character rate. You can retrieve a usage [summary](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) via the `/usage` endpoint in the dashboard.

### Is there a free tier for the Grammarly API?

Grammarly offers a limited sandbox with 100 K characters per month for [evaluation](/posts/gamma-accuracy-test-2026-best-benchmarks-for-ai-models), but it does not include plagiarism detection or real‑time latency guarantees. The sandbox is intended for [testing](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) only; production use requires a paid plan.

### Can I get a discount for annual commitment?

Yes. Enterprise sales reps typically provide a 10–15% discount for a 12‑month upfront commitment, and an additional 5% for multi‑year contracts. Negotiated rates can bring the per‑character cost down to $0.0005 for volumes above 100 M characters.

### How does the plagiarism detection cost compare across providers?

Grammarly charges $0.0002 per character for plagiarism, while ProWritingAid adds $0.00015 per character as an optional add‑on. LanguageTool does not include a native plagiarism engine; users must integrate third‑party services, which can add $0.0001–$0.0003 per character depending on the provider.

### What latency can I expect [for real](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-real-estate-agents)‑time editing?

Grammarly guarantees sub‑80 ms response times for enterprise [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026), ProWritingAid averages 120 ms, and LanguageTool averages 150 ms on its cloud tier. Latency can increase during peak traffic if you exceed the rate limits of your plan.

### Are there hidden fees for support or over‑usage?

Grammarly includes support in enterprise contracts; lower tiers must purchase a support add‑on ($199/month). ProWritingAid’s business plan bundles email support, while LanguageTool’s cloud tier includes basic email support at no extra cost. Over‑usage is billed [automatic](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)ally at the listed overage rates—no surprise surcharges.
