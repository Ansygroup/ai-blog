## What Is Cursor Pricing Breakdown 2026?

The “cursor pricing breakdown 2026” refers to the detailed cost structure that Cursor, the AI‑driven [code](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) completion platform, released in March 2026. Unlike its 2023 version, which bundled most features into a single flat fee, the 2026 model separates core usage (tokens), collaboration tools, and premium add‑ons.  

A *token* is a unit of text processed by Cursor’s LLM; roughly 4 characters or one word. The pricing now scales with token consumption, mirroring how OpenAI and Anthropic price their APIs. This shift allows hobbyists to stay free while giving [power user](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings)s transparent, pay‑as‑you‑go options.  

In practice, a senior engineer [writing](/posts/copy-ai-vs-notion-ai) 2 k lines of code per week will consume about 150 k tokens, landing them squarely in the Pro tier’s 250 k token allotment. Teams that generate large codebases, run batch refactors, or integrate Cursor into CI pipelines will quickly exceed that and need the Team or Enterprise [plans](/posts/copilot-api-cost-comparison-2026).

## FAQ

### How many tokens does the [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) actually give me?

The free tier provides **10 k tokens per month**. Once you exceed that, the platform temporarily disables autocomplete until the next billing cycle or you [upgrade](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version).

### Can I get a discount for annual billing?

Yes. Cursor offers a **15 % discount** on Pro and Team plans when you pay annually. For example, Pro drops from $19/mo to $16.15/mo, saving $34.20 per year per user.

### Is there a hidden cost for using the VS Code extension?

No. The VS Code extension is included in all tiers at no extra charge. Only token consumption and optional add‑ons (Premium Model, data retention) affect the bill.

### How does Cursor’s token pricing compare to Claude 2 for a 1 M token month?

Cursor’s Team plan includes 1 M tokens for $49, effectively **$0.000049 per token**. Claude 2 charges $0.00012 per token after its 200 k free quota, resulting in a cost of **$120** for the same usage—more than double Cursor’s price.

### Does Cursor offer a money‑back guarantee?

Cursor provides a **30‑day money‑back guarantee** on Pro and Team subscriptions. Enterprise contracts are negotiated individually and typically include performance‑based clauses rather than a standard refund policy.

### What happens to my code snippets if I downgrade from Enterprise to Team?

When downgrading, any data stored beyond the Team plan’s 30‑day retention window is **archived for 90 days** before permanent deletion, giving you a grace period to export or migrate your assets.
