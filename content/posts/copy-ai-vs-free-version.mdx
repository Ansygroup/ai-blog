## Brand Voices and Infobases: Scaling Personalization

One of the biggest struggles with generic AI engines like ChatGPT is making the output sound like *you*. Copy.ai solves this with two features](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/best-jasper-desktop-app-review-2026): **Brand Voices** and **Infobases**.

### Brand Voices
A Brand Voice is a saved profile that analyzes your existing [writing](/posts/best-ai-writing-tools-for-fiction-2026) style (such as your blog posts, emails, or brand guidelines) and instructs the AI to mimic that exact tone, vocabulary, and sentence structure in every [future](/posts/notion-ai-review-2026) output.
- **Free Version:** You are limited to **1 Brand Voice**. This is fine if you are a solopreneur running a single brand. But if you run a side hustle, have a personal brand alongside your main [business](/posts/copy-ai-for-small-business), or write for different audiences, one voice is incredibly limiting.
- **Paid [Version](/posts/writesonic-vs-free-version):** You get **unlimited Brand Voices** on the Starter plan and above. This is a game-changer for agencies or [freelancers](/posts/ai-tools-for-freelancers-2026) who write for multiple clients, allowing you to instantly switch from a "quirky B2C [SaaS](/posts/build-ai-saas-weekend-2026) tone" to a "conservative B2B financial tone" with a single dropdown selection.

### Infobases
An Infobase is a digital filing cabinet where you store key facts about your business, product [features](/posts/gemini-api-guide), pricing, or team. Instead of pasting your product details into every single [prompt](/posts/copy-ai-prompt-guide), you can simply type `#OurProduct` in the chat, and Copy.ai will pull the accurate [data](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial) from your Infobase.
- **Free Version:** Limited to **1 Infobase entry**. You can save one product description](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-automated-youtube-video-description-writing-for-seo) or one company bio.
- **Paid [Version](/posts/writesonic-vs-free-version):** Offers **unlimited Infobase entries**. You can upload your entire product catalog, team bios, brand FAQs, and case studies, making the AI highly contextualized and accurate.
