## What Are AI-Driven Website Accessibility Auditing Tools?

AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools use artificial intelligence to scan websites for accessibility issues, providing recommendations for remediation. These tools help e-commerce businesses ensure their sites are compliant with regulations like the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and the European Union's Accessibility Act.

While AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools can help ensure compliance, they are not a substitute for human oversight and manual testing.
