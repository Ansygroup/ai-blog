## What Are AI-Driven Website Accessibility Auditing Tools?

AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools use artificial intelligence to scan websites for accessibility [issues](/posts/otter-limitations-2026-key-issues-workarounds), providing recommendations for remediation. ### What are some common accessibility issues found on e-commerce sites?

### Can AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools ensure compliance with regulations?
While AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools can help ensure compliance, they are not a substitute for human oversight and manual testing.
