## What Are AI-Driven Website Accessibility Auditing Tools?

AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools use artificial [intelligence](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-data-visualization-for-ecommerce-business-intelligence) to scan websites for accessibility issues, providing recommendations for remediation. While AI-driven website accessibility auditing tools can help ensure compliance, they are not a substitute for human oversight and manual testing.
