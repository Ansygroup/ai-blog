Midjourney is a popular choice among artists and designers. It offers a free plan with 100,000 images and a user-friendly interface. ## Firefly Review

Firefly is a close competitor to Midjourney. It provides a free plan with 10,000 images and a more extensive dataset of 100 million images. Firefly's AI model is more advanced, but its interface can be overwhelming for beginners. Firefly's pricing plan starts at $9.99/month.

## Dalle [2026 Review](/posts/copy-ai-for-small-business)
