## What Are AI Image Generators?

AI image generators use machine learning algorithms to create images from text prompts. These algorithms analyze patterns in images and text to generate unique, high-quality images. Midjourney, Firefly, and DALL-E are leading AI image generators that have gained significant attention in recent years.
