## Midjourney: Features and Pricing

Midjourney is a popular AI image generator that offers high-quality images at an affordable price. With a resolution of up to 1024x1024, Midjourney is ideal for users who need detailed images. The tool is available for $10/month, with a free trial option.

## Firefly: The Budget-Friendly Option

Firefly is a budget-friendly AI image generator that offers a free plan with limited features. The tool generates images in 1-2 minutes, making it a great option for users who need quick results. Firefly also offers a paid plan for $5/month, which includes additional features like higher resolution and more prompts.

## Dalle 2: Advanced Editing Features

Dalle 2 is an advanced AI image generator that offers a range of editing features, including layer support and masking. The tool is ideal for users who need precise control over their images. Dalle 2 is available for $20/month, with a free trial option.
