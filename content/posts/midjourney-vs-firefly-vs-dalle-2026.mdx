## Introduction to AI Image Generation
AI image generators create realistic images from text prompts. Midjourney, Firefly, and Dalle are popular options. Each has its strengths and weaknesses.

## The Midjourney Advantage
Midjourney is user-friendly and produces high-quality images. It's ideal for beginners and professionals alike. With Midjourney, you can generate images up to 1,024x1,024 pixels for $10/month.

## Firefly's Unique Selling Point
Firefly stands out with its 15 styles, including realism, impressionism, and abstract. This versatility makes it perfect for artists and designers. Firefly's pricing starts at $5/month for 100 prompts.

## Dalle's API Capabilities
Dalle's API is geared towards developers and businesses. It offers custom integration and pricing starting at $0.00085 per prompt. Dalle's API is ideal for large-scale image generation projects.
