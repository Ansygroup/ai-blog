## Licensing, Commercial Rights, and Ethical Guardrails

All three platforms now provide commercial‑use licenses, but the fine print differs:

* **Midjourney**: Users retain ownership of generated images, but must credit Midjourney when publishing on public platforms. The “Standard” plan allows unlimited commercial use; the “Pro” plan adds a royalty‑free guarantee for up to 10 M images per month.
* **Firefly**: Adobe’s “Generative Assets License” grants unconditional commercial rights with no attribution required, and it automatically flags any content that resembles copyrighted material in Adobe Stock. This is a major win for brand‑sensitive teams.
* **DALL·E 3**: OpenAI’s “Content Policy” permits commercial use, but images that contain recognizable trademarks or faces of public figures trigger a “watermark” overlay unless the user provides proof of rights.

Ethical guardrails are strongest in Firefly (Adobe’s “Responsible AI” framework) and DALL·E 3 (OpenAI’s “Safety Layer”). Midjourney’s moderation relies on community reporting, which can be slower for edge cases.

### How do the safety filters differ between the three services?
