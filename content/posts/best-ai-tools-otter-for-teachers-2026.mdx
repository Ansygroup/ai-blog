## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai for teachers with heavy classroom noise?
Otter’s Word Error Rate (WER) averages 6 % in quiet rooms and climbs to 13 % when background noise exceeds 55 dB. Using a lapel mic reduces the error to under 5 %, making it reliable for most K‑12 settings.

### Can I export Otter transcripts directly into Google Classroom?
Yes. Otter offers a one‑click “Export to Google Classroom” button that creates a post with the transcript, highlights, and a shareable link. The integration works on both desktop and mobile apps.

### Is student data safe under FERPA when using Otter and its AI partners?
Otter.ai, ChatGPT (via Azure OpenAI), and Gradescope all sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with U.S. schools, ensuring FERPA compliance. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and transcripts can be set to auto‑delete after 90 days.

### How does the cost of Otter compare to Rev.com for large districts?
Rev.com charges $0.30 per audio minute, which at 10 hours of weekly recordings equals $540 / month. Otter’s Premium plan at $8.33 /mo per teacher (with bulk discounts) caps at roughly $250 / month for a 30‑teacher department, delivering a 53 % cost reduction.

### Do the AI tools work offline or require constant internet?
Otter’s mobile app offers offline recording, but transcription and AI features (ChatGPT, Gradescope) need an internet connection. For schools with limited bandwidth, downloading transcripts for later processing is a viable workaround.

### Can I integrate Otter with LMS platforms other than Canvas?
Otter provides native plugins for Canvas, Moodle, and Blackboard, plus a generic LTI link that works with any LMS supporting LTI 1.3. The integration typically takes under 15 minutes to configure.
