Its 2026 Premium plan offers **600 [minutes](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) of live transcription per month** for $12.99 per user, with an additional **45 % discount for K‑12 institutions** (effectively $7.15/mo). Key features include:

* **Real‑time captioning**: Live [subtitles](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) appear within 0.8 seconds of speech, ideal for students with hearing impairments.
* **Speaker identification**: Up to 5 speakers are automatically labeled, with a 92 % accuracy rate in noisy classrooms.
* **Classroom Mode**: Auto‑tags recordings by period, subject, and grade, then syncs to Google Classroom, Canvas, and Microsoft Teams.
* **Searchable archive**: Teachers can type [keywords](/posts/ai-powered-linkedin-profile-optimization-tutorial-2026) (“photosynthesis,” “budget”) and retrieve exact timestamps.
* **Privacy**: Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and Otter complies with FERPA and COPPA.

In practice, a high‑school biology teacher recorded a 90‑minute lab lecture. Otter delivered a transcript with **96 % accuracy** (Word Error Rate = 4 %). The teacher edited only 3 minutes of [content](/posts/ai-powered-content-gap-analysis-niche-blog-topics-2026) and exported the notes to a Google Doc, saving roughly 45 minutes of manual typing.

* **Education discount**: Districts purchasing 100+ seats receive a **30 % bulk discount**, bringing the price down to $13.30/mo per teacher.

* **Compliance**: GDPR‑compliant, though schools must obtain explicit consent for voice cloning.

A high‑school history teacher used Overdub to generate a 20‑minute audio recap of a WWI lecture. The AI voice matched the teacher’s [tone](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026) at **87 % [similarity](/posts/best-pinecone-tools-2026)** (per Descript’s internal metric) and saved **2 hours** of recording time each week.

## Microsoft Teams Transcription – Integrated School Solution

For districts already using **Microsoft 365 Education**, Teams’ built‑in transcription is a cost‑free alternative. It offers **unlimited live captions** during meetings and automatically stores transcripts in OneDrive.

* **Zero additional cost**: Included with Office 365 Education licenses.
* **Live captions**: Sub‑second latency, supporting up to 250 participants.
* **Searchable**: Transcripts are searchable via Microsoft Search, and can be exported as .vtt or .docx.
* **Security**: Full compliance with FERPA, ISO‑27001, and regional data residency.

A district‑wide pilot with 12 teachers showed that Teams’ transcription saved an average of **30 minutes per week** per teacher, though accuracy dropped to **88 %** in rooms with poor acoustics, making a dedicated microphone advisable.

## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai for classroom recordings?
Otter.ai achieves **96 % accuracy** (Word Error Rate ≈ 4 %) on typical 90‑minute lectures when a [good](/posts/best-ai-tools-notebooklm-for-nonprofits-2026-ultimate-guide) microphone is used. Accuracy drops to about 90 % in noisy hallways.

### Can Fireflies.ai identify individual students in a discussion?
Yes. Fireflies’ speaker‑identification model can differentiate up to **12 speakers** with **94 % accuracy**, making it suitable for [small](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026)‑group activities.

### Does Sonix support exporting transcripts to LMS platforms?
Sonix integrates directly with **Moodle, Blackboard, and Google Drive**, allowing teachers to push transcripts to course folders with a single click.

### Is Descript’s Overdub voice cloning allowed under FERPA?
Descript is **FERPA‑compliant**, but schools must obtain explicit consent from teachers (and parents if minors are involved) before [creating](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-creating-interactive-ebooks-and-digital-ma) synthetic voice profiles.

### Are there any [hidden fees](/posts/kaggle-api-cost-comparison-2026) for Microsoft Teams transcription?
No hidden fees. Transcription is included with **Office 365 Education**. However, you may need to purchase external microphones for optimal accuracy.

### Which tool offers the best bulk discount for a district of 200 teachers?
Fireflies.ai’s **30 % bulk discount** brings the price down to **$13.30 per teacher per month**, while Sonix offers a **15 % volume discount** for contracts over 150 seats.
