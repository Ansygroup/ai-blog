## What Is NotebookLM for Teams 2026?

NotebookLM for Teams 2026 is Google’s enterprise‑focused extension of its large‑[language](/posts/suno-vs-chatgpt-2026)‑[model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) (LLM) notebook [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026), originally launched for individual power users in 2023. The platform blends a traditional notebook UI with AI features such as auto‑summarization, intelligent search, and real‑time [content](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content) generation. In the “for Teams” version, Google adds granular permission controls, shared workspaces, and admin dashboards that let IT departments enforce compliance policies.

Key capabilities include:

1. **AI‑Driven Summaries** – Upload a 10‑page research doc and get a concise 150‑word executive brief in seconds. Independent [testing](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) shows a 97 % factual accuracy rate.
2. **Contextual Search** – Type a natural‑language query (“What were the Q3 revenue projections for Project Phoenix?”) and the engine pulls the exact paragraph from any notebook in the organization.
3. **Collaborative Editing** – Multiple users can [edit](/posts/copilot-vs-cursor-vs-windsurf-ai-code-editor-comparison) a notebook simultaneously, with AI suggestions appearing inline, similar to Google Docs’ “Smart Compose.”
4. **Automation Scripts** – Built‑in Python and JavaScript runtimes let teams embed [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) AI workflows, like auto‑tagging tickets from Jira.

The platform is marketed as a “single source of truth” for [know](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026)ledge work, aiming to replace a patchwork of separate note‑taking, wiki, and AI‑[assistant](/posts/chatgpt-vs-gemini) tools.

## FAQ

### How does NotebookLM for Teams differ from the consumer NotebookLM?

The Enterprise version adds admin dashboards, SSO/SAML integration, data residency controls, and higher token limits. Consumer NotebookLM is limited to personal notebooks, lacks granular permissions, and caps AI usage at 500 k tokens per month.

### Can I export notebooks to other formats like Markdown or Confluence?

Export options include PDF, HTML, and Google Docs. Direct Markdown export is not yet supported, but you can copy content into a Markdown editor or use the API to convert HTML to Markdown programmatically.

### Is the AI model customizable with my own data?

Yes. Enterprise customers can upload up to 10 GB of domain‑specific documents (e.g., product manuals) to fine‑tune the model. This reduces irrelevant suggestions by roughly 40 % after the first training cycle.

### What SLA does Google offer for NotebookLM for Teams?

For the Enterprise + Premium Support tier, Google guarantees a 99.9 % uptime SLA and a response time of under 2 hours for critical incidents. Standard Enterprise tier has a 99.5 % uptime SLA with a 4‑hour response window.

### How does pricing compare if I already have a Google Workspace Enterprise license?

Google Workspace Enterprise customers receive a **10 % discount** on the NotebookLM Enterprise tier, [effective](/posts/copy-ai-prompting-guide-2026)ly bringing the cost down to $17.99 per user per month when billed annually.

### Does NotebookLM support multi‑language summarization?

Yes. The underlying PaLM 2‑X model supports 27 languages, and internal tests show summarization accuracy above 90 % for French, German, and Japanese documents.
