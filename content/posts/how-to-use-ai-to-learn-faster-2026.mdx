## How to Use AI to Learn Faster

To maximize the benefits of AI-powered learning, consider the following techniques:

### Spaced Repetition

Spaced repetition involves reviewing material at increasingly longer intervals to help solidify it in your [long](/posts/how-to-use-ai-to-summarize-long-documents-2026)-term memory. AI-powered flashcard apps like Quizlet and Anki can automate this process for you.

### Active Recall

Active recall involves actively recalling information from memory rather than simply re-[reading](/posts/best-tablets-ereaders-2026) it. AI-powered study tools like Photomath and Socratic can help you practice active recall through interactive exercises and quizzes.

### Chunking

Chunking involves breaking down complex information into smaller, more manageable chunks. AI-powered learning [platforms](/posts/comparison-ai-powered-influencer-marketing-platforms-beauty-industry) like Knewton can help you chunk information and create personalized learning [plans](/posts/jasper-pricing-explained).
