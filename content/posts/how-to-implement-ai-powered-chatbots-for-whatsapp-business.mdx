## What Is WhatsApp Business](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026) API?

WhatsApp Business API is a [paid service](/posts/ai-driven-customer-service-chatbots-ecommerce-review) that allows businesses to interact with customers programmatically. It enables businesses to send and receive messages, automate tasks, and integrate with CRM systems. To use WhatsApp [Business API, businesses need to apply for approval through WhatsApp's parent company, Meta.
