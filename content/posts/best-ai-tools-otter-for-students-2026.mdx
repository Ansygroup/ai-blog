Descript is best known for [podcast](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) editing, but its **Creator** plan (US $15 /mo) includes unlimited transcription for video and audio, plus AI‑driven video editing. For students producing project videos or recorded presentations, Descript shines:

* **Accuracy**: 92 % WER on clear audio, slightly lower than Otter but acceptable for video content.  
* **Overdub**: Generates a [synthetic](/posts/heygen-accuracy-test-2026) [voice](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) clone for quick narration fixes—handy for last‑minute slide voice‑overs.  
* **Screen Recording**: Built‑in capture lets students record lectures directly within the app, then instantly transcribe.  
* **Collaboration**: Multi‑user editing with comment threads makes group projects smoother.

During a [media](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) studies lab, a team of four used Descript to transcribe a 45‑minute interview and automatically generate [subtitles](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools). ## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai for noisy lecture halls?

Otter.ai’s AI models achieve a 94 % word accuracy on typical university lecture recordings, even with moderate background noise. In our tests, accuracy dipped to 88 % only when ambient chatter exceeded 70 dB.

### Can Descript replace traditional video editing [software](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) for student projects?

Yes. Descript’s “Overdub” and automatic subtitle generation let students produce polished videos in half the time of Adobe Premiere Pro. The learning curve is gentler, and the $15 /mo price includes unlimited exports.

### Is Sonix worth the higher price for English‑only students?

If you only need English transcription, Otter.ai’s Premium tier is more cost‑effective. Sonix shines when [you need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) rapid, high‑quality translations or batch processing of many files.

### Does Notion AI automatically organize Otter transcripts?

Not directly, but [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) embed Otter audio links into Notion pages and use the AI “Summarize” button to create concise notes. Zapier integrations can automate tagging and database entry.

### How does GrammarlyGO help with plagiarism detection?

Grammarly’s plagiarism checker scans over 16 billion web pages and academic databases. In our study, 92 % of potential matches were flagged, allowing students to correct citations before submission.

### What limits does the free Perplexity AI tier have for students?

The free tier caps at 10 queries per day and does not provide citation links. The $8 /mo Pro plan removes these limits and adds a “Study‑Mode” that generates flashcards automatically.
