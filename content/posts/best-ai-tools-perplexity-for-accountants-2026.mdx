## What Is Perplexity AI for Accountants?

Perplexity AI started as a next‑generation search assistant that answers complex questions with citations. In 2024 the company released an **Enterprise API** that lets third‑party developers embed its “grounded‑answer” engine into niche workflows. For accountants, this means you can ask natural‑language queries like “What was the depreciation expense for equipment purchased in Q2 2025?” and receive a line‑item‑ready response with source documents attached.

The real power lies in **contextual memory**: the model remembers prior prompts within a session, allowing it to build multi‑step calculations (e.g., “Apply the 2025 tax rate to the net profit after adjusting for deferred revenue”). Because the engine pulls from both internal data lakes and public tax codes, it stays up‑to‑date with the latest IRS guidance—crucial for compliance.

Several accounting‑focused platforms have built “Perplexity‑powered” modules. Botkeeper, for instance, uses the API to generate journal entries from scanned receipts, while Vic.ai leverages it for intelligent expense categorization. In practice, firms that pair Perplexity with a robust ERP (like NetSuite or QuickBooks Online) see a **30‑40 % reduction** in manual reconciliation time.
