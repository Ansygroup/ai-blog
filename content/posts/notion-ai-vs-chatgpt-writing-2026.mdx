Notion AI and ChatGPT both help you write, but they sit in different places. Notion AI works inside the pages, databases and meeting notes your team already keeps. ChatGPT is a separate chat app with its own models, memory and projects. As of November 2026, the price gap is small, and the real question is where your drafts live. Last tested: 27 November 2026. Notion figures are from notion.com/pricing and Notion's 19 October 2026 blog post. ChatGPT prices come from a third party review, [Sumibako](https://sumibako.com/blog/chatgpt-pricing), updated 18 November 2026, because OpenAI's page showed feature lists without dollar amounts.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writing inside your team's docs and wiki | Notion AI | It works on the pages and databases where the work already lives |
| Long drafts from a blank page | ChatGPT | Dedicated chat, projects, custom GPTs and memory on Plus |
| Cheapest way to try AI writing | ChatGPT | Free is $0 and Go is $8 a month |
| Meeting notes in the same workspace | Notion | AI Meeting Notes is listed on Business |
| Choosing which model writes | Both | ChatGPT Plus lists GPT-6.2 reasoning, Notion Select credits let you pick the model |
| Heavy research drafts | ChatGPT | Plus lists expanded deep research, Pro lists maximum |

## What Notion charges

| Plan | Price per seat a month | AI on the plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | None listed |
| Plus | $10 | Trial of Notion AI |
| Business | $20 | Notion Agent, Custom Agents, AI Meeting Notes, 1,500 Standard and 500 Select credits a month |
| Enterprise | Custom | Zero data retention with LLM providers, SCIM, audit log |

Notion's page does not label the billing period for these prices in our fetch, and its help text says plans can be monthly or annual. Check the toggle. Notion's October post says Business and Enterprise seats get 1,500 Standard credits a month, where Notion picks the model, and 500 Select credits, where you pick the model or run Custom Agents. Admins can buy more credits. The post says most users are unlikely to run out.

## What ChatGPT charges

| Plan | Price a month | Writing relevant features |

| Free | $0 | Everyday tasks |
| Go | $8 | More messages, uploads and longer memory |
| Plus | $20 | Reasoning models, projects, custom GPTs, expanded memory and deep research |

Sumibako gives these prices as of 11 November 2026. OpenAI's page lists a 256K reasoning context window on Go and Plus and 400K on Pro.

## Privacy

Notion says Notion AI will not use your data to train its models unless you opt in to share it, and that its AI subprocessors are bound by contract. Enterprise adds zero data retention with model providers. For ChatGPT, read OpenAI's data controls for your plan, since we did not verify them for this article.

## What changed in Notion's credits

Notion's 19 October 2026 post says the new credit system went live that day for new customers and moved existing customers over on 2 November. It also says you no longer need to buy credits to start building custom agents, since included Select credits let you try Custom Agents first. AI tasks use credits at very different rates, which is why Notion keeps the two pools apart. Standard credits cover everyday use where Notion picks the best model. Select credits cover model choice and agents. If your team runs agents on a schedule, watch the Select pool.

## Refunds and billing

Notion's help text says it refunds the full amount if you downgrade within three days of signing up for monthly billing, or within 30 days of signing up for annual billing. Seat counts sync each billing period, so adding a member mid month adds a prorated charge. Plan your seat count before you commit, since a writing helper priced per seat grows with the team.

## Where each one fails

Notion AI fails when you want a long, free-form conversation. It is tied to your workspace, and heavier models use credits, so a power user can drain the allowance. Its useful tier is Business at $20 a seat, twice Plus. ChatGPT fails on context. It does not see your Notion pages unless you paste them or connect them, and the writing you do there ends up outside your wiki.

## Who should skip which

Skip Notion AI if you do not already use Notion, because you would pay for a workspace to get a writing helper. Skip ChatGPT Plus if all your writing happens in team docs and meeting notes. ## Test it yourself

1. Take one real document: a project update with data in a table.
2. Ask both tools for a summary and a rewrite for a different audience.
3. Check each for invented facts against the table.
4. Time how long it takes to get the result into your final document.
5. Note how many Notion credits or ChatGPT messages the task used.

## Which should you pick

If your team lives in Notion, pick Business and use Notion AI where the content is. For personal and long form writing, pick ChatGPT Go at $8 or Plus at $20 depending on how often you hit limits. Many people end up with both, and that is fine if each has a clear job. Prices were read on 27 November 2026 and will change.
