### Which AI tool gives the most accurate citations for a research paper?
Perplexity AI’s Pro plan (US$9/mo) delivers a 98 % correct citation rate when using its “Citation Export” feature, outperforming ChatGPT‑4 (94 %) and Grammarly (91 %). Always double‑check against your institution’s style guide.

### Can I get a [free version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) of Notion AI for school projects?
Notion AI offers a free tier limited to five pages per month, which is enough for [short](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-instagram-reels-2026) essays but not for semester‑long theses. The student plan at $10/mo unlocks unlimited pages and the built‑in citation manager.

### How does Khanmigo differ from regular ChatGPT for tutoring?
Khanmigo is [train](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial)ed on Khan Academy’s curriculum and can walk you through step‑by‑step problem solving in math, science, and humanities, while also [generating](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) practice quizzes. ### Is it safe to use AI tools with sensitive research data?
All tools featured in our runway comply with GDPR and FERPA. ### Do these AI tools work offline?
Only a few, like **Anki’s AI Add‑on** and **GitHub Copilot X (local mode)**, offer limited offline functionality. The rest require an internet [connection](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026) [for real](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-real-estate-agents)‑time model inference.
