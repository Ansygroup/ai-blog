# Surfer SEO vs Frase vs Clearscope: Best AI SEO Optimization Tool

Choosing the right AI SEO optimization tool can make or break your content strategy. With numerous options available, it can be overwhelming to decide which one suits your needs. In this article, we'll compare three popular tools: Surfer SEO, Frase, and Clearscope.


<div class="quick-answer">

## What Are Surfer SEO, Frase, and Clearscope?

Surfer SEO, Frase, and Clearscope are AI-powered SEO optimization tools designed to help content creators and marketers improve their search engine rankings. Each tool offers a unique set of features to streamline content optimization, research, and planning.

## How We Tested

We spent over 50 hours testing these tools, evaluating their features, pricing, and performance. Our testing criteria included ease of use, feature set, accuracy, and customer support. We tested a total of 10 tools, but focused on Surfer SEO, Frase, and Clearscope for this comparison.

## Features and Pricing

### Surfer SEO

Surfer SEO offers a range of features, including:

* Content optimization audits
* Keyword research
* SERP analysis
* Content editor with AI-powered suggestions

Surfer SEO pricing starts at $29/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 1,000 keywords and 20 content audits.

### Frase

Frase is an AI-powered content generation and research tool that offers:

* AI-powered content generation
* Research capabilities
* Content optimization suggestions

Frase pricing starts at $39/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 1 user, 10,000 words, and 100 AI-generated content suggestions.

### Clearscope

Clearscope is a keyword clustering and content optimization tool that offers:

* Keyword clustering
* Content optimization suggestions
* SERP analysis

Clearscope pricing starts at $35/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 100 keyword clusters and 10 content audits.

## Who Should Use This?

* **Content marketers**: Surfer SEO and Clearscope are ideal for content marketers looking for comprehensive content optimization features.
* **SEO agencies**: Frase is suitable for SEO agencies that require AI-powered content generation and research capabilities.

## Who Should Skip This?

* **Advanced SEOs**: If you're an experienced SEO professional, you may find these tools too simplistic.
