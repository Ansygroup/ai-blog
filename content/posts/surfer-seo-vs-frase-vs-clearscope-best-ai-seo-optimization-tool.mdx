## What Are Surfer SEO, Frase, and Clearscope?

## Features and Pricing

### Surfer SEO

Surfer SEO offers a range of features, including:

* Content optimization audits
* [Keyword research](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-keyword-research-2026)
* SERP analysis
* Content editor with AI-powered suggestions

Surfer SEO pricing starts at $29/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 1,000 keywords and 20 content audits.

### Frase

Frase is an AI-powered content [generation](/posts/how-to-use-midjourney-2026) and research tool that offers:

* AI-powered content generation
* [Research](/posts/best-ai-research-tools-2026) capabilities
* [Content](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content) optimization suggestions

Frase pricing starts at $39/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 1 [user](/posts/ai-driven-website-testing-tools-ux-comparison), 10,000 words, and 100 AI-generated content suggestions.

### Clearscope

Clearscope is a [keyword](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-keyword-research-2026) clustering and content optimization tool that offers:

* Keyword clustering
* [Content](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) optimization suggestions
* SERP [analysis](/posts/best-ai-data-analysis-visualization-tools-small-business)

Clearscope pricing starts at $35/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 100 keyword [clusters](/posts/ai-for-seo-content-clusters-2026) and 10 content audits.
