## Features and Pricing

### Surfer SEO

Surfer SEO offers a range of features, including:

* Content optimization audits
* Keyword research
* SERP analysis
* Content [editor](/posts/cursor-editor-deep-dive-2026) with AI-powered suggestions

Surfer SEO pricing starts at $29/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 1,000 [keywords](/posts/ai-powered-linkedin-profile-optimization-tutorial-2026) and 20 content audits.

### Frase

Frase is an AI-powered content [generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) and research tool that offers:

* AI-powered content generation
* Research capabilities
* Content optimization suggestions

Frase pricing starts at $39/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 1 [user](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings), 10,000 words, and 100 AI-generated content suggestions.

### Clearscope

Clearscope is a [keyword clustering](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) and content optimization tool that offers:

* Keyword clustering
* Content optimization suggestions
* SERP analysis

Clearscope pricing starts at $35/month (billed annually) for the "Basic" plan, which includes 100 keyword clusters and 10 content audits.
