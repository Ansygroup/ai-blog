## 13 November: ChatGPT Wallet launches

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on 13 November. Its agents, called dots, can [complete](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) purchases, for now only for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers. The shopper approves each purchase with Face ID or a fingerprint, and a one-time card number is used so the agent and the store never see the real card. Sources: [The Parameter](https://theparameter.ai/a/black-friday-ai-shopping-agents/) and [IBM Think](https://www.ibm.com/think/news/black-friday-ai-agents-online-shopping).

## 20 and 24 November: Authors Guild v. OpenAI reaches final briefs

On 20 November the parties filed final reply briefs in the author case, which is part of a larger proceeding. The Guild argues that even if training is found to be fair use, that would not excuse downloading and distribution. It filed its [summary](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) judgment motion on 4 September, and 52 amicus briefs followed on 30 October. Source: [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/final-briefs-in-authors-guild-v-openai/), posted 24 November.

## 22 November: AI search share, six estimates

Beket AI compared six estimates of ChatGPT against Google. They run from 0.09% of referrals (Statcounter, October 2026) to about 20% (a company claim from OpenAI). The spread comes from what each counts: questions, visits or clicks. Source: [Beket AI](https://beket.ai/blog/ai-search-market-share/).

## 23 November: Shopify turns on agentic checkout

On 23 November Shopify said its agentic checkout for ChatGPT is on by default for eligible stores. Source: [The Parameter](https://theparameter.ai/a/black-friday-ai-shopping-agents/).

## 26 November: Black Friday is the first test

IBM reports that Adobe forecasts AI tool traffic to US retail sites will nearly double this Black Friday, yet only 16% of US shoppers surveyed said they would let AI complete a purchase. Source: [IBM Think](https://www.ibm.com/think/news/black-friday-ai-agents-online-shopping), 26 November.

## 1 and 2 December: two dated deadlines

ElevenLabs says its Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale ends on 1 December, with up to 55% off annual plans. Creator annual is listed at $99 for the year during the sale. Source: [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing). On 2 December, two new EU AI Act prohibitions apply under the Digital Omnibus: non-consensual intimate image generation and AI-generated child sexual abuse material. Source: [DAZR](https://compliance.dazr.eu/news/ai-act-digital-omnibus-new-dates).

## How the items connect

Three of the seven items are about agents spending money. ChatGPT Wallet (13 November) is the payment layer, Shopify's default checkout (23 November) is the store side, and the Black Friday figures on 26 November [show](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-podcasters-2026) how much shoppers trust it. IBM's reading is that AI is catching on faster for finding products than for buying them, and this year's numbers point that way: traffic from AI tools is forecast to nearly double, while 16% of surveyed US shoppers would let AI pay. Two items are about rules, the copyright briefs and the EU dates. One is about measurement, the search share spread. If you build or sell with AI, the useful question is which of these changes your own numbers before the end of the year. For [voice](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) tools, the sale deadline is the only dated item, so see [best AI voice generators](/posts/best-ai-voice-generators-2026) if you are choosing before 1 December.
