## What Is Claude 4 Opus?

Claude 4 Opus is Anthropic's flagship AI model, boasting unparalleled [performance in various domains. With its advanced](/posts/gemini-advanced-vs-google-one-ai-premium) [architecture, Opus excels in tasks requiring deep](/news/gemini-deep-research-2026-vs-perplexity-vs-chatgpt) reasoning, such as [coding, mathematics, and complex problem-solving. For instance, Opus achieved a 90% accuracy rate on the GSM8K math benchmark, outperforming competitors like Google](/news/gemini-2-0-features-2026)'s PaLM 2.

## What Is Sonnet?

Sonnet, on the other hand, is Anthropic's more agile AI model, designed for [natural language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing and conversational tasks. Sonnet's strengths lie in its ability to understand and generate human-like text, making it an excellent choice for applications like chatbots and [content generation. Paired with a reliable laptop like the Dell XPS 13, Sonnet can handle demanding tasks.
