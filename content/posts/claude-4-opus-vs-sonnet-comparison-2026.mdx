## What Is Claude 4 Opus?

Claude 4 Opus is Anthropic's flagship AI model, boasting unparalleled performance in various domains. With its advanced architecture, Opus excels in tasks requiring deep reasoning, such as coding, mathematics, and complex problem-solving. For instance, Opus achieved a 90% accuracy rate on the GSM8K math benchmark, outperforming competitors like Google's PaLM 2.

## What Is Sonnet?

Sonnet, on the other hand, is Anthropic's more agile AI model, designed for natural language processing and conversational tasks. Sonnet's strengths lie in its ability to understand and generate human-like text, making it an excellent choice for applications like chatbots and content generation. Paired with a reliable laptop like the Dell XPS 13, Sonnet can handle demanding tasks.
