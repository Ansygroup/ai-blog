## What Is the Jasper Mobile App?

The Jasper mobile app is a companion to the popular AI writing tool, Jasper. It allows users to create content on-the-go, using the same AI models and templates as the web app. With a focus on ease of use and accessibility, the app aims to help content creators stay productive, no matter where they are.

## Getting Started with the Jasper Mobile App

To get started with the Jasper mobile app, simply download it from the Apple App Store or [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) [Play](/posts/elevenlabs-vs-play-ht-vs-murf-2026) Store. Once installed, log in with your existing Jasper account or create a new one.
