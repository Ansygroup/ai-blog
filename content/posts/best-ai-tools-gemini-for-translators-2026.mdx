## FAQ

### How does Gemini differ from traditional neural machine translation?

Gemini combines large‑scale multimodal training (text + images + audio) with a transformer architecture that can attend to visual cues, resulting in higher contextual fidelity. Traditional NMT models only process text, which can miss meaning embedded in surrounding media.

### Can I use Gemini with my existing CAT tool like SDL Trados?

Yes. Google Gemini Translator, DeepL’s Gemini Boost, and Lilt Gemini all provide plug‑ins or API endpoints that integrate directly into Trados, memoQ, and Wordfast. Installation typically involves adding an API key in the tool’s “Machine Translation” settings.

### Is my translation data stored by Google or Microsoft when I use Gemini?

Both Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure store data temporarily for quality monitoring, but they offer “no‑log” options for enterprise contracts. Memsource Gemini’s private‑cloud version guarantees that no data leaves your own infrastructure.

### What is the typical latency for Gemini‑based translation on a 1,000‑character sentence?

Latency varies by provider: Google Gemini averages 180 ms, DeepL + Gemini around 210 ms, Microsoft Azure Gemini 240 ms, and self‑hosted Memsource Gemini 260 ms. Open‑source Gemini‑Lite can be faster (≈150 ms) but at the cost of lower accuracy.

### How do I calculate the cost of translating a 5‑million‑character project?

Multiply the total characters (5,000,000) by the provider’s per‑1k rate. For Google Gemini: 5,000 × $0.004 = $20. For DeepL + Gemini: 5,000 × ($0.020 + $0.015) = $175. Adjust for any volume discounts you have negotiated.

### Are there any free alternatives to Gemini for translators?

Open‑source Gemini‑Lite and community‑maintained models like **M2M‑100** are free to download and run locally. However, they lack the massive training data of Google’s cloud version, leading to lower BLEU scores (≈80) and higher post‑editing effort.
