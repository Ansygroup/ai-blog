## What Is Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) and Why Is It Important?

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) represents the total amount a customer is expected to spend on a business over their lifetime. Accurate CLV estimation helps e-commerce businesses prioritize high-value customers, optimize marketing spend, and improve customer retention strategies.

## How AI Enhances Predictive Modeling for CLV Estimation

AI and machine learning algorithms can analyze vast amounts of customer data, including purchase history, browsing behavior, and demographic information. This enables businesses to create highly accurate predictive models that forecast future customer behavior and spending patterns.
