## What Are AI-Powered Website Accessibility Auditing Tools?

AI-powered website accessibility auditing tools use artificial [intelligence](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-data-visualization-for-ecommerce-business-intelligence) to scan websites for accessibility issues, providing recommendations to improve compliance with standards like the Web [Content](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). These tools help [small](/posts/chatgpt-for-small-business) businesses identify and fix barriers that might prevent people with disabilities from using their websites [effectively](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-social-media-crisis-management).

## [Features](/posts/chatgpt-api-guide) to Look for in AI-Powered Website Accessibility Auditing Tools

When selecting an AI-powered website accessibility auditing tool, consider the following features:
- **[Automated](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) Scanning**: Quickly scan your website for accessibility issues.
- **Detailed Reports**: Receive [comprehensive](/posts/chatgpt-for-beginners) reports on accessibility issues and recommendations for fixes.
