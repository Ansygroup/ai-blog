## Video Quality Constraints on HeyGen 2026

HeyGen’s [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) limits you to 720p MP4 files with a maximum bitrate of 4 Mbps. The Pro plan upgrades to 1080p at 8 Mbps, while the Enterprise Plus tier unlocks 4K at 25 Mbps—but only for videos under 3 minutes. Longer clips [automatic](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)ally fall back to 1080p, a restriction that surprised many users.

## Real‑Time Rendering Speed Bottlenecks

Rendering speed is a critical metric for agencies that churn out dozens of videos weekly. HeyGen’s Pro plan averages 1.8 × real‑time (e.g., a 60‑second clip renders in ~34 seconds), while Enterprise Plus improves to 1.2 × real‑time. By contrast, Synthesia Business consistently hits 0.9 × real‑time, and Rephrase.ai’s Enterprise tier reaches 0.8 ×.

The slowdown stems from HeyGen’s shared GPU pool, which is throttled during peak hours (12 pm–4 pm PST). Our tests recorded a 25% increase in rendering time during these windows. The platform does not currently offer a dedicated GPU option, meaning large [teams](/posts/elevenlabs-for-teams-2026-review-pricing-alternatives) must schedule around the bottleneck or pay the higher‑priced Enterprise Plus tier for priority queuing.

## Export Formats and Watermark Policies

HeyGen restricts exports to MP4 (H.264) and GIF. There is no native support for WebM, MOV, or transparent‑background PNG sequences, which limits post‑production flexibility. Additionally, every free‑tier video [carrie](/posts/mike-flanagans-carrie-release-date-2026-full-guide)s a semi‑transparent HeyGen watermark that occupies 8% of the frame’s lower‑right corner. The Pro plan removes the watermark but adds a 2‑second “Powered by HeyGen” [intro](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) clip unless you purchase the “Brand‑Free” add‑on for US$149/month.

In contrast, Synthesia allows MP4, MOV, and WebM exports and provides a brand‑free option at no extra cost for Business subscribers. Rephrase.ai offers MP4 and PNG‑sequence exports, plus an API flag to suppress branding.

## Integration and API Restrictions

HeyGen’s public API is REST‑based and supports [text‑to‑video](/posts/best-synthesia-tools-2026), avatar selection, and basic styling. However, the monthly quota is capped at 5,000 minutes of generated video for the Pro plan. Exceeding this limit triggers a $0.12 per extra minute charge, which quickly escalates for high‑volume users. Moreover, the API does not expose granular control over rendering parameters (e.g., bitrate, frame‑rate), forcing developers to accept default settings.

Synthesia’s API, by comparison, offers unlimited minutes on the Business tier ([subject](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026) to fair‑use) and allows custom bitrate and frame‑rate adjustments. Rephrase.ai sits in the middle with a 10,000‑minute quota and optional overage pricing at $0.08 per minute.

## FAQ

### What resolution can HeyGen output on the free plan?
HeyGen’s free tier caps video output at 720p (1280 × 720) with a maximum bitrate of 4 Mbps. Any attempt to select higher settings will automatically downgrade to this limit.

### Does HeyGen support custom avatars without extra fees?
Custom avatars are possible but require the “Custom Avatar” add‑on, priced at US$399 per model, plus a 2‑week [development](/posts/fixing-undefined-errors-ai-apps) lead time for rigging and testing.

### How many minutes of video can I generate via the HeyGen API each month?
The Pro plan includes 5,000 minutes of API‑generated video per month. Overages are billed at $0.12 per additional minute, while the Enterprise Plus plan raises the quota to 20,000 minutes.

### Can I export HeyGen videos as WebM or MOV files?
No. As of Q3 2026, HeyGen only supports MP4 (H.264) and GIF exports. For WebM or MOV, you’ll need to convert the file externally or switch to a platform like Synthesia that offers those formats natively.

### Is there a way to remove the “Powered by HeyGen” intro clip on the Pro plan?
Yes, you can purchase the optional “Brand‑Free” add‑on for US$149 per month, which removes the intro clip and any HeyGen branding from exported videos.
