## FAQ

### What are the main differences between NotebookLM and Notion AI?
Notion AI offers a larger word quota (100 k vs. 50 k), real‑time collaboration, and a marketplace of 5,000+ templates. NotebookLM is limited to Google Docs‑style editing and a 1 GB data cap, making Notion AI more versatile for team use.

### Is Obsidian AI [truly](/posts/chatgpt-vs-gemini) offline‑only?
Yes. When you run the AI model locally (via Ollama or a self‑hosted OpenAI endpoint), no data leaves your device. Cloud sync is optional and encrypted end‑to‑end.

### Can Mem ingest Slack messages automatically?
Mem’s Pro tier includes an **auto‑ingest connector** that pulls messages from Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Gmail into your knowledge graph without manual copy‑pasting.
