The SEO audit tool landscape is changing. Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Moz are leading the way.

## Ahrefs

Ahrefs is strong in backlink analysis](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) with 16 trillion links. Its [Content Gap tool helps identify missed opportunities. Ahrefs' reports are useful for understanding a website's SEO performance.

## SEMrush

SEMrush offers a [suite that includes technical SEO audits and competitor](/posts/midjourney-for-teams-2026-review) [analysis. Its integrated approach makes it ideal for agencies. SEMrush is a good choice for those who want a single tool for](/posts/copy-ai-review-2026-best-ai-copywriting-tool-for-ecommerce) all their SEO needs.

## Moz

Moz is strong in [keyword research](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-market-research-2026) with robust SERP analysis. Its Link Explorer provides valuable insights. While not as [comprehensive as Ahrefs, Moz is a strong contender.

- Digital marketing](/posts/ai-driven-email-marketing-automation-tools-review) agencies: SEMrush's integrated suite makes it ideal.
