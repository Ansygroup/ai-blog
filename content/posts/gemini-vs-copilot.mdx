## [Features](/posts/claude-api-guide) and Pricing
Both Gemini and Copilot offer a range of features, including code completion, code review, and [project management](/posts/ai-tools-for-project-management-2026). However, their pricing models differ. Gemini offers a free plan, as well as two paid plans: the Pro plan, which costs $29 per month, and the [Business](/posts/gemini-for-small-business) plan, which costs $49 per month. Copilot, on the other hand, offers a free trial, as well as two [paid](/posts/jasper-vs-free-version) plans: the Personal plan, which costs $19 per month, and the Team plan, which costs $39 per month.
