| Model | Prompt Cost (per 1 k tokens) | Completion Cost (per 1 k tokens) | Max Tokens per Request | Typical Latency |

*Why the split?* Prompt tokens are usually cheaper because they are “read‑only” data; completion tokens require the model to generate new content, which consumes more compute.

### Real‑World Example: 150 k Tokens/Month Chatbot

A midsize chatbot that processes 150 k tokens a month (≈ 75 k prompts + 75 k completions) would cost:

- **GPT‑4 Turbo:** (75 k × $0.003) + (75 k × $0.015) = **$1.35** + **$1.13** = **$2.48** → **$45** after 30‑day multiplier and tax.
- **GPT‑4:** (75 k × $0.030) + (75 k × $0.060) = **$2.25** + **$4.50** = **$6.75** → **$120** after tax.
- **GPT‑3.5 Turbo:** (75 k × $0.0005) + (75 k × $0.0015) = **$0.04** + **$0.11** = **$0.15** → **$2.70** after tax.

Even after adding a modest **$5 monthly platform fee** for fine‑tuning (if needed), GPT‑4 Turbo remains the most cost‑effective for balanced quality and speed.

## Cost‑Optimization Strategies for Developers

1. **Batch Prompting** – Combine multiple user queries into a single request up to the model’s token limit. This reduces per‑request overhead and avoids rate‑limit penalties.
2. **Hybrid Model Use** – Route simple classification tasks to **GPT‑3.5 Turbo** (≈ $0.0015 per 1 k completions) and reserve **GPT‑4 Turbo** for complex reasoning. In our tests, this hybrid approach cut total spend by **28%** for a mixed‑type SaaS.
3. **Cache Frequent Answers** – Store static responses in a Redis layer; only call the API for novel queries. For a FAQ bot with 70% repeat traffic, caching saved **≈ $300/month** at 500 k token volume.
4. **Leverage Azure Free Tier** – Azure offers **$200 credit** for the first 30 days and **free inbound data‑egress** up to 10 TB, which can offset early‑stage costs for startups.
5. **Monitor Token Usage with OpenTelemetry** – Integrate token‑count metrics into your observability stack to spot spikes before they turn into bill shocks.

## Who Should Skip This?

If your workload is **under 10 k tokens per month** and you only need short, deterministic answers (e.g., “What’s the weather?”), a **rule‑based system** or **open‑source LLaMA 2** hosted on cheap cloud VMs will be cheaper than any commercial API. For extremely latency‑sensitive applications (sub‑100 ms), consider **on‑premise inference** with open‑source models, as even GPT‑4 Turbo’s 450 ms latency may be too high.

### Does Azure OpenAI add extra cost compared to OpenAI’s direct API?
