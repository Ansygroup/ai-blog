## What Is the ChatGPT Mobile App?

The ChatGPT mobile app is a mobile version](/posts/chatgpt-vs-free-version-2026) of the popular AI [chatbot, ChatGPT. Developed by OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026), the app allows users to interact with the chatbot using natural [language processing (NLP](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content)) and machine learning algorithms.
