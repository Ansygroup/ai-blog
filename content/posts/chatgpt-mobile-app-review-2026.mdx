## What Is the ChatGPT Mobile App?

The ChatGPT mobile app is a mobile [version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) of the popular AI chatbot, ChatGPT. Developed by OpenAI, the app allows users to interact with the chatbot using natural [language](/posts/best-ai-claude-tools-translators-2026) processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms. With the app, users can ask questions, [generate](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) text, and even use voice input to communicate with the chatbot.

## Pricing Overview

The ChatGPT mobile app offers a [free version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) with limited features, as well as a premium subscription starting at $19.99/month. The premium subscription offers additional features, including:

* **Priority access**: Users get priority access to the chatbot, reducing wait times.
* **Increased context window**: The chatbot can understand longer conversations and provide more accurate responses.
* **Advanced features**: Users get access to advanced features, such as [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) chatbot development.
