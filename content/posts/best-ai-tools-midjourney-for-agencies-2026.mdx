## FAQ

### Which AI tool gives the fastest image [generation for](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) agencies?

### Can I keep client brand guidelines enforced automatically?
Yes. Adobe Firefly’s Brand‑Lock and Midjourney’s Style‑Lock both embed brand palettes and fonts directly into the generation process, reducing manual revisions by up to 42 %.

### Is on‑premise AI generation affordable for mid‑size agencies?
Stable Diffusion XL’s pay‑as‑you‑go model [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) about $0.10 per 1 M pixels. For an agency generating 20 M pixels daily, that’s roughly $60/month—far cheaper than per‑image cloud fees.

### How do these tools integrate with existing design software?
All featured platforms offer RESTful APIs and plug‑ins for Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and Figma. Runway also provides a direct integration with Premiere Pro, while Leonardo AI exports OBJ/GLTF files compatible with Blender and Unity.

### What are the data [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) implications of using cloud‑based AI?
Enterprise contracts for Midjourney, Runway, and Adobe include GDPR‑compliant data handling and the option to disable prompt logging. For stricter compliance, Stable Diffusion XL can be self‑hosted, ensuring no data leaves your [network](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026).

### Are there any [hidden](/posts/kaggle-api-cost-comparison-2026) costs I should watch out for?
