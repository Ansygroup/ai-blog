## What Is AI Content Localization?

AI content localization uses artificial [intelligence](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-data-visualization-for-ecommerce-business-intelligence) to adapt content for different languages and cultural contexts. This process involves machine [translation](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-translation-2026), cultural adaptation, and automated [workflows](/posts/ai-automation-with-zapier-2026). For example, a company expanding into Japan can use AI tools to translate their [website](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-website-builders) and [marketing](/posts/ai-for-influencer-marketing-how-to-find-and-collaborate-with) materials into Japanese.
