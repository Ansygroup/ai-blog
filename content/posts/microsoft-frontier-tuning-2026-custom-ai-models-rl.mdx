## How Reinforcement Learning Works

Reinforcement learning is a type of machine learning where an AI [agent](/posts/claude-code-review-2026) learns to make decisions by interacting with its environment. The [agent](/posts/microsoft-scout-2026-always-on-personal-agent) receives rewards or penalties for its actions, and over [time](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026), it learns to make optimal decisions. In the [context](/posts/model-context-protocol-mcp-2026-why-it-matters) of Microsoft Frontier Tuning 2026, RL enables the [creation](/posts/review-of-ai-driven-online-course-creation-tools-thinkific-teachable-kajabi) of highly customized AI models that can be tailored to specific tasks and industries.
