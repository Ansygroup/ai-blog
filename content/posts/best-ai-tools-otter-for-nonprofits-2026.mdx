## Security, Compliance, and Accessibility Considerations

Nonprofits handling donor data, protected health information (PHI), or vulnerable populations must prioritize compliance. Otter Business meets **SOC 2 Type II**, **ISO‑27001**, and **GDPR** standards. For U.S. nonprofits that receive federal grants, the platform also supports **FedRAMP‑ready** environments through its enterprise offering.

Accessibility is another legal and ethical requirement. Otter automatically produces **captions** (SRT/VTT) that can be embedded in videos for the ADA‑compliant web. Fireflies and Sonix also provide caption exports, but only Otter and Descript guarantee **real‑time captioning** during live Zoom calls—a feature that can be a game‑changer for webinars targeting deaf or hard‑of‑hearing audiences.

When evaluating security, look for:

* **End‑to‑end encryption** during audio upload.  
* **Role‑based access control** (RBAC) to limit who can view or edit transcripts.  
* **Data residency options** (US vs. EU servers) for organizations with strict jurisdictional rules.  

Otter’s admin console lets you enforce MFA for all users, a critical control for NGOs that often have volunteer staff with varying security awareness.

## FAQ

### How do I qualify for Otter’s nonprofit discount?

Visit Otter’s pricing page, click “Verify Nonprofit,” and upload a copy of your IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter. Verification is typically completed within 24 hours, after which the 40 % discount is applied to the Business plan.

### Can Otter transcribe non‑English languages?

Otter’s AI supports English, Spanish, French, and German with comparable accuracy. For languages beyond these, Sonix or Rev.com offer broader coverage, though at higher per‑minute costs.

### Is the transcription data stored securely?

Yes. Otter encrypts audio files in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES‑256). The Business plan also offers data residency options in the EU for GDPR‑heavy organizations.

### How does Otter’s real‑time speaker identification work?

During a live Zoom or Teams call, Otter’s AI parses voice signatures and assigns speaker labels on the fly. Accuracy improves after the first 10 minutes as the model learns each participant’s cadence.

### What happens if I exceed my minutes on the Free or Pro plan?

Otter will pause transcription and prompt you to upgrade. No hidden fees are incurred; you simply lose access until you purchase additional minutes or switch to Business.

### Can I export transcripts directly into my donor management system?

Through Zapier, you can set up a workflow that sends new Otter transcripts to Donorbox or Salesforce as attached notes, automatically linking them to donor IDs.
