## FAQ

### What are the ads like in [ChatGPT](/posts/best-chatgpt-desktop-app-2026)'s free tier?
The ads in ChatGPT's free tier are designed to be non-intrusive and relevant to the conversation. They appear interspersed with the AI's responses.

### Can I avoid ads on [ChatGPT](/posts/best-chatgpt-desktop-app-2026)?
Yes, you can avoid ads by upgrading to ChatGPT Plus, which offers an ad-free experience for $20/month.

### How does ChatGPT compare to [Claude](/posts/openai-codex-desktop-2026-review) AI?
Claude AI offers a competitive AI experience without ads in its free tier. It focuses on safety [and accuracy](/posts/claude-best-settings-2026), making it a strong [alternative](/posts/best-chatgpt-free-alternative-2026).

### Will ChatGPT's free tier become more expensive?
There's no official announcement on making ChatGPT's free tier more expensive, but users should be aware that ad-supported [models](/posts/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl) can evolve over [time](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026).

### Can I opt out of ads on ChatGPT?
Currently, users cannot opt out of ads on the free tier. Upgrading to the paid tier is the only way to avoid ads.
