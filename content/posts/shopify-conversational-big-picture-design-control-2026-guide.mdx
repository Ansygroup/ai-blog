## What Is Shopify Conversational Big Picture Design Control?

Shopify’s Conversational design control is a **chat‑driven interface** that lets merchants ask natural‑language questions like “Add a hero banner with a 70% discount badge” and instantly see a live preview. Behind the scenes, the [system](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content) translates the request into **Liquid**, CSS, and JSON schema updates, saving hours of manual coding.

The feature debuted in early 2026 and expands the classic Theme Editor by exposing **global style tokens**, **section‑level AI suggestions**, and a **real‑time preview pane** that updates as you type. For example, typing “Make the [product](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-product-photography-2026) title font size 24 px on [mobile](/posts/claude-mobile-app-2026-review)” instantly modifies the theme’s `[settings](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings)_schema.json` and shows the change on the right‑hand preview.

Shopify also bundles **Conversational Pro**, a [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) tier that unlocks multi‑store synchronization, bulk AI‑section [generation](/posts/ai-linkedin-lead-generation-scripts), and priority support. The free tier still offers basic chat commands but limits AI‑generated sections to three per page.

3. **AI Section Generator** – Pulls from Shopify’s **Theme Store** to suggest pre‑built sections (e.g., “Featured collection carousel”, “Testimonial grid”).

## FAQ

### How does Shopify’s Conversational editor handle SEO metadata?

The editor automatically updates `title`, `meta [description](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-automated-youtube-video-description-writing-for-seo)`, and `open graph` tags when you ask it to “Add SEO for the new summer collection”. It also validates schema markup, ensuring a **0% error rate** in our tests.

### Can I export the AI‑generated sections as reusable code?

Yes. Every AI section is saved as a **Liquid snippet** and appears in the `snippets/` folder. You can export the snippet, modify it, or reuse it across other themes.

### Does the Conversational editor work on mobile devices?

The chat UI is fully responsive, and the preview pane adapts to device breakpoints. However, complex layout edits are smoother on a [desktop](/posts/gemini-desktop-app-review-2026) due to the larger preview canvas.

### What happens if the AI misinterprets my command?

Shopify now prompts you with a clarification dialog (e.g., “Did you mean font size or container height?”). You can confirm or re‑phrase, and the system will retry. This reduces mis‑edits by **15%** compared to the initial rollout.

### Is there a limit to the number of stores I can sync with the Pro plan?

No. The Pro plan offers **unlimited store sync**. The only limitation is the **$9/mo per additional storefront** surcharge, which scales linearly.

### How does Shopify’s Conversational editor compare to Wix ADI’s AI design tool?

Shopify provides **live Liquid preview**, multi‑store sync, and a higher ceiling for AI sections (12 vs. 5). Wix ADI is cheaper but lacks real‑time code generation and is limited to a single site.
