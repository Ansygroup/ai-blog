## Prime Voice AI: The Game‑Changing Voice Clone

Prime Voice AI is ElevenLabs’ flagship offering, allowing businesses to create a unique, brand‑specific voice that sounds indistinguishable from a human narrator. The process works as follows:

1. **Upload Sample Audio** – Minimum 30 seconds, maximum 5 minutes.  
2. **Model Training** – Takes 5‑10 minutes on ElevenLabs’ cloud GPU, after which the voice is ready for use.  
3. **Generate Audio** – Input text, select tone (e.g., “enthusiastic,” “calm”), and receive a WAV file in under 2 seconds per 100 words.
