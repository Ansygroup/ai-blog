Copilot Pro no longer exists. Microsoft stopped selling it on 1 October 2025 and now bundles Copilot into Microsoft 365 [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026). Google, for its part, no longer uses the name Gemini Advanced on its plan pages. As of November 2026, the choice between Gemini and Microsoft Copilot is really a choice between two subscriptions: Google AI plans on one side, Microsoft 365 plans on the other. Last tested: 27 November 2026. All prices come from Blue Hat Web's [Gemini and Copilot price guide](https://www.bluehatweb.com/guides/ai-at-work/plans-and-prices/gemini-and-copilot-plans/), which says it read the providers' official pages on 10 November 2026. Prices may have changed since.

## The [verdict](/posts/descript-vs-chatgpt-2026) first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work mostly in Gmail and Google Docs | Gemini | Google AI Plus and Pro add Gemini in Gmail, Docs and Vids |
| Work mostly in Word, Excel and Outlook | Copilot | Microsoft 365 Personal and Premium include Copilot |
| Cheapest entry | Gemini | Google AI Plus is $4.99 a month, against Microsoft 365 Personal at $9.99 |
| Storage with the AI | Gemini | Pro includes 5 TB and Ultra at least 20 TB |
| Annual billing discount | Copilot | Microsoft lists yearly prices, while Google's pages showed monthly prices only |
| Heavy use | Gemini Ultra | Two tiers at $99.99 and $199.99, with 5x and 20x the usage of Pro |

## Google AI plans (US prices, monthly)

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google AI Plus | $4.99 | Twice the usage of free, 400 GB storage, Gemini in Gmail and Vids |
| Google AI Pro | $19.99 | Four times free usage, 5 TB storage, Gemini in Gmail, Docs and Vids |
| Google AI Ultra, 5x | $99.99 | Five times the usage of Pro, at least 20 TB |
| Google AI Ultra, 20x | $199.99 | Twenty times the usage of Pro |

These plans [need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) a personal Google account, not a work account. Google says you can cancel any time with no refund for a partial period unless the law requires one. The source found no annual prices.

## Microsoft 365 plans with Copilot (US prices)

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal | $9.99 | $99.99 |
| Family | $12.99 | $129.99 |
| Premium | $19.99 | $199.99 |
| Pro | $99.99 | Monthly only |

Premium adds extended AI usage and some exclusive Copilot [features](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026). The Researcher and Analyst [agents](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) are in Premium and Pro, not in Personal and Family. In Family, Premium and Pro, the AI features belong to the subscription owner only, and Microsoft 365 Basic has none. Microsoft also showed limited time first month or first year discounts in the US, and a one month free trial; renewals use the regular price.

Our arithmetic: twelve months of Google AI Pro at $19.99 is $239.88, while Microsoft 365 Premium on annual billing is $199.99, which is $39.89 less over a year. Google's pages showed monthly prices only, so the [comparison](/posts/ai-youtube-chapter-timestamp-generators-2026) favors Microsoft on annual billing and is even on a month by month basis, since both list $19.99. At the cheap end, Google AI Plus at $4.99 is $59.88 a year, and Microsoft 365 Personal at $99.99 a year is $40.11 more. The mid plans cost about the same, so the choice rests on which apps you use.

Storage is the other number. Google AI Plus includes 400 GB and Pro includes 5 TB, so part of the price is [cloud](/posts/pinecone-setup-guide-2026) storage. If you already pay for Google One storage, Pro may replace that bill. Check whether you pay for extra storage before you count the AI as the whole cost.

## What happened to Copilot Pro

Microsoft launched Copilot Pro on 15 January 2024 at $20 a month in the US. On 16 January 2025 it added Copilot to Personal and Family and raised their US price by $3 a month. On 1 October 2025 it stopped selling Copilot Pro and launched Premium, which combines Family with what Copilot Pro offered. If a guide you are reading still sells Copilot Pro, it is out of [date](/posts/mike-flanagans-carrie-release-date-2026-full-guide).

## Where each one fails

Gemini fails if your work lives in Microsoft Office, since the plans put the AI inside Google apps, and a work account cannot buy them. Copilot fails on sharing. The AI features in Family, Premium and Pro are reserved for the owner, so a household of four does not get four seats of Copilot. Neither source we read lists a hard usage count, so [watch](/posts/watch-mayday-air-disasters-on-tubi-its-free-2026-guide) for [limits](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026).

## Who should skip which

Skip Gemini plans if your company runs on Microsoft 365 and you only want AI in Word and Excel. Skip Microsoft 365 Premium if you only want a [chat](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026) [assistant](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-developers-2026) and do not need Office, since the free plans and rivals may be enough. For wider context, see [ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-gemini) and [AI model pricing changes in November 2026](/posts/ai-model-pricing-changes-november-2026).

## Test it yourself

1. List the three apps you [open](/posts/top-hugging-face-alternatives-2026) most each day.
2. Try the free Gemini and free Copilot on a real task in each app.
3. Compare the output on a spreadsheet question and an [email](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026) draft.
4. Check whether you are using a personal or a work account, since it changes [what you](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) can buy.
5. Check for a current promotion or free trial before paying.

## Which should you pick

Pick Google AI Pro at $19.99 if Google apps are your daily tools, and Plus at $4.99 if you want a taste. Pick Microsoft 365 Personal at $9.99 or Premium at $19.99 if Office is your daily tool. Prices were last verified on 10 November 2026 through the source above.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I still buy Copilot Pro?

No. Microsoft stopped selling Copilot Pro on 1 October 2025 and launched Microsoft 365 Premium at $19.99 a month in the US.

### How much is Google AI Pro?

$19.99 a month in the US as of 10 November 2026, with 5 TB of storage and Gemini in Gmail, Docs and Vids.

### Is Copilot included in Microsoft 365 Personal?

Yes. Personal at $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year includes Copilot, but the Researcher and Analyst agents are only in Premium and Pro.
