## What Is Best AI Perplexity Tools for Doctors 2026?

“Perplexity” refers to the statistical measure of how well a language model predicts a sequence of words; lower perplexity means higher confidence and relevance. In healthcare, AI tools that achieve low perplexity on medical corpora can generate more reliable answers, cite peer‑reviewed sources, and stay within regulatory bounds. The term “best AI tools perplexity for doctors 2026” therefore captures a niche of LLM‑powered assistants that are not only smart but also vetted for patient safety, data privacy, and integration with electronic health records (EHR).
