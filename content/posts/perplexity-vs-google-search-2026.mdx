## FAQ

### What is Perplexity, and how does it differ from Google Search?
Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine focusing on in-depth answers with detailed source citations, while Google Search offers broader results and real-time information.

### Can Perplexity replace Google Search?
Perplexity excels in specific areas but may not fully replace Google Search for all user needs, especially those requiring real-time information.

### Is Perplexity free to use?

### How accurate is Perplexity compared to Google Search?

### Can I use both Perplexity and Google Search?
Yes, users can utilize both platforms based on their specific needs and preferences.
