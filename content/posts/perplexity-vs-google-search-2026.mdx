# Perplexity vs Google Search 2026: AI-Powered Search Faceoff

The AI-powered search landscape is evolving rapidly. Perplexity and Google Search are two leading contenders.

## What Is Perplexity vs Google Search 2026?

Perplexity and Google Search are two prominent players in the search engine landscape. Perplexity focuses on AI-generated answers with detailed source citations, while Google Search offers a broader range of results, including real-time information.

## How We Tested

## Features and Capabilities

Perplexity offers:
- AI-generated answers with detailed explanations
- Source citations for transparency
- Focus on in-depth responses

Google Search provides:
- Diverse results, including web pages, images, and videos
- Real-time information retrieval
- Robust query handling for broader searches

## Strengths and Weaknesses

Perplexity excels in providing detailed, AI-generated answers with transparent source citations. However, it may struggle with real-time information retrieval.

Google Search offers broad query handling and real-time results, but its AI-generated answers are less detailed compared to Perplexity.

## User Experience

Perplexity's interface is clean and focused on AI-generated responses. Google Search provides a more comprehensive results page with various content types.
