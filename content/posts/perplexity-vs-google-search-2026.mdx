## Strengths and Weaknesses

Perplexity excels in providing detailed, AI-generated answers with transparent source citations. However, it may struggle with real-time information retrieval.

Google Search offers broad query handling and real-time results, but its AI-generated answers are less detailed compared to Perplexity.
