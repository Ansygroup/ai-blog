## FAQ

### What is the difference between ChatGPT Enterprise and ChatGPT Plus for marketing teams?
ChatGPT Enterprise offers unlimited tokens, dedicated instances, and SOC 2 compliance at $20/user/month, while ChatGPT Plus provides 100 k tokens per month for $20 per account and lacks admin controls. Enterprise is built for team collaboration and data security, making it the better fit for marketing departments.

### Can Jasper Chat replace a full‑featured SEO platform?
Jasper Chat excels at copy creation and brand‑voice consistency but does not include native keyword research or on‑page SEO scoring. For comprehensive SEO, pair Jasper with **Surfer SEO** or **MarketMuse**.

### How does HubSpot AI Hub integrate with existing marketing workflows?
HubSpot AI Hub embeds ChatGPT directly into email, landing‑page, and social publishing modules. Users can generate personalized copy, schedule posts, and run predictive lead scoring without leaving the HubSpot dashboard, streamlining the funnel from acquisition to conversion.
