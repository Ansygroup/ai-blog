## Writing](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-resume-writing-2026) Test: Claude Wins

We gave each tool the same brief: "[Write a 1,500-word blog post intro about sustainable packaging for a B2B audience."

**ChatGPT** produced clean](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026), structured [copy but with telltale "in today's fast](/posts/best-ssds-storage-ai-2026)-paced world" filler phrases.

**Claude** produced prose that [read like a senior copywriter wrote it. Sentence variety was best; the AI-tell was almost zero](/posts/best-free-ai-tools-2026).

**Gemini** produced accurate, well-structured [copy but slightly more formal/robotic than the others.

**Winner](/posts/copy-ai-vs-jasper-2026): Claude.** The prose felt human.
