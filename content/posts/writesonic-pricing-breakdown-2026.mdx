## What Is Writesonic Pricing?

Writesonic is a SaaS platform that blends GPT‑4, Stable Diffusion, and its own proprietary models to generate copy, blog posts, ads, and even visual assets. In 2026 the service shifted from a simple “credits‑per‑word” model to a hybrid system that bundles **credits**, **tokens**, and **feature caps**. Each credit roughly equals 200 words of output, though the exact count varies by content type (e.g., longer SEO articles consume more tokens than short ad copy).  

The pricing structure is tiered: a **Free tier**, a **Starter** (individual), a **Professional** (team), and **Enterprise** (custom). Credits roll over monthly on paid plans, and unused credits can be banked up to 12 months—useful for seasonal content spikes. Writesonic also offers **add‑on packs** (e.g., extra image credits at $5 for 100 images) and a **pay‑as‑you‑go API** that charges $0.015 per 1,000 tokens after the plan’s allocation is exhausted.  

Understanding this breakdown helps you avoid surprise overages and choose the plan that aligns with your content volume, team size, and budget.

## FAQ

### How many words does one Writesonic credit equal?
One credit typically generates about **200 words** of plain text. Complex formats like long‑form SEO articles consume slightly more tokens, so you might see 150‑180 words per credit in those cases.

### Does Writesonic offer a discount for annual billing?
Yes. Switching to an annual subscription gives a **15 % discount** on the Starter and Professional plans (e.g., Professional drops from $49/mo to $41.65/mo when paid yearly).

### Can I buy extra credits without upgrading my plan?
Absolutely. Writesonic sells **credit packs** ranging from 100 credits ($9) to 2,000 credits ($150). These are added to your existing allocation and roll over like regular credits.

### How does Writesonic’s API pricing compare to its UI pricing?
The API follows a **pay‑as‑you‑go** model after you exceed the plan’s token quota: $0.015 per 1,000 tokens for Starter, $0.012 for Professional, and $0.010 for Enterprise. This is slightly higher than OpenAI’s base rate but includes built‑in safety filters and brand‑kit enforcement.

### Is there a limit on image generation for paid plans?
Paid plans (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) offer **unlimited AI‑generated images**. The only restriction is a **rate limit of 30 images per minute** to prevent abuse.

### What happens to unused credits at the end of the month?
Unused credits **roll over** for up to **12 months**. If you exceed the 12‑month cap, the oldest credits are forfeited, encouraging consistent usage.
