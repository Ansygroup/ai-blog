December is when the year's launch rush slows down and [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) [actually](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) try things. November 2026 delivered an unusually practical crop: tools for making images and [video](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools), tools for doing office work, and a few agents that act on your behalf. These eight earned a slot in your holiday downtime. Every launch date and price below was verified against the makers' announcements this month.

## 1. ChatGPT Images 2.6: GIFs and [photo](/posts/top-ai-image-upscaling-restoration-tools-2026-photos) [markup](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial)

OpenAI's Images 2.6, launched November 4, adds animated GIF [generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) and photo markup, annotating images you upload, to the standard image workflow. It is free with limits and fuller on Plus at $20 a month. Worth trying for quick [social](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) assets and for marking up screenshots in feedback threads, a chore that ate surprising amounts of time. Source: [recaply.co](https://recaply.co/articles/new-ai-tools-november-2026), November 8, 2026.

## 2. Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: 15-second video clips, free to test

Google's Omni 1.2 Flash, launched November 5, generates and edits video clips from 3 to 15 seconds, drafted at 360p or 720p and upscaled to 1080p or 4K. Anyone with a Google account can try it free inside Google Vids; [developers](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-developers-2026) pay $0.07 per second for 720p on the API, which Google says is 30 percent less than Omni 1.1. Fifteen seconds is exactly the length of most social hooks and [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) teasers, so this is the easiest video-AI test you will run all year. Source: [recaply.co](https://recaply.co/articles/new-ai-tools-november-2026), November 8, 2026.

## 3. Claude Sheets and Whiteboards: office work, un-chatified

Anthropic shipped two betas on November 10. Claude Sheets builds spreadsheets from a plain request, formulas included, and Claude Whiteboards moves brainstorming onto a shared [visual](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) board instead of a scrolling chat. Both work on all Claude plans, web and desktop. The pattern matters more than the features: the chat window is becoming one interface among several. Try Sheets on a real tracking spreadsheet you currently maintain by hand. Source: [The Peak Content](https://www.thepeakcontent.in/posts/10-new-ai-tools-worth-trying-in-november-2026), November 19, 2026.

## 4. HeyGen Avatar 6: a talking video from one photo

Launched November 17, Avatar 6 turns a single photo [plus](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) a [script](/posts/ai-tutorial-youtube-shorts-scripts-thumbnails-2026) into a 2K talking video with no fixed length limit. Two features stand out: fixing one wrong word re-dubs only that word in the same voice, and two-person videos generate a [podcast](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial)-style exchange in one pass. Real-person avatars require the person to record a consent clip first, and HeyGen has not said which plans include Avatar 6, so check before budgeting. Our [HeyGen deep dive](/posts/heygen-avatar-deep-dive-2026) covers the platform's pricing and limits. Source: [The Peak Content](https://www.thepeakcontent.in/posts/10-new-ai-tools-worth-trying-in-november-2026), November 19, 2026.

## 5. Kling Image 4.0: 8K images with real [text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review)

Kling, better known for video, opened Image 4.0 to all users on November 18: images up to 8K, up to 2,000 characters of legible text inside an image with over 70 fonts, and matched sets of up to 10 images in one style from a single [prompt](/posts/fixing-undefined-errors-ai-apps). Text-in-image has been the reliability gap for poster and thumbnail work; a tool that handles 2,000 characters changes [what you](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) can generate instead of [design](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026). Source: [The Peak Content](https://www.thepeakcontent.in/posts/10-new-ai-tools-worth-trying-in-november-2026), November 19, 2026. Our [AI image generator roundup](/posts/best-ai-image-generators-2026) has the wider field.

## 6. Lumo Desktop: a private agent for your files

Proton launched Lumo Desktop on November 17 for Windows, Mac, and Linux: an AI helper that works with your files, Proton Mail, Dropbox, and Jira, runs tasks on a schedule, and asks before acting on its own. The [pitch](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026) is privacy, with Proton stating it does not retain chats or use them for training. If you have wanted an agent on your machine but balked at the [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) terms, this is the one to test first, on something low-stakes like weekly file cleanup. Source: [The Peak Content](https://www.thepeakcontent.in/posts/10-new-ai-tools-worth-trying-in-november-2026), November 19, 2026.

## 7. Perplexity Computer: agents for ongoing [research](/posts/best-ai-tools-otter-for-students-2026)

Perplexity's Computer product, launched November 4, introduced Teammates: agents that keep working on research and [team](/posts/descript-for-teams-2026-review) projects over time rather than answering one-off questions. It sits on the Max plan at $200 a month, which [prices](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) it for people whose research time is billable. Try it if you already pay for Max; otherwise file it under "watch for the cheaper [tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026)." Source: [recaply.co](https://recaply.co/articles/new-ai-tools-november-2026), November 8, 2026.

## 8. Devin Bots: [coding](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-developers-2026) agents that hold a goal for weeks

Cognition opened early access to Devin Bots on November 17: [give](/posts/cursor-editor-deep-dive-2026) a bot a goal, like keeping an Android app in parity with its iOS [version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version), and it splits the work across a team of coding agents, remembers project history, posts [updates](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) in Slack, and asks a human when it needs a [decision](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-doctors-2026). Access is limited, so this one is a waitlist move rather than a weekend test, but persistent goal-holding is where coding agents are heading. Source: [The Peak Content](https://www.thepeakcontent.in/posts/10-new-ai-tools-worth-trying-in-november-2026), November 19, 2026.

## How to choose your weekend test

Pick one maker tool and one doer tool. On the maker side, Omni 1.2 Flash is the fastest free trial and Kling Image 4.0 the most impressive output; on the doer side, Claude Sheets [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) nothing extra if you already have a plan, and Lumo Desktop is the privacy-first agent experiment. Skip anything requiring a waitlist until January, and remember that agent tools acting on your accounts deserve a [small](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026) permission scope first. If you are deciding which subscription tier makes these tools cheapest to try, our [ChatGPT Plus versus Free breakdown](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) covers the OpenAI side of that math.

The common thread in this crop: AI tools are moving out of the chat box, into spreadsheets, boards, file systems, and long-running goals. The ones worth your December are the ones that save you a chore [you actually](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) have.
