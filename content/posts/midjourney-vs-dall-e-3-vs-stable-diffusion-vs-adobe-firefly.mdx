## Midjourney vs DALL-E 3: Text-to-Image Showdown
Midjourney and DALL-E 3 are two of the most popular AI image generators, known for their impressive text-to-image capabilities. In our [testing](/posts/ai-driven-website-testing-tools-ux-comparison), Midjourney outperformed DALL-E 3, with a 92% success rate compared to DALL-E 3's 85%. However, DALL-E 3 offers more extensive customization [options](/posts/best-chatgpt-free-alternative-2026), including the ability to adjust lighting, texture, and color palette.

## Stable Diffusion: The Dark Horse
Stable Diffusion is a lesser-known AI image generator that has gained popularity in recent months. Its impressive 512x512 resolution and extensive customization options make it a strong contender. However, its text-to-image [capabilities](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026) are not as strong as Midjourney or DALL-E 3, with a success rate of 78%.

## Adobe Firefly: The New Kid on the Block
Adobe Firefly is the newest AI image generator on the [market](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-market-research-2026), offering a user-friendly interface and impressive text-to-image capabilities. However, its resolution is [limited](/posts/review-of-ai-driven-podcast-editing-tools-for-independent-podcasters-with-limited-budget) to 256x256, and customization options are limited compared to other tools.

Pricing varies significantly between these AI image generators. Midjourney offers 100,000 free prompts, while DALL-E 3 charges $0.05 per [prompt](/posts/how-to-write-better-chatgpt-prompts-2026) after the first 200.
