## Midjourney vs DALL-E 3: Text-to-Image Showdown
Midjourney and DALL-E 3 are two of the most popular AI image generators, known for their impressive text-to-image capabilities. In our testing, Midjourney outperformed DALL-E 3, with a 92% success [rate](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026) compared to DALL-E 3's 85%. However, DALL-E 3 offers more extensive customization options, including the ability to adjust lighting, texture, and color palette.

## Stable Diffusion: The Dark Horse
Stable Diffusion is a lesser-known AI image generator that has gained popularity in recent months. Its impressive 512x512 resolution and extensive customization options make it a strong contender. However, its text-to-image capabilities are not as strong as Midjourney or DALL-E 3, with a success rate of 78%.
![Adobe Firefly: The New Kid on the Block](/images/illustrations/midjourney-vs-dall-e-3-vs-stable-diffusion-vs-adobe-firefly-illo1.svg)
## Adobe Firefly: The New Kid on the Block
Adobe Firefly is the newest AI image generator on the market, offering a user-friendly interface and impressive text-to-image capabilities. However, its resolution is limited to 256x256, and customization options are limited compared to other tools.

## Pricing Overview
Pricing varies significantly between these AI image generators. Midjourney offers 100,000 free prompts, while DALL-E 3 charges $0.05 per prompt after the first 200. Stable Diffusion is free and open-source, while Adobe Firefly offers a basic [plan](/posts/cursor-pricing-breakdown-2026) for $10/month.

## Who Should Use This?
We recommend Midjourney [for beginners](/posts/jasper-for-beginners), DALL-E 3 for advanced users, and Stable Diffusion for those on a budget. Adobe Firefly is suitable for users who prioritize ease of use and text-to-image capabilities.

## Who Should Skip This?
