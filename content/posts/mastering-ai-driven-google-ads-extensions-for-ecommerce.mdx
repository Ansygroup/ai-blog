## Setting Up AI-Driven Google Ads Extensions

To set up AI-driven [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Ads extensions, follow these steps:

1. **Link your Google Ads account to Google Analytics** for [better](/posts/gpt-5-5-instant-2026-fewer-hallucinations) tracking and insights.
2. **Choose the right AI tool**, such as **Google's [Smart](/posts/best-smart-home-devices-2026) Bidding** or **Optmyzr**, which offers AI-driven ad optimization.
3. **Create and configure your ad extensions**, focusing on **[Automated](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit)** extensions like **Sitelink** and **Callout** extensions.
