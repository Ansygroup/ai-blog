## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai for legal jargon?

Otter.ai’s AI model achieves **95% accuracy** on clear audio and **90%** when legal terminology (e.g., “habeas corpus”, “amicus curiae”) is present. Uploading a custom legal glossary can push accuracy to **~97%**.

### Can Fireflies.ai flag privileged statements automatically?

Fireflies includes a **privilege detection toggle** that highlights phrases like “Attorney‑Client Privilege” or “Work Product”. The feature is **80% reliable** and should be reviewed by a human before finalizing any transcript.

### Is Rev.com’s human‑backed service compliant with GDPR and HIPAA?

Yes. Rev.com signs **Business Associate Agreements (BAA)** for HIPAA and adheres to **GDPR** through EU‑based transcriptionists and encrypted data transfers.

### Does Sonix.ai support real‑time transcription in multiple languages?

Sonix offers **near‑real‑time** transcription (latency < 5 seconds) in 40+ languages, but live translation is limited to **English ↔︎ target language** with a slight delay (≈ 10 seconds). It works best for pre‑recorded audio.

### Which tool integrates directly with Clio Manage?

Otter.ai, Rev.com (via API), and Sonix.ai all have **native Clio integrations**. Fireflies.ai requires a Zapier bridge to sync notes into Clio.

### What security certifications should law firms look for?

At minimum, **SOC 2 Type II** and **ISO 27001** are essential. Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Rev.com all hold these certifications. Sonix.ai holds **ISO 27001** and **SOC 2** for its Premium tier.
