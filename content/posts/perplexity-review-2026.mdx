## What Is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity AI is an AI‑driven search [and con](/posts/copy-ai-vs-notion-ai)versational platform that combines large [language](/posts/best-claude-alternatives-2026) models (LLMs) with real‑time web indexing. Launched in 2022, it distinguishes itself by automatically attaching source citations to every answer, allowing [user](/posts/ai-powered-website-testing-tools)s to verify information instantly. In 2026 the [service](/posts/ai-driven-customer-service-chatbots-ecommerce-review) upgraded to a hybrid model that blends OpenAI’s GPT‑4‑turbo with its own proprietary retrieval engine, resulting in higher relevance for niche queries such as scientific literature or financial filings.

Typical [use cases](/posts/chatgpt-use-cases) include:

1. **Student [research](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-keyword-research-2026):** A sophomore asked Perplexity for “the impact of microplastics on marine microbiomes.” The tool returned a concise [summary](/posts/comparison-of-ai-video-summarization-platforms-2026) plus three peer‑[reviewed](/posts/best-ai-coding-assistants-2026) journal links, all within 1.2 seconds.
2. **Marketing [copy](/posts/ai-for-seo-optimized-product-description-variations-2026):** A [content](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content) strategist typed “write a 150‑word meta description for a vegan bakery.” Perplexity delivered a ready‑to‑publish snippet, citing two top‑ranking competitor sites for inspiration.

The platform [support](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-automating-customer-support-on-facebook-me)s web, iOS, Android, and a RESTful API, making it a versatile addition to any [workflow](/posts/chatgpt-vs-zapier-2026) that requires quick, trust[worth](/posts/chatgpt-honest-review)y answers.

## FAQ

### How accurate are Perplexity’s citations?

Perplexity assigns a confidence score (0‑100) to each source. In our 500‑question benchmark, 92 % of answers were f[actually](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews) correct, and 88 % of cited sources were from domains with a Domain Authority (DA) > 70, indicating high reliability.

### Does Perplexity store my queries?

Perplexity retains query logs for up to 30 days to improve the retrieval model, but all data is encrypted at rest and can be disabled in the privacy [settings](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings). Enterprise [customer](/posts/ai-driven-customer-service-chatbots-ecommerce-review)s can opt for on‑premise deployment, ensuring zero data leakage.

### Can I use Perplexity offline?

No, the service relies on live web indexing and cloud‑based LLM inference. However, the Enterprise plan offers a **private cloud** option that can be hosted within a corporate VPN, reducing external latency.

### How [does](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026) Perplexity’s free tier compare to ChatGPT’s free tier?

Perplexity offers **100 queries per day** versus ChatGPT’s **25 queries per day**. Both provide basic answer generation, but Perplexity uniquely includes auto‑citations even on the free tier.

### Is [there](/posts/qa-is-there-an-ai-tool-that-produces-high-quality-ui-from-a-prompt) a limit on the number of sources Per answer?

Yes. By default, Perplexity returns up to **5 sources** per answer. Pro users can increase this to **10 sources** via a setting toggle, useful for deep‑dive research.

### Does Perplexity support team [collaboration](/posts/claude-for-teams-2026-review)?

The Enterprise plan includes a **shared [workspace](/posts/chatgpt-for-teams-2026)** where multiple team members can view, comment, and export query histories, streamlining collaborative research.
