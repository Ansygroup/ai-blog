## Who Should Skip AI [Chatbots](/posts/best-claude-free-alternative-2026)
While AI-driven customer service chatbots offer many benefits, they may not be suitable for all businesses. Additionally, businesses with complex products or [services](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-customer-journey-mapping-tools-for-financial-services) may require more advanced technical support, which may not be suitable for chatbots.
