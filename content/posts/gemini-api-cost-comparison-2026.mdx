## Gemini API Pricing Structure in 2026

Google introduced a tiered token‑based pricing model for Gemini in early 2026. The base rates are:

| Resource | Input | Output | Vision (image) | Embeddings |
|----------|-------|--------|----------------|------------|
| **Gemini 1.5 Pro (text)** | **$0.0030 / 1 K** | **$0.0060 / 1 K** | N/A | N/A |
| **Gemini 1.5 Vision** | $0.0030 / 1 K | $0.0060 / 1 K | **$0.012 / 1 K** | N/A |
| **Gemini 1.5 Embedding** | N/A | N/A | N/A | **$0.0004 / 1 K** |

### Free Tier & Volume Discounts
- **Free tier**: 5 M text tokens (input + output) and 1 K image tokens per month.  
- **Discounts**: After 100 M tokens, input drops to $0.0024 / 1 K and output to $0.0048 / 1 K. Vision discounts start at 50 M image tokens, reducing the rate to $0.009 / 1 K. Embeddings receive a 20% discount after 10 M tokens.

### Rate Limits
- **Requests per second (RPS)**: 300 RPS for text, 50 RPS for vision.  
- **Concurrent connections**: Up to 1,000 per project, which is ample for most production workloads.

Overall, Gemini’s pricing is designed to reward high‑volume [developers](/posts/copilot-limitations-2026-what-developers-need-to-know) while keeping entry‑level costs](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-pricing-[breakdown](/posts/chatgpt-pricing-breakdown-2026)-2026) low enough for hobbyists.
