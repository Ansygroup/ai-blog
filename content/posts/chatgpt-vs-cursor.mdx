## What Is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an AI [chatbot](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude) developed by [OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026), released in November 2022. It's a conversational AI tool that can understand natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) and respond accordingly. ChatGPT has gained immense popularity due to its ability to generate human-like text, answer questions, and even create [content](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation).

## What Is Cursor?

Cursor is a relatively new player in the AI [market](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-market-research-2026), but it has gained significant attention due to its innovative approach to coding.
