## What Is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an AI-powered [chatbot](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude) developed by [OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026), designed to understand and respond to natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) inputs. It uses a large language [model](/posts/claude-4-opus-vs-sonnet-comparison-2026) to generate human-like text based on the input it receives.

## Getting Started with [ChatGPT](/posts/best-chatgpt-desktop-app-2026)

To start using ChatGPT, simply visit the OpenAI website and sign up for an account. You can choose between a [free version](/posts/chatgpt-vs-free-version-2026) with limited features and a paid version, ChatGPT Plus, which offers more advanced [features](/posts/claude-api-guide) for $20/month.

## ChatGPT Features and [Applications](/posts/gemini-use-cases-2026)

ChatGPT offers a wide range of features and applications, including:

* [Content](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) creation: ChatGPT can generate high-quality content, including blog posts, articles, and [social](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) media posts.
* Customer support: ChatGPT can be used to provide 24/7 [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) support, answering frequently asked questions and helping customers with their queries.
* Language [translation](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-translation-2026): ChatGPT can translate text from one language to another, making it a useful [tool for](/posts/copy-ai-review-2026-best-ai-copywriting-tool-for-ecommerce) businesses and individuals who need to communicate with people in different languages.
