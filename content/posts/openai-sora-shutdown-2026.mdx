## What Is OpenAI Sora?

OpenAI Sora was an AI-powered video generation tool designed to create realistic videos from [text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review) [prompts](/posts/copy-ai-prompting-guide-2026). It utilized OpenAI's advanced [language](/posts/best-ai-claude-tools-translators-2026) models to understand and interpret user input, [generating](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) high-quality video content. For instance, users could input a prompt like "a cat playing the piano," and Sora would produce a corresponding video. This technology had numerous applications in [advertising](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial), education, and entertainment.
