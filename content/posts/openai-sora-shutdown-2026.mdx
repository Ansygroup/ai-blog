## What Is OpenAI Sora?

OpenAI Sora was an AI-powered video generation tool designed to create realistic videos from text prompts. It utilized OpenAI's advanced language models to understand and interpret user input, generating high-quality video content. For instance, users could input a prompt like "a cat playing the piano," and Sora would produce a corresponding video. This technology had numerous applications in advertising, education, and entertainment.
