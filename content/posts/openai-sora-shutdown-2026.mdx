## What Is OpenAI Sora?

OpenAI Sora was an [AI-powered video generation](/posts/how-to-use-midjourney-2026) tool designed to create realistic videos from text [prompts. It utilized OpenAI's advanced language](/posts/ai-language-learning-platforms) [models to understand and interpret user input, generating](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-generating-amazon-product-descriptions) high-quality video [content.
