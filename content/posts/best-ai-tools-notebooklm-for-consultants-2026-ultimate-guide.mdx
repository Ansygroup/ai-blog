### How does NotebookLM compare to other AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for consulting work?
NotebookLM stands out for its deep integration with Google Workspace, real‑time collaborative editing, and built‑in document ingestion that lets you feed PDFs, slides, and spreadsheets directly into the model. While ChatGPT and Claude excel at conversational assistance, NotebookLM adds notebook‑style organization, version control, and the ability to embed AI‑generated tables and visualizations alongside your own notes, making it especially useful for consultants who need to combine analysis with client‑ready documentation.

### Is NotebookLM secure enough for handling confidential client data?
Yes. In 2026 NotebookLM complies with ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR standards. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and enterprise admins can enforce data‑loss‑prevention policies, restrict external sharing, and set AI‑model usage controls to ensure that confidential client information never leaves the organization’s trusted domain.
