## What Is Content Repurposing?

Content repurposing involves reusing existing content in new formats or contexts. For example, turning a blog post into a [video](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) or social media posts. This strategy maximizes content value and reaches wider audiences.
