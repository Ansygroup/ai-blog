## FAQ

### What is the main difference between Gemini 1.0 and Gemini 1.5 Pro?
The primary difference is the token limit and performance. Gemini 1.5 Pro supports 2 million tokens, compared to 512,000 for Gemini 1.0, and offers 15% lower pricing.

### Can I [upgrade](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) from Gemini 1.0 to Gemini 1.5 Pro?
Yes, upgrading is straightforward. Google provides a seamless migration path, ensuring minimal disruption to your [workflow](/posts/best-ai-copy-tools-for-translators-2026).

### Is Gemini 1.5 Pro [worth](/posts/grammarly-for-teams-2026-review) the cost?
For most users, yes. The enhanced performance, longer context window, and lower pricing make it a valuable investment.

### How does Gemini compare to ChatGPT?
Gemini 1.5 Pro offers a larger token limit and competitive pricing. However, ChatGPT's pricing is slightly lower at $6 per million tokens.

### What are the [limitations](/posts/copy-ai-limitations-2026) of Gemini 1.0?
Gemini 1.0 has a smaller token limit (512,000) and slightly lower performance metrics compared to Gemini 1.5 Pro.
