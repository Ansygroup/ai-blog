## Introduction to AI Website Builders
AI-powered](/posts/review-of-ai-powered-web-scraping-tools) website builders have revolutionized the way we create and manage [websites. With the help of artificial intelligence](/news/repository-intelligence-github-2026-ai-code-context)), these [platforms can assist in designing, building, and optimizing](/posts/ai-for-google-ads-optimization) [websites for better](/posts/descript-vs-riverside) user experience and [search engine](/posts/building-an-ai-powered-search-engine-for-your-website-using-) rankings. Wix is our top recommendation for beginners due to its user-friendly interface and extensive template library.
