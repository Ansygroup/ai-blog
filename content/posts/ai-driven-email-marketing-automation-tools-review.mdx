## What Is AI-Driven [Email](/posts/ai-personalized-email-newsletters) [Marketing](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-influencer-marketing-campaigns) [Automation](/posts/ai-automation-with-zapier-2026)?

AI-driven email marketing automation uses artificial intelligence to optimize and automate email marketing campaigns. These tools analyze customer [data](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial), predict behavior, and send personalized emails at scale. For example, an AI tool can automatically segment your audience based on their engagement levels and send targeted emails to increase conversions.

## [Features](/posts/chatgpt-api-guide) and [Capabilities](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026)

AI-driven email marketing automation tools offer various features, including:

- [Predictive](/posts/ai-for-predictive-modeling-in-ecommerce-clv-estimation) analytics: Tools like Marketo use machine learning algorithms to predict [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) behavior and [optimize](/posts/gemini-best-settings-2026) [email](/posts/ai-personalized-email-newsletters) campaigns.
- Personalization: ActiveCampaign allows for advanced personalization, including dynamic content and [automated](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-real-estate-agents) email sequences.
- Automation workflows: Mailchimp offers a visual workflow builder to create complex [automation](/posts/ai-automation-with-zapier-2026) sequences.
