## Features and Capabilities

### Course Creation and Content Generation

Thinkific's AI-powered content suggestions help users create engaging courses. Teachable's drag-and-drop [editor](/posts/cursor-editor-deep-dive-2026) makes course [building](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026) easy. Kajabi's AI-driven content generation produces high-quality course materials.

| Tool | AI-Powered Content Generation | Course Customization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thinkific | Content suggestions | Drag-and-drop editor |
| Teachable | Limited AI features | Drag-and-drop editor |
| Kajabi | AI-driven content generation | Customizable [templates](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026) |

### User Experience and Support

Teachable excels in user experience, with an intuitive [interface](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-real-estate-agents-2026-top-picks) and extensive support resources. Thinkific offers robust support, including a knowledge base and community forum. Kajabi provides personalized support through its dedicated customer success team.

### What is the best AI-driven online course creation tool [for beginners](/posts/jasper-for-beginners)?
Thinkific and Teachable are both suitable for beginners, but Teachable's ease of use makes it a great starting point.

### Can I use these tools [for creating](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-creating-interactive-ebooks-and-digital-ma) and selling online courses?

### How do I choose between Thinkific, Teachable, and Kajabi?

### Are there any free [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) available?
Yes, Thinkific offers a free plan, while Teachable and Kajabi offer free trials.

Yes, all three tools offer integrations with popular website builders and [platforms](/posts/best-ai-powered-push-notification-ab-testing-platforms-2026).
