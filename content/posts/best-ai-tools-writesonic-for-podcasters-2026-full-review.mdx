## FAQ

### How accurate is Writesonic’s AI‑generated script [for niche](/posts/ai-powered-content-gap-analysis-niche-blog-topics-2026) topics?

Writesonic pulls from a curated knowledge base updated weekly. In our niche test on “quantum‑resistant cryptography,” the script contained **92% factual accuracy**, with only two minor outdated references that were flagged by the built‑in fact‑check.

### Can I export the AI‑generated voice as a separate audio file?

Yes. The voice‑over module lets you download the narration as an **MP3 (128 kbps) or WAV (44.1 kHz)** file, ready for import into any audio editor.

### Does Writesonic [support](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-doctors-2026) multiple languages for podcasts?

Currently, Writesonic offers **English, Spanish, French, German, and Mandarin** voice packs. Script generation works in all five languages, though SEO suggestions are strongest for English.

### How does the SEO dashboard calculate traffic forecasts?

The dashboard aggregates data from Apple Podcasts charts, Spotify’s “Trending” list, and Google Search [trends](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026). It then applies a weighted regression model to predict potential impressions, displaying a **“Projected Reach”** number for each suggested keyword.

### Is there a limit on the number of episodes I can publish through the API?

The API itself is unlimited; however, voice‑over minutes are capped by your plan. Publishing itself does not incur additional charges.

### What happens to my voice‑over credits at the end of the month?

Unused voice minutes **do not roll over**. We recommend [monitoring](/posts/top-ai-powered-compliance-platforms-finance-2026) usage via the dashboard and scaling up to the Pro plan if you regularly exceed the limit.
