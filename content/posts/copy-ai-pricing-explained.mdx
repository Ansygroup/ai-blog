## Copy.ai Pricing [Plans](/posts/jasper-pricing-explained)

Copy.ai offers several pricing plans to cater to different [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026) needs. Here's a breakdown of the plans:

| Plan | Words per Month | [Price](/posts/jasper-for-students) (Monthly) | Price (Annually) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 2,000 | $0 | $0 |
| Pro | 10,000 | $49 | $490 (billed annually) |
| Team | 100,000 | $199 | $1,990 (billed annually) |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom |

## Features and Limitations

The free plan allows users to generate up to 2,000 words per month, making it suitable for small projects or testing the tool. The Pro plan, priced at $49/month (or $490 annually), offers 10,000 words per month and additional features like:

* Access to over 90 content templates
* Support for 25 languages
* Customizable tone and style

The Team plan, priced at $199/month (or $1,990 annually), offers 100,000 words per month and adds features like:

* Advanced analytics
* Custom [workflows](/posts/ai-automation-with-zapier-2026)

## FAQ

### What is Copy.ai pricing explained?
Copy.ai pricing starts with a free plan offering 2,000 words per month. Paid plans include Pro ($49/month for 10,000 words) and Team ($199/month for 100,000 words).

### Is Copy.ai worth the cost?
Copy.ai is worth the cost if you need high-quality content generation and are willing to commit to a paid plan. The Pro plan offers [good](/posts/jasper-honest-review-2026) value for small businesses and marketers.

### What are the [limitations](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026) of Copy.ai's free plan?
The free plan limits users to 2,000 words per month and offers limited features compared to paid plans.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my Copy.ai plan?
Yes, Copy.ai allows users to upgrade or downgrade their plans at any time.

### Is Copy.ai [better](/posts/chatgpt-vs-copilot) than Writesonic?
Copy.ai and Writesonic offer similar features and pricing. The choice between the two ultimately depends on your specific needs and preferences.

### Can I use Copy.ai for commercial purposes?
Yes, Copy.ai can be used for commercial purposes, including generating content for clients or promoting products.

## Final Verdict

**Copy.ai's Pro plan** is a great option for small businesses and marketers looking for an affordable AI content generation tool.
