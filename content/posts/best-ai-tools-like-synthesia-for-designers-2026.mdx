*Key features*:

*Pricing*: The “Creator” tier [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) $99 /mo for unlimited renders, with a 30‑day free trial. Higher tiers unlock 4K output and priority GPU queues. In our benchmark, a 3‑second kinetic typography clip rendered in 12 seconds—four times faster than Synthesia’s comparable output.

## Workflow Integration: How Designers Implement These Tools

A typical designer workflow in 2026 looks like this:

1. **Storyboard in Figma** – Use the Synthesia or Runway plugin to import frames directly.  
2. **Generate AI Video** – Paste the script; choose avatar or motion style.  
3. **Fine‑Tune in After Effects** – Export a transparent‑background MOV and add kinetic typography.  
4. **Add Subtitles via Pictory** – Run the exported clip through Pictory to auto‑generate multilingual subtitles.  
5. **Publish** – Upload to Vimeo, embed in Webflow, or stream live with DeepBrain.

The biggest time‑saver is the **single‑click export** from these platforms into a 1080p MP4 that retains the original composition’s vector data, meaning designers rarely need to re‑render after minor [copy](/posts/best-ai-copy-tools-for-nonprofits-2026) changes.

## FAQ

### Can Synthesia generate videos in 4K resolution in 2026?

Yes. The Enterprise tier unlocks native 4K rendering, and you can also purchase a 4K add‑on for the Professional plan at $49 /mo. Rendering a 2‑minute 4K video typically takes 1 minute 20 seconds.

### How does Runway Gen‑2 handle brand colors?

Runway includes a **Color Palette Import** feature. Upload a HEX or Adobe S[watch](/posts/watch-mayday-air-disasters-on-tubi-its-free-2026-guide) file, and the AI applies those colors to generated backgrounds, lighting, and motion elements automatically.

### Is there a free trial for D‑ID Creative Studio?

D‑ID offers a 7‑day free trial with 2 minutes of HD video credit. No credit card is required, allowing designers to test avatar realism before committing.

### Can DeepBrain AI Studios be used for recorded videos, not just live streams?

Yes. While its primary strength is live streaming, you can record the live session and export it as MP4. However, the exported file inherits the live‑stream bitrate (up to 1080p / 30 fps).

### Does Pictory AI support custom music tracks?

Pictory lets you upload your own [audio](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) file or choose from its royalty‑free library. The AI automatically syncs visual cuts to the beat of the music, saving designers up to 20 % of editing time.

### Are these tools GDPR‑compliant for European designers?

All five platforms provide GDPR‑compliant data processing agreements and allow you to store assets within EU‑based data centers (Synthesia, Runway, and D‑ID have EU regions; DeepBrain and Pictory offer EU‑hosted [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) on request).
