## Top AI Tools for Designers

### 1. Figma AI

Figma AI is a game-changer for designers. This AI-powered design assistant offers features like auto-layout, smart fill, and design suggestions. With Figma AI, [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) save up to 30% of your time on repetitive tasks.

* Price: $12/user/month (billed annually)
* Features: AI-powered design assistant, auto-layout, smart fill, design suggestions

### 2. Adobe Fresco

Adobe Fresco is a powerful AI-powered drawing and painting tool. With features like AI-assisted brushstrokes and color palettes, you can create stunning artwork with ease.

* Price: $20.99/month (basic [plan](/posts/cursor-pricing-breakdown-2026))
* Features: AI-assisted brushstrokes, color palettes, [cloud](/posts/pinecone-setup-guide-2026)-based [collaboration](/posts/descript-for-teams-2026-review)

### 3. Canva

Canva is a popular [graphic](/posts/best-ai-tools-jasper-for-designers-2026) design platform that offers a range of AI-powered features, including design suggestions and automated layout tools.

* Price: Free (basic plan), $12.95/month (pro plan)
* Features: AI-powered design suggestions, automated layout tools, vast [template](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026) library
