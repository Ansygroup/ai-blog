Runway sells Kling. That is the strangest fact in this comparison, and it is printed on Runway's own pricing page. As of 27 November 2026, Runway lists "Kling 4.0 1080p" at 118 credits for a 5 second clip, with a 25% discount on Kling 4.0 generations until 30 November. So the question "Kling or Runway" is partly a question of whether you buy Kling from Kling or from a reseller with a different credit system.

That changes how to read both price lists. Credits mean different things on each site, and neither vendor publishes a clean cost per clip for the same model. What follows uses only the numbers each company prints, and marks where the arithmetic is ours.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One subscription, many video models | Runway | Plans give access to all video and image models, including Kling 4.0, Gen-4.5, Aleph 2.0 and Seedance 2.0 Pro |
| Lowest entry price | Kling | Standard is $79.20 for the first year on annual billing, about $6.60 a month |
| Cheapest credits at volume | Kling | Premier lists $0.76 per 100 credits against $1.00 on Standard |
| Clear price per clip | Runway | Each model shows its credits per generation, such as 60 credits for a 5 second Gen-4.5 clip |
| Free trial that does not expire | Runway | Free plan gives 125 credits once, and they do not expire |
| Video extension up to three minutes | Kling | Member benefits list extension multiple times up to a maximum of 3 minutes |

## What Runway charges

Runway runs a Free plan and three paid tiers. Prices below are per month, shown as monthly billing then annual billing at 20% off.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual rate | Credits per month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 | 125 one time |
| Standard | $15 | $12 | 625 |
| Pro | $35 | $28 | 2,250 |
| Max | $95 | $76 | 9,500 (114,000 a year) |

Credits are the unit for everything: video, images and audio. Runway's FAQ says Gen-4.5 uses 12 credits per second of video. On that rate a 5 second clip costs 60 credits, which the plan table confirms, and Runway says that works out to 125 clips a year on Standard, 450 on Pro and 1,900 on Max, on annual plans.

Our arithmetic from those numbers: at monthly billing, a 60 credit Gen-4.5 clip costs about $1.44 on Standard, $0.93 on Pro and $0.60 on Max. Annual billing lowers each of those by 20%. The Max tier is where the credit price falls hardest, which is the opposite of how most creators buy.

Two traps sit in the FAQ. On Standard and Pro, unused monthly credits do not roll over and reset within 24 hours of your billing date. On Max, up to one month rolls over. Extra credits can be bought from 1,000 credits at a time and never expire. If you miss a month on Standard, those credits are gone.

## What Kling charges

Kling sells membership by the year on its own site, with a 34% discount for yearly billing and a 12% discount for monthly. The page shows these tiers.

| Plan | Yearly price | Credits per month | Price per 100 credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Free | Daily log-in credits | None |
| Standard | $79.20 (list $120) | 660 | $1.00 |
| Pro | $293.04 (list $444) | 3,000 | $0.81 |
| Premier | $728.64 (list $1,104) | 8,000 | $0.76 |

The page says "Next Year: $79.2 [34% Off]", which reads as a renewal price, but the list price shown struck through is $120, so check the checkout screen to see what year two costs before you subscribe. Paid tiers add image upscaling, video extension, watermark removal, professional mode and fast track generation. Premier and Pro add priority access to new features.

What we could not verify is credits per clip on Kling's own site. The membership page does not list them, so we cannot give you a cost per video there. Runway's number for Kling 4.0, 118 credits for 5 seconds at 1080p, is the only per-clip figure on either page for Kling's model.

## The reseller effect

Because Runway hosts Kling 4.0, you can test it on Runway before committing to Kling's annual plan. A 5 second clip at 118 credits is about $2.83 on Standard monthly billing by our arithmetic, and the 25% promotion until 30 November takes that credit cost to 89. That is a cheap way to find out whether Kling's motion style suits your work.

The catch is that you are paying Runway's credit rate for Kling's model. If you plan to run it heavily, Kling's own Pro or Premier credits at $0.81 and $0.76 per 100 will be cheaper, as long as the per-clip credit cost on Kling's side is comparable. Test first, then decide.

## Where each one fails

Runway fails on cost at the entry tier. The Standard plan's 625 monthly credits buy about ten 5 second Gen-4.5 clips, and they expire at the end of the cycle. Anything beyond casual use pushes you to Pro.

Kling fails on transparency. The membership page gives credits per month but not credits per generation, and the renewal wording is easy to misread. Plan for a surprise at year two unless you check.

## Who should skip which

Skip Runway if you only want Kling's model and plan to generate hundreds of clips, because its credits are priced for breadth. Skip Kling if you want to compare several video models side by side in one place, since the Runway table puts four models and their costs in the same grid. ## Which should you pick

If you are not sure what you need, start on Runway Standard, spend one month of credits on Gen-4.5 and Kling 4.0, and keep the clips you like. If Kling wins, move to Kling's yearly Pro plan, which has the better credit price. If you want one place to try models, stay on Runway and move to Max when you hit the credit ceiling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does Runway cost?

Runway has a free plan with 125 one-time credits, then Standard at $15 a month, Pro at $35 and Max at $95 on monthly billing. Annual billing is 20% off, which makes them $12, $28 and $76.

### How much does Kling cost?

Kling's yearly plans are Standard at $79.20, Pro at $293.04 and Premier at $728.64, giving 660, 3,000 and 8,000 credits a month. There is also a free Basic tier with daily log-in credits.

### Can I use Kling inside Runway?

Yes. Runway's plan table lists Kling 4.0 at 1080p for 118 credits per 5 second clip, with a 25% discount on Kling 4.0 generations until 30 November 2026.

### Do Runway credits roll over?

Not on Standard or Pro, where credits reset within 24 hours of your billing date. On Max up to one month of unused credits rolls over, and purchased credits never expire.
