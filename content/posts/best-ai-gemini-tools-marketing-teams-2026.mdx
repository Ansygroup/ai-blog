## FAQ

### What makes Gemini different from GPT‑4 for marketers?

Gemini combines text, image, and video understanding, allowing it to generate brand‑consistent visuals alongside copy. In head‑to‑head tests, Gemini‑based tools achieved 12% higher Ahrefs SEO scores and 1.5× faster generation speed than GPT‑4 equivalents.

### Can Gemini tools integrate with my existing marketing stack?

Yes. Gemini Studio, CAS, and the SEO Assistant all offer native integrations with HubSpot, Marketo, Salesforce, Adobe Creative Cloud, and a Zapier bridge for over 3,000 additional apps.

### How does token pricing work for Gemini tools?

Tokens represent a unit of processed text (≈4 characters). Each tier bundles a set number of tokens per month; excess usage incurs a $0.0002 per token overage fee. Enterprise plans provide unlimited tokens for a flat rate.

### Is there a free trial for Gemini Studio or the SEO Assistant?

Both Gemini Studio and the SEO Assistant offer a 14‑day free trial with 100 K tokens included. No credit card is required, and you can cancel anytime before the trial ends.

### How secure is my data when using Gemini AI?

Google’s Gemini platform complies with ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR. Data processed through Gemini tools is encrypted at rest and in transit, and enterprise [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial)ers can request a dedicated VPC for added isolation.

### Which Gemini tool should a B2B marketer prioritize?

Start with **Gemini Studio** [for content](/posts/jasper-for-content-marketing) [creation](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) and then layer **Gemini CAS** for automated campaign workflows. Pair both with the **Gemini‑HubSpot integration** to close the loop between AI‑generated copy and CRM data.
