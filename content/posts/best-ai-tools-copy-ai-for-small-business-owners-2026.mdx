**Why Small Businesses May Skip It:** The price is steep for startups with < $5 K monthly [marketing](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) spend. However, for e‑commerce stores scaling beyond $50 K/month, the ROI quickly outweighs the cost.

### How accurate are the SEO recommendations in Writesonic?

### What’s the biggest drawback of using AI [copy tools](/posts/best-ai-copy-tools-for-nonprofits-2026) for a small business?
