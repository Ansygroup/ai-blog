## Core Capabilities: Video Generation vs Text Generation

Synthesia’s core engine turns a script into a 1080p video with a selectable avatar, background, and subtitle track. In 2026, the platform supports **30 languages**, and its new “Dynamic Speech Style” lets you choose [tone](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026) (e.g., corporate, casual) with **±5 dB volume normalization**. The average rendering time for a 60‑second clip is **1.8 seconds** per second of final video, thanks to GPU‑accelerated inference.

ChatGPT, by contrast, processes **text [prompts](/posts/copy-ai-prompting-guide-2026)** into responses up to 8,192 tokens (≈12,000 words). GPT‑4.5 Turbo’s improvements include **30 % lower hallucination rates** and **real‑time tool use**—the [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) can fetch live [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial), run Python snippets, or generate SVG graphics on demand. For content teams, this translates into faster copy generation, [automated](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) summarization, and even code snippets for internal tools.
