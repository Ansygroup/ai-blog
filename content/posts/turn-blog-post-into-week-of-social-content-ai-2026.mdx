By the end of this [tutorial](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) you will have a repeatable workflow that turns one published blog post into seven days of platform-native social content: a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a Threads post, an Instagram caption, a short video script, a newsletter blurb, and a quote graphic brief. Not the same link pasted seven times, but seven pieces built for where they will live.

**Time required:** about 90 [minutes](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) the first time, under an hour once your prompts are saved.
**Cost:** a chatbot [subscription](/posts/grammarly-pricing-breakdown-2026). ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro are both $20 a month as of November 2026; Claude's longer context is nicer for pasting [full](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-marketing-teams-2026) posts.

## Prerequisites

- A published blog post of at least 800 words, with a clear argument and at least three distinct claims or insights.
- A chatbot account.
- A short [brand](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026)-voice note: five lines about how you [sound](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026), two example posts you liked, and topics or words you avoid.

## Step 1: Extract the atoms, not the [summary](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026)

Most repurposing fails here: people ask for "social posts about my article" and get seven beige summaries. Instead, ask the [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) to mine the post for its atoms:

```
Read this blog post. List: (1) the single strongest claim, (2) three
specific insights with their supporting detail, (3) two contrarian or
surprising points, (4) one personal story or concrete example, (5) any
number or stat [worth](/posts/grammarly-for-teams-2026-review) quoting. Quote the post's exact wording where it
is sharp. [paste post]
```

Seven to ten atoms is a week of raw material. Each atom becomes content; the post itself never gets "summarized for social," which is how you avoid the beige.

## Step 2: Generate per-platform pieces with real constraints

One atom per piece, one prompt per platform, and [give](/posts/cursor-editor-deep-dive-2026) the model the platform's actual physics:

- **LinkedIn:** "Turn this atom into a LinkedIn post. First line must work as a standalone hook visible before 'see more.' Under 150 words, short paragraphs, no hashtags at the top, end with a genuine question."
- **X thread:** "Turn this atom into a thread of 4 to 6 posts, each under 280 characters, each understandable alone. First post is the hook and makes no mention of a thread."
- **Instagram caption:** "Caption for a [carousel](/posts/how-to-use-ai-to-generate-linkedin-carousel-posts-that-drive-engagement-2026-guide) [version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) of this atom: 3 short paragraphs, conversational, 3 to 5 hashtags at the end only."
- **Short video script:** "45-second vertical video script from this atom: spoken hook in the first sentence, one idea only, end with a comment prompt."

- **Threads:** "Adapt this atom for Threads: under 100 words, looser and more personal than LinkedIn, no hashtags, one idea, ends with a light question."
- **Newsletter blurb:** "Turn this atom into a 60-word newsletter section: one sentence of context, the insight, and a natural reference to the full post."
- **Quote graphic brief:** "Write the text for a quote card: one sharp sentence from this atom under 15 words, [plus](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) a one-line [design](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) note (what the card should show)."

Then apply your brand-voice note to every output in one more pass: "Rewrite in this voice: [note]. Keep the structure, change the words."

## Step 3: Edit like a publisher

Budget at least a third of your time here. The pass: cut every hedge ("might," "could potentially"), delete any sentence that could appear under anyone else's post, and add one thing only you could say to each piece: a number from your work, a named example, a mistake you made. If a piece has nothing only-you in it, cut the piece, not the standard.

## Step 4: Sequence and schedule the week

Order matters. Lead Monday with your strongest atom on your strongest platform, spread formats so no two adjacent days feel like reruns, and put the blog link in at most two of the seven pieces (as a natural "full [breakdown](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026)" reference, not a bare link). Load everything into your scheduler of choice in one sitting; the batching is the time saving.

## Step 5: Close the loop

After the week, note which atom and which format outperformed, and feed that [back](/posts/cursor-editor-deep-dive-2026) into step 1 next time: "my audience responds to contrarian points more than how-tos" changes [what you](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) mine. Repurposing without this loop plateaus in a month.

## What can go [wrong](/posts/jasper-limitations-2026-what-ai-writers-get-wrong)

- **Sameness across [platforms](/posts/best-ai-powered-push-notification-ab-testing-platforms-2026).** Identical text on LinkedIn, X, and Threads reads as spam to anyone who follows you twice. Different atoms, different angles, or do not cross-post.
- **The AI accent.** Em-dash-heavy hooks, "Here's the truth:" openers, triads of adjectives. Your [edit](/posts/copilot-vs-cursor-vs-windsurf-ai-code-editor-comparison) pass exists to remove these.
- **Link-only posts dying.** Platforms throttle outbound links; a bare "new post, link below" underperforms a real piece with no link. Let some atoms stand alone and build authority instead of traffic.
- **Recycling the hook.** If every piece opens with the post's headline, you published the same post seven times. Each atom gets its own angle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to repurpose a blog post with AI?

About 90 minutes the first time with this workflow, under an hour once your prompts and voice note are saved. Editing takes at least a third of that time; do not skip it.

### Why do my AI social posts sound generic?

Because 'write social posts about my article' asks for summaries. Mine the post for specific atoms (claims, numbers, contrarian points, stories) and build one piece per atom, then add one detail only you could say to each.

### Should every repurposed post link back to the blog?

No. Put the link in at most two of seven pieces. Platforms throttle outbound links, and standalone pieces build the authority that makes people want the full post.

### Which chatbot is better for repurposing content?

Both work at $20 a month (as of November 2026). Claude's longer context handles full-post pastes comfortably; ChatGPT is faster for iterating on variants. The prompts matter more than the model.
