The [comparison](/posts/ai-youtube-chapter-timestamp-generators-2026) most people are still searching for is a [generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) out of date. As of 27 November 2026, Anthropic's live price sheet lists Claude Fable 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Haiku 5.5. Google's lists Gemini 4 Carbon, Gemini 4 Argon, Gemini 4 Neon and Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Claude 4 and Gemini 3 were the matchup that mattered in early 2026. Both lineups have moved on, and the move changed the arithmetic that decides which one your team should pay for.

That arithmetic also has an expiry date this week. Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon [cost](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) $2.00 per million input tokens and $10.00 per million output tokens through 30 November 2026. On 1 December 2026 the same two models go to $4.00 and $20.00. Anyone [building](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026) a budget from today's page is budgeting against a discount with four days left on it.

Anthropic's top model is Fable 5.2 at $8 in and $40 out per million tokens, with Mythos 5.2 beside it in limited availability at the same rate. Opus 5.5 is the general workhorse at $4 and $20, Sonnet 5.5 the middle tier at $2 and $10, and Haiku 5.5 the cheap tier starting at $0.10 and $0.50. All four current models take a 1M token context window and return up to 128K tokens. On the Batch API the same models will return up to 300k output tokens if you send the beta header for it.

Google's lineup is harder to hold in your head because the names are new and the tiers overlap. Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon carry identical list [prices](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) today, $2 and $10, and Google describes Argon as the more efficient of the two, with faster responses and lower token spend per turn. ## The details that decide real bills

Three small print items do more damage than the headline rates.

The first is caching. Claude cache reads cost 10% of the base input price on most models, but 2.5% on Fable 5.2 and Fable 5.1, and 5% on Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5. Gemini's implicit caching on Carbon and Argon is $0.10 per million tokens through 30 November and $0.20 from 1 December, and explicit caching is not supported on those two models at all. If your workload leans on one large stable prompt, the ability to [control](/posts/shopify-conversational-big-picture-design-control-2026-guide) a cache is worth more than the sticker price.

The second is search grounding. Gemini includes 5,000 free search requests a month across Gemini 3 and newer models, then charges $14 per 1,000 requests with a daily cap of 1,500. Claude charges $10 per 1,000 searches and does not include the tokens those searches produce. Under roughly 14,000 grounded queries a month Gemini is cheaper; above that Claude is. The daily cap is the part that breaks pipelines. A ceiling of 1,500 requests a day means a batch job that suddenly scales will fail rather than bill, and failing quietly at 2am is worse than a bigger invoice.

The third is that the flagship model is metered on subscriptions too. On Claude Max at $100 a month and Ultra at $200 a month, Fable is included up to half your weekly limit, and past that you spend usage credits. Ultra gives 10x Pro's weekly usage with no 5-hour limit for verified accounts, and both [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) include monthly API credits, $100 on Max and $200 on Ultra. Pro is $20 a month, or $17 a month if you pay $200 up front for the year.

## Where each one fails

Claude's failure mode is cost drift on [agentic](/posts/perplexity-comet-browser-2026-review) work. Every retry, every tool round trip and every re-read of a file is billed input. A 1M token window is not an invitation to fill it: on Fable 5.2, one [full](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-marketing-teams-2026)-window prompt costs $8 before the model writes a word.

Gemini's failure mode is naming and deprecation churn. Four frontier names, two of them identically priced, one deprecated Flash model rerouting in December, and caching rates stepping up twice. A team that pins a model string and stops reading the pricing page will get surprised, and the surprise arrives as a budget line, not an error message.

## Who should skip which

Skip Claude if your workload is mostly grounded search, or if [you need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) a free API tier to prototype. Skip Gemini if your [finance](/posts/best-ai-claude-tools-for-accountants-2026) team needs a rate card that holds still for two quarters, or if you depend on explicit cache control on the frontier models. If you are picking an assistant for general use rather than an API, [the three-way assistant comparison](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-gemini) covers the chat products separately from these prices.

## Which should you pick

For an agent product that runs long and recovers from its own mistakes, Claude Opus 5.5 at $4 and $20 is the safer buy today, and it is still $4 and $20 on Tuesday. For document work where the input is enormous and the output is long, Gemini 4 is the only one of the two that decodes up to a million tokens.

For everything else the honest answer is uncomfortable: until 1 December 2026 the two are priced the same, and after that date the cheaper of the two frontier families is Anthropic's. Put that date in the spreadsheet, not the benchmark chart.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Claude 4 still available?

Mostly not. Anthropic's current model sheet lists Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4 as retired except on Google Cloud and Bedrock, and the shipping lineup is Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5.

### Which is cheaper, Claude or Gemini?

Today they are level: Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Gemini 4 Carbon both cost $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. From 1 December 2026 Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon double to $4 and $20, which leaves Sonnet 5.5 at half the price.

### Do I need a [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) plan to try either one?

Gemini's API has a free tier on Gemini 4 Hydrogen, and the other Gemini 4 models have none. Claude's API pricing sheet lists no free tier, though both companies have free consumer plans in their [apps](/posts/best-notebooklm-tools-2026).

### Can I switch models later without rewriting my product?

Both vendors expose model strings you can change in configuration, and both keep older models live for a while. The real cost of switching is prompt tuning and re-testing, not code changes.
