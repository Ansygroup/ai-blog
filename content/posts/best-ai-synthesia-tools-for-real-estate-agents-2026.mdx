Beyond property tours, Synthesia can produce agent [intro](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) videos, market updates, and client testimonials. By using the same avatar across all content, agents build a recognizable personal brand without the fatigue of on‑camera appearances.

## Integration and Workflow: How to Add AI Video into Your Listings

Seamless [integration](/posts/best-ai-tools-suno-for-developers-2026) is essential to avoid adding friction to an agent’s [workflow](/posts/best-ai-copy-tools-for-translators-2026). Here’s a step‑by‑step blueprint that works across the top platforms:

1. **Data Pull** – Export property details (price, square footage, photos) from your MLS into a CSV.  
2. **Script Generation** – Use a GPT‑4 [prompt](/posts/fixing-undefined-errors-ai-apps) (e.g., “Create a 60‑second script for a 3‑bedroom condo in downtown Seattle”) and feed the output into the AI video UI.  
3. **Avatar Selection** – Choose an avatar that matches your [brand voice](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026). Synthesia allows you to upload a custom headshot for a fully branded avatar; Rephrase.ai offers pre‑made real‑estate agents.  
4. **Video Rendering** – Trigger rendering via API (Synthesia, Deepbrain, Colossyan) or manual click (Veed.io). Average render time: 12 seconds per 10 seconds of video for Synthesia’s [cloud](/posts/pinecone-setup-guide-2026)‑based GPU.  
5. **Embedding** – Once rendered, embed the video URL into your listing page, [email](/posts/ai-driven-email-marketing-automation-tools-review) campaign, or [social media](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) post. Most platforms provide an iframe snippet that auto‑optimizes for mobile.  
6. **Analytics Loop** – Pull watch‑time data from the platform’s dashboard and feed it back into your CRM to score leads.

By automating steps 1‑4 with Zapier or Integromat, agents can generate a video for every new listing within 5 minutes of data entry—turning video [marketing](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) from a “nice‑to‑have” into a “must‑have”.

### How long does it take to create a Synthesia video for a property?

Typically 10–15 minutes from script entry to final render, thanks to Synthesia’s GPU‑accelerated cloud processing. Complex videos with multiple scenes may take up to 30 minutes.

### Can I use my own voice instead of an avatar’s voice?

Yes. Synthesia and Deepbrain AI both offer voice‑cloning services. Upload a 30‑second sample, and the platform will generate a [synthetic](/posts/heygen-accuracy-test-2026) voice that matches yours for all [future](/posts/gamma-limitations-2026) videos.

### Are the AI‑generated videos MLS‑compliant?

All major platforms provide customizable disclaimer overlays and watermarks to meet fair‑housing and MLS guidelines. You should still verify local regulations, but the tools make compliance straightforward.

### What languages are supported for real‑estate video [subtitles](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)?

### Is there a free trial I can use before committing?
