# Training a Custom AI [Model](/posts/claude-4-vs-gpt-4o-vs-gemini-1-5-2026) with No [Code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) Using [Google](/posts/gemini-in-chrome-android-2026-native-browser-ai) AutoML
You can train a custom AI model with Google AutoML. No [coding](/posts/best-ai-coding-assistants-2026-comparative-review) is required.

<div class="quick-answer">

## Introduction to No-[Code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) AI
No-code AI platforms like [Google](/posts/gemini-in-chrome-android-2026-native-browser-ai) AutoML, H2O AutoML, and BigML are changing the field of artificial intelligence. These tools allow non-technical users to train custom AI [models](/posts/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl) without [writing](/posts/best-ai-writing-tools-for-fiction-2026) code.

## How [Google](/posts/gemini-in-chrome-android-2026-native-browser-ai) AutoML Works
[Google](/posts/google-managed-agents-2026-production-agentic-deployments) AutoML has a simple interface for training custom AI [models](/posts/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl). The platform offers pre-trained models for text, [image](/posts/best-ai-image-generators-2026), and tabular [data](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-data-analysis-2026), making it easy to get started. For example, you can use AutoML to [build](/posts/ai-for-seo-content-clusters-2026) a text classification model to categorize [customer feedback](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-data-analysis-2026) as positive, negative, or neutral.


![Choosing the Right [Model](/posts/claude-4-vs-gpt-4o-vs-gemini-1-5-2026)](/images/illustrations/auto-ml-tutorial-illo2.svg)
## Choosing the Right [Model](/posts/claude-4-vs-gpt-4o-vs-gemini-1-5-2026)
When selecting a pre-trained [model](/posts/claude-4-vs-gpt-4o-vs-gemini-1-5-2026) on Google AutoML, consider the type of [data](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-data-analysis-2026) you're working with and the task you want to accomplish. If you're working with [image](/posts/best-ai-image-generators-2026) data, the AutoML Vision [model](/posts/claude-4-vs-gpt-4o-vs-gemini-1-5-2026) may be a good choice. If you're working with text [data](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial), the AutoML Natural Language model may be a [better](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude) fit.

## Training a Custom Model
Training a custom model with [Google](/posts/gemini-in-chrome-android-2026-native-browser-ai) AutoML is straightforward. Upload your dataset, configure the hyperparameters, and [click](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026) the "Train" button. The process takes less than 10 minutes, and you can monitor the training progress in real [time](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026).

## Evaluating Model Performance
After training a [custom](/posts/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl) model, evaluate its performance using metrics like accuracy, precision, and recall. [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) AutoML provides evaluation tools, including confusion matrices and ROC curves.

## Deploying a Trained [Model](/posts/claude-4-vs-gpt-4o-vs-gemini-1-5-2026)
Once you've trained and evaluated a custom model, you can deploy it to [platforms](/posts/comparison-ai-powered-influencer-marketing-platforms-beauty-industry) like Google Cloud, AWS, and Azure. You can also use the model to make predictions on new [data](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-data-analysis-2026).

## Who Should Use Google AutoML
Google AutoML is ideal for non-technical users who want to train custom AI models without writing code. This includes [business](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026) analysts, data scientists, and [marketers](/posts/prompt-engineering-for-marketers) who want to leverage AI to drive [business](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026) insights.

## Who Should Skip Google AutoML
If you're an experienced [data](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial) scientist or machine learning engineer, you may prefer more advanced platforms like TensorFlow or PyTorch. If you're working with sensitive [data](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial), you may want to consider more secure platforms like Amazon SageMaker.

## FAQ

### What is Google AutoML?
Google AutoML is a no-[code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) AI platform that allows users to train custom AI models without writing code.

### How [long](/posts/how-to-use-ai-to-summarize-long-documents-2026) does it take to train a custom model with Google AutoML?
Training a custom model with Google AutoML takes less than 10 minutes.

### What types of [models](/posts/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl) can I train with Google AutoML?
You can train text, [image](/posts/best-ai-image-generators-2026), and tabular [models](/posts/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl) with Google AutoML.

### Can I use Google AutoML for free?
No, [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) AutoML is a paid platform, but it offers a free trial to get started.

### How [does](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026) Google AutoML compare to H2O AutoML?
Google AutoML is more user-friendly and offers [faster](/posts/how-to-use-ai-to-learn-faster-2026) training times than H2O AutoML.

### Can I deploy a trained [model](/posts/claude-4-opus-vs-sonnet-comparison-2026) to AWS or Azure?
Yes, you can deploy a trained model to platforms like Google Cloud, AWS, and Azure.

## Final [Verdict](/posts/jasper-ai-review-2026)
Google AutoML is our top pick for no-[code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) AI model training.
