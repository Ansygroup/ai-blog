## Introduction to No-Code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) AI
No-code AI [platforms like Google](/news/gemini-in-chrome-android-2026-native-browser-ai) AutoML, H2O AutoML, and BigML are changing the field of artificial intelligence. These tools allow non-technical users to train custom AI [models without writing](/posts/best-ai-writing-tools-for-fiction-2026) code.

## How [Google AutoML Works
Google](/news/google-managed-agents-2026-production-agentic-deployments) AutoML has a simple interface for training custom AI [models. The platform offers pre-trained models for text, image](/posts/best-ai-image-generators-2026), and tabular [data, making it easy to get started. For example, you can use AutoML to build](/posts/ai-for-seo-content-clusters-2026) a text classification model to categorize [customer feedback as positive, negative, or neutral.

## Choosing the Right Model](/posts/claude-4-vs-gpt-4o-vs-gemini-1-5-2026)
When selecting a pre-trained [model on Google AutoML, consider the type of data](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-data-analysis-2026) you're working with and the task you want to accomplish. If you're working with [image data, the AutoML Vision model](/posts/claude-4-vs-gpt-4o-vs-gemini-1-5-2026) may be a good choice. If you're working with text [data, the AutoML Natural Language model may be a better](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude) fit.

## Training a Custom Model
Training a custom model with [Google AutoML is straightforward. Upload your dataset, configure the hyperparameters, and click](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026) the "Train" button. The process takes less than 10 minutes, and you can monitor the training progress in real [time.

After training a [custom model, evaluate its performance using metrics like accuracy, precision, and recall. Google](/news/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) AutoML provides evaluation tools, including confusion matrices and ROC curves.

## Who Should Use Google AutoML
