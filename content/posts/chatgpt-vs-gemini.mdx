## LLM Architecture and Context Windows Compared

When evaluating the underlying technology of ChatGPT vs Gemini, the conversation inevitably turns to context windows. A context window determines how much information the AI can hold in its active memory during a single conversation.

```
CONTEXT WINDOW COMPARISON
ChatGPT (GPT-4o) | [███] 128,000 Tokens (~90k words)
-----------------------------------------------------
Gemini Advanced | [████████████████████] 2,000,000 Tokens (~1.5M words)
```

OpenAI’s GPT-4o and reasoning models typically offer a **128,000-token context window**. This is roughly equivalent to a 300-page book or about 90,000 words. While this is more than enough for everyday](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/qa-what-are-the-best-ai-productivity-tools-for-everyday-work)](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/qa-what-are-the-best-ai-productivity-tools-for-everyday-work) queries, [writing](/posts/chatgpt-vs-copy-ai) short articles, or debugging isolated scripts](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/ai-instagram-reel-scripts), it quickly falls short when you try to upload massive codebases or multiple [academ](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/gemini-for-students-2026)ic](/posts/claude-for-students-2026) textbooks.

Google’s Gemini 1.5 Pro, available](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/qa-what-is-the-best-ai-image-generator-available-today](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/qa-what-is-the-best-ai-image-generator-available-today)) in Gemini Advanced, complete](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/midjourney-for-coding)ly shatters this limitation with a **2-million token context window**. * Up to an hour of high-definition [video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison)
* Over 30,000 lines of [code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026)
* Up to 1.5 million words of text (equivalent to several massive novels or decades of financial reports)

In our testing, we uploaded a 1,200-page PDF of financial regulations into both systems.
