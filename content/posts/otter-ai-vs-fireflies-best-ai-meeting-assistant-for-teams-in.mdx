## What Are AI Meeting Assistants?

AI meeting assistants use artificial intelligence to record, transcribe, and analyze meetings. They help teams stay organized, improve communication, and make data-driven decisions.

## Otter AI Review

Otter AI is a popular AI meeting assistant known for its real-time transcription and seamless integrations with Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. It offers a free plan with limited features and several paid plans starting at $8.33/user/month.
