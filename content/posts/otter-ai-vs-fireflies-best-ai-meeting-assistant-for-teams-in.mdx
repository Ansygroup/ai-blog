# Otter AI vs Fireflies: Best AI Meeting Assistant for Teams in 2026

In 2026, AI meeting [assistants](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) are revolutionizing team collaboration. Two top contenders are Otter AI and Fireflies. Let's compare them.

<div class="quick-answer">

## What Are AI [Meeting](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-meeting-notes-2026) [Assistants](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026)?

AI meeting assistants use artificial [intelligence](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-data-visualization-for-ecommerce-business-intelligence) to record, transcribe, and analyze meetings. They help teams stay organized, improve communication, and make [data](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial)-driven decisions.

## Otter AI Review

Otter AI is a popular AI meeting assistant known for its real-[time](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026) [transcription](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-transcription-services-for-podcaste) and seamless integrations with [Google](/posts/gemini-2-0-features-2026) Meet, Zoom, and [Microsoft](/posts/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl) Teams. It offers a free plan with limited [features](/posts/chatgpt-api-guide) and several paid plans starting at $8.33/user/month.

## Fireflies Review

Fireflies is another top AI meeting assistant that offers [advanced](/posts/gemini-advanced-vs-google-one-ai-premium) AI-powered analysis and insights. It integrates with popular [video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) conferencing platforms and offers a free plan with limited features. Paid plans start at $10/user/month.

## Integrations and Compatibility

Both Otter AI and Fireflies integrate with popular [video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) conferencing [platforms](/posts/comparison-ai-powered-influencer-marketing-platforms-beauty-industry), including [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. However, Otter AI offers more extensive integrations with third-party [apps](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026).

## AI-Powered Analysis and Insights

Fireflies offers more advanced [AI-powered](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-customer-journey-mapping-tools-for-healthcare) [[analysis](/](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-data-analysis-and-visualization-for-small-business-owners)posts/best-ai-[data](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial)-[analysis](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis)-visualization-tools-small-business) and insights, including sentiment analysis and topic [modeling](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-predictive-modeling-in-cybersecurity-threat-detection). Otter AI provides basic analytics and reporting.

## Pricing and Plans

Otter AI offers a free plan [with limited](/posts/review-of-ai-driven-podcast-editing-tools-for-independent-podcasters-with-limited-budget) features, while Fireflies offers a 14-day free trial. Both tools offer several [paid](/posts/jasper-vs-free-version) plans, with Otter AI starting at $8.33/user/month and Fireflies starting at $10/user/month.

## [User Experience](/posts/ai-driven-website-testing-tools-ux-comparison) and Support

Both Otter AI and Fireflies offer user-friendly interfaces and robust [[customer](/post](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-customer-support-2026)s/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) support. However, Otter AI provides more extensive documentation and tutorials.

## Who Should Use Otter AI?

Otter AI is ideal for teams that prioritize real-time [transcription](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-automated-twitch-stream-transcription) and seamless integrations. It's a great choice for teams already using Google Meet, Zoom, or [Microsoft](/posts/microsoft-project-polaris-copilot-2026) Teams.

## Who Should Use Fireflies?

Fireflies is perfect for teams that need advanced AI-powered [[analysis](/](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026)posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) and [insights](/posts/claude-reddit-reviews-2026). It's a great choice for teams that want to gain deeper insights into their meetings and discussions.

## Who Should Skip These Tools?

Teams with limited budgets or simple [meeting](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-meeting-notes-2026) needs may not need AI meeting assistants. Other alternatives like [Notion](https://www.notion.so/) or [Evernote](https://evernote.com/) may be more suitable.
