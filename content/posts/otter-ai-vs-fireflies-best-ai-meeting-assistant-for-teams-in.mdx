# Otter AI vs Fireflies: Best AI Meeting Assistant for Teams in 2026

In 2026, AI [meeting](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) assistants are revolutionizing team collaboration. Two top contenders are Otter AI and Fireflies. Let's compare them.

## What Are AI Meeting Assistants?

AI meeting assistants use artificial intelligence to record, transcribe, and analyze meetings. They help [teams](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-marketing-teams-2026) stay organized, improve communication, and make [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial)-driven decisions.

## Otter AI Review

Otter AI is a popular AI meeting assistant known for its [real-time](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026) [transcription](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) and seamless integrations with Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. It offers a free plan with limited features and several paid plans starting at $8.33/user/month.

## Fireflies Review

Fireflies is another top AI meeting assistant that offers advanced AI-powered [analysis](/posts/ai-powered-content-gap-analysis-niche-blog-topics-2026) and insights. It integrates with popular [video](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) conferencing platforms and offers a free plan with limited features. Paid plans start at $10/user/month.

## Integrations and Compatibility

Both Otter AI and Fireflies integrate with popular video conferencing platforms, including Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. However, Otter AI offers more extensive integrations with third-party [apps](/posts/best-notebooklm-tools-2026).

## AI-Powered Analysis and Insights

Fireflies offers more advanced AI-powered analysisposts/best-ai-data-analysis-visualization-tools-small-business) and insights, including [sentiment analysis](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026) and topic modeling. Otter AI provides basic [analytics](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-marketing-teams-2026) and reporting.

## Pricing and Plans

Otter AI offers a free plan with limited features, while Fireflies offers a 14-day free trial. Both tools offer several paid plans, with Otter AI starting at $8.33/user/month and Fireflies starting at $10/user/month.

## User Experience and Support

Both Otter AI and Fireflies offer user-friendly interfaces and robust customers/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) [support](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-doctors-2026). However, Otter AI provides more extensive [documentation](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026) and [tutorials](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial).

## Who Should Use Otter AI?

Otter AI is ideal [for teams](/posts/descript-for-teams-2026-review) that prioritize real-time transcription and seamless integrations. It's a great choice for teams already using Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams.

## Who Should Use Fireflies?

Fireflies is perfect for teams that [need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) advanced AI-powered analysisposts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) and insights. It's a great choice for teams that want to gain deeper insights into their meetings and discussions.

## Who Should Skip These Tools?

Teams with limited budgets or simple [meeting needs may not need AI meeting assistants. Other alternatives like [Notion](https://www.notion.so/) or Evernote](https://evernote.com/) may be more suitable.
