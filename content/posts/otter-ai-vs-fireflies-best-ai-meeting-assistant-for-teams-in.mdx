## What Are AI [Meeting Assistants](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026)?

AI meeting assistants use [artificial intelligence](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-data-visualization-for-ecommerce-business-intelligence) to record, transcribe, and analyze meetings. They help teams stay organized, improve communication, and make [data-driven decisions.

## Otter AI Review

Otter AI is a popular AI meeting assistant known for its real-[time transcription](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-transcription-services-for-podcaste) and seamless integrations with [Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft](/news/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl) Teams. It offers a free plan with limited [features and several paid plans starting at $8.33/user/month.

Fireflies is another top AI meeting assistant that offers [advanced AI-powered analysis and insights. It integrates with popular video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) conferencing platforms and offers a free plan with limited features. Paid plans start at $10/user/month.
