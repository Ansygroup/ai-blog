2. **Chatbot Support**: Implement AI-powered chatbots to enhance attendee engagement and provide real-time support. ManyChat's chatbots can be integrated with popular event platforms, such as Zoom and [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Meet.
