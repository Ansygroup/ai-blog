## [Features](/posts/gemini-2-0-features-2026) and Capabilities

### [Descript](/posts/descript-vs-riverside)

Descript offers a range of advanced features, including:
* Overdubbing: create realistic-sounding [voice](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-voiceover-production) clones
* Multitrack editing: [work](/posts/how-to-make-money-with-ai) with multiple [audio](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) tracks simultaneously
* AI-powered noise reduction: automatically remove background noise

Descript's pricing starts at $15/month (billed annually). You can also pair Descript with a [Shure SM7B microphone](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00006IUWB?tag=ansy07-20) for [high](/posts/review-of-ai-driven-seo-audit-tools-for-ecommerce-websites-with-high-traffic)-quality recordings.

### Riverside

Riverside excels in remote recording and live streaming capabilities:
* 4K [video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) recording: capture high-quality video and audio
* Live [streaming](/posts/best-webcams-streaming-gear-2026): [stream](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-automated-twitch-stream-transcription) directly to popular [platforms](/posts/comparison-ai-powered-influencer-marketing-platforms-beauty-industry)
* Automatic post-[production](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-voiceover-production): [AI-powered](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-customer-journey-mapping-tools-for-healthcare) editing and mixing

Riverside's pricing starts at $15/month (billed annually). For high-quality video recording, consider a [Logitech C920 webcam](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B001E4KFG0?tag=ansy07-20).

### Zencastr

Zencastr provides a user-friendly interface and seamless integration with popular platforms:
* High-quality [audio](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026) recording: up to 48kHz sample rate
* Automatic post-production: AI-powered editing and mixing
* Integration with popular [platforms](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-online-course-platforms-for-education): easily publish to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and more

Zencastr's pricing starts at $12/month (billed annually).

## FAQ

### What is [the best](/posts/the-best-ai-tools-for-content-repurposing) AI podcast editing tool for beginners?
For beginners, Zencastr is a great option due to its [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026)-friendly interface and seamless integration with popular platforms.

### Can I use Descript for [video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) editing?
While Descript is primarily an audio editing tool, it [does](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026) offer some basic video editing features. However, for more advanced video editing, consider a dedicated [video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) editing tool.

### [Does](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026) Riverside offer a free plan?
Yes, Riverside offers a free plan [limited](/posts/review-of-ai-driven-podcast-editing-tools-for-independent-podcasters-with-limited-budget) to 1 hour of [video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) [recording](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-podcasters-2026).

### Can I use Zencastr for live streaming?
Yes, Zencastr offers live streaming capabilities, although it's not its primary focus.

### Is Descript [worth the](/posts/cursor-ai-editor-review-2026) price?
[For podcasters](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-podcasters-2026) seeking advanced AI-powered editing features, Descript is worth the price. However, for those on a tight [budget](/posts/rytr-review-2026), more affordable [options](/posts/best-free-ai-tools-2026) may be available.
