## Features](/posts/gemini-2-0-features-2026) and Capabilities

### [Descript

Descript offers a range of advanced](/posts/chatgpt-prompting-guide-2026-advanced-techniques-tools) features, including:
* Overdubbing: create realistic-sounding [voice clones
* Multitrack editing: work](/posts/how-to-make-money-with-ai) with multiple [audio tracks simultaneously
* AI-powered noise reduction: automatically remove background noise

Descript's pricing starts at $15/month (billed annually). You can also pair Descript with a Shure SM7B microphone for high](/posts/review-of-ai-driven-seo-audit-tools-for-ecommerce-websites-with-high-traffic)-quality recordings.

### Riverside

Riverside excels in [remote recording and live](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026) [streaming capabilities](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026):
* 4K [video recording: capture high-quality video and audio
* Live streaming](/posts/best-webcams-streaming-gear-2026): [stream directly to popular platforms](/posts/comparison-ai-powered-influencer-marketing-platforms-beauty-industry)
* Automatic post-[production: AI-powered](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-customer-journey-mapping-tools-for-healthcare) editing and mixing

Riverside's pricing starts at $15/month (billed annually). For high-quality [video recording, consider a Logitech C920 webcam.

### Zencastr

Zencastr provides a user-friendly](/posts/best-ai-powered-resume-tailoring-ats-optimization-2026) interface and seamless [integration with popular platforms:
* High-quality audio](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026) recording: up to 48kHz sample rate
* Automatic post-production: AI-powered editing and mixing
* Integration with popular [platforms: easily publish to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and more

Zencastr's pricing starts at $12/month (billed annually).
