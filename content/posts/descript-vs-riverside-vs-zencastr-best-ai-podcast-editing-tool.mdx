## Features and Capabilities

### Descript

Descript offers a range of advanced features, including:
* Overdubbing: create realistic-sounding [voice](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) clones
* Multitrack editing: [work](/posts/best-ai-tools-otter-freelancers-2026) with multiple [audio](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) tracks simultaneously
* AI-powered noise reduction: automatically remove background noise

Descript's pricing starts at $15/month (billed annually). You can also pair Descript with a Shure SM7B microphone for high-quality recordings.

### Riverside

Riverside excels in [remote](/posts/best-ai-tools-otter-freelancers-2026) recording and live [streaming](/posts/bahrain-grand-prix-in-malaysia-f1-free-2026-guide) capabilities:
* 4K [video](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) recording: capture high-quality video and audio
* Live streaming: stream directly to popular [platforms](/posts/best-ai-powered-push-notification-ab-testing-platforms-2026)
* Automatic post-production: AI-powered editing and mixing

Riverside's pricing starts at $15/month (billed annually). For high-quality video recording, consider a Logitech C920 webcam.

### Zencastr

Zencastr provides a user-friendly interface and seamless [integration](/posts/best-ai-tools-otter-real-estate-agents-2026) with popular platforms:
* High-quality audio recording: up to 48kHz sample rate
* Automatic post-production: AI-powered editing and mixing
* Integration with popular platforms: easily publish to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and more

Zencastr's pricing starts at $12/month (billed annually).
