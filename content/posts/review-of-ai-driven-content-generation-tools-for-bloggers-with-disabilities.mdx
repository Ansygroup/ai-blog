## What Are AI-Driven Content Generation Tools?

AI-driven content generation tools use artificial intelligence to create content, such as articles, blog posts, and [social media](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) updates. These tools can help bloggers with disabilities by automating tasks, suggesting ideas, and generating content.
