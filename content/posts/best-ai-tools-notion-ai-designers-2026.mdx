## FAQ

### How does Notion AI generate images inside a Notion page?

Notion AI leverages the DALL·E 2 API. When you type a prompt in an “Image Generation” block, Notion sends the request to OpenAI, receives a 1024×1024 PNG, and embeds it directly. The process takes 2–5 seconds for most prompts, and you can regenerate up to 5 times per minute on the free tier.

### Can I export Figma AI designs directly to Notion?

Yes. Using the official **Notion‑Figma connector**, you can select a frame in Figma, click “Publish to Notion,” and the frame appears as an interactive embed. Updates in Figma sync automatically, keeping the Notion page current.

### Is Midjourney’s Discord integration secure for corporate use?

Midjourney runs on a private Discord server. While the data is transmitted over encrypted channels, the company does not store prompts long‑term. For [enterprise](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-agencies-2026)s with strict [compliance](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) needs, we recommend using **Stable Diffusion** on a self‑hosted instance instead.

### What’s the biggest limitation of Notion AI for designers right now?

The primary limitation is the **lack of vector‑format generation**. Notion AI can only produce raster images via DALL·E 2, which means designers often need to re‑import assets into Figma or Illustrator for scaling. Pairing Notion with a vector‑focused tool like Adobe Firefly mitigates this issue.
