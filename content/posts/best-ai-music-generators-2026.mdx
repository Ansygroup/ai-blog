## Top AI Music Generators 2026

### Suno

Suno is a leading AI [music generator](/posts/suno-vs-udio-vs-musicfx-best-ai-music-generator-2026) that offers [advanced](/posts/gemini-advanced-vs-google-one-ai-premium) text-to-music capabilities. Suno's algorithm is trained on a vast music dataset, ensuring that the generated music sounds [natural](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) and engaging. **Suno offers a free plan, with paid plans starting at $10/month**.

### Udio

Udio is another top AI music generator that excels in melody generation and [audio](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026) editing. Udio's interface is user-friendly, making it easy for musicians and producers to create and refine their music. **[Udio](/posts/suno-vs-udio-2026) offers a free trial, with paid plans starting at $20/month**.

### Amper Music

Amper Music is a popular AI music generator that offers a range of [features](/posts/gemini-2-0-features-2026), including melody generation, harmony [creation](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation), and audio editing. Amper Music is ideal for musicians and producers looking for a comprehensive music [creation](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) tool. **Amper Music offers a free trial, with paid plans starting at $15/month**.
