## Top AI Music Generators 2026

### Suno

Suno is a leading AI music generator that offers advanced [text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review)-to-music capabilities. With Suno, users can input text [prompts](/posts/copy-ai-prompting-guide-2026) and receive high-quality music outputs. Suno's algorithm is trained on a vast music dataset, ensuring that the generated music sounds natural and engaging. **Suno offers a free plan, with paid [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) starting at $10/month**.

### Udio

Udio is another top AI music generator that excels in melody [generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) and audio editing. Udio's interface is user-friendly, making it easy for musicians and producers to create and refine their music. **Udio offers a free trial, with paid plans starting at $20/month**.

### Amper Music

Amper Music is a popular AI music generator that offers a range of [features](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026), including melody generation, harmony [creation](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial), and audio editing. Amper Music is ideal for musicians and producers looking for a comprehensive music creation tool. **Amper Music offers a free trial, with paid plans starting at $15/month**.
