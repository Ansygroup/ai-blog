## FAQ

### What is the main difference between [Claude](/posts/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-cursor-windsurf-claude-code-codex) Pro and Claude Team?
The main difference is the token limit and collaboration [features](/posts/claude-api-guide). Claude Pro has a 10,000 token limit and no team features, while Claude Team has a 100,000 token limit and supports multi-[user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026) collaboration.

### Can I upgrade from Claude Pro to Claude Team?
Yes, you can upgrade from Claude Pro to Claude Team at any [time](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026), and your remaining token balance will be transferred to the new plan.

### How [does](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026) Claude Pro compare to Language Tool?
Claude Pro and Language Tool offer similar AI-powered writing assistance features, but Language Tool's [premium](/posts/gemini-advanced-vs-google-one-ai-premium) plan costs $59/month with 20,000 tokens, while Claude Pro costs $49/month with 10,000 tokens.

### Can I [use Claude](/posts/how-to-use-claude-for-coding-2026) Team for individual writing needs?
While it's technically possible, Claude Team is designed for collaborative environments and is priced accordingly. For individual writing needs, Claude Pro is the more [cost](/posts/best-free-ai-tools-2026)-effective option.

### What is the refund policy for Anthropic plans?
[Anthropic](/posts/claude-mythos-2026-anthropic-most-capable-model-review) offers a 30-day money-back guarantee for both Claude Pro and Claude Team plans.
