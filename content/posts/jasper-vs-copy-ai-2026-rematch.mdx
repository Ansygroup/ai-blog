Jasper and Copy.ai used to compete on the same shelf: AI writing for marketers, sold by the seat. In 2026 they have drifted apart. Jasper is a per-seat marketing platform with brand voice controls and agents. Copy.ai now sells chat seats and workflow credits aimed at go-to-market teams. As of November 2026, the right pick depends on whether you want a writing workspace or an automation engine. Last tested: 27 November 2026, from both live pricing pages.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo marketer who wants on-brand copy | Jasper | Pro includes 2 Brand Voices, 5 knowledge assets and 3 audiences |
| Lowest entry price for a small team | Copy.ai | Chat plan is $29 a month and includes 5 seats |
| Automated sales and GTM workflows | Copy.ai | Plans are sized by workflow credits and seat counts |
| Choice of underlying models | Copy.ai | Chat lists access to OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini models |
| Governance, API and custom agents | Jasper Business | Custom pricing, no-code agent builder, API and admin controls |
| Predictable per-person cost | Jasper | $59 or $69 per seat per month |

## Jasper pricing

| Plan | Price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | $69 a month per seat, or $59 a month billed yearly | 1 seat, Canvas editor, agents for core marketing workflows, 2 Brand Voices, 5 knowledge assets, 3 audiences |
| Business | Custom | Everything in Pro, plus agents for GEO, translations and deep research, a no-code agent builder, Jasper Grid, unlimited brand voices, API access, admin controls |

The page says annual billing saves about 20% and carries a 12 month commitment. Monthly billing costs more but you can cancel, or pause for 30 days. Business plans start with a 12 month commitment. Some usage based features, such as GEO Hub reports and Agents, run on credits next to seat pricing on Business, while standard writing in Chat and Canvas does not use credits.

## Copy.ai pricing

| Plan | Price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chat | $29 a month, 20% off annually | 5 seats, unlimited words in chat, unlimited chat projects, OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini models |
| Advanced | Price not captured in our fetch | 75 seats, 20,000 workflow credits a month |
| Expansion | $2,000 a month, billed $24,000 a year | 150 seats, 45,000 workflow credits a month |
| Scale | $3,000 a month, billed $36,000 a year | 200 seats, 75,000 workflow credits a month |
| Enterprise | Custom | API access, bulk workflow runs, unlimited workflows, dedicated team |

Our arithmetic: Scale at $36,000 a year for 75,000 monthly credits is $3,000 for the same amount each month, so the price per 1,000 credits is $40. Expansion is about $44 per 1,000. Those numbers only matter if you know how many credits a workflow run uses, which the page did not state.

## What you get beyond the writing box

Jasper lists 100+ purpose-built marketing agents, a document editor with rephrase and rewrite, remix for repurposing content into other channels, and an image suite for generation and editing. It supports 30+ languages. Its Brand Voice analyzes your content's tone and applies formatting rules, and its style guide shows updates in an x-ray view in the editor.

Copy.ai positions itself around implementation. Its enterprise copy describes guided onboarding with a timeline of kickoff on day 1, testing in week 1 and production on day 15, and integrations with 20+ go-to-market tools. Those are vendor claims about the enterprise plan, not something we tested.

## How to size your seats

Count the people who will write every week, not everyone who might read. Jasper Pro lists one seat, so a team of four on annual billing is 4 x $59 = $236 a month, our arithmetic. Copy.ai Chat includes five seats for $29, so for the same four people it costs far less, but you give up Jasper's brand voice controls. Price both against your real headcount before you compare features.

## Where each one fails

Jasper fails on price for a team. At $59 to $69 per seat, ten writers cost $590 to $690 a month on the Pro plan, and the Pro plan lists one seat. Brand voice limits of 2 voices will bind an agency fast.

Copy.ai fails on clarity. The self serve ladder jumps from a $29 chat plan to plans priced in the thousands, and the mid tier price is not visible on the page. The pricing page we read also shows nothing about a template library, so confirm that on a trial if templates matter to you.

## Who should skip which

Skip Jasper if you are one person who only needs an assistant, since a general chatbot costs less. See [ChatGPT vs Jasper](/posts/chatgpt-vs-jasper) for that comparison. Skip Copy.ai if you want a polished brand voice editor. See [ChatGPT vs Copy.ai](/posts/chatgpt-vs-copy-ai) for the other side.

## Test it yourself

1. Take one real brief: a landing page headline set and a 150 word product blurb.
2. Run it in a Jasper free trial and in Copy.ai chat.
3. Score each output for tone match against a paragraph of your own writing.
4. Count the edits you made. The tool with fewer edits per piece wins for your voice.
5. For Copy.ai, build one workflow and note how many credits a single run costs before you scale.

## Which should you pick

Pick Jasper Pro if brand consistency is the job and you will pay $59 a month yearly for it. Pick Copy.ai Chat at $29 for a small team that wants several models and a shared workspace, and only look at the credit plans when you have a workflow worth automating. Prices were read on 27 November 2026.
