## What Are AI-Driven Customer Service Chatbots?

AI-driven customer service chatbots are [software](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) applications that utilize natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML) to simulate human-like conversations with customers. These chatbots can be integrated into various channels, such as websites, mobile apps, messaging platforms, and [social media](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit), to provide 24/7 support and enhance customer engagement.
