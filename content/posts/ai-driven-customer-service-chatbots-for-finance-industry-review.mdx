## What Are AI-Driven Customer Service Chatbots?

AI-driven customer service chatbots are [software](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) applications that utilize natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML) to simulate human-like [conversations](/posts/gemini-prompt-guide) with customers. These chatbots can be integrated into various channels, such as websites, [mobile](/posts/chatgpt-mobile-app-review-2026) apps, messaging [platforms](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-online-course-platforms-for-education), and [social](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) media, to provide 24/7 support and enhance customer engagement.
