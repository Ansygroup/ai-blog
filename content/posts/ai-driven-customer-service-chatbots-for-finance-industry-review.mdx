## What Are AI-Driven Customer Service Chatbots?

AI-driven customer service chatbots are software applications that utilize natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML) to simulate human-like conversations with customers. These chatbots can be integrated into various channels, such as websites, mobile apps, messaging platforms, and social media, to provide 24/7 support and enhance customer engagement.
