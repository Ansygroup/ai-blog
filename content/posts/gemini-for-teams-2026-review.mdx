### Can Gemini for Teams be used with non‑Google apps?

Yes. The Gemini Marketplace offers connectors for over 150 third‑party services, including Salesforce, Asana, and Figma. You can invoke these add‑ons directly from a Gemini‑enhanced doc without leaving the Google Workspace environment.
