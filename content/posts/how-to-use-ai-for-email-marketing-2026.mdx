## AI vs Human Copywriting: When to Use Each

AI excels at three things: volume, personalization at scale, and rapid A/B iteration. Humans excel at three others: original voice, emotional resonance, and contrarian takes.

The rule of thumb we use: if the email is written to a single high-value account (>$50k ACV), the human should write the first draft and AI should optimize it. If the email is going to 1,000+ prospects, AI should write the first draft and the human should review the](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-mobile](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-mobile-app)-app) top 10% before send. Pure-AI with no human edit works for transactional and re-engagement emails where the reader has low expect](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/gemini-2-0-features-2026)ations.

The biggest 2026 mistake we see: brands using AI to write "thought leadership" newsletters. Readers can smell it. AI is a power tool, not a thought leader.
