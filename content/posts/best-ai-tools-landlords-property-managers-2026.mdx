A landlord's worst week usually starts with one [text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review) at 10 pm: the heater is out, the tenant is cold, and the plumber's number is somewhere in an old [email](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026). Property management is mostly [small](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026) decisions made late. AI tools help when they handle the repeatable parts, such as listings, screening paperwork, rent reminders and the first reply to a maintenance request, and when you keep the [legal](/posts/best-ai-chatgpt-tools-for-lawyers-2026) and safety calls to yourself.

Prices below were read on vendor pages on November 27, 2026. Last [tested](/posts/gamma-review-2026-ai-presentation-tool-tested-in-depth): November 27, 2026.

## TurboTenant: start free

TurboTenant describes itself as free [software](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) for landlords to market units, collect applications and manage rentals. Paid tiers read $12.42 and $16.58 per month on the page. A landlord forms pack was $199, and a standalone tenant [placement](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) service was a one-time $1,500 flat fee. The page also describes an Autopilot service that can be added unit by unit.

The free start matters because [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) test your process before committing. For one rental, the free tools [plus](/posts/chatgpt-plus-vs-free-what-you-get-2026) a clear checklist are often enough.

Where it fails: the [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) does not remove your obligations. Fair housing rules apply to every ad you write and every applicant you reject, regardless of the software. Use a written, consistent screening standard and apply it to every applicant.

## Buildium: for [scale](/posts/synthesia-for-teams-2026-review) and owner reporting

Buildium's page showed [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) starting at $65, $202 and $420 per month. It listed eSignature at $5 per document on the Essential plan and $1 on Growth, tenant screening at $17 per screening or $3 when the applicant pays, and enhanced screening at $20 on higher plans. Incoming EFT payments were $2.35 per transaction on Essential.

The details are the point. A small portfolio can pay more in per-transaction charges than in [subscription](/posts/grammarly-pricing-breakdown-2026). With 20 units each paying rent by EFT, $2.35 per payment is $47 a month. Ask whether tenants or owners bear that charge and whether your lease allows passing it on.

Where it fails: the lower tiers have limits on units and [features](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026). Compare the plan table against your unit count and the reports your owners expect.

## Claude for listings and notices

Claude Pro [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) $17 per month annually or $20 monthly. Give it the unit's facts and ask for a listing that states what is included, the fees and the move-in date, with no phrases [like](/posts/best-ai-tools-grammarly-for-accountants-2026) "perfect for professionals" that can imply a preferred tenant. Ask it to turn a repair [update](/posts/ai-regulation-update-creators-november-2026) into a calm, specific tenant message with a date and what happens next.

Where it fails: legal notices. Entry, late fee, eviction and deposit rules vary by place. Use official local forms or a lawyer's template, and let the tool only help with [tone](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026) and clarity around them.

## Airtable for the maintenance log

Airtable's Team plan was $20 and Business $45 per user per month billed annually. A simple base with one row per request, status, vendor and cost will show you that the same unit keeps having the same problem. That pattern, not any single repair, is where landlords save money.

## What the vendor pages do not say

Tenant retention is cheaper than tenant placement. Buildium's own screening fees and TurboTenant's $1,500 placement figure show what finding a new tenant costs once you add vacancy and cleaning. A tenant who stays an extra year because repairs were answered in 24 hours is [worth](/posts/grammarly-for-teams-2026-review) more than any software feature. Use AI to draft a same-day acknowledgment for every request, even when the fix is days away.

## A turnover checklist worth keeping

Between tenants, the days a unit sits empty cost the most. Keep one checklist: [photos](/posts/top-ai-image-upscaling-restoration-tools-2026-photos) at move-out, a repair list with costs, the cleaning booked, the listing [live](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026) before the keys are returned, and showings scheduled in the first week. Ask Claude to turn your repair list into a vendor request with the scope, access instructions and a deadline. Store the checklist where you track maintenance so each turnover is faster than the last.

## Who should skip this list

A landlord with a single unit and a long-term tenant needs a spreadsheet and a [good](/posts/best-ai-tools-notebooklm-for-nonprofits-2026-ultimate-guide) plumber. Property managers on a bigger platform such as an [enterprise](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-agencies-2026) system should ask that vendor what [drafting](/posts/best-ai-powered-legal-contract-drafting-tools-2026) is built in before adding another tool.

## What to buy first

Start with TurboTenant free for the next listing. Write a one-page screening standard before you review any application. Add Claude Pro for listings and tenant messages. Consider Buildium when you manage for owners or pass about 20 units and need [accounting](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-for-accountants-2026-top-7-picks) and owner statements in one place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can AI screen tenants?

It should not make the decision. Use a written screening standard applied equally, since [fair](/posts/kaggle-api-cost-comparison-2026) housing rules apply regardless of software.

### Is TurboTenant really free?

The page read describes free software to start, with paid tiers at $12.42 and $16.58 per month and separate service fees for some products.

### When does Buildium make sense?

When you manage for owners or pass roughly 20 units and need accounting and owner statements, with plans starting at $65 per month.
