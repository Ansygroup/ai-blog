## FAQ

### What is the difference between Gemini Flash and Gemini Pro?

Gemini Flash prioritizes speed and cost, delivering responses in ~0.45 seconds per call with a lower token limit (8 K). Gemini Pro focuses on depth, reasoning, and factual accuracy, offering up to 32 K tokens per call and a slightly higher latency (~0.78 seconds). Pro is best for](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-gemini-coding) complex, long‑form tasks; Flash excels at quick, high‑volume queries.

### Can I use Gemini tools for multilingual content?

As of 2026, Gemini officially supports 12 languages with high accuracy (English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, Hindi, and Arabic). For languages outside this set, you’ll need to supplement Gemini with translation APIs like DeepL or Google Translate.
