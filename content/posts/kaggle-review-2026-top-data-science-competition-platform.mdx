## FAQ

### Is Kaggle completely free in 2026?

Yes, the core platform—including competitions, datasets, and 30 hrs/month of GPU compute—is free. Paid options only apply to additional compute (Kaggle Pro Compute) or external cloud credits.

### How can I increase my chances of winning a Kaggle competition?

Focus on three pillars: **feature engineering**, **model ensembling**, and **community collaboration**. Review top kernels, join discussion threads, and experiment with stacking models. In 2026, the average winning solution uses at least **three different algorithms** and leverages **automated hyperparameter tuning**.

### What are the best Kaggle courses for beginners?

- “Intro to Python” (4 hrs, 5‑star rating)  
- “Pandas for Data Manipulation” (3 hrs)  
- “Machine Learning Basics” (12 hrs, 2.3 M completions)  

These three form the foundation of the “Data Scientist” learning path.

### Can I use Kaggle notebooks for commercial projects?

Yes, notebooks can be exported as Jupyter files and deployed elsewhere. However, the **free compute tier** is intended for learning and prototyping; for production workloads, you should migrate to a paid cloud provider or the **Kaggle Pro Compute** add‑on.

### How does Kaggle compare to AIcrowd for competition variety?

Kaggle offers **~10,200** active competitions (average prize $12.5k) and a broader dataset library (3,200+). AIcrowd hosts **~2,800** challenges with a focus on AI safety and reinforcement learning, often providing **higher compute credits** but smaller prize pools. For sheer variety and community size, Kaggle leads; for niche AI research contests, AIcrowd is stronger.
