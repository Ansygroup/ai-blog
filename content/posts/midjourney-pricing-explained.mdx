## What Is Midjourney?

Midjourney has gained popularity among artists, designers, and marketers due to its ability to produce high-quality images quickly.

## FAQ

### What is Midjourney's free trial [policy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026)?
Midjourney offers a free trial with 100 images. This trial has limited features and is suitable for casual users.

### Can I [upgrade](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) or downgrade my Midjourney plan?
Yes, [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) upgrade or downgrade your Midjourney plan at any time. However, be aware that changing plans may affect your image credits and features.

### How does Midjourney compare to DALL-E?
Midjourney and DALL-E are both AI art generators, but they have different pricing [plans and](/posts/langchain-api-cost-comparison-2026) features. Midjourney offers more images and faster processing, but DALL-E is more affordable.

### Is Midjourney [worth](/posts/grammarly-for-teams-2026-review) the cost?
Midjourney is worth the cost if you're a heavy user or need high-quality images quickly. However, if you're on a tight budget, consider alternative options.

### Can I use Midjourney for commercial purposes?
Yes, Midjourney allows commercial use of its generated images. However, be sure to review their [terms](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) of service and licensing agreements.
