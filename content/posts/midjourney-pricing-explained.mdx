## What Is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an AI-powered art generator that creates stunning images from text prompts. It uses a deep learning [model](/posts/claude-4-opus-vs-sonnet-comparison-2026) to understand the input [prompt](/posts/gemini-prompt-guide) and generate a corresponding image. Midjourney has gained popularity among artists, designers, and [marketers](/posts/ai-driven-email-marketing-automation-tools-review) due to its ability to produce high-quality images quickly.

## FAQ

### What is Midjourney's free trial policy?
Midjourney offers a free trial with 100 images. This trial has limited [features](/posts/claude-api-guide) and is suitable for casual users.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my Midjourney plan?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your Midjourney plan at any time. However, be aware that changing plans may affect your image credits and features.

### How [does](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026) Midjourney compare to DALL-E?
Midjourney and DALL-E are both AI art generators, but they have different pricing plans and [features](/posts/claude-api-guide). Midjourney offers more images and faster processing, but DALL-E is more affordable.

### Is Midjourney [worth the](/posts/cursor-ai-editor-review-2026) cost?
Midjourney is worth the cost if you're a heavy user or need high-quality images quickly. However, if you're on a tight budget, consider alternative options.
