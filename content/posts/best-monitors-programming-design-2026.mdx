### What to Look For
When shopping for a monitor for programming and design, there are several key factors to consider. First and foremost, screen size and resolution are crucial, as they directly impact the overall viewing experience. A larger screen with a higher resolution, such as 4K or QHD, provides more screen real estate and sharper text, making it ideal for coding and graphic design. Additionally, look for monitors with good color accuracy, as measured by their color gamut and Delta-E rating.

Another important aspect is connectivity, as programmers and designers often need to connect multiple devices to their monitor. Look for monitors with a range of ports, including USB-C, HDMI, and DisplayPort, to ensure seamless connectivity. Ergonomics is also essential, as a monitor with adjustable height, tilt, and swivel can help reduce eye strain and improve productivity. Furthermore, consider the monitor's refresh rate and response time, as a faster refresh rate and lower response time can reduce screen tearing and ghosting.

Lastly, consider the monitor's adjustability and durability. A monitor with a sturdy build and adjustable stand can provide a comfortable viewing experience and withstand heavy use. Some monitors also come with built-in features such as speakers, webcams, and USB hubs, which can enhance the overall user experience. By considering these factors, you can find the perfect monitor for your programming and design needs.
