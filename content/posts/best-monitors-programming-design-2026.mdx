### What to Look For
When choosing a monitor for programming and design, there are several key factors to consider. Firstly, resolution and display quality are crucial, with 4K UHD (3840x2160) being the benchmark for professional-grade monitors. Additionally, color accuracy and gamut are essential for design work, with features like HDR10 and DCI-P3 90% ensuring vibrant and true-to-life colors. 
Another important aspect is connectivity, with USB-C, DisplayPort, and HDMI being the most common ports. Ergonomic design, including adjustable stands and flicker-free technology, is also vital for reducing eye strain and improving comfort during extended use.
Furthermore, features like adaptive sync technology, such as G-Sync, can enhance the overall viewing experience, especially for gamers and those working with graphics-intensive applications.
