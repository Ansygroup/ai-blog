### What to Look For
When shopping for a monitor for programming and design, there are several key factors to consider. First and foremost, resolution is crucial, with 4K and QHD options offering the sharpest and most detailed images. Additionally, look for monitors with high refresh rates, such as 144Hz or 240Hz, to reduce eye strain and improve overall viewing experience. A good monitor should also have a range of connectivity options, including USB-C, HDMI, and DisplayPort, to accommodate multiple devices.

Another critical aspect to consider is the monitor's color accuracy and gamut, particularly for design professionals who require precise color representation. Look for monitors with wide color gamuts, such as DCI-P3 or Adobe RGB, and high color accuracy ratings. Ergonomics is also essential, with adjustable stands and VESA mounting options allowing for customized positioning and comfort.

Furthermore, consider the monitor's size and aspect ratio, with larger screens and ultrawide formats offering more immersive and productive experiences. Some monitors also feature built-in speakers, webcams, and software features like screen splitting and color calibration, which can enhance overall usability and convenience.
