## What Is Otter Pricing Breakdown 2026?

Otter.ai, now part of the broader **Otter Voice Meeting Notes** eco[system](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content), provides AI‑driven transcription, speaker identification, and searchable archives. The 2026 pricing breakdown reflects three core tiers—Free, Pro, and Business—plus a customizable Enterprise layer for large organizations. Each tier is defined by three primary metrics: monthly transcription minutes, feature set (e.g., live [caption](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)s, export formats), and collaboration tools (shared folders, admin dashboards).  

The **Free [plan](/posts/cursor-pricing-breakdown-2026)** is [design](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026)ed for solo users who need occasional meeting notes, while **Pro** targets freelancers, [podcast](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial)ers, and small businesses that require higher minute caps and advanced export options. The **Business** tier adds team‑level admin controls, bulk user onboarding, and priority support, whereas **Enterprise** offers unlimited minutes, custom integrations (e.g., Slack, Zoom, Microsoft Teams), and SLA guarantees.  

In 2026, Otter has also [intro](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026)duced **Add‑On Credits** for users who exceed their minute caps, priced at $0.10 per additional minute—a rate that remains competitive compared with per‑minute pricing models from Rev.com ($1.25/min) and Descript ($0.15/min).

## FAQ

### How many minutes does Otter’s Free plan include per month?

The Free plan provides **600 minutes** (10 hours) of transcription each month, with a maximum of 40 minutes per individual recording.

### Can I get a discount for multiple Business users?

Yes. Otter offers **volume discounts** for Business accounts exceeding 25 seats, typically reducing the per‑user price from $25/mo (annual) to $22/mo for 50‑plus users.

### Does Otter support real‑time captions in Zoom meetings?

Absolutely. Otter’s live caption feature integrates directly with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, providing **real‑time subtitles** and searchable transcripts during the meeting.

### What is the accuracy rate of Otter’s AI transcription in 2026?

Independent [benchmark](/posts/heygen-accuracy-test-2026)s in 2026 show an average **Word Error Rate (WER) of 2.1%**, equating to roughly **97.9% accuracy** for clear‑speech English audio. Accuracy drops to ~94% for heavily accented speech.

### How does Otter’s per‑minute overage cost compare to Rev.com?

Otter charges **$0.10 per extra minute**, whereas Rev.com charges **$1.25 per minute** for human‑edited transcripts—a difference of **$1.15 per minute**.

### Are there any hidden fees for API usage?

Otter’s API is included in Business and Enterprise plans with a **rate limit of 1,000 requests per day** on the Business tier. Exceeding this limit incurs a $0.02 per additional request fee, which is still cheaper than most competitors.
