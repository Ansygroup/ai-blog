## What Is Otter Pricing Breakdown 2026?

Otter.ai, now part of the broader **Otter Voice Meeting Notes** ecosystem, provides AI‑driven transcription, speaker identification, and searchable archives. The 2026 pricing breakdown reflects three core tiers—Free, Pro, and Business—plus a customizable Enterprise layer for large organizations. Each tier is defined by three primary metrics: monthly transcription minutes, feature set (e.g., live captions, export formats), and collaboration tools (shared folders, admin dashboards).  

The **Free plan** is designed for solo users who need occasional meeting notes, while **Pro** targets freelancers, podcasters, and small businesses that require higher minute caps and advanced export options. The **Business** tier adds team‑level admin controls, bulk user onboarding, and priority support, whereas **Enterprise** offers unlimited minutes, custom integrations (e.g., Slack, Zoom, Microsoft Teams), and SLA guarantees.  

In 2026, Otter has also introduced **Add‑On Credits** for users who exceed their minute caps, priced at $0.10 per additional minute—a rate that remains competitive compared with per‑minute pricing models from Rev.com ($1.25/min) and Descript ($0.15/min).

## Free Plan: Limits, Features & Ideal Use‑Cases

The Free tier remains a popular entry point for students and casual note‑takers. It offers:

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly transcription minutes | **600 minutes** (10 hours) |
| Max length per recording | **40 minutes** |
| Export formats | Text (TXT), PDF only |
| Live captions | **No** |
| Collaboration | Individual only; no shared folders |
| AI features | Basic speaker identification, searchable keywords |
| Support | Community forum |

At $0, the Free plan’s **$0 per minute** cost is unbeatable, but the 600‑minute cap translates to roughly **15 × 40‑minute meetings** per month. Users who need longer recordings (e.g., webinars) quickly outgrow this tier. The lack of DOCX or SRT exports also makes the Free plan less suitable for content creators who require subtitle files.

**Real‑world example:** A college sophomore used the Free plan to transcribe three 45‑minute lectures per week, staying within the 600‑minute limit and saving $0 on transcription fees. However, the same student later needed SRT subtitles for a video project and had to upgrade.

## FAQ

### How many minutes does Otter’s Free plan include per month?

The Free plan provides **600 minutes** (10 hours) of transcription each month, with a maximum of 40 minutes per individual recording.

### Can I get a discount for multiple Business users?

Yes. Otter offers **volume discounts** for Business accounts exceeding 25 seats, typically reducing the per‑user price from $25/mo (annual) to $22/mo for 50‑plus users.

### Does Otter support real‑time captions in Zoom meetings?

Absolutely. Otter’s live caption feature integrates directly with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, providing **real‑time subtitles** and searchable transcripts during the meeting.

### What is the accuracy rate of Otter’s AI transcription in 2026?

Independent benchmarks in 2026 show an average **Word Error Rate (WER) of 2.1%**, equating to roughly **97.9% accuracy** for clear‑speech English audio. Accuracy drops to ~94% for heavily accented speech.

### How does Otter’s per‑minute overage cost compare to Rev.com?

Otter charges **$0.10 per extra minute**, whereas Rev.com charges **$1.25 per minute** for human‑edited transcripts—a difference of **$1.15 per minute**.

### Are there any hidden fees for API usage?

Otter’s API is included in Business and Enterprise plans with a **rate limit of 1,000 requests per day** on the Business tier. Exceeding this limit incurs a $0.02 per additional request fee, which is still cheaper than most competitors.

## Final Verdict

For most individual professionals and midsize teams, **Otter Pro** (annual) offers the best blend of cost, minute allowance, and feature richness, beating out Rev’s per‑minute pricing and Descript’s bundled editor cost. **Business** is the clear winner for collaborative environments, delivering unmatched admin controls at a low per‑minute rate. While Enterprise pricing requires a custom quote, its unlimited minutes and dedicated support make it a solid choice for large corporations with strict compliance needs. **Overall, Otter’s 2026 pricing structure is transparent, competitive, and scalable—making it the top recommendation for AI‑powered transcription in 2026.**
