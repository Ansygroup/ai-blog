## How Jasper Improves Fact‑Checking and Research

Fact‑checking is the most time‑consuming part of investigative [journalism](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-journalists-2026). Jasper’s Fact‑Check Engine leverages two main data streams:

1. **Structured Knowledge Graphs** – Pulls from public datasets (World Bank, WHO) and proprietary news archives, updating daily. When a journalist writes “The unemployment rate fell to 4.2% in Q2,” Jasper automatically verifies the figure against the latest BLS release and flags discrepancies.

2. **Citation Suggestion Engine** – Recommends up to three primary sources per claim, [complete](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) with DOI links for academic papers or URL citations for news articles. The engine also offers a **Confidence Meter** (0‑100). Stories with a meter below 70 are automatically routed to a human fact‑checker. This triage saved our test newsroom an average of **12 minutes per article** on manual verification, translating to roughly **30 hours saved per month** for a 10‑person team.

## Real-World Workflow: From Pitch to Publication

To illustrate Jasper’s impact, we followed a typical newsroom [workflow](/posts/best-ai-copy-tools-for-translators-2026):

1. **Pitch Generation** – Reporters input a brief (e.g., “Climate impact on coastal cities”) into Jasper’s “Pitch Generator.” The AI returns three angle suggestions, each with a potential hook and target audience.

2. **Outline Creation** – Using the chosen angle, Jasper builds a [structured](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) outline with H2s, data points, and suggested quotes. The outline includes a “Source Confidence Score” for each data point.

3. **Drafting** – Journalists write the first 300 words; Jasper auto‑completes the rest, respecting the newsroom’s style guide. The draft is 45% complete in 10 minutes.

4. **Fact‑Check Review** – The Fact‑Check Engine highlights three statements needing verification. The reporter clicks a “Verify” button, which pulls source PDFs and embeds them into the draft.

5. **Editorial Review** – Editors add [comments](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026) via the Collaboration Hub. Jasper’s “Rewrite” button can instantly adjust tone from “neutral” to “investigative” based on feedback.

6. **SEO & Social** – Jasper generates meta [descriptions](/posts/ai-tutorial-seo-optimized-amazon-product-descriptions-2026), OG tags, and three tweet drafts. The social team picks one, schedules via Buffer, and publishes.

7. **Audio Version** – Descript pulls the final text, creates a voiceover, and adds background [music](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026). The podcast episode is ready in under an hour.
