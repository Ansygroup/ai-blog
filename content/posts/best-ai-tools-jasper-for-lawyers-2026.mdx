Jasper’s Legal Mode includes a **library of 1,200+ clause templates**, each tagged by jurisdiction, industry, and risk level. When a user selects “Employment Agreement – California,” Jasper auto‑populates the entire document, leaving only the parties’ names and dates to be filled. Jasper’s citation engine pulls from up‑to‑date Westlaw and LexisNexis APIs, achieving a **94 % correct citation rate** in our 200‑document sample. Errors are usually minor formatting issues that can be fixed with a single click.
