## What Is n8n Free Tier Limits 2026?

The n8n free tier in 2026 is the entry‑level offering of the open‑source workflow automation engine that markets itself as “fair‑code.” It provides a cloud‑hosted environment with no credit‑card requirement, plus a self‑hosted Docker image you can run on any server at zero cost. The primary limits are:

1. **Execution quota** – 2,000 workflow runs per month across all active workflows.
2. **Active workflow count** – Up to 100 workflows can be saved and triggered.
3. **Data size per node** – Each node can pass a maximum of 30 MB of JSON or binary data.
4. **Integration count** – All built‑in nodes are available, but third‑party credential storage is limited to 5 credential sets.
5. **Support** – Community‑only; no SLA or live chat.

These caps are designed to keep the platform sustainable while still delivering a fully functional automation suite for developers, marketers, and small businesses. The free tier’s “no‑card” onboarding eliminates friction, making it a popular choice for proof‑of‑concept projects and personal productivity hacks.

## Core Limits of the Free Tier

The most visible restriction is the **2,000‑execution monthly quota**. In practice, this translates to roughly **66 executions per day** if you spread usage evenly. For a small e‑commerce store sending order confirmations, that’s ample; for a SaaS product with hundreds of daily sign‑ups, you’ll hit the ceiling within a week.

**Active workflow cap** of 100 is generous compared with Zapier’s 5‑workflow limit on its free tier, but the **30 MB payload ceiling** can be a hidden blocker. For instance, when processing PDF invoices via the “PDF Generator” node, files larger than 25 MB trigger a “Payload Too Large” error, forcing you to either truncate data or upgrade.

Another nuance: **credential storage** is limited to five distinct credential sets. If you need to connect to three databases, two APIs, and an OAuth service, you’ll be forced to reuse credentials or move to a paid plan.

Finally, **rate limiting** on the cloud instance caps API calls at **20 requests per second** per workflow. Heavy‑duty data syncs that poll external APIs every few seconds may encounter throttling, causing retries and longer runtimes.

Overall, the free tier is robust for low‑to‑moderate automation, but the combination of execution count, payload size, and credential limits means you must design workflows with efficiency in mind.

## When You’ll Hit the Ceiling: Scaling Beyond the Free Tier

If your automation needs grow, you’ll encounter three primary friction points:

1. **Execution Quota Exhaustion** – High‑traffic e‑commerce sites often generate **10‑20 k orders** per month. Even a single “order‑fulfillment” workflow per order would require **10‑20 k executions**, far exceeding 2,000. Upgrading to the $20/mo Starter plan removes the execution cap.

2. **Large File Handling** – Media‑rich workflows (e.g., video transcoding, large PDF generation) can easily exceed the **30 MB node payload**. n8n’s paid tiers raise this limit to **100 MB**, and the “Enterprise” tier offers **1 GB** per node.

3. **Credential Set Saturation** – Complex integrations often need more than five credential sets (e.g., multiple databases, third‑party APIs, OAuth services). The Starter plan expands this to **20 credential sets**, while Enterprise provides **unlimited**.

A practical scaling path many of our readers follow is:

- **Step 1:** Move to self‑hosted Docker on a low‑cost VPS (e.g., $5/mo on Hetzner) to retain zero‑cost execution but gain full control over data residency.
- **Step 2:** Upgrade to n8n’s Starter plan for unlimited runs and higher payload limits.
- **Step 3:** If you need advanced SLA, single‑sign‑on, and dedicated support, consider the Enterprise tier (custom pricing, typically $200+/mo).

## FAQ

### How many workflow executions does n8n’s free tier allow per month in 2026?
The free tier provides **2,000 executions** per month across all active workflows. Unused executions do not roll over to the next month.

### Can I self‑host n8n for free and avoid the cloud limits?
Yes. By running the open‑source Docker image on your own server, you keep the **execution quota unlimited** (subject only to your hardware resources). The only limitation is the 30 MB per‑node payload, which remains the same.

### How does n8n’s free tier compare to Zapier’s free plan in terms of integrations?
n8n offers **250+ built‑in nodes** (including HTTP, GraphQL, and custom JavaScript) without extra cost, while Zapier’s free tier limits you to **3 connected apps** and a total of **100 tasks** per month.

### What happens when I exceed the 2,000‑execution limit?
When the quota is reached, n8n will pause workflow execution until the next billing cycle. You’ll receive an email notification and can either upgrade to the Starter plan or wait for the quota reset.

### Is there a way to increase the 30 MB payload limit without upgrading?
Not within the free tier. The payload limit is hard‑coded to protect shared cloud resources. To handle larger files, you must either upgrade to a paid plan or process the file externally (e.g., store in AWS S3 and pass a URL instead of the binary data).

### Does n8n provide any SLA or uptime guarantee on the free cloud service?
No. The free tier runs on shared infrastructure with **no formal SLA**. For guaranteed uptime, you need a paid plan or self‑hosted deployment.
