The invoice is where this comparison starts, because the two most popular AI voice APIs bill in completely different units, and teams routinely pick the wrong one for their workload. ElevenLabs sells subscriptions with character credits. OpenAI sells text-to-speech by the million characters or by token. We ran the same production scripts through both, and the answer depends almost entirely on two questions: how many minutes of audio do you ship, and does anyone hear the voice for more than thirty seconds?

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audiobook or long narration | ElevenLabs | Emotional range holds up over hours, not seconds |
| App feature reading short strings | OpenAI | Per-character billing beats a subscription at low volume |
| Voice cloning a real person | ElevenLabs | Professional cloning is the core product |
| Dozens of languages, one API | ElevenLabs | Broader and more consistent multilingual coverage |
| Already paying for OpenAI infrastructure | OpenAI | One bill, one SDK, no second vendor |
| Lowest possible cost per minute | OpenAI | tts-1 at $15 per million characters is hard to undercut |

## Voice quality: the thirty-second test

Both sound impressive in a demo clip. The difference shows up at minute ten. ElevenLabs voices keep micro-variation in pacing and emphasis, so long narration stays listenable. OpenAI's voices are clean and natural in short bursts but flatten into a steady broadcast cadence over long passages. For a voiceover on a two-minute product video, nobody will notice. For a course module or an audiobook chapter, listeners feel it without being able to name it. This is also why ElevenLabs dominates our roundup of [AI voiceover tools for audiobooks](/posts/ai-powered-voiceover-creation-for-audiobooks-review-2026).

## Languages and voices

ElevenLabs offers thousands of community and stock voices, professional voice cloning, and multilingual models that keep a cloned voice's identity across languages. OpenAI ships a small set of curated voices with no true voice cloning in the standard API. If your product needs "our brand voice," that sentence ends the comparison: OpenAI does not sell that, ElevenLabs does. Our [voice cloning tools comparison](/posts/ai-voice-cloning-tools-podcast-hosts-2026-comparison) goes deeper on the cloning landscape.

## Pricing in plain terms

ElevenLabs, as of late November 2026: Free at $0, Starter at $6 a month, Creator at $22 a month with about 121,000 credits (text-to-speech uses one credit per character on the web app), Pro at $99 a month with 600,000 credits, Scale at $299, and Business at $990, where low-latency TTS is quoted as low as 5 cents per minute. API pricing on Turbo and Flash models costs half a credit per character. Note that a Black Friday sale cutting annual plans by up to 55 percent ends December 1, 2026, so numbers you see this week may not be the numbers next month.

OpenAI: tts-1 costs $15 per million characters and tts-1-hd costs $30. The newer gpt-4o-mini-tts is billed in tokens, at $0.60 per million text input tokens plus $12 per million audio output tokens. In practice, a million characters is roughly three hours of finished audio, so a full audiobook chapter costs pennies on OpenAI and a visible slice of your credit quota on ElevenLabs.

## Latency, control, and the production details

For real-time features, both vendors now offer low-latency paths. ElevenLabs quotes low-latency TTS as low as 5 cents per minute on its Business tier and half-credit-per-character pricing on its Turbo and Flash API models, which are the ones you want for conversational agents. OpenAI's realtime audio models are built for voice agents first, which is a different product from batch TTS and priced accordingly, so do not compare a realtime session price against a tts-1 render price.

Control is the quieter difference. ElevenLabs gives you stability and similarity sliders, per-voice settings, and studio tools for fixing individual sentences after the fact. OpenAI gives you a prompt string and steering through instructions, which is simpler and less precise. If your workflow includes a human polishing the 3 percent of lines that come out wrong, ElevenLabs has the tools for that pass. If your workflow is fully automated and nobody ever listens before the user does, OpenAI's simplicity is a feature, because there is nothing to misconfigure.

## Where each one fails

ElevenLabs fails on cost structure. Subscriptions strand unused credits (they roll over only two months on paid plans, capped), dubbing runs 2,000 to 13,500 credits per minute, and heavy API users find the ladder of plans gets expensive before it gets generous.

OpenAI fails on expression and identity. Few voices, no cloning, limited fine control over delivery, and a noticeable sameness on emotional material. If the voice carries your brand, "cheap per character" stops mattering. See also our [ElevenLabs vs ChatGPT breakdown](/posts/elevenlabs-vs-chatgpt-2026) for how the two stacks compare beyond speech.

## Which should you pick

Shipping narration people listen to for minutes at a time, or any cloned voice: ElevenLabs, and Creator at $22 covers more than most indie projects expect. Shipping a feature that speaks UI strings, notifications, or short summaries at unpredictable volume: OpenAI, where you pay only for what you render. Many production apps use both, routing the flagship voice to ElevenLabs and the utility chatter to tts-1.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ElevenLabs better than OpenAI text to speech?

For long-form narration, voice cloning, and multilingual identity, yes. For cheap, short, utilitarian speech inside an app, OpenAI's per-character pricing usually wins.

### How much does OpenAI TTS cost?

As of November 2026, tts-1 is $15 per million characters, tts-1-hd is $30, and gpt-4o-mini-tts bills $0.60 per million text input tokens plus $12 per million audio output tokens.

### Does OpenAI offer voice cloning?

No. The standard OpenAI TTS API offers a small set of curated voices only. ElevenLabs offers professional voice cloning on paid plans.

### How much does ElevenLabs cost per month?

Plans run from a free tier to Starter at $6, Creator at $22, Pro at $99, Scale at $299, and Business at $990 per month, as of late November 2026.
