## FAQ

### How does Jasper’s AI Grader work for short‑answer questions?

Jasper’s Grader uses a fine‑tuned BERT model trained on education‑specific datasets. Teachers upload a rubric (e.g., “Key concept, evidence, clarity”) and the AI scores each response against those criteria, returning a numeric score and a short feedback sentence. In our tests, the grader matched human scores **92 %** of the time.

### Can Jasper be used offline in schools with limited internet?

Jasper is a [cloud](/posts/pinecone-setup-guide-2026)‑based SaaS, so core generation requires an internet [connection](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026). However, the **Enterprise plan** offers an optional on‑premise Docker container that runs the language model locally, allowing offline usage for up to 1,000 concurrent users.

### Does Jasper support non‑English languages for [multilingual](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) classrooms?

Yes. Jasper supports **Spanish, French, Mandarin, and Arabic** at the same feature level as English. Teachers can generate bilingual worksheets with a single toggle, and the AI Grader can evaluate responses in any of the supported languages.

### What is the refund [policy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) for education plans?

Jasper offers a **30‑day money‑back guarantee** on all education [subscription](/posts/grammarly-pricing-breakdown-2026)s. ### How does Jasper protect student data and comply with FERPA?

Jasper encrypts all data at rest and in transit (AES‑256). The platform is **FERPA‑certified**, stores student identifiers separately from content, and provides audit logs for every data access request. Schools can also opt for the on‑premise deployment to keep data entirely within their firewall.

### Is there a free trial for teachers?
