## Hidden Fees & Usage Limits You Need to Watch

1. **Avatar Rendering Over‑age** – Each avatar render beyond the free quota (30 renders for Starter, 150 for Pro) incurs $0.004 per extra render.  
2. **High‑Resolution Upscaling** – Switching from 1080p to 4K adds $0.009/min, which can double the cost for long videos.  
3. **API Call Limits** – Starter tier caps at 10 k API calls per month; exceeding this triggers a $0.0015 per call surcharge.  
4. **Data Retention** – Storing generated videos beyond 30 days costs $0.0002 per MB per day.  

For example, a 2‑minute video rendered in 4K with a premium avatar and stored for 60 days would cost:

- Base: 2 min × $0.018 = $0.036  
- 4K surcharge: 2 min × $0.009 = $0.018  
- Avatar over‑age (if beyond quota): $0.004  
- Storage: (2 min × 500 MB) × $0.0002 × 60 days ≈ $1.20  

Total ≈ **$1.26** per video, illustrating why budgeting for storage and resolution [upgrade](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version)s is essential.
