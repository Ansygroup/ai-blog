## What Is the ChatGPT Mobile App?

The **ChatGPT mobile app** is [OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026)’s official, dedicated application designed to bring the [capabilities](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026) of its Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) models to iOS and Android [devices](/posts/best-smart-home-devices-2026). Launched initially in mid-2023 and continuously updated through 2026, the app transitions [ChatGPT](/posts/best-chatgpt-desktop-app-2026) from a static web-based text box into an interactive, multimodal companion. 

Unlike third-party wrapper [apps](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) that clutter the App Store and [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) [Play](/posts/elevenlabs-vs-play-ht-vs-murf-2026) Store with predatory subscription fees, the official app is free to download, ad-free, and directly linked to your [OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026) account. It is powered by OpenAI's frontier models, including GPT-4o and GPT-4o-mini, allowing it to process text, voice, and images simultaneously.

For example, a [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026) can snap a photo of a broken kitchen pipe, ask ChatGPT via voice how to fix it, and receive a step-by-step guide in real-time. The app also [features](/posts/claude-api-guide) native integration with **Whisper**, OpenAI’s open-source speech recognition system, ensuring that [voice](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-voiceover-production) dictation is incredibly precise regardless of background noise.
