## What Is the ChatGPT Mobile App?

The **ChatGPT mobile app** is OpenAI’s official, dedicated application designed to bring the capabilities of its Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) models to iOS and Android devices. Launched initially in mid-2023 and continuously updated through 2026, the app transitions ChatGPT from a static web-based text box into an interactive, multimodal companion. 

Unlike third-party wrapper apps that clutter the App Store and Google Play Store with predatory subscription fees, the official app is free to download, ad-free, and directly linked to your OpenAI account. It is powered by OpenAI's frontier models, including GPT-4o and GPT-4o-mini, allowing it to process text, voice, and images simultaneously.

For example, a user can snap a photo of a broken kitchen pipe, ask ChatGPT via voice how to fix it, and receive a step-by-step guide in real-time. The app also features native integration with **Whisper**, OpenAI’s open-source speech recognition system, ensuring that voice dictation is incredibly precise regardless of background noise.
