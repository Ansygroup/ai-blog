## Tips to Avoid Geo‑Blocking and Ensure Reliable Access

1. **Choose the Right Server:** For F1 TV Pro, UK or US servers give the lowest latency. Use the VPN’s “Speed Test” feature before race day.
2. **Enable Split Tunneling:** Route only the streaming app through the VPN to reduce load on your home network.

4. **Refresh DNS Cache:** On Windows, run `ipconfig /flushdns` after connecting to the VPN to avoid stale DNS entries that could reveal your location.
5. **Set Up a VPN Router:** If you have multiple devices (TV, phone, tablet), configuring the VPN on your router ensures every device inherits the same IP, simplifying management.
