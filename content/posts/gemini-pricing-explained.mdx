## What Is Gemini?

Gemini is Google's AI model, designed to compete with other major players like ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot. It's an advanced language model capable of understanding and generating human-like text. Gemini comes in various versions, each with different features and pricing.

## Gemini Pricing Plans

Gemini offers several pricing plans to cater to different user needs:

| Plan | Price | Features | Prompts per Day |
|------|------|----------|----------------|
| Free | $0 | Limited features | 100 |

The free version of Gemini is great for casual users, but it has limitations. With the free plan, you get:

The Gemini Advanced plan offers:

The Gemini Ultra plan provides:

- Advanced features
