## What Are ChatGPT Settings?

ChatGPT settings allow users to customize the AI's behavior. These settings include temperature, [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) version, and response style.
![Understanding ChatGPT's Temperature Setting](/images/illustrations/chatgpt-best-settings-2026-illo2.svg)
## Understanding ChatGPT's Temperature Setting

The temperature setting controls the randomness of responses. A lower temperature (e.g., 0.1) yields more predictable answers, while a higher temperature (e.g., 1.0) produces more varied responses.

## ChatGPT Settings for Accuracy

For factual accuracy, use the "Precise" mode with a temperature of 0.1. This setting is ideal for tasks requiring specific information.

## ChatGPT Settings for Creativity

For [creative](/posts/best-ai-runway-tools-for-designers-2026-top-picks) writing or brainstorming, switch to "Creative" mode with a temperature of 0.7. This configuration encourages innovative responses while maintaining coherence.
