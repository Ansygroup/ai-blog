## FAQ

### How does NotebookLM handle PDF security for confidential documents?

NotebookLM encrypts PDFs at rest with AES‑256 and processes them in isolated sandbox environments. Users can enable a “Confidential Mode” that prevents AI‑generated excerpts from being shared outside the workspace, and all access is logged for audit purposes.

### Can I migrate existing notes from Notion or Evernote into NotebookLM?

Yes. NotebookLM offers a bulk import tool that supports .md, .html, and .enex (Evernote) formats. During migration, the AI automatically tags and indexes content, preserving searchability without manual re‑tagging.

### Is there a limit to how many notebooks a small business can create?

The Pro plan allows **unlimited notebooks** per workspace. Only the free tier imposes a soft cap of **50 notebooks**, after which you must upgrade or archive older notes.

### What kind of customer support does NotebookLM provide to SMBs?

Pro subscribers receive **24/7 email support** and a **dedicated Slack channel** for rapid troubleshooting. Enterprise customers add **phone support** and a **technical account manager** for onboarding assistance.

### How does NotebookLM compare to Notion AI in terms of cost per token?

NotebookLM’s Pro plan provides **unlimited tokens** for $12 / user / mo, effectively $0 per token. Notion AI Business caps at 5 k tokens for $10 / user / mo, which translates to **$0.002 per token** if you fully utilize the quota—making NotebookLM more cost‑effective for heavy users.
