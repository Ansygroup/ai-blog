## What Is Predictive Modeling in Cybersecurity?

Predictive modeling in cybersecurity involves using machine learning algorithms to analyze data and predict potential cyber threats. This approach enables small businesses to stay ahead of cyber threats by identifying vulnerabilities and taking proactive measures to prevent attacks.

## How AI Enhances Threat Detection

AI-powered predictive modeling enhances threat detection by analyzing vast amounts of data, including network traffic, system logs, and threat intelligence feeds. AI algorithms can identify patterns and anomalies that may indicate a potential cyber threat, allowing small businesses to respond quickly and effectively.
