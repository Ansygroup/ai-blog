## What Is Grammarly Pricing Breakdown 2026?

Grammarly has evolved from a simple grammar [checker](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026) into a full‑[feature](/posts/copy-ai-vs-chatgpt-2026)d writing platform that offers real‑time suggestions, plagiarism detection, tone adjustments, and a browser‑extension that works across Gmail, Google Docs, and most SaaS [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026)s. The **Grammarly pricing breakdown 2026** consists of four tiers:

1. **Free** – Unlimited checks for basic grammar, spelling, and punctuation, limited to 5,000 characters per document.  
2. **Premium** – Adds advanced [style](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026), vocabulary, genre‑specific checks, plagiarism detection (up to 20 M words per month), and a tone detector.  
3. **Business** – Designed [for teams](/posts/elevenlabs-for-teams-2026-review-pricing-alternatives) of five or more, includes a centralized admin console, usage analytics, and priority support.  
4. **Enterprise** – Custom‑[price](/posts/suno-vs-chatgpt-2026)d for large organizations, offering SSO, dedicated account management, and API access.

Each tier builds on the previous one, but the price jump from Free to Premium is the most significant because it unlocks the AI core that powers Grammarly’s “Smart Compose” and “Con[text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review)ual Synonyms.” In 2026, the company also [intro](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026)duced a **Grammarly for Education** bundle (currently in beta) that mirrors Business pricing but adds classroom analytics.

- **Single Sign‑On (SSO)** – Integration with Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace for seamless onboarding.

## FAQ

### How much does Grammarly Premium cost per year in 2026?
Grammarly Premium costs **$359.40 per year** when billed annually ($29.95 per month). This is a 25% discount compared to the month‑to‑month price of $39.95.

### Does Grammarly Business include plagiarism detection?
Yes. Business users receive the same plagiarism detection limits as Premium (up to 20 M words per month) plus additional team‑level reporting.

### Can I get a discount for non‑profits or educators?
Grammarly offers special pricing for qualified non‑profits and educational institutions. You must contact [sales](/posts/best-of-top-ai-platforms-for-generating-personalized-sales-proposals) through the “Education” portal to receive a custom quote, typically a 15% reduction on Business rates.

### Is there a free trial for the Premium plan?
Grammarly provides a **7‑day free trial** of Premium for new users, giving full access to all features without entering payment details. After the trial, you’ll be [prompt](/posts/fixing-undefined-errors-ai-apps)ed to choose a billing cycle.

### How does Grammarly’s AI differ from ProWritingAid’s?
Grammarly uses a deep‑learning transformer model trained on billions of sentences, allowing context‑aware suggestions like “Contextual Rewrite.” ProWritingAid relies more on rule‑based algorithms and a smaller AI component, which can miss nuanced tone or genre‑specific advice.

### What happens if I cancel my [subscription](/posts/suno-pricing-breakdown-2026)?
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Grammarly does not offer refunds for the unused portion of a pre[paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) annual plan, but you retain access until the period expires.
