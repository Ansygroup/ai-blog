## What Is Descript API Cost Comparison 2026?

A “Descript API cost comparison 2026” examines the monetary landscape surrounding Descript’s suite of AI‑powered audio services as they exist in the current fiscal year. Descript, originally known for its desktop editing platform, launched a public API in 2023 that lets developers access three core engines:

1. **Transcribe** – high‑accuracy speech‑to‑text that supports over 30 languages.  
2. **Speaker Diarization** – automatic identification of who is speaking, useful for podcasts and meeting minutes.  
3. **Over‑dub** – AI‑generated voice cloning that can replace or augment recorded speech.

In 2026 the API is billed primarily on a per‑minute basis, with tiered subscription plans that bundle minutes, add‑on features, and priority support. The cost comparison also measures how these rates stack up against direct competitors such as AssemblyAI, Rev.ai, Deepgram, and OpenAI’s Whisper API. By the end of this section you’ll understand the three pricing levers (subscription tier, per‑minute usage, and add‑ons) that shape the total cost of ownership.

## FAQ

### How much does Descript charge for the first 1,000 minutes?

Descript’s free tier provides 1,000 minutes of transcription, speaker diarization, and up to 30 minutes of overdub each month at no cost. Once you exceed that quota, the overage rate is $0.030 / min for transcription and $0.015 / min for diarization.

### Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes. Descript offers a 15 % discount on the Starter and Pro plans when you commit to an annual contract, reducing the Pro plan to $25.50 / month (instead of $30) while keeping the 15‑hour bundle intact.

### Can I switch between plans mid‑month?

Descript allows plan changes at any time. If you upgrade, the remaining minutes for the current billing cycle are prorated, and you receive an immediate credit toward the higher tier. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle to avoid service interruption.
