## Setting Up Midjourney
To get started with Midjourney, simply sign up for an account on their website and choose a plan that suits your needs. The basic plan costs $10/month and includes access to all templates, as well as 10 minutes of video export per month. You can also upgrade to the pro plan for $20/month, which includes 30 minutes of video export and priority customer support.

## Creating Your First Video
Once you've signed up for Midjourney, you can start creating your first video. The process is straightforward: choose a template, customize it to your liking, and export the video. Midjourney offers a wide range of templates, from explainer videos to social media clips. You can also customize your template by adding your own images, music, and text.
