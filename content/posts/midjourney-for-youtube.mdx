## Introduction to Midjourney
Midjourney is an AI-powered video creation tool that helps YouTube creators produce high-quality content quickly. With its [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026)-friendly interface and extensive template library, Midjourney has become a favorite among YouTubers. ## Setting Up Midjourney
To get started with Midjourney, simply sign up for an account on their website and choose a plan that suits [your needs](/posts/gemini-review-2026). The basic plan costs $10/month and includes access to all templates, as well as 10 minutes of video export per month. You can also upgrade to the pro plan for $20/month, which includes 30 minutes of video export and priority [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) support.

## Creating Your First Video
Once you've signed up for Midjourney, you can start creating your first video. The process is straightforward: choose a template, customize it to your liking, and export the video. Midjourney offers a wide range of templates, from explainer videos to [social](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) media clips. You can also customize your template by adding your own images, [music](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026), and text.
