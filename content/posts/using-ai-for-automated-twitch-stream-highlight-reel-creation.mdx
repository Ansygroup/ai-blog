## Tips for Optimizing Your Highlight Reels

To get the most out of your highlight reels, consider the following [tips](/posts/heygen-workflow-examples-2026):

* **Keep it [short](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-instagram-reels-2026)**: Aim for a highlight reel that's 2-5 [minutes](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) long, showcasing the most engaging moments from your stream.

* **Add context**: Include text overlays or [captions](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-automated-youtube-video-closed-captioning-and-subtitling) to provide context and highlight important moments in your stream.
