Asking how much of search AI has taken gets answers from under 1% to about 20%, depending on who is counting. The numbers do not conflict so much as measure different things: questions asked, visits to the AI site or clicks sent to other websites. This piece is a state of play for the end of November 2026, not a December report, since December data does not exist yet. It uses one source, [Beket AI's comparison](https://beket.ai/blog/ai-search-market-share/) of 22 November 2026, which checked its figures on 21 November. Beket sells an AI visibility audit, so read its framing with that in mind. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

## Quick answer

| Estimate | Source and date | What it counts | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| About 20% | OpenAI's Adam Fry, November 2026 | ChatGPT questions that trigger a web search, against Google's query volume | Company claim |
| About 12% | Ahrefs, 11 February 2026 | 65% of 2.5 billion daily ChatGPT prompts against 13.7 billion daily Google searches | Modeled |
| About 7.5% | seoClarity, 28 October 2026 | 1.24 billion search-equivalent ChatGPT messages a day against a modeled 16.44 billion Google queries | Modeled |
| About 6.8% | Beket's calculation from Similarweb, October 2026 | 6.05 billion chatgpt.com visits against 88.44 billion google.com visits | Site visits |
| About 1.4% | Beket's calculation from Ahrefs, October 2026 | 4.3 million visits ChatGPT sent to tracked sites against 297.8 million from Google | Referral clicks |
| 0.09% | Statcounter, October 2026 | Page views referred by ChatGPT against Google plus ChatGPT | Referral share |

Beket says the gap between the first group and the last is the main story, and it is not a measurement error.

## Why the numbers differ

The top three try to count questions. The Similarweb row counts visits to the AI product for any purpose. The bottom two count clicks AI sends to other sites. ChatGPT answers inside the chat and offers a few links, so most people do not click, and a referral share will always look small next to a question share.

The two referral figures differ too, because they use different site samples. Ahrefs follows 118,671 sites that use its analytics, where Google sent 24.74% of traffic and ChatGPT 0.36% in October 2026. Statcounter measures a panel of over a million member sites. Both miss AI visits that arrive without a referrer, which Similarweb says land in direct traffic.

There is one more blind spot. All six estimates treat Google as traditional search, but Google now answers many queries with its own AI. A Google AI Overview or AI Mode answer sits on the Google side of every fraction above, so ChatGPT against Google understates how much search is answered by AI.

Beket says to compare the order, not the decimals. ChatGPT leads in every source and Gemini is second in both share based sources. Similarweb's trend shows ChatGPT down from about 76% over the June 2025 to May 2026 period. Claude is near 9% of visits in Similarweb but 2.3% of referrals in Statcounter, which Beket reads as more use staying inside the chat.

## What market share does not tell you

Beket makes a point most share debates skip: market share says how many people ask, and nothing about what the answer says about you. A brand with a small AI referral line can still be named, or left out, in many answers that never produce a click. It cites an opt-in panel where only 6.9% of US ChatGPT conversations showed purchase intent, but says those are the conversations where a shortlist gets made. That is the vendor's view, and it sells a tool for checking this, so verify it yourself by asking the assistants directly.

## What could go wrong

The usual error is quoting one number without saying what it counts. A headline that says AI has 1% of search and one that says 20% can both be accurate. Another error is treating a company claim as measured data, since the 20% figure comes from OpenAI and divides by a Google figure that was not disclosed.

## Who should skip it

Skip the detail if you only want to know which assistant to try. ChatGPT and Gemini lead every table. If you run a site, the useful number is the AI referral line in your own analytics.

## Test it yourself

1. Open your analytics and filter referrals from chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, perplexity.ai and claude.ai for October.
2. Divide by your Google organic referrals.
3. Compare with the 1.4% and 0.09% figures above.
4. Ask each assistant five questions about your brand and note whether you are named.
5. Report both numbers together, not one.

AI search share is somewhere between 0.09% and 20%, and each end is correct for its own definition. Plan with your own data. For related reading, see [ChatGPT vs Perplexity](/posts/chatgpt-vs-perplexity) and [ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-gemini).
