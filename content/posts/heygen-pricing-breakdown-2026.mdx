## FAQ

### How many minutes does HeyGen give per dollar on the Pro plan?
HeyGen Pro provides **30 minutes** for **$29**, which works out to roughly **0.034 minutes per cent** (or **~29 seconds per dollar**). This is about 50 % more minutes than Synthesia’s comparable tier.

### Can I [upgrade](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) or downgrade my HeyGen plan mid‑month?
Yes. HeyGen allows **instant plan changes**. If you upgrade, the new plan’s minutes are prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next month to avoid minute loss.

### Are there any hidden fees for exporting videos in 4K?
HeyGen’s 4K export is only available on the **Enterprise** tier. There are no per‑export fees, but the custom pricing includes a **$0.10 /min** rendering surcharge for ultra‑high‑resolution output, which is disclosed in the contract.

### How does HeyGen’s API pricing compare to its UI [subscription](/posts/grammarly-pricing-breakdown-2026)?
The API is **not included** in Free or Pro plans. Business users can add the API for **$199 /mo**, while Enterprise customers receive API access as part of the custom agreement. This cost is separate from the base subscription.

### Does HeyGen offer a discount for annual billing?
Yes. All paid tiers provide a **15 % discount** when billed annually. For example, the Pro plan drops from $29 /mo to **$24.99 /mo** (total $299.88 per year).

### What support channels are available for Enterprise clients?
Enterprise clients receive **24/7 live chat**, a **dedicated account manager**, and **quarterly strategy sessions**. They also get a **priority SLA** guaranteeing a response within 30 minutes for critical [issues](/posts/otter-limitations-2026-key-issues-workarounds).
