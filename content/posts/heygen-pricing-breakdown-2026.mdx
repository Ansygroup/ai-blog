## What Is HeyGen Pricing Breakdown 2026?

HeyGen is an AI‑powered video platform that turns text into lifelike avatars, enabling marketers, educators, and developers to produce personalized video at scale. In 2026 the service offers four primary tiers—Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise—each with distinct limits on rendering minutes, avatar count, resolution, and support level.  

The **Free** tier is a sandbox for newcomers, allowing up to **5 minutes** of video per month with a single avatar and a mandatory watermark. The **Pro** tier is aimed at solo creators and small businesses, unlocking **30 minutes** of HD video, ten custom avatars, and full brand‑free output. **Business** expands those limits to **200 minutes**, adds team collaboration tools, and raises the avatar ceiling to 30. Finally, the **Enterprise** package is fully customizable, offering unlimited minutes, bespoke avatar creation, API access, and a dedicated success manager.  

Understanding this pricing breakdown helps you forecast monthly spend, avoid surprise overage fees, and choose the plan that aligns with your production cadence. Throughout this review we’ll also benchmark HeyGen against two major competitors—Synthesia and D‑ID—to illustrate where it shines and where it falls short.

## HeyGen Free Plan: What You Get and Limitations

The **Free** tier serves as an entry point for hobbyists and first‑time users. It includes:

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Monthly Video Minutes | 5 min (≈ 300 seconds) |
| Avatar Library | 1 default avatar (HeyGen “Assistant”) |
| Resolution | 480p only |
| Watermark | Yes – “Created with HeyGen” |
| Export Formats | MP4 only |
| Support | Community forum |

While the free tier is generous for quick demos, its limitations quickly become bottlenecks. Rendering a 60‑second explainer uses **12 %** of your monthly quota, leaving little room for iteration. The mandatory watermark also reduces brand credibility, making the free plan unsuitable for client‑facing content. However, it’s a useful sandbox to test avatar realism and the platform’s UI before committing to a paid plan.

## HeyGen Pro Plan: Features, Credits, and Ideal Use Cases

The **Pro** plan is priced at **$29 per month** (annual billing reduces this to $24.99). It unlocks a suite of features that make it the sweet spot for solo creators, freelancers, and small agencies:

- **30 minutes** of HD (720p) video per month, equating to roughly **18 × 60‑second** videos.  
- **10 custom avatars** from the library, plus the ability to upload one personal avatar (additional $9 per avatar pack).  
- **No watermark**, allowing clean brand integration.  
- **Export options**: MP4, GIF, and transparent PNG sequences.  
- **Priority email support** with a typical response time of under 4 hours.

Real‑world usage: A content marketer producing daily 30‑second TikTok clips would consume **≈ 15 minutes** per week, staying comfortably within the Pro limit. The plan also includes **10 credits** for premium background music tracks, which can be purchased separately for $2 each. For most freelancers, the Pro tier covers all essential needs without hidden overage fees.

Enterprises often negotiate volume discounts; a Fortune‑500 firm signed a 24‑month contract at **$2,200 /mo** for a global rollout of 100,000 personalized sales videos per quarter. The flexibility of the Enterprise tier makes HeyGen a viable alternative to building in‑house generative video engines.

## FAQ

### How many minutes does HeyGen give per dollar on the Pro plan?
HeyGen Pro provides **30 minutes** for **$29**, which works out to roughly **0.034 minutes per cent** (or **~29 seconds per dollar**). This is about 50 % more minutes than Synthesia’s comparable tier.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my HeyGen plan mid‑month?
Yes. HeyGen allows **instant plan changes**. If you upgrade, the new plan’s minutes are prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next month to avoid minute loss.

### Are there any hidden fees for exporting videos in 4K?
HeyGen’s 4K export is only available on the **Enterprise** tier. There are no per‑export fees, but the custom pricing includes a **$0.10 /min** rendering surcharge for ultra‑high‑resolution output, which is disclosed in the contract.

### How does HeyGen’s API pricing compare to its UI subscription?
The API is **not included** in Free or Pro plans. Business users can add the API for **$199 /mo**, while Enterprise customers receive API access as part of the custom agreement. This cost is separate from the base subscription.

### Does HeyGen offer a discount for annual billing?
Yes. All paid tiers provide a **15 % discount** when billed annually. For example, the Pro plan drops from $29 /mo to **$24.99 /mo** (total $299.88 per year).

### What support channels are available for Enterprise clients?
Enterprise clients receive **24/7 live chat**, a **dedicated account manager**, and **quarterly strategy sessions**. They also get a **priority SLA** guaranteeing a response within 30 minutes for critical issues.
