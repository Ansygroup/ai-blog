### Does HeyGen support custom voice cloning?

Yes. HeyGen offers a **Voice Cloning** add‑on ($199/mo) that lets you upload up to 30 seconds of reference audio and generate a synthetic voice that matches your brand’s tone.

### Can I host the rendered videos on my own CDN?

Absolutely. HeyGen returns a signed URL that you can download and re‑host on any CDN (e.g., CloudFront, Akamai). The API also supports **direct streaming** via HLS if you enable the “stream” flag in the request payload.
