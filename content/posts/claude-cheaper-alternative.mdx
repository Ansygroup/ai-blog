## What Are AI Models and Why Do They Matter?

AI models like Claude, Llama, and Gemini are large language models (LLMs) that process and generate human-like text. These models are crucial for various applications, from chatbots to content creation. With the increasing demand for AI capabilities, finding cost-effective solutions has become essential.

## Understanding Claude's Pricing and Performance

Claude, developed by Anthropic, is a powerful AI model known for its high performance and safety features. However, its pricing can be steep, with costs ranging from $1.00 to $3.00 per million tokens, depending on the model size and usage. For many users, especially small businesses and individual developers, these costs can be prohibitive.

## Llama 3: A Cost-Effective Alternative to Claude
