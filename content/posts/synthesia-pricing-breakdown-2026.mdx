## What Is Synthesia Pricing Breakdown 2026?

Synthesia is a web‑based AI [video](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) platform that turns text into lifelike talking‑head videos without cameras or actors. In 2026 the company refined its pricing to three core tiers—Starter, Pro, and Enterprise—plus a menu of à la carte add‑ons such as premium voice packs, custom avatars, and higher‑resolution rendering. The pricing breakdown reflects the platform’s shift from a “pay‑as‑you‑go” model to a more predictable subscription structure, while still allowing large brands to negotiate bespoke contracts.

The primary [value](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) proposition remains the same: generate videos in [minutes](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026), not days, with a library of over 80 AI‑generated presenters and [multilingual](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) support for 60+ languages. The 2026 pricing breakdown introduces a **30‑minute monthly video quota** for the Pro plan, a notable increase from the 2023 limit of 20 minutes, and adds **API call limits** that make it feasible for SaaS integrations.

Understanding the exact cost per minute, the impact of add‑ons, and how Synthesia stacks up against competitors like **D‑ID** and **Runway** is [essential](/posts/notion-ai-limitations-2026-essential-guide-review) for marketers, educators, and [developer](/posts/claude-vs-gpt-4-for-coding-2026-who-wins-the-code-battle)s who need to forecast budgets accurately.

## Synthesia Starter Plan: Low‑Cost Entry for Solo Creators

The Starter plan is marketed toward f[reel](/posts/best-ai-video-summarization-tools-2026)ancers, educators, and hobbyists who need occasional video [content](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content). Priced at **$30 / month**, it includes:

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Video minutes | 10 minutes of HD video per month |
| Avatars | Access to 25 standard avatars |
| Languages | 30 languages (incl. English, Spanish, Mandarin) |
| Export formats | MP4, GIF |
| Support | Community forum + [email](/posts/ai-driven-email-marketing-automation-tools-review) (24‑hour response) |

**Real‑world use case:** A language tutor produced a [series](/posts/mike-flanagans-carrie-release-date-2026-full-guide) of 2‑minute pronunciation [guides](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-email-marketing-2026), consuming 8 minutes in the first month and staying well within the limit. The cost per minute works out to **$3 / minute**, which is higher than the Pro tier but acceptable for occasional use.

**Limitations:** The minute cap is strict; any video exceeding 10 minutes incurs an **overage fee of $5 per extra minute**. Addition[ally](/posts/copy-ai-vs-zapier-2026), Starter users cannot access the API, limiting automation possibilities.

**Why it matters:** For creators who produce fewer than 5 videos per month, the Starter plan offers a low‑[risk](/posts/top-ai-powered-compliance-platforms-finance-2026) entry point. However, the per‑minute cost spikes quickly once [you need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) more output, making the Pro plan a more economical choice for growing channels.

## Synthesia Pro Plan: The Sweet Spot for Small Teams

- **40+ avatars**, including premium “Executive” and “Diverse” collections
- **Full API access** with 10,000 calls per month
- **Custom voice packs** (up to 5 free voices, additional voices at $15 each)

**Cost breakdown example:** A [marketing](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) agency creates four 2‑minute product videos per week (≈ 32 minutes/month). The Pro plan covers the quota, leaving a surplus of 2 minutes that can be used for internal training clips. The effective cost per minute drops to **$3.75** (including the $120 base fee).

**Add‑on pricing:** If the agency wants a premium voice like “Emma (British RP)”, they pay $15 per month per voice. Adding three premium voices adds $45, raising the total to **$165** for a fully featured [setup](/posts/copy-ai-setup-guide-2026-complete-installation-step-by-step).

**Comparison to competitors:** D‑ID’s “Pro” tier offers 15 minutes for $49, while Runway’s “Pro” provides 25 minutes for $99. ## FAQ

### How much does Synthesia actually cost per minute of video?

The base cost per minute varies by plan: Starter is $3 / minute, Pro is $4 / minute (30 min for $120), and Enterprise is negotiated—typical contracts average $12‑$16 / minute after add‑ons.

### Can I get a discount for annual billing?

Yes. Synthesia offers a **10% discount** for an annual commitment on Starter and Pro plans, reducing the Pro price to **$108 / month** (billed $1,296 yearly).

### Are there any hidden fees I should [watch](/posts/watch-mayday-air-disasters-on-tubi-its-free-2026-guide) out for?

Overage fees ($5 per extra minute) apply if you exceed your quota. Premium voice packs, custom avatars, and 4K rendering are additional [line](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026) items. Enterprise contracts may include setup fees for on‑premise deployment.

### How does Synthesia’s API limit compare to D‑ID and Runway?

Synthesia provides **10,000 calls per month** on the Pro plan, while D‑ID caps at 5,000 and Runway at 8,000. This makes Synthesia the most generous for developers [building](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026) [automated](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) video pipelines.

### Is there a free trial, and how long does it last?

Synthesia offers a **14‑day trial** with 5 minutes of free video [generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) and access to a limited avatar set. No credit card is required, but the trial does not include API access.

### Does Synthesia support [subtitles](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) or closed captions?

Yes. All plans include [automatic](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) subtitle generation in up to 30 languages, with manual editing available in the editor. Exported videos can embed SRT files for accessibility compliance.
