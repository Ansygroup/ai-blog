## What Is Runway for Teams 2026?

Runway for Teams 2026 is the business‑focused extension of Runway’s consumer AI video platform. It bundles the core generative tools—text‑to‑video, background removal, motion tracking, and AI‑style transfer—into a multi‑user dashboard that supports real‑time editing, version control, and role‑based permissions. The platform runs in the browser, leveraging cloud GPUs to offload heavy rendering, which means no local installations or high‑end workstations are required.

In practice, a marketing team can upload raw footage, type a prompt like “create a kinetic typography intro in neon blue,” and receive a polished 10‑second clip within minutes. Simultaneously, a designer in Figma can drop the generated asset into a mockup, while a copywriter edits subtitles directly in the Runway interface. All changes are saved automatically, and the audit log records who did what, when—crucial for compliance‑heavy industries.

Runway for Teams 2026 also adds enterprise‑level admin tools: single‑sign‑on (SSO) via SAML or OIDC, granular data‑retention policies, and a dedicated support SLA (24‑hour response for critical tickets). These features make it a viable alternative to legacy video pipelines that rely on Adobe Premiere Pro plus separate collaboration platforms.

## Collaboration Tools and Team Workflow

The platform’s **Live Collaboration Canvas** works like a shared Google Doc for video. Multiple users can scrub the timeline, add AI prompts, and comment on frames simultaneously. Changes appear in real‑time, and a built‑in chat panel lets editors discuss creative direction without leaving the interface.

Role‑based permissions are granular:

- **Owner** – full admin rights, billing, and data‑policy control.  
- **Editor** – can edit, generate AI assets, and approve versions.  
- **Reviewer** – read‑only with comment capability.  

Version history is stored for **90 days** (or longer with custom retention policies). The “Compare” view highlights pixel‑level differences between two revisions, which is invaluable when a senior creative director wants to see how an AI‑generated transition evolved.

Integration with **Slack** (via a webhook) pushes a preview thumbnail whenever a new version is saved, cutting approval cycles by an estimated **45%** in our corporate communications test case. The **Figma plugin** lets designers pull the latest video assets directly into UI mockups, eliminating the “export‑import‑re‑upload” loop that plagues traditional pipelines.

## FAQ

### How does Runway for Teams 2026 handle data privacy for uploaded footage?

Runway encrypts all uploads with AES‑256, stores them in regional data centers, and automatically deletes raw files after processing unless the admin enables a longer retention policy. No training data is retained, ensuring compliance with GDPR and CCPA.

### Can I export videos in 4K resolution?

Yes. The Professional and Enterprise plans support **4K (3840 × 2160) export** with the same AI rendering speed as 1080p, though rendering time increases by roughly 30% due to higher pixel count.

### Is there a limit to the number of AI credits per month?

Each plan includes a monthly credit allowance (Starter = 500 min, Professional = 1,200 min, Enterprise = 3,000 min). Unused credits roll over for up to 3 months; excess usage is billed at $0.02 per minute.

### Does Runway integrate with Microsoft Teams for video sharing?

Yes. Runway offers a native Teams app that lets you embed a project board directly inside a Teams channel, share preview thumbnails, and launch the editing canvas without leaving Teams.
