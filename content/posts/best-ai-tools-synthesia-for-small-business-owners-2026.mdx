## What Is Synthesia?

Synthesia is an AI‑powered video platform that turns plain text into professional‑looking videos with lifelike digital presenters. Launched in 2017, the service has evolved to include over 70 avatars, real‑time lip‑sync, and a library of 60+ languages. Small business owners use Synthesia for product demos, onboarding, social ads, and internal communications—any scenario where a human presenter would be too costly or time‑consuming.

In practice, a user types a script, selects an avatar (e.g., “Emma – Business Professional”), chooses a background, and clicks “Generate.” Within minutes, the platform outputs an MP4 ready for upload to YouTube, Instagram, or a landing page. Because the AI handles voice‑over, subtitles, and translation, a single video can serve global audiences without extra recording sessions.

Synthesia’s core value proposition for SMBs is **speed + scalability**. A 30‑second explainer that would normally cost $250‑$500 to produce with a freelance videographer can be created for **$0.10 per minute of final video** after the subscription fee, dramatically lowering the barrier to video marketing.

When comparing head‑to‑head, Synthesia wins on **avatar variety, speed, and multilingual depth**, while Pictory wins on **budget friendliness** and **stock footage integration**.
