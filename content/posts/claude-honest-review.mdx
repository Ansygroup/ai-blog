## What Is Claude?

Claude is an AI tool developed by [Anthropic](/posts/claude-mythos-2026-anthropic-most-capable-model-review), designed to process and generate human-like text. It's primarily used for conversational tasks, [[content](/posts](/posts/claude-for-youtube)/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) creation, and [research](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-market-research-2026) assistance. Claude's interface is user-friendly, allowing users to input prompts and receive detailed responses.

Claude's knowledge cutoff is December 2023, limiting its real-time information. When asked about recent events or developments, [Claude](/posts/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-cursor-windsurf-claude-code-codex) often responds with:
