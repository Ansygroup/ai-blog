## Hidden Fees and Overages in Gamma API

Beyond the head[line](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026) per‑token rates, Gamma applies a few ancillary charges that can affect the bottom line:

1. **Latency Surcharge** – If average response time exceeds 400 ms for the “Scale” tier, a 5% surcharge is added to the monthly bill. Most [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026)s stay under this threshold with the optional dedicated instance.  
2. **APAC Traffic Fee** – Requests routed to Gamma’s Singapore data center incur a 7% premium due to higher bandwidth costs.  
3. **Data‑Privacy Vault** – As noted, $0.004 per 1k tokens for encrypted storage.  
4. **Support Escalation** – “Premium Support” (phone line, 1‑hour SLA) costs an extra $500/mo for Scale and Enterprise tiers.

These fees are disclosed in Gamma’s pricing FAQ, but they are easy to overlook. By contrast, OpenAI’s pricing is more transparent: a flat overage rate with no regional adjustments. However, OpenAI does charge for **fine‑tuning** ($0.12 per 1k tokens) and **embedding storage** ($0.0004 per 1k tokens), which can add up for specialized workloads.

## Discount Programs and Volume Commitments

Gamma rewards long‑term commitments and high‑volume usage with tiered discounts:

- **Annual Commitment** – 10% off any tier’s base price when billed yearly.  
- **Volume Commitment** – 5% discount after 100 M tokens/month, scaling to 20% after 500 M tokens/month.  
- **Non‑Profit Grant** – Eligible NGOs receive a 30% discount on the Growth tier, capped at $500/mo.  
- **Beta Feature Access** – Early adopters of Gamma’s “Multimodal Fusion” endpoint receive a 15% token discount for the first six months.

These programs can dramatically lower the total cost of ownership. For example, a **mid‑market SaaS** with a 12‑month contract and 80 M tokens/month would see its base price drop from $799 to **$719**, and the overage rate fall to **$0.012** after volume discounts—resulting in a total monthly bill of **$1,150** versus $1,400 without discounts.
