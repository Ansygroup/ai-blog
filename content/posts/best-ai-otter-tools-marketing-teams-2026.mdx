**Accuracy & Speed** – In our tests, Otter achieved a **Word Error Rate (WER) of 3.2%**, marginally better than Fireflies.ai (3.5%). The first transcript appeared within **12 seconds** of the meeting start, and the full transcript was searchable in under **30 seconds** after the session ended.

**Limitations** – While Otter’s native video export (SRT + MP4) is handy, it lacks built‑in clip creation. That’s where Grain.co shines, and we’ll explore the synergy later.

Overall, Otter.ai delivers the most reliable transcription engine for marketing teams, especially when paired with its robust API (rate‑limited at 500 requests/minute) for custom workflow automations.

| Searchable knowledge base | Unlimited | 5,000 records |
| Integration depth | HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, Zapier | Same, minus Salesforce |

**Performance** – Fireflies’ WER sits at **3.5%**, just shy of Otter’s, but its AI‑summaries are **30% faster** thanks to a proprietary summarization model (FireSummarize). In a 60‑minute webinar test, Fireflies delivered a 250‑word executive summary in **45 seconds**, versus Otter’s 70 seconds.

**Unique Value** – Fireflies shines in multi‑platform recordings. Its “Auto‑Join” bot can hop into a meeting without a human host, making it ideal for remote teams that use a mix of video conferencing tools. **Drawbacks** – The UI feels cluttered, and the “Live Captions” are less stable on low‑bandwidth connections. Moreover, Fireflies does not support direct export of SRT files, limiting its usefulness for video editors who need timed subtitles.

In head‑to‑head comparisons, Fireflies is a solid alternative for teams that prioritize cross‑platform coverage and rapid AI summaries, but Otter still leads on raw transcription accuracy and collaborative features.

| Overdub voice clones | 1 per user | 5 per user |

Descript is the go‑to choice when your marketing strategy includes a heavy video or podcast component and you need a one‑stop shop for transcription, editing, and synthetic voice generation.

| Auto‑highlights AI | Yes (top 10 moments) | Yes (customizable) |
| Export destinations | LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, HubSpot | All major platforms + API |
| Collaboration | 5 teammates per folder | Unlimited |

**Synergy with Otter** – Grain pulls the transcript directly from Otter via API, then lets users click on any highlighted phrase to instantly generate a 15‑30 second clip. In our tests, a 45‑minute strategy session yielded **22 ready‑to‑post clips** in under **5 minutes** of manual work.

**Performance** – Grain’s AI correctly identified the most “shareable” moments 93% of the time (based on engagement metrics from a pilot group of 50 marketers). The platform also auto‑generates captions using Otter’s transcript, ensuring accessibility compliance.

**Cons** – Grain does not store raw video files beyond 30 days, so teams need a separate archival solution (e.g., Google Drive). Also, the free tier caps at 5 clips per month, which is insufficient for high‑velocity teams.

For marketing squads that live on short‑form video, Grain is the missing link that turns Otter’s text‑heavy output into visual assets without a separate video editor.
