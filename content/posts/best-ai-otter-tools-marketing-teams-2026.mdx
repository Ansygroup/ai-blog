## What Is Otter AI and Why It Matters for Marketing Teams?

Otter AI is an AI‑driven transcription platform that turns spoken content into searchable, editable text. In 2026, it supports live captioning, speaker identification, and automatic summary generation—all powered by a proprietary deep‑learning model trained on millions of hours of audio. Marketing teams leverage Otter to capture brainstorming sessions, interview footage, and webinar Q&A without manual note‑taking.

Beyond raw transcription, Otter integrates with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, allowing teams to import recordings automatically. Its **Collaboration Hub** lets multiple users annotate, highlight, and export notes in formats like CSV, PDF, or directly to a content‑management system (CMS). For a typical agency handling 15 client calls per week, Otter can reduce manual transcription labor by **up to 85 %**, freeing up creative resources.

Key features for marketers:

- **Overdub**: Create a synthetic voice clone to generate quick voice‑overs for ads.

- **Multitrack Editing**: Sync video clips with transcripts for rapid social‑media video production.
- **Collaboration**: Real‑time editing with comments and version history.

Descript’s transcription accuracy sits at **2.8 % WER**, marginally better than Otter. In a pilot, a content team reduced the time to produce a 30‑second Instagram Reel from **4 hours** (using traditional editing software) to **1.5 hours** with Descript’s transcript‑driven editing.
