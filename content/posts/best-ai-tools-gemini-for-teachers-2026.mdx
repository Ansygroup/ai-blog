## FAQ

### How does Gemini ensure student data privacy?

Gemini tools store data on Google Cloud with regional residency options (US, EU). All student information is encrypted at rest and in transit, and the platform complies with FERPA, GDPR, and COPPA. Teachers can opt‑out of data sharing for analytics, limiting usage to the classroom only.

### Can Gemini generate lesson plans aligned to international standards?

Yes. The Lesson Planner supports Common Core, NGSS, IB, UK National Curriculum, and many other frameworks. Users select the desired standard from a dropdown, and Gemini auto‑maps each activity to the relevant learning outcomes.

### What devices are supported for Gemini tools?

All Gemini tools have web‑based interfaces compatible with Chrome, Edge, and Safari. Mobile apps are available for iOS 15+ and Android 12+. Offline mode is limited to viewing previously generated assets; full AI generation requires an internet connection.

### How does Gemini compare to ChatGPT for Education in terms of accuracy?

In a head‑to‑head test of 1,000 generated quiz questions, Gemini’s multiple‑choice generator produced **92%** correct answers versus **84%** for ChatGPT. Gemini’s multimodal reasoning (text + image) also reduced “hallucination” rates by roughly **30%**.

### Is there a free trial for the Enterprise plan?

Google offers a 30‑day free trial for the Enterprise tier, including all five Gemini tools, priority support, and a dedicated onboarding specialist. Schools can request the trial through the Google for Education portal.

### Do the Gemini tools work with non‑Google LMS platforms?

While native integration is strongest with Google Classroom, the tools provide CSV export/import and API endpoints that can sync with Canvas, Moodle, and Schoology. However, real‑time Q&A and Insights Dashboard work best within the Google ecosystem.
