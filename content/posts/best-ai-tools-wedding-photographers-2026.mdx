A wedding photographer shoots for eight hours and then edits for forty. The camera is the fun part. The real cost of the business is the 3,000 frames waiting on a hard drive after every Saturday, and the couple who emails in week three asking where their gallery is. AI tools for wedding photographers have one honest job: shrink the editing queue so delivery happens in weeks, not months.

Prices below were read on vendor pages on November 27, 2026. Last tested: November 27, 2026. Several vendors were running Black Friday offers; list prices are used where the page showed them.

## The short answer

| Scenario | Pick | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cull 3,000 frames to 600 | Aftershoot Select | From $8 per month on the yearly plan |
| Edit in your own style | Aftershoot Edit or Imagen | Learn your look from past work |
| Deliver galleries | Pixieset | Free tier with 3 GB, paid from $8 per month |
| Contracts, invoices, inquiries | HoneyBook | From $18 per month |

## Aftershoot: culling and editing as separate purchases

Aftershoot's page shows a price table for each workflow, per month. On the yearly plan: Select $8, Edit $24, Retouch $16, Select plus Edit $32 and Complete $36. On monthly billing the same plans were $15, $35, $25, $50 and $55, and a middle option billed annually but paid monthly was $12, $32, $22, $44 and $50. The yearly prices carried a Black Friday label, so check whether they have reverted.

Culling is the best first purchase. Choosing the keepers from a wedding is mechanical work: remove blinks, near-duplicates and out-of-focus frames. The page claims users save five to seven hours per shoot on selection. Treat that as the vendor's figure, and measure your own on the first wedding.

Where it fails: culling decisions on emotional moments. A blink that catches a tear may be the picture the couple frames. Always scan the rejected set before delivering, at least on the ceremony and the first look.

## Imagen: pay per photo or go unlimited

Imagen's page listed pay-as-you-go pricing starting at $0.05 per photo with a $7 monthly base fee, and an unlimited plan at $129 billed annually or $165 billed monthly. A Black Friday offer showed a first month at $5. Culling plans started from $12 per month.

The sums are simple. At $0.05 per photo, a 3,000-photo wedding costs $150 in color correction. An unlimited plan at $129 a month pays for itself within a single wedding if you edit every frame. For fewer than a wedding a month, pay-as-you-go can be cheaper.

Where it fails: both Imagen and Aftershoot learn from your past edits. If your past edits are inconsistent, the output will be too. Feed them 2,000 or more photos you would be proud to deliver, not your whole archive.

## Pixieset for galleries

Pixieset's free tier gives 3 GB of storage. Paid plans on the page read were $8 per month billed annually ($10 monthly) for 10 GB, then $16 for 100 GB and $24 and $40 for larger plans. A wedding delivered at full resolution can exceed 10 GB, so size the plan by your total archive, not one gallery. The page also lists add-ons, such as $15 per month per additional 1,000 GB.

## HoneyBook for the paperwork

HoneyBook's page showed plans from $18 per month, with card payments at 2.7% plus 10 cents and ACH at 1.5%. For a $4,000 package paid by ACH, the fee is $60 instead of about $108 by card. Encourage bank transfer for large balances.

## What the vendor pages do not say

Speed is a marketing asset that no pricing table shows. Couples remember the day they got a sneak peek of 20 photos, often within 48 hours. Use the culling tool the evening after the wedding, edit 20 images in your style and send them. That one email prevents most "where are our photos?" messages and earns reviews.

Backups are the second gap. AI editing does not replace storage discipline. Keep two copies of every wedding on separate drives until the gallery is delivered, and one off-site copy for as long as your contract says you retain files.

## Who should skip this list

A photographer shooting fewer than ten weddings a year may find manual editing faster than training a style profile. If your style depends on heavy individual retouching, AI editing will give you a starting point, not a finished frame.

## What to buy first

Buy Aftershoot Select or a month of Imagen culling for your next wedding and compare hours. If culling saves you more than four hours, add an editing plan. Keep Pixieset's free tier until a single wedding exceeds 3 GB. Add HoneyBook when you lose track of inquiries, not before.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should a wedding photographer automate first?

Culling. It is mechanical, and the vendor claims five to seven hours saved per shoot. Measure your own on the first wedding.

### Is Imagen cheaper than Aftershoot?

It depends on volume. Imagen starts at $0.05 per photo with a $7 base fee, or $129 to $165 monthly for unlimited, while Aftershoot sells separate workflows from $8 per month.

### Will AI edit in my style?

Both tools learn from your past edits, so output matches the quality of what you train them on. Train them on your best work only.
