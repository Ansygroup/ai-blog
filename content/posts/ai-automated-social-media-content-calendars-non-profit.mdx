## What Is a Content Calendar?

A content calendar is a planning tool used to organize and schedule [social](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) media content in advance. It helps you visualize your [content](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-content-localization) strategy, ensure consistency, and save time by planning and preparing [content](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-content-localization) in bulk. A good content calendar should include the following elements:

* Date and time of posting
* Type of content (image, [video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison), text-only)
* Target audience
* Engagement goals
* Hashtags and keywords
* Links to relevant blog posts or landing pages
