## What Is a Content Calendar?

A content calendar is a planning tool used to organize and schedule social media content in advance. It helps you visualize your content strategy, ensure consistency, and save time by planning and preparing content in bulk. A good content calendar should include the following elements:

* Date and time of posting
* Type of content (image, video, text-only)
* Target audience
* Engagement goals
* Hashtags and keywords
* Links to relevant blog posts or landing pages
