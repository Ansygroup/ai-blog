## FAQ

### How does Jasper ensure data confidentiality for consulting clients?

Jasper’s Secure Workspace encrypts all uploaded files with AES‑256 and stores them in ISO‑27001‑certified data centers. Access logs are retained for 90 days, and the platform offers role‑based permissions, ensuring only authorized consultants can view sensitive content.

### Can Jasper replace a junior analyst for market research summaries?

Jasper can generate first‑draft market summaries in minutes, but it lacks the nuanced judgment of a seasoned analyst. It’s best used as a **drafting accelerator**, with a human reviewer polishing insights and validating sources.

### What is the difference between Jasper’s “Consultant Mode” and the regular plan?

Consultant Mode adds a **Glossary Sync**, industry‑specific tone presets, and higher‑security controls. It also includes pre‑built templates for RFP responses, SWOT analyses, and ROI calculations—features absent from the standard plan.

### Is the Zapier integration native or do I need a third‑party connector?

Jasper provides a **native Zapier app** with pre‑configured triggers (e.g., “New Task in Asana”) and actions (e.g., “Generate Email”). No additional middleware is required, simplifying setup for consulting teams.

### How does Jasper’s pricing compare to Copy.ai for a team of five consultants?

For five seats, Jasper’s Chat + Content Improver bundle totals **$320/mo** (5 × $64). Copy.ai’s comparable Creative Suite costs **$195/mo** (5 × $39) but lacks the security and consulting‑specific templates, potentially leading to higher hidden costs in time and compliance.

### Does Jasper support multiple languages for global consulting projects?

Yes. Jasper supports **English, Spanish, French, German, and Mandarin** with comparable accuracy scores (≈90% for business tone). Language packs are included in the Enterprise tier at no extra charge.
