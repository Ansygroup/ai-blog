Jasper’s Secure Workspace encrypts all uploaded files with AES‑256 and stores them in ISO‑27001‑certified data centers. Access logs are retained for 90 days, and the platform offers role‑based permissions, ensuring only authorized consultants can view sensitive content.

### Can Jasper replace a junior analyst for market research summaries?

Jasper can generate first‑draft market summaries in [minutes](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026), but it lacks the nuanced judgment of a seasoned analyst. It’s best used as a **drafting accelerator**, with a human reviewer polishing insights and validating sources.

### What is the difference between Jasper’s “Consultant Mode” and the regular plan?

Consultant Mode adds a **Glossary Sync**, industry‑specific tone presets, and higher‑security controls. ### Is the Zapier integration native or do I need a third‑party connector?

Jasper provides a **native Zapier app** with pre‑configured triggers (e.g., “New Task in Asana”) and actions (e.g., “Generate Email”).
