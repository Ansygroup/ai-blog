## Who Should Use Gamma?

Gamma suits professionals and businesses seeking a balanced feature set and user-friendly interface. It's ideal for [marketing](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) teams, sales professionals, and educators.

## Who Should Use Tome?

Tome is perfect [for teams](/posts/descript-for-teams-2026-review) and organizations prioritizing collaboration. Its free plan makes it suitable for startups and small businesses.

## Who Should Use Beautiful AI?

Beautiful AI is geared towards [designers](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-designers-2026), creatives, and those prioritizing presentation aesthetics. It's ideal for agencies, marketing firms, and event planners.

## Who Should Skip This?

Those seeking basic presentation [software](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) might find these tools overwhelming. Simpler alternatives like PowerPoint or Google Slides might suffice.
