## Who Should Use Gamma?

Gamma suits [professionals](/posts/runway-gen-3-review-2026-best-ai-video-generator-for-professionals) and businesses seeking a balanced feature set and user-friendly interface. It's ideal for [marketing](/posts/ai-for-influencer-marketing-how-to-find-and-collaborate-with) teams, [sales](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-ecommerce-2026) professionals, and educators.

## Who Should Use Tome?

Tome is perfect for teams and organizations prioritizing collaboration. Its free plan makes it suitable for startups and [small](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-data-analysis-and-visualization-for-small-business-owners) businesses.

## Who Should Use Beautiful AI?

Beautiful AI is geared towards [designers](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-designers-2026), creatives, and those prioritizing presentation aesthetics. It's ideal for agencies, [marketing](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-influencer-marketing-campaigns) firms, and event planners.

## Who Should Skip This?

Those seeking basic presentation software might find these tools overwhelming. Simpler [alternatives](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026) like PowerPoint or Google Slides might suffice.
