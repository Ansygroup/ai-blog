## What Is Midjourney and Why Freelancers Love It?

Unlike DALL·E 3 or Stable Diffusion, Midjourney’s proprietary “v5” engine (released early 2025) produces richer textures and more consistent lighting, which translates into fewer post‑production tweaks. Freelancers—graphic designers, NFT creators, and marketing copywriters—choose Midjourney because it can deliver a portfolio‑ready visual in 30–45 seconds, allowing them to pitch multiple concepts in a single client meeting.

Real‑world examples illustrate the impact. ## FAQ

### How much does a Midjourney subscription cost in 2026?

Midjourney offers three plans: Basic at $10/mo (200 GPU minutes), Standard at $30/mo (15 hours GPU time), and Stealth at $30/mo plus unlimited private mode. Most freelancers find the Standard plan sufficient for 30‑40 projects per month.

### Can I use Midjourney without Discord?

Officially, Midjourney runs exclusively on Discord, but third‑party integrations like **Midjourney Bot for Slack** and **Zapier** can bridge the gap, allowing you to submit prompts from other platforms while still leveraging Discord’s backend.

### Are the AI upscalers safe for copyrighted material?

Yes. Tools like Topaz Gigapixel and Let’s Enhance operate on the image file you upload and do not store or share it. Always review the provider’s privacy policy; most reputable services guarantee 100 % data deletion after processing.

### How do PromptHero’s keyword heatmaps improve my prompts?

Heatmaps highlight which words in a prompt most influence Midjourney’s style engine. By focusing on high‑impact terms, freelancers can craft shorter, more effective prompts that achieve the desired aesthetic in fewer iterations, cutting revision cycles by up to 40 %.

### Is Notion AI worth the $10/mo for a solo freelancer?

For solo creators who manage client briefs, revisions, and invoicing in one place, Notion AI’s task automation and AI‑generated meeting notes typically save 5‑10 hours per month—worth at least $150 in hourly value, making the $10/mo cost highly justifiable.

### What’s the difference between PromptBase and PromptHero?

PromptBase is a marketplace where you buy premium prompts from other creators, often with royalty tracking. PromptHero is a prompt‑building platform with templates and analytics. Use PromptBase for niche, high‑conversion prompts; use PromptHero for daily prompt generation and testing.
