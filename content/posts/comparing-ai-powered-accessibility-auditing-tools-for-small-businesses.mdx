## What Is AI-Powered Website Accessibility Auditing?

AI-powered website accessibility auditing tools use artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) algorithms to analyze a website's [code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026), structure, and [content](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-content-localization), detecting [potential](/posts/jasper-best-settings) accessibility issues. These tools can identify issues related to color contrast, font size, image descriptions, [and more](/posts/midjourney-desktop-app-review-2026), providing recommendations for improvement.
