When shopping for the best laptop for AI and development, there are several key factors to consider, including processor speed, memory, and storage. The type of graphics card is also crucial, as integrated graphics may not be sufficient for complex AI tasks. The operating system is also an essential consideration, with Windows 11 and macOS being popular choices among developers.
