When buying a laptop for AI and development, there are several factors to consider, including the processor, memory, storage, and graphics card. Additionally, a dedicated graphics card such as NVIDIA RTX can significantly improve performance in tasks such as machine learning and data visualization.

The display and keyboard are also important considerations, as they can affect productivity and comfort. A laptop with a high-resolution display, such as 4K or FHD+, and a backlit keyboard can provide a better user experience. Another crucial aspect is the operating system, with options such as Windows 11 or macOS, each with its own strengths and weaknesses. Lastly, the price is a significant factor, with options ranging from budget-friendly to high-end, and it's crucial to find a balance between features and budget.
