## Introduction to Brain.fm
Brain.fm is a music app designed to help users concentrate, relax, or sleep [better](/posts/chatgpt-vs-claude) using AI-generated soundscapes. With over 1 million downloads, it's a popular choice among [students](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-students-2026), programmers, and anyone looking to boost their productivity. Brain.fm's AI technology creates music that adapts to individual brain waves, ensuring a [personalized](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-personalized-podcast-advertising) experience.

## How Brain.fm Works
The app uses a complex algorithm to generate music that stimulates the brain's focus, relaxation, or sleep centers. Users can choose from various modes, including focus, relax, and sleep, each with its own unique soundscapes. Brain.fm also allows users to customize their experience by adjusting the music's intensity, tempo, and genre.
