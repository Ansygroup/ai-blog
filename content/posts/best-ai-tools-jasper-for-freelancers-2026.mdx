## FAQ

### How does Jasper’s SEO Optimizer differ from using Surfer directly?

Jasper’s SEO Optimizer pulls Surfer’s keyword recommendations into the writing interface, allowing you to see optimal keyword density and related [terms](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) **while you draft**. This eliminates the need to copy data between two separate dashboards, cutting optimization time by roughly **30%**.

### Can I use Jasper for non‑English languages?

Yes. Jasper supports **English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, and Portuguese** in 2026. Word limits apply across languages, and the SEO Optimizer currently works best for English, but [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) still benefit from tone‑control and plagiarism checks in other languages.

### Is there a [free version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) of Jasper for freelancers?

Jasper offers a **5‑day trial** with 10 k words and limited Boss Mode. After the trial, the **Starter plan** at $29/mo is the lowest [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) tier. There is no permanently free tier, but the trial is sufficient to test core features before committing.

### How does Jasper handle data [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) for client briefs?

All content is processed under **GDPR‑compliant** servers located in the EU and US. Jasper does not store client data longer than 30 days unless you enable the “Project Archive” feature, which encrypts files at rest. For highly confidential projects, you can toggle “Secure Mode,” which disables data logging entirely.

### What is [the best](/posts/best-ai-tools-accountants-bookkeeping-2026) prompt structure for a freelance blog post?

A proven structure is:

```
Topic] + Target Audience] + Desired Word Count] + Tone] + SEO Keywords]
```

Example: “Write a 1,500‑word blog post for [small](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026)‑[business owners](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026) about AI [automation](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026), in a conversational tone, targeting keywords ‘AI workflow automation’, ‘small business AI tools’.”

### Does Jasper integrate with invoicing tools like FreshBooks?

Direct integration isn’t native, but you can connect Jasper to **Zapier**, then set up a Zap that creates a FreshBooks invoice each time a “Project Completed” tag is added in Notion. This indirect workflow saves about **10 minutes per invoice**.
