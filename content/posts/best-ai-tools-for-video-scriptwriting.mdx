## Lumen5 Review
Lumen5 is a popular AI video scriptwriting tool that offers a range of features, including a free [plan](/posts/cursor-pricing-breakdown-2026) with 50 [templates](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026), a user-friendly [interface](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-real-estate-agents-2026-top-picks), and high-quality exports. It's ideal for [social media](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-non-profit) videos, explainer videos, and blog videos. Lumen5 also integrates with popular platforms like YouTube, Facebook, and Twitter.

## Raw Shorts Review
Raw Shorts is another top contender in the AI video scriptwriting space. It offers a 14-day free trial with unlimited exports, a vast template library, and advanced [customization](/posts/best-ai-powered-resume-tailoring-ats-optimization-2026) options. Raw Shorts is suitable for businesses, marketers, and entrepreneurs who want to create [professional](/posts/ai-powered-linkedin-profile-optimization-tutorial-2026)-looking videos without breaking the bank.
