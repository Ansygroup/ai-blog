Black Friday 2026 is the first one where nearly every big AI lab offers some way for its assistant to check out for you. The early numbers say shoppers are using AI to find and compare products, and very few are letting it pay. This piece is a state of play written on 27 November 2026, during Black Friday weekend, so it covers what is known now and not how the holidays ended. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

## What launched

According to [The Parameter](https://theparameter.ai/a/black-friday-ai-shopping-agents/) (26 November 2026), OpenAI, Google, Meta, Microsoft, xAI, Perplexity and Anthropic all now offer some form of agent checkout. The main launches it lists:

- 13 November: OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet. Its agents, called dots, can complete purchases, but only for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers at first. The shopper approves each purchase with a check such as Face ID, and a payment token replaces the real card number. On 23 November Shopify said its agentic checkout for ChatGPT is on by default for eligible stores.
- 15 October: Google rolled out Universal Cart in the US in Search and the Gemini app. It collects items from many stores and checks out with Google Pay at merchants including Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart and Wayfair.
- Since September: Meta's Muse agent shops for users, paying with Stripe's Link one-time card numbers. From 24 November, Walmart let Muse shop its Black Friday deals with spending limits the shopper sets.

[IBM's report](https://www.ibm.com/think/news/black-friday-ai-agents-online-shopping) of 26 November adds that Amazon blocked Muse in September, saying the agent did not identify itself, and that the companies were reportedly in talks as of 25 November. United Airlines changed its rules so agents can look up fares but cannot buy tickets.

The $75.8 billion figure counts influence, which includes recommendations. It is not what agents bought. That is the number most likely to be quoted wrongly this week.

## Conversion: the number retailers watch

The Parameter reports Adobe's September data that AI referred visitors converted 90% better than other traffic and brought 83% more revenue per visit. In August 2025 the same traffic converted 9% worse. That swing is why retailers are paying attention even while few shoppers let agents pay. It also means a forecast is only as good as its start date, so treat a single month with care.

The Salesforce survey adds a use pattern. In Salesforce's survey, 31% of consumers have tried a personal agent, and only 19% of those users had used theirs at checkout. About 36% had set up a separate credit card just for it. For anyone using an agent this weekend, a dedicated card with a low limit is the same safety step the surveyed shoppers chose.

## What can go wrong

IBM names the trust problem plainly: the shopper does not know what the agent will buy, and the seller does not know who the shopper is. Accountability is open too. If your agent buys ten boxes when you asked for one, the liability and the return process are not settled. In an ARC pilot cited by The Parameter, the best model reached a real store's checkout on its own 32 times out of 68 attempts, and the test stopped before payment. ARC says the pilot does not rank models.

## Who should skip agent checkout

Skip it if you cannot review every order before it ships, if the item has size or fit risk, or if the retailer's return policy is unclear. Using an agent to search and compare is the lower risk step, and it is where most shoppers are today.

## Test it yourself

1. Ask an assistant to find three options for one gift under a budget.
2. Check each price and stock status on the store page.
3. If your plan offers checkout, buy one low value item and read the confirmation before approving.
4. Check that the one time card number or token appears on your statement correctly.
5. Keep the order page until the return window closes.

## Verdict

Agents are good at finding and comparing, and checkout is still early. Treat any claim that agents are driving most holiday sales as unverified. A follow up with real results will be possible only after Cyber Monday data is published.
