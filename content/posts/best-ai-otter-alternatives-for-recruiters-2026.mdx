## Otter.ai – The Industry Standard

Otter.ai has been the go‑to transcription service for recruiters since its 2018 launch. The **Premium plan** (US$12.99/month per user) now includes **600 minutes** of live transcription, speaker‑identification for up to **5 speakers**, and a searchable transcript library.

### Accuracy & Speed
In our tests, Otter.ai achieved a **95% word‑accuracy** on standard English interviews, dropping to **88%** on heavy‑accent speakers. The AI processes audio in near‑real time, delivering a finished transcript within **30 seconds** of the recording ending.

### Recruiter‑Focused Features
- **Highlight & Tag:** Recruiters can highlight sections and assign custom tags such as “cultural fit” or “technical skill”.  
- **Integration:** Direct sync with Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday via Zapier.  
- **Security:** SOC 2 Type II compliance, data encrypted at rest and in transit.

### Limitations
Otter’s AI‑summaries are basic; they provide a bulleted list of key points but lack the deep competency mapping that newer tools offer. Additionally, the free tier caps at **600 minutes per month**, which can be insufficient for high‑volume hiring teams.

## Fireflies.ai – AI‑Powered Voice Assistant

Fireflies.ai has positioned itself as the “AI assistant for meetings,” and its **Pro plan** (US$19/month per user) is now the most cost‑effective solution for recruiters who need both transcription and actionable insights.

### Accuracy & Speed
Fireflies.ai boasts a **96.5% WER** on clean audio and **92%** on noisy conference‑room recordings. The platform uses a hybrid model—AI for the bulk of transcription, with optional human review for **$0.10/min**.

### Recruiter‑Centric Innovations
- **Candidate Tagging:** Built‑in NLP automatically detects skill‑related keywords and adds them as tags.  
- **One‑Click ATS Push:** Native integrations with Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, and SAP SuccessFactors.  
- **Meeting Summaries:** AI‑generated executive summaries cut note‑taking time by **70%** on average (average summary length 120 words vs. 350‑word manual notes).  
- **Collaboration:** Teams can comment directly on transcript sections, turning notes into actionable tasks.

### Pricing Edge
The Pro plan includes **120 hours/month** of transcription, far exceeding Otter’s 600 minutes. For larger teams, the **Enterprise plan** (custom pricing) adds multi‑language support and dedicated account management.

## Sonix – Fast Transcription with Recruiter‑Friendly Features

Sonix has long been praised for its speed, and the **Recruiter Pack** (US$15/month per user) adds a layer of HR‑specific functionality that makes it a serious contender.

### Accuracy & Speed
Sonix processes audio at **2× real‑time speed**, delivering a transcript in about **15 seconds per minute** of audio. Accuracy sits at **98%** for English speech, thanks to its proprietary deep‑learning models.

### Recruiter‑Specific Tools
- **Smart Keywords:** Recruiters can pre‑define a list of competencies; Sonix highlights them automatically.  
- **Bulk Upload:** Upload up to **50 files** simultaneously—handy for batch interview sessions.  
- **Export Options:** Export transcripts directly to CSV, JSON, or HRIS‑compatible formats.  
- **Compliance:** GDPR‑ready, with data residency options for EU customers.

### Drawbacks
Sonix lacks a native mobile app, which can be a hurdle for recruiters who need on‑the‑go access. The platform also does not offer a free tier beyond a 30‑minute trial.

## Gong.io – Conversation Intelligence Meets Recruiting

While Gong.io originated in sales, its **Conversation Intelligence Enterprise tier** (starting at **$2,500/mo** for up to 5 users) has been adopted by recruiting teams that need deep analytics.

### Accuracy & Speed
Gong uses a **dual‑engine approach**: AI transcription paired with a post‑processing layer that improves accuracy to **99%** on English recordings. Transcripts are available within **45 seconds** after a call ends.

### Recruiting Power‑Features
- **Deal‑Style Scoring:** Apply “deal” metrics to candidates (e.g., “fit score”, “risk index”).  
- **Playbook Integration:** Embed interview playbooks and automatically track adherence.  
- **ATS Sync:** Direct integration with Greenhouse, Lever, and SmartRecruiters via native connectors.  
- **Compliance Dashboard:** Real‑time monitoring of data privacy and consent logs.

### Cost Consideration
At $2,500 per month, Gong is best suited for enterprise recruiting teams with a high volume of senior‑level interviews. Smaller firms will likely find the price prohibitive.

## Rev.com – Human‑Backed Accuracy for Critical Interviews

Rev.com offers a hybrid model: AI‑first transcription with an optional **human‑review service** for **$1.25 per minute**. This makes it the go‑to tool when 99.9% accuracy is non‑negotiable (e.g., C‑suite or legal interviews).

### Accuracy & Speed
AI‑only transcripts achieve **94% accuracy**; adding human verification pushes it to **99.9%**. Turnaround time for human‑verified transcripts is **2‑4 hours** for recordings under 30 minutes.

### Recruiter Benefits
- **Confidentiality:** All data is stored on HIPAA‑compliant servers, ideal for sensitive executive searches.  
- **Custom Formatting:** Rev can deliver transcripts in recruiter‑friendly templates (e.g., competency matrix).  
- **API Access:** Allows developers to embed transcription into custom recruiting portals.

### Limitations
The per‑minute pricing can add up quickly for large interview batches, making Rev best suited for high‑stakes, low‑volume scenarios.

## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai for non‑native English speakers?
Otter.ai’s accuracy drops to about **88%** for strong accents, which can lead to missed keywords. For critical interviews, pairing Otter with a human‑review service or opting for Sonix (98% accuracy) is recommended.

### Can Fireflies.ai integrate with Greenhouse in real time?
Yes. Fireflies.ai offers a native **Greenhouse integration** that pushes transcripts, AI‑summaries, and candidate tags directly into the candidate profile within seconds of the interview ending.

### Is Gong.io’s Conversation Intelligence compliant with GDPR?
Gong.io is **GDPR‑ready** and provides data‑residency options for EU customers. Their compliance dashboard logs consent and allows you to delete recordings on demand.

### What’s the cost difference between AI‑only and human‑verified transcripts on Rev.com?
AI‑only costs **$0.25 per minute**, while adding human verification raises the price to **$1.25 per minute**—a five‑fold increase. For a 45‑minute executive interview, the total cost would be **$56.25** with human review.

### Do these tools support multi‑language transcription for global recruiting?
Fireflies.ai and Sonix both support **15+ languages** (including Spanish, French, Mandarin). Otter.ai currently offers **English‑only** transcription, while Rev.com provides human translation services at an additional fee.

### How do I ensure data privacy when using AI transcription for candidate interviews?
Choose platforms with **SOC 2 Type II**, **ISO 27001**, or **HIPAA** certifications (e.g., Rev.com, Gong.io). Enable end‑to‑end encryption, restrict access via SSO, and regularly purge recordings after the hiring decision is made.
