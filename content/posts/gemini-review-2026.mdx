Gemini is an AI model developed by Google that excels in natural [language](/posts/best-ai-claude-tools-translators-2026) processing and multimodal capabilities. It's designed to understand and respond to complex queries, making it a versatile tool for various applications.

## Features and Capabilities

Gemini offers a range of features that make it an attractive option for individuals and businesses. Some of its key features include:

* **Multimodal capabilities**: Gemini can understand and respond to queries that involve images, videos, and [audio](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) files.
* **Language support**: Gemini supports over 100 languages, making it a great option for users around the world.
* **Accuracy**: Gemini's accuracy is impressive, with a reported accuracy rate of 95%.
