## Introduction to AI Video Editing
AI video editing has transformed the way content creators produce and edit videos. With the help of artificial intelligence, editors can now automate tasks such as color correction, [audio](/posts/elevenlabs-voice-isolator-review-2026) ducking, and object detection, saving time and increasing [productivity](/posts/notion-ai-review-2026). In this article, we will explore [the best](/posts/ai-tools-for-freelancers-2026) AI tools for video editing in 2026, including Descript, Runway, and Luma.
