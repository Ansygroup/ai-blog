## Hidden Fees, Rate Limits, and Overages

Gamma’s pricing sheet is clean, but a few nuances can affect the bottom line:

1. **Rate Limits** – Pro accounts are capped at **200 RPS** (requests per second). Exceeding this triggers a **throttling delay** rather than a fee, but it can impact latency‑critical [apps](/posts/best-notebooklm-tools-2026). Enterprise [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026)s can negotiate higher limits (up to 5 k RPS).

2. **Overage Billing** – Once the token allowance is exhausted, the **$0.025 per 1 k token** overage rate applies. This is still cheaper than OpenAI’s $0.10 overage, but developers should monitor usage via Gamma’s dashboard to avoid surprise spikes.

3. **Data‑Retention Charges** – Storing logs longer than 30 days incurs $0.002 per 1 k tokens stored. For most workloads, this adds less than $1 per month, but high‑volume logging (e.g., audit trails) can accumulate.

4. **Currency Con[version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version)** – Gamma bills in USD only; international users may see a 1‑2 % variance due to bank conversion fees. OpenAI offers multi‑currency billing, which could be a deciding factor for non‑US teams.

Overall, the hidden fees are **transparent and predictable**, especially compared to competitors that hide overage costs behind “premium usage” tiers.
