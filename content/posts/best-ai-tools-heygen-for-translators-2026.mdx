## DeepL Pro: The Benchmark for Text Translation

DeepL has maintained its reputation as the **most accurate neural machine translation (NMT) engine** for European languages. The 2026 Pro tier adds **custom glossaries**, **API rate limits up to 10 M characters per day**, and **real‑time collaboration** for teams.

## Lilt AI: Adaptive Translation for Enterprises

Lilt’s standout feature is its **Adaptive Neural Machine Translation (Adaptive NMT)**, which learns from translator corrections in real time. The platform integrates directly with popular TMSs (e.g., XTM, Smartcat) and offers a **translation memory (TM) that updates instantly**.

During the test, Lilt reduced post‑editing effort by **30 %** after the first 5,000 corrected segments, thanks to its **Continuous Learning Loop**. The UI includes a **side‑by‑side view** of source, AI suggestion, and human edit, streamlining the workflow for large teams.

Pricing for Lilt’s “Business” tier is **$0.02 per word**, with a minimum monthly commitment of $500. While more expensive than DeepL per word, the **productivity gains** for enterprise‑scale projects often justify the cost.

## Phrase (Formerly Memsource) AI: Integrated Localization Platform

Phrase combines a full‑featured TMS with AI translation engines, including **Phrase AI**, a proprietary NMT model fine‑tuned on customer data. The platform supports **over 150 file formats**, automated quality checks, and a **single‑sign‑on (SSO) enterprise security** suite.

Our comparison highlighted Phrase’s ability to **auto‑detect video captions** in formats like SRT and VTT, then push them through its AI for instant multilingual subtitle generation. While its voice‑over capabilities are limited compared to HeyGen, Phrase excels at **end‑to‑end project management**.

The “Starter” plan starts at **$29 / user / month**, with unlimited projects and **10 GB of AI‑generated content**. For small translation agencies handling mixed media, Phrase offers the best balance of cost and functionality.

### Can I use HeyGen’s API to translate subtitles for a 10,000‑minute video library?

Yes. HeyGen’s batch API supports **up to 500 videos per request** and processes each video in parallel. For a 10,000‑minute library, you’d split the workload into 200 batches; at an average processing speed of **1.5 minutes per minute of video**, the entire library could be localized in roughly **150 hours** of compute time.

### Does DeepL Pro support terminology management for specialized fields like legal or medical?

DeepL Pro offers **Glossary and Custom Dictionary** features that let you enforce preferred translations for domain‑specific terms. In our legal document test, the glossary reduced inconsistent translations from **12 % to 2 %**.

### How does Lilt’s Adaptive NMT improve over time?

Lilt’s Adaptive NMT learns from each correction you make. After the first **5,000 words**, the engine’s suggestions aligned with the final human translation **30 %** more often, cutting post‑editing time dramatically. The learning is continuous, so larger projects see even greater efficiency gains.

### Is Phrase’s AI capable of generating voice‑overs, or do I need a separate tool?

Phrase AI focuses on **text translation and TM management**. It can generate subtitles but does not produce synthetic speech. For voice‑over, you’ll need a dedicated solution like HeyGen or a third‑party TTS provider.
