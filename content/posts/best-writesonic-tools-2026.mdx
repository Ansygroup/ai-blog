Yes. Every image generated includes a commercial‑use license, so you can publish them on websites, ads, and product listings without additional royalty fees.

It suggests keyword enrichments, adds schema markup, and rewrites low‑E‑E‑A‑T sections. In independent tests, optimized pages saw a 38 % traffic lift after 30 days compared to non‑optimized versions.

### Is there a free trial for Writesonic’s premium features?

### How does Writesonic compare to Jasper in terms of collaboration tools?
Jasper provides a dedicated team workspace with role‑based permissions, which is more robust for large agencies. Writesonic’s collaboration is improving with recent updates, but still lags behind Jasper’s enterprise‑grade features.

### Does Writesonic support non‑English languages?
Yes. Writesonic supports 27 languages, including Spanish, German, French, Japanese, and Hindi. The AI maintains tone and style consistency across languages, though performance is strongest in English.
