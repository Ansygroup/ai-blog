## What Is HeyGen Free Tier Limits 2026?

HeyGen is an AI‑driven video creation platform that lets users generate talking‑head videos, product demos, and social clips by typing simple prompts. In 2026, the company continues to offer a **free tier** aimed at hobbyists, educators, and early‑stage startups. The free tier’s primary constraints are **monthly video minutes**, **avatar selection**, **resolution**, **watermark enforcement**, and **export formats**.

Specifically, HeyGen allows **up to 10 minutes of video per calendar month**, measured in cumulative runtime across all projects. Users can pick from **five pre‑built AI avatars** (e.g., “Emma”, “Liam”, “Sofia”, “Kai”, “Ava”) and generate **up to 5 avatar renders** before the limit resets. All exported videos are **watermarked** with the HeyGen logo and limited to **720p (1280 × 720)** resolution. Audio can be uploaded or generated via the built‑in text‑to‑speech engine, but only **English, Spanish, and Mandarin** are supported on the free plan.

These limits are designed to showcase HeyGen’s core capabilities while nudging power users toward paid subscriptions. Understanding each restriction helps you decide whether the free tier can sustain a regular content cadence or if you’ll need to budget for a paid plan.

The most visible restriction is the **10‑minute monthly video cap**. In practice, this means you can produce:

If you exceed the limit, the platform blocks further rendering until the next month’s reset. Unlike a rolling 30‑day window, the reset occurs on the **first calendar day**, which can cause a “burst” of available credits at month‑start and a “dry spell” toward month‑end.

The free tier also limits **avatar renders** to five per month. Each render counts when you generate a video using a specific AI avatar, even if you reuse the same script. For example, creating a 30‑second video with “Emma” consumes one render; switching to “Liam” for the next video consumes another. Once you hit five renders, you must wait for the next reset or upgrade.

**Resolution** is capped at **720p**, which is acceptable for most social platforms (Instagram, TikTok) but falls short for YouTube or professional presentations that demand 1080p or higher. The watermark—a semi‑transparent HeyGen logo in the lower‑right corner—cannot be removed without a paid subscription.

## Export Options and Watermark Policies on the Free Plan

HeyGen’s free tier supports **MP4 export** only. Advanced formats such as MOV, GIF, or transparent‑background videos are locked behind the $19 “Starter” plan. The mandatory watermark appears on **every frame**, making the video look less polished for client‑facing work. However, the watermark is relatively subtle (≈ 5 % opacity) and does not obstruct subtitles or on‑screen text.

If you attempt to download a video without the watermark, the platform returns an error code **ERR‑WATERMARK‑FREE** and prompts you to upgrade. This strict enforcement is consistent across all export options, reinforcing HeyGen’s “freemium” model: you get a functional video, but branding removal is a paid feature.

## Limitations and Workarounds: Getting the Most Out of Free Credits

While the free tier’s constraints are clear, savvy users can stretch them:

- **Batch Scripts:** Combine multiple short scripts into a single 10‑minute video to reduce the number of renders consumed.
- **Watermark Masking:** Overlay your own logo in post‑production (e.g., using DaVinci Resolve) to hide the HeyGen watermark without violating terms.
- **Avatar Rotation:** Plan a content calendar that reuses the same avatar for multiple videos, conserving the five‑render limit.
- **Resolution Upscaling:** Use AI upscalers (e.g., Topaz Video Enhance AI) to convert 720p exports to 1080p, though quality gains are modest.

These tactics can keep you productive on the free plan for up to **three months** before the need for a paid upgrade becomes evident.

## FAQ

### How many minutes can I export per month on HeyGen’s free tier in 2026?
You can export up to **10 minutes of video** each calendar month. The limit resets on the first day of the month.

### Is the watermark removable on the free plan?
No. The HeyGen watermark is **mandatory** on all free‑tier exports. Removing it requires upgrading to at least the **Starter** ($19/mo) plan.

### Can I use HeyGen’s free tier for commercial projects?
Technically you can, but the watermark and limited resolution make it unsuitable for most client‑facing work. For professional use, a paid plan is recommended.

### How does HeyGen’s free avatar selection compare to Synthesia’s free plan?
HeyGen offers **five free avatars** (Emma, Liam, Sofia, Kai, Ava) while Synthesia provides **only one** (Sarah) on its free tier, giving HeyGen a clear advantage in variety.

### Does HeyGen provide an API on the free tier?
No. API access is reserved for **paid plans** (Starter and above). Free users must generate videos manually through the web UI.

### What happens if I exceed the 5 avatar render limit?
The platform blocks further avatar‑based renders until the next month’s reset. You can still edit existing videos but cannot create new ones with a free avatar.
