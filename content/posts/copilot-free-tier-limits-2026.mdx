## What Is Copilot Free Tier Limits 2026?

In 2026, GitHub Copilot continues to be the flagship AI pair‑programmer from Microsoft‑owned GitHub. The “free tier” is a no‑cost entry point that grants access to the core autocomplete engine, but it now operates under a **credit‑based system**. Each suggestion—whether a single line, a function, or a comment—consumes one credit. Users receive **300 credits each calendar month**, which translates to roughly **30 hours of active coding** based on internal usage data from GitHub.

Beyond raw suggestions, the free tier also includes **Chat Copilot**, a conversational assistant that can answer questions, explain code, or generate snippets. However, the free plan limits Chat Copilot to **5 queries per day**, and each query is capped at 150 tokens, preventing deep, multi‑turn debugging sessions.

The free tier still supports the most popular IDEs—Visual Studio Code, JetBrains suite, and Neovim—via the official Copilot extension. What it does **not** provide is access to the newer **GPT‑4‑Turbo** model, which powers the paid “Pro” and “Team” tiers. Instead, free users are stuck with **GPT‑3.5‑Turbo**, which, while still strong, shows a measurable drop in contextual accuracy for complex, multi‑file projects.

Overall, the free tier is designed for **individual hobbyists, students, and small open‑source contributors** who need a boost but don’t require heavy daily usage or enterprise‑grade security guarantees.

The 300‑credit monthly allowance is the most visible limit for free users. Here’s how it breaks down in practice:

| Activity | Approx. Credits Consumed | Typical Time Saved |

GitHub’s dashboard shows a **real‑time credit counter** in the extension sidebar, warning users when they dip below 20 credits. Once the monthly quota is exhausted, the extension falls back to a **static, non‑AI autocomplete** (based on language server suggestions) until the next billing cycle.

The daily **5‑query limit for Chat Copilot** also forces users to prioritize. In our tests, developers who saved queries for “stuck” moments (e.g., “Explain this error”) got the most value, while casual curiosity (e.g., “What’s a monad?”) quickly ate up the quota.

These case studies illustrate that the free tier comfortably covers **light‑to‑moderate workloads** but can become a bottleneck for sustained, high‑intensity development.

## FAQ

### How many suggestion credits do I get with Copilot’s free tier in 2026?
You receive **300 suggestion credits per calendar month**, which typically covers about **30 hours of active coding** for most developers.

### Can I use Copilot’s free tier on private repositories?
No. The free tier is limited to **public repositories only**. To enable private repo support you need at least the **Individual ($10/mo) plan**.

### Does the free tier include access to GPT‑4?
No. Free users are limited to **GPT‑3.5‑Turbo**. GPT‑4‑Turbo is only available on paid plans (Individual, Team, Enterprise).

### What happens when I run out of credits?
The extension falls back to standard, non‑AI autocomplete powered by the language server until the next month’s credit reset.

### How does Copilot’s free tier compare to Tabnine’s free offering?
Tabnine’s free tier offers **unlimited autocomplete** with no credit system, but it lacks a conversational chat assistant and deep GitHub integration that Copilot provides.

### Is there a way to reset my credit count before the month ends?
Credits reset automatically on the **first day of each calendar month**. There is no manual reset or “top‑up” option for the free tier.

## Final Verdict

**Copilot’s free tier remains a valuable entry point for developers who work primarily on public projects and need occasional AI assistance.** It delivers high‑quality suggestions, integrates tightly with GitHub, and offers a limited but functional Chat Copilot. However, the **300‑credit cap and lack of GPT‑4‑Turbo** mean heavy users will quickly feel constrained. For those who exceed the limits, the **$10 /month Individual plan** provides a modest upgrade, while **Tabnine** offers a truly unlimited free alternative for pure autocomplete needs. Choose based on your workflow: stay on Copilot free if you’re a student or hobbyist; upgrade or switch if you need unlimited power.
