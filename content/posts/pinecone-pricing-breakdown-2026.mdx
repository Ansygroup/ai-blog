## Core Tier Pricing: Free & Starter

The **Free tier** is [design](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026)ed for developers [testing](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) concepts. It includes:

| Feature | Limit | Cost |
|---------|-------|------|
| Queries | 1 M per month | Free |
| Writes (upserts) | 500 K per month | Free |
| Storage | 2 GB total (vector + metadata) | Free |
| Replicas | Single‑node, no SLA | Free |
| Support | Community forum | Free |

While generous for sandbox projects, the Free tier caps **vector dimensions** at 512 and **metadata fields** at 10, which can be restrictive for large [language](/posts/gamma-vs-chatgpt-2026) model (LLM) embeddings that often exceed 1 024 dimensions. Exceeding any limit triggers an [automatic](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) upgrade prompt.

The **Starter tier** (formerly “Growth Lite”) costs **$49 per month** and raises [limits](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) to 5 M queries, 10 GB storage, and 2 GB of metadata. It also adds a 99.9 % uptime SLA and [email](/posts/ai-driven-email-marketing-automation-tools-review) support. For a small startup that runs a chatbot with 200 K monthly queries, the Starter tier translates to $4.80 in query fees (well under the flat $49) plus $3 for storage, making the flat rate a modest over‑pay for predictable budgeting.

## Growth Tier Pricing: Standard & Pro

The **Standard tier** is the [work](/posts/loom-limitations-2026-full-review-alternatives)horse for production workloads. Its pricing is usage‑based, which aligns cost with demand:

| Metric | Rate (2026) |
|--------|-------------|
| Queries | $0.12 per 1 M |
| Writes (upserts) | $0.08 per 1 M |
| Storage (vector data) | $0.30 per GB‑month |
| Metadata indexing | $0.10 per GB‑month |
| High‑throughput replica | $0.02 per GB‑hour |

A realistic scenario: 10 TB of vectors (≈10 000 GB) + 150 M queries per month yields:

- Storage: 10 000 GB × $0.30 = **$3,000**
- Queries: 150 M ÷ 1 M × $0.12 = **$18**
- Writes: 20 M upserts ÷ 1 M × $0.08 = **$1.60**
- Metadata (2 TB) = 2 000 GB × $0.10 = **$200**
- Optional replica (1 replica, 24 h) = 10 000 GB × $0.02 × 24 = **$4,800** (if [need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026)ed for ultra‑low latency)

Total ≈ **$7,020** per month. The tier includes auto‑scaling, built‑in [monitoring](/posts/top-ai-powered-compliance-platforms-finance-2026), and a 99.95 % SLA.

The **Pro tier** adds dedicated VPC peering and priority support for **$0.02 per GB‑hour** on top of Standard rates, plus a $0.005 per 1 M query discount for committed annual spend over $10,000. Enterprises that need isolated [network](/posts/ai-linkedin-connection-outreach-automation-tutorial-2026) connectivity typically adopt Pro to meet compliance requirements.

## FAQ

### How is Pinecone’s query cost calculated?

Pinecone bills queries in increments of one million. The Standard tier charges $0.12 per 1 M queries, while Enterprise contracts can negotiate as low as $0.05 per 1 M. The count includes both read‑only similarity searches and hybrid (vector + keyword) queries, each billed at the same rate unless you enable the hybrid add‑on, which adds $0.07 per 1 M hybrid queries.

### Does Pinecone charge [for data](/posts/best-kaggle-tools-2026) transfer out of the [service](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026)?

Outbound data transfer is included in the base tier pricing up to 5 TB per month. Beyond that, Pinecone applies a $0.09 per GB egress fee, similar to major [cloud](/posts/pinecone-setup-guide-2026) providers. This is important for batch export jobs or when feeding vectors into downstream analytics pipelines.

### Can I switch tiers mid‑month without penalty?

Yes. Pinecone allows tier changes at any time. The platform prorates usage based on the exact day of the switch, so moving from Free to Standard on the 15th will only charge Standard rates for the remaining half of the month. However, committed Enterprise contracts require a 30‑day notice for downgrades.

### What happens if I exceed my storage limit?

If you surpass the storage quota for your tier, Pinecone automatically moves you into the next pricing bracket and charges the overage at the higher per‑GB rate (e.g., $0.45 per GB‑month for Standard over 10 TB). You receive a real‑time alert via email and the dashboard, allowing you to either add more storage or negotiate a custom plan.

### Is there a discount for long‑term commitments?

Enterprise [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026)s who sign 12‑month or 36‑month contracts receive volume‑based discounts ranging from 10 % to 30 % on query and storage rates. Pinecone also offers a “commit‑and‑save” program for the Standard tier: pre‑pay for 6 months and lock the query price at $0.10 per 1 M queries.

### How does Pinecone handle data durability and backups?

All vectors are stored with **multi‑zone replication** by default, providing 99.95 % durability. On‑demand backups can be exported at $0.15 per GB, and [automated](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) nightly snapshots are included at no extra cost for Standard and higher tiers. Restores are performed within minutes via the Pinecone console or CLI.
