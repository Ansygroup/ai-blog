## FAQ

### How does Gemini ensure HIPAA compliance?

Gemini runs on Google Cloud’s Health‑Grade infrastructure, which offers encrypted data at rest and in transit, audit logging, and Business Associate Agreements (BAA). All models process PHI inside Confidential Computing VMs, preventing unauthorized access.

### Can Gemini integrate with my existing EHR without a developer team?

Yes. Gemini provides out‑of‑the‑box FHIR connectors for Epic, Cerner, and Allscripts. Most installations complete within **2–4 weeks** with minimal IT involvement, thanks to pre‑configured OAuth scopes and API templates.

### What languages does Gemini Clinical Docs support besides English?

The current release supports **Spanish, Mandarin, Hindi, and French** with accuracy rates above 94 % for each language. Additional languages are slated for 2027 release.

### Is there a free trial or pilot program available?

Google offers a **30‑day free trial** for Gemini Clinical Docs and Gemini Vision, including up to 1,000 patient interactions or 1,000 scans. Enterprise customers can request a custom sandbox environment for up to **90 days**.

### How does Gemini handle updates to medical terminology or new CPT codes?

Gemini’s underlying LLM receives quarterly updates from Google’s medical knowledge graph. Users can also upload custom vocabularies via the admin console, which are incorporated into the model within **24 hours**.
