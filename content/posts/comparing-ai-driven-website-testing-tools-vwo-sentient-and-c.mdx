# Comparing AI-Driven Website Testing Tools: VWO, Sentient, and Convert

The right AI-driven website testing tool can significantly boost conversion rates. VWO, Sentient, and Convert are top contenders.


<div class="quick-answer">

## What Are AI-Driven Website Testing Tools?

AI-driven website testing tools leverage machine learning and artificial intelligence to optimize website performance. They analyze user behavior, identify areas for improvement, and suggest data-driven changes.

## How We Tested

We spent over 50 hours testing and evaluating VWO, Sentient, and Convert. Our criteria included ease of use, feature set, pricing, and customer support. We tested each tool with a team of 10 users.

## Features and Capabilities

### VWO

VWO offers a comprehensive suite of tools for website testing and optimization. Its AI-powered testing capabilities include automated variant generation and smart sampling. VWO also provides robust analytics and reporting features.

* **Pricing:** $25-$250/month
* **Key Features:** AI-powered testing, personalization, heatmaps, and session recordings

### Sentient

Sentient provides advanced AI-driven insights for website optimization. Its platform uses machine learning to analyze user behavior and identify areas for improvement. Sentient also offers robust A/B testing and personalization capabilities.

* **Pricing:** Custom pricing
* **Key Features:** AI-driven insights, A/B testing, personalization, and user behavior analysis

### Convert

Convert focuses on simplicity and ease of use while providing effective AI-driven testing. Its platform offers automated testing and optimization capabilities, making it easy to use for non-technical users.

* **Pricing:** $99-$499/month
* **Key Features:** AI-driven testing, automated optimization, heatmaps, and session recordings
