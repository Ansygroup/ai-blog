## What Is Synthesia Accuracy Test 2026?

An “accuracy test” in the con[text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review) of AI video [generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) measures how closely the synthetic output matches human‑level expectations. For Synthesia, this involves three core dimensions:

1. **Text‑to‑speech (TTS) fidelity** – how accu[rate](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026)ly the spoken words match the input [script](/posts/ai-tutorial-youtube-shorts-scripts-thumbnails-2026), measured in word‑error rate (WER).  
2. **Lip‑sync precision** – the temporal alignment between [audio](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) phonemes and avatar mouth movements, expressed in milliseconds of deviation.  
3. **Visual realism** – [subject](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026)ive scoring of avatar texture, lighting, and motion smoothness in a blind [user](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings) study.

In 2026, the industry has standardized these metrics thanks to the **AI Video Benchmark (AIVB) v3**, which aggregates over 10,000 test [cases](/posts/notion-ai-vs-zapier-2026) across languages, accents, and lighting conditions. Synthesia’s latest [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial), **Synthesia‑V5**, claims “near‑human” performance, but we needed hard numbers to verify that claim.
