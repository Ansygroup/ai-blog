## What Is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant developed by Anthropic, designed to handle a wide range of tasks. With a 100,000-token context window, Claude can process and respond to complex queries. It supports multiple file formats, including PDFs, Word documents, and text files.
![What Is Perplexity?](/images/illustrations/claude-vs-perplexity-illo1.svg)
## What Is Perplexity?

Perplexity is an AI-powered [search](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) engine and chatbot that excels in conversational AI. Its strength lies in its ability to engage in natural-sounding dialogues and provide accurate information through its internet search capabilities.

## Features and Capabilities

Claude and Perplexity have distinct [feature](/posts/copy-ai-vs-chatgpt-2026) sets. Claude offers:

* A 100,000-token context window
* Support for multiple file formats
* Advanced [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) analysis capabilities
* Strong [content](/posts/ai-powered-content-gap-analysis-niche-blog-topics-2026) [generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) features

Perplexity, on the other hand, excels in:

* Conversational AI
* Internet search capabilities
* Providing accurate and up-to-date information
