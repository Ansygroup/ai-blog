## What Is Content Localization?

Content localization is the process of adapting content to meet the linguistic, cultural, and regional preferences of target markets. This includes translating text, images, and videos to ensure that the content resonates with local audiences. Effective content localization is crucial for businesses operating in multiple markets, as it helps to build trust, increase engagement, and ultimately drive sales.
