### How does LanguageTool’s custom rule builder work for terminology?

You can create regex‑based rules that flag or replace specific terms. For example, a rule `/\bcloud computing\b/i → "computación en la nube"` will [automatic](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)ally highlight any non‑preferred term in Spanish drafts.
