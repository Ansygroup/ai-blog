## FAQ

### What does “cursor” mean in AI podcast tools?

“Cursor” refers to AI that operates at the point of interaction—whether you’re typing a script, moving the playhead, or clicking a button. The AI reacts instantly, offering suggestions, generating text, or cleaning audio without requiring you to leave the editing interface.

### Can AI voice cloning replace a human host?

AI voice cloning (e.g., Descript Overdub) can fill gaps, correct mistakes, or generate short inserts, but it struggles with spontaneous humor and emotional nuance. Most podcasters use it as a **supplement**, not a replacement, to maintain authenticity.

### How accurate is AI transcription for multi‑speaker podcasts?

Otter.ai’s live transcription achieves **97 % word‑level accuracy** for up to four simultaneous speakers. Accuracy drops to ~93 % when more than six speakers are present, so a manual review is still advisable for complex panels.

### Is Cleanvoice AI safe for copyrighted music in the background?

Cleanvoice AI focuses on speech‑related noise removal and does **not** alter music tracks. It safely isolates voice from background music, preserving copyright‑protected audio. However, it won’t remove copyrighted music entirely—use a dedicated music‑clearing service for that.

### Do these tools support languages other than English?

Cursor AI Pro and Cleanvoice AI Professional support **Spanish, French, German, and Mandarin** (beta). Descript Overdub currently offers voice cloning in **English, Spanish, and Portuguese**. Otter.ai provides transcription in **over 12 languages**, though accuracy varies by language.

### How do I integrate these AI tools into my existing workflow?

All four tools provide **RESTful APIs** and native plugins for popular DAWs (Audacity, Reaper) and podcast platforms (Anchor, Libsyn). You can set up a Zapier or Make.com automation that triggers Cursor AI after a recording finishes, passes the raw file to Cleanvoice AI, then sends the cleaned audio to Descript for Overdub and final export.
