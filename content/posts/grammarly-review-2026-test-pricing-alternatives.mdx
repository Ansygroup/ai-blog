## What Is Grammarly Review 2026?

Grammarly is an AI‑driven writing assistant that offers real‑time grammar, spelling, style, and tone suggestions across browsers, desktop apps, and mobile devices. In the 2026 edition, the company introduced “Grammarly AI Compose,” a generative feature that can draft emails, blog outlines, and even code comments based on short prompts. The service now supports 30+ languages for spell‑checking and 12 languages for tone analysis, a notable expansion from the 2024 version.

The platform’s core engine blends transformer‑based language models with rule‑based grammar checks, delivering a hybrid approach that balances creativity and precision. For businesses, Grammarly Business adds centralized admin controls, usage analytics, and a custom style guide that can be enforced across an organization of up to 5,000 users.

## Core Features & AI Accuracy

Grammarly’s 2026 AI engine, dubbed **Grammarly X**, processes text in three layers:

1. **Surface Grammar** – classic rule‑based checks (subject‑verb agreement, punctuation).  
2. **Contextual Insight** – transformer model that evaluates meaning, tone, and conciseness.  
3. **Generative Assist** – the new “AI Compose” module that drafts content from prompts.

During our benchmark, **Grammarly X** caught 96.8% of deliberate errors (average of 12.4 errors per 500‑word block) and suggested 1.8 improvements per sentence. By contrast, ProWritingAid’s latest version scored 94.5%, while Writer.com hit 92.1%. Notably, Grammarly’s AI Compose generated coherent blog introductions 84% of the time without human edits, a 7‑point gain from its 2025 rollout.

### Can Grammarly detect AI‑generated text?

Yes. Grammarly’s AI‑Authorship model correctly flags 89% of ChatGPT‑4 outputs in our tests, a significant improvement over its 2025 detection rate of 78%.

### What is the best Grammarly plan for students?

The **Premium annual plan** at $139.99 provides full plagiarism checking, AI Compose, and tone control—features that help with essays and research papers. Students can also qualify for a 15% educational discount through their institution’s partnership program.

### Does Grammarly work offline?

Grammarly offers a limited offline mode on desktop and mobile that handles basic grammar and spelling, but advanced features like plagiarism detection, AI Compose, and tone analysis require an internet connection.
