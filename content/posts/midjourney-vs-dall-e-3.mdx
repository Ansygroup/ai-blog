## Pros and Cons
The following sections [highlight](/posts/using-ai-for-automated-twitch-stream-highlight-reel-creation) the pros and cons of Midjourney and DALL-E 3:

### Midjourney
Midjourney is easy to use and has a fast image [generation](/posts/how-to-use-midjourney-2026) speed. It supports various image styles and customization [options](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-translation-2026), and integrates with popular platforms like Discord and Slack. However, it has limited image resolution and text [prompt](/posts/how-to-write-better-chatgpt-prompts-2026) length, and batch processing is limited to 10 images at a [time](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026).

### DALL-E 3
DALL-E 3 generates high-quality images with high resolution and advanced customization options. It supports [long](/posts/how-to-use-ai-to-summarize-long-documents-2026) text [prompts and](/posts/how-to-use-chatgpt-for-seo) integrates with popular platforms like Adobe Creative Cloud and [Microsoft](/posts/microsoft-frontier-tuning-2026-custom-ai-models-rl) Office. However, it has a steeper learning curve, slower image [generation](/posts/how-to-use-midjourney-2026) speed, and is more expensive than Midjourney.
