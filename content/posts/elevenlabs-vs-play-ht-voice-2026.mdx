PlayHT is now PlayAI. The company's pricing page lives at the same play.ht address, but the title, the FAQ and the plan names all say PlayAI. If you are searching for a PlayHT comparison, you are comparing ElevenLabs against a product that changed its name, and its plan shape is different enough from ElevenLabs that the sticker prices do not line up. As of 27 November 2026, here is what each one sells, and what a thousand characters of speech costs on each.

ElevenLabs also has a sale you should know about. Its Black Friday and Cyber Monday offer runs until 1 December 2026 and takes up to 55% off annual plans. That affects the maths today more than any feature does.

## What ElevenLabs charges

ElevenLabs sells credits. Text to speech costs 1 credit per character on the website, and through the API the Turbo, Flash and v3 Conversational models cost 0.5.

| Plan | Monthly price | Credits per month | Annual price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Limited | $0 |
| Starter | $1 first month, then $6 | Not shown on the page we fetched | $60 a year |
| Creator | $11 first month, then $22 | 121,000 | $220 a year |
| Pro | $99 | 600,000 | $990 a year |
| Scale | $299 | 1,800,000, 3 seats | $2,990 a year |
| Business | $990 | 6,000,000, 10 seats | $9,900 a year |

Our arithmetic: Creator at $22 for 121,000 credits works out to about $0.18 per 1,000 characters, and about $0.15 on the $220 annual price. Pro at $99 for 600,000 credits is about $0.17 per 1,000 characters on monthly billing.

Three details are worth knowing. Unused credits roll over for up to two months on paid plans, capped at 2x your quota. Top-ups expire 12 months after purchase. And you get 2 free regenerations within 2 hours if the text, voice and model stay the same, but that does not apply to the API.

## What PlayAI charges

PlayAI sells characters, not credits.

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 1,000 characters, 1 instant voice clone |
| Creator | $31.20 a month, $374.40 billed annually | 3 million characters a year, 10 instant voice clones, high fidelity clones, API |
| Unlimited | $49 a month on the limited time deal, $588 billed annually | Unlimited characters, unlimited instant clones, 3 high fidelity clones |
| Enterprise | Custom | Team access, SSO, commercial and resale rights |

Our arithmetic: Creator's 3 million characters for $374.40 is about $0.12 per 1,000 characters, cheaper than ElevenLabs at the same tier. But note the unit. That is characters per year, so you cannot burn 3 million in January and have none in February without checking how the allowance is metered.

The "unlimited" plan is not unlimited. PlayAI's FAQ states a fair usage limit of 2.5 million characters a month and 30 million a year. That is still a lot of narration, and few people will hit it, but anyone planning an audiobook factory should read that line before buying.

## The refund trap

This is the detail marketing pages hide. PlayAI says all refund requests must be made within 24 hours of purchase, and the character usage must be below 5,000 characters. It does not offer refunds for change of mind or dissatisfaction. If you buy an annual plan, run a long test and then decide the voices are wrong, you may not get the money back. ElevenLabs has a free plan that covers a wide range of products, so test there first and pay later.

## Where each one fails

ElevenLabs fails on cost per character at the entry tier and on complexity. Dubbing alone ranges from 2,000 to 13,500 credits a minute depending on the product, so one video can drain a Creator plan. Credits also reset each billing cycle, and the rollover has a cap.

PlayAI fails on clarity and on trust signals. A rebrand inside the same domain makes old reviews hard to match to the current product, and the limited time deal price on Unlimited could change. The refund rule leaves little room for mistakes.

## Who should skip which

Skip ElevenLabs if all you need is plain narration at huge volume and you want a flat price. Skip PlayAI if you need dubbing, music or sound effects, or want to test for free before buying. ## Which should you pick

For most people, start on ElevenLabs Free, then take Starter at $1 for the first month if you need more. For a high-volume narration pipeline where cost per character is the only number that matters, price PlayAI's Creator and Unlimited plans against your real monthly character count, and read the fair usage line twice before you pay for a year.

### Do either ElevenLabs or PlayAI charge extra for premium voices or custom voice cloning?

Both services also have over‑age fees if you exceed the monthly hour allowance, so it’s important to monitor usage through their dashboards to avoid unexpected charges.
