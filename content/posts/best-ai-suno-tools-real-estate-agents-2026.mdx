## Resemble AI – Real‑Time API for Custom Workflows

Resemble AI shines for tech‑savvy agents who build custom pipelines. Its **API‑first approach** lets you generate audio on the fly from a CRM trigger (e.g., new listing added). Pricing starts at **$35/mo** for 5,000 characters per month, with a **pay‑as‑you‑go** add‑on.

Key advantages:

- **Dynamic voice modulation** (tone, speed) via API parameters.

The downside is the **lack of a visual dashboard**, making it less approachable for non‑technical users.

## FAQ

### How accurate is Suno’s voice for real‑estate jargon?

Suno’s “Professional” voice handles industry terms like “walk‑through”, “escrow”, and “HOA” with a MOS of **4.6/5**. You can add custom pronunciation dictionaries in the settings for rare acronyms.

### Can I integrate Suno audio directly into Zillow or Realtor.com listings?

While Zillow doesn’t support native audio embeds, you can upload the Suno‑generated MP3 to a public URL (e.g., your website) and add the link in the MLS description field. Realtor.com now allows a “media URL” field, where Suno audio works seamlessly.

### Is there a limit to the number of speakers I can use in Suno Studio?

The Unlimited Voice plan provides **12 regional accents**. If you need a truly custom voice, you’ll need to upgrade to the Enterprise tier, which offers bespoke voice cloning at a negotiated price.

### How does Suno compare to Descript Overdub in terms of SEO impact?

Suno produces plain audio files, which need separate transcripts for SEO. Descript automatically generates a searchable transcript, boosting keyword density on the page. However, Suno’s batch export can be paired with AI‑generated blog copy (e.g., using ChatGPT) to achieve similar SEO results.

### What is the learning curve for non‑technical agents?

Suno’s UI is designed for marketers, with a **5‑minute onboarding video** and step‑by‑step wizards. Most agents can start generating audio after **one training session**. Resemble AI, by contrast, requires basic knowledge of REST APIs.

## Final Verdict

**Suno AI Studio is the clear winner for real‑estate agents in 2026**, delivering unlimited, high‑quality voice generation at $29/mo, batch processing, and native CRM integrations that shave hours off daily workflows. **Descript Overdub** remains a solid runner‑up for agents who need video editing and built‑in transcription, but its single‑voice limitation and higher time cost make Suno the more efficient choice for pure audio needs.
