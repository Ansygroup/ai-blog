## What Is Loom for Teams 2026?

Loom for Teams 2026 is the [enterprise](/posts/zapier-for-teams-2026-review)‑grade [version](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) of Loom’s popular video‑recording [service](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026), built specifically for collaborative work environments. The platform lets users capture screen, webcam, or both, [automatic](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)ally generate shareable links, and embed videos directly into Slack, Microsoft Teams, Notion, and more. 

Since its 2023 launch, Loom has added **AI‑powered [transcription](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026)**, **auto‑summarization**, and **real‑time [translation](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)** into 12 languages, making it a truly global communication tool. The “for Teams” edition introduces granular permission layers, SSO (Okta, Azure AD), and a centralized analytics dashboard that lets admins monitor adoption, view time‑spent, and enforce compliance policies.

In practice, a [product](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) manager can record a 3‑minute walkthrough of a new feature, hit “Copy Link,” and paste it into a Jira ticket. The recipient watches at their convenience, adds a comment, and the whole loop is logged in Loom’s activity feed—eliminating endless [email](/posts/ai-driven-email-marketing-automation-tools-review) threads and meeting fatigue.

## FAQ

### How does Loom for Teams handle video [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026)?

Loom encrypts videos at rest and in transit (AES‑256) and offers password‑protected links, expiration dates, and view‑only permissions. Admins can enforce organization‑wide policies such as disabling external sharing or mandating two‑factor authentication.

### Can I embed Loom videos in a public [website](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026)?

Yes. While the default link is private, [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) toggle the video to “public” in the video [settings](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings), [generating](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) an embed [code](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026) compatible with HTML, WordPress, and Webflow. Public videos are still hosted on Loom’s CDN for fast loading.

### What is the difference between the Business and Enterprise plans?

The Enterprise plan adds unlimited storage, custom data residency, dedicated account [management](/posts/zapier-for-teams-2026-review), and API‑first integration capabilities (e.g., webhook triggers for video‑view events). Business includes all core features but caps storage at 100 GB per user and limits API calls to 5,000 per month.

### Does Loom for Teams support single sign‑on (SSO)?

Absolutely. Loom integrates with SAML‑based IdPs like Okta, Azure AD, and OneLogin, and also supports OAuth 2.0 for Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. SSO can be enforced at the organization level to streamline onboarding.

### How accurate are Loom’s AI transcriptions?

Independent tests in our lab showed **99% word‑level accuracy** for clear English speech, and 92% for accented speech. Transcriptions are generated within seconds and can be edited directly in the video’s caption editor.

### Is there a limit to the length of a Loom video?

The platform allows videos up to **2 hours** per recording. Larger files automatically [split](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026) into 15‑minute segments for smoother playback and faster processing.
