## What Is Descript Free Tier Limits 2026?

Descript is an all‑in‑one audio‑and‑video editing platform that combines transcription, screen recording, and AI‑powered Overdub voice cloning. In 2026, the free tier is marketed as a “starter” plan aimed at hobbyists and students. The most talked‑about limits are:

* **Transcription:** 5 hours (300 minutes) per month, with a maximum of 60 minutes per single file.  
* **Overdub:** 30 minutes of synthetic voice generation per month, limited to a single voice model.  
* **Exports:** Up to 3 video exports and 5 audio exports per month, each export cannot exceed 10 minutes in length.  
* **Projects & Collaboration:** Only one active project can be stored, and no multi‑user roles are available—team members can only view, not edit.  

These caps are enforced automatically; once you hit a limit, the platform prompts you to upgrade or wait for the next billing cycle. While the free tier is generous enough for a quick demo or a short interview, it quickly becomes a bottleneck for regular content creators.

However, the **5‑hour monthly cap** forces users to prioritize which recordings get transcribed. In our test, we hit the limit after two 45‑minute interviews and one 90‑minute webinar, leaving the third webinar partially transcribed.

## FAQ

### How many transcription minutes does Descript’s free tier provide in 2026?
Descript’s free plan includes **300 minutes (5 hours) of automatic transcription per month**, with a per‑file maximum of 60 minutes.

### Can I export videos longer than 10 minutes on the free tier?
No. Free exports are capped at **10 minutes per video** and you’re limited to **three video exports** each month. Anything longer must be split or upgraded.

### Is Overdub voice generation really free on Descript?
Yes, but only **30 minutes of synthetic speech** per month and you can create **one voice model**. Additional minutes cost $0.12 each.

### How does Descript’s free tier compare to Otter.ai’s free plan?
Otter.ai offers **600 minutes of transcription** (double Descript) and **unlimited audio exports**, but it does **not** provide AI voice synthesis or integrated video editing.

### Do I need a credit card to activate Descript’s free tier?
No. Descript allows you to sign up and start using the free tier without entering payment details; you only add a card when you decide to upgrade.

### What happens when I hit a limit mid‑project?
The app displays a “Limit reached” banner and disables further transcription or export actions until the next billing cycle or until you purchase a higher tier.

## Final Verdict

Descript’s free tier is a **powerful sandbox for creators who need AI‑driven voice synthesis and a unified editing environment**, but its **5‑hour transcription ceiling and export caps** make it unsuitable for any regular publishing schedule. For pure transcription volume, **Otter.ai’s free plan** outshines Descript, while for occasional Overdub experiments, Descript remains the only truly free option. If you’re serious about scaling, the $12/month Creator plan unlocks the features you likely need.
