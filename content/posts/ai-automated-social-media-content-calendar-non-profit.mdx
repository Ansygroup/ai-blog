## Setting Up an AI-Powered Social Media Content Calendar

To set up an AI-powered social media content calendar, follow these steps:

1. Choose an AI-powered social media management tool that meets your non-profit's needs.
2. Connect your social media accounts to the tool.
3. Define your content strategy and goals.
4. Configure the tool's AI settings to generate content based on your strategy and goals.
5. Review and adjust the generated content calendar as needed.
