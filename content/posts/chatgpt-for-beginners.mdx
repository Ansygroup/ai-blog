## What Is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an AI-powered chatbot developed by OpenAI. It uses natural [language](/posts/best-ai-claude-tools-translators-2026) processing (NLP) to understand and respond to user input. 
ChatGPT can be used for a wide range of tasks, from answering questions and providing information to [generating](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) text and even creating content.
