## What Is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an AI-powered chatbot developed by [OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026). It uses natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing ([NLP](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content)) to understand and respond to [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026) input. 
ChatGPT can be used for a wide range of tasks, from answering questions and providing information to generating text and even creating [content](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation).
