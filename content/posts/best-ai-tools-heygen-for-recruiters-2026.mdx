## What Is HeyGen and Why It Matters for Recruiters?

HeyGen is an AI‑powered video [generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) platform that lets users create lifelike avatar videos from plain text. In recruitment, the platform is used to craft personalized outreach messages, employer‑branding snippets, and interview prep videos without a camera crew. For example, a recruiter can type “Hi [Candidate Name], we loved your experience at [Company] and think you’d thrive at [Our Company]” and instantly receive a 30‑second video featuring a professional avatar speaking the script.

The 2026 update adds multi‑[language](/posts/best-ai-claude-tools-translators-2026) support for 45 languages, real‑time facial expression mapping, and a built‑in analytics dashboard that tracks view‑through rates, click‑throughs, and sentiment. These features enable recruiters to A/B test different [scripts](/posts/ai-tutorial-youtube-shorts-scripts-thumbnails-2026), measure ROI, and iterate quickly—critical in today’s talent‑war environment.
