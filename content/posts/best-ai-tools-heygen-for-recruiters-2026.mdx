## What Is HeyGen and Why It Matters for Recruiters?

HeyGen is an AI‑powered video generation platform that lets users create lifelike avatar videos from plain text. In recruitment, the platform is used to craft personalized outreach messages, employer‑branding snippets, and interview prep videos without a camera crew. For example, a recruiter can type “Hi [Candidate Name], we loved your experience at [Company] and think you’d thrive at [Our Company]” and instantly receive a 30‑second video featuring a professional avatar speaking the script.

The 2026 update adds multi‑language support for 45 languages, real‑time facial expression mapping, and a built‑in analytics dashboard that tracks view‑through rates, click‑throughs, and sentiment. These features enable recruiters to A/B test different scripts, measure ROI, and iterate quickly—critical in today’s talent‑war environment.
