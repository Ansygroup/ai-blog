## Crafting Effective Claude Prompts

1. **Be specific**: Clearly define the task, provide relevant context, and specify the desired output. For example: "Summarize the key points of the attached PDF (1000 words) and provide a 2-paragraph overview."
2. **Use natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026)**: Write in a natural, conversational tone to help Claude [better](/posts/gpt-5-5-instant-2026-fewer-hallucinations) understand the context and intent.
3. **Provide examples**: Include relevant examples or anecdotes to illustrate the topic [and guide](/posts/chatgpt-mobile-app-review-2026) Claude's [response](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-cold-email-outreach-2026).

## Advanced Claude Prompt Techniques

1. **Role-playing**: Use prompts that simulate real-world scenarios, such as: "Act as a [marketing](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-influencer-marketing-campaigns) expert and provide a campaign proposal for [product]."
2. **Multi-step tasks**: Break down complex tasks into smaller, manageable steps, and use prompts like: "First, summarize the article. Then, provide a critical analysis of the author's arguments."
