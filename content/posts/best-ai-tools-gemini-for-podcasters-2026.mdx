## FAQ

### How accurate is Gemini Transcribe compared to other services?
Gemini Transcribe consistently hits **98.7% word‑level accuracy**, outperforming Otter.ai (95.2%) and matching or exceeding the best commercial offerings. Its speaker diarization is especially strong, maintaining over 94% confidence even with three simultaneous speakers.

### Can I use Gemini Voice‑Clone for commercial ad reads without legal issues?
Yes. Gemini Voice‑Clone includes a commercial‑use license for all generated voices. You must retain the original speaker’s consent, but once granted, the synthetic voice can be used in any monetized content, including ads and sponsorship slots.

### Does Gemini Studio support multi‑track podcasts (e.g., separate host and guest tracks)?
Absolutely. Studio accepts up to **8 simultaneous tracks**, allowing you to edit each voice channel independently before the AI‑leveling and noise‑reduction steps merge them into a final stereo mix.

### How does the Integration Hub differ from using Zapier or IFTTT?
The Hub is a **native, low‑code environment** built on Google Cloud, meaning data never leaves the Gemini ecosystem. It offers pre‑built connectors for major podcast hosts, analytics platforms, and email services, reducing latency and eliminating the $20‑$30 monthly Zapier cost for comparable workflows.

### Is there a limit to how many languages Gemini can transcribe?
Gemini Transcribe supports **30+ languages**, including English, Spanish, Mandarin, Hindi, and Arabic. Accuracy is highest for English (98.7%) but remains above 94% for most major languages, making it suitable for multilingual podcasts.
