## What Is Customer Journey Mapping in Financial Services?

Customer journey mapping is a visual representation of the customer's experience across multiple touchpoints and interactions with a financial institution. AI-powered customer journey mapping tools help banks and financial institutions to identify pain points, optimize processes, and improve customer satisfaction.
