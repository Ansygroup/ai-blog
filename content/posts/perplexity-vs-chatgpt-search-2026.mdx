OpenAI stopped selling web search as a separate product, and that single decision settles most of this comparison before either tool answers a query. ChatGPT's web search is switched on for Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts, and OpenAI's own help documentation says even people who are not signed in can use it. Perplexity's free tier exists, but the features most people mean when they say "Perplexity" sit behind a subscription that starts at $17 a month.

That does not make Perplexity the worse tool. It makes the two products answer different questions. ChatGPT search is a feature inside an assistant you may already pay for. Perplexity sells research as the product, and prices it that way.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free, everyday searching with citations | ChatGPT | Search is included on every plan including Free and signed-out use, subject to plan usage limits |
| Deep research reports on a budget | Perplexity | Deep research is part of Pro at $17 a month billed annually, and Pro also unlocks model selection |
| Local results and restaurant bookings | ChatGPT | Reservation search pulls live availability from OpenTable, Resy and Yelp |

| Team or managed workspace | ChatGPT | Business, Enterprise and Edu plans exist; Perplexity's consumer tiers do not map onto admin controls |
| Running multi-step agent tasks, not just reading | Perplexity | Computer is metered by credits on both Pro and Max |

## What free actually gets you

ChatGPT gives away the search itself. On a Free account you get web search with citations, source previews on desktop, and a View sources menu on every answer that used the web. The limit is volume: OpenAI states plainly that searches are subject to your plan's usage limits, and a hit limit is the most common reason the feature appears to break. The fix in their troubleshooting guide is to start a new chat and confirm you have not capped out.

Perplexity's free tier is a real product too, but the paid tiers change what the tool is. Pro adds deep research, the ability to select between models, memory, more usage, and what Perplexity calls expanded Computer access. Max goes further: 10,000 monthly credits, expert-level research, frontier models, the highest usage limits, and priority access to new features.

## The part that decides your results, not your bill

Both tools rewrite your question before they search, and the way they do it changes what you see.

OpenAI's help documentation is unusually candid here. ChatGPT sometimes sends your query to third-party search providers, typically rewriting it into one or more targeted queries first, then following up with narrower queries based on what comes back. It uses an approximate location inferred from your IP address and may share that general location with those providers. It does not share the IP address itself or your account details. If memory is on, saved facts can enter the rewritten query, which is why two accounts asking the same question can get different local results.

Perplexity's model is a smaller set of stronger models plus a browsing agent. On Pro you choose the model, which means the answer quality is partly your decision and partly your problem. That is a real difference in kind: ChatGPT hides the search plumbing, Perplexity hands you the controls.

## The booking feature with a plan-shaped hole

The most useful detail in either documentation set is one almost nobody reads. ChatGPT can check restaurant availability and reservations through OpenTable, Resy and Yelp. It is not available on every plan. OpenAI's documentation limits reservation search to eligible consumer plans using GPT-5.6 Sol or GPT-5.6 Luna, and states it is not available in ChatGPT Work or on Business, Enterprise or Edu plans.

So a team that upgrades to a business workspace for admin controls quietly loses a consumer feature, and an individual on Plus who switches models mid-conversation can lose it too. Perplexity has no equivalent, which is not a knock: it never promised one.

## Pricing in plain terms

Perplexity Pro is $17 a month when billed annually, and that price carries a $40 bonus in Computer credits as a limited-time offer. Max is $167 a month billed annually with a $350 bonus, roughly ten times the price of Pro for maximum usage and 10,000 monthly credits. Both tiers inherit the free plan, and neither is sold as a per-seat business product in the way OpenAI sells Business.

ChatGPT is sold as plans, not as a research subscription: Free, Go, Plus and Pro, with Pro starting at $100 a month and offering three usage tiers of its own. If you already pay for ChatGPT for coding or writing, the marginal cost of its search is zero. That is the whole argument, and it is a strong one.

## Where each one fails

ChatGPT search fails quietly. Citations can be incomplete, outdated or simply wrong, and OpenAI says so in its own documentation, telling users to open the cited source and check the publication date. Because search is bundled, there is no line item to look at when quality drops, and no lever except changing plans.

Perplexity fails on price shape. The jump from $17 to $167 is a tenfold step, and the metering unit on Computer is credits, which is the kind of unit that is easy to burn and hard to forecast. If your team standardises on Perplexity, budget for the Max tier, not the Pro tier.

## Who should skip which

Skip ChatGPT search if your work is research as a deliverable, with model choice and long reports, and you want the tool to be the product rather than a tab inside one. Skip Perplexity if you already pay for ChatGPT, search short queries with citations, or need a managed workspace with admin controls.

## Which should you pick

Start with ChatGPT search because it costs nothing extra and it is good enough for most lookups. Move to Perplexity Pro when you find yourself asking for reports rather than answers, and treat the $17 as a trial for the $167 tier rather than a destination. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ChatGPT web search free?

Yes. OpenAI lists web search on Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu plans, and says signed-out users can use it too. Individual searches count against your plan's usage limits.

### How much does Perplexity Pro cost?

Pro is $17 a month when billed annually and includes deep research, model selection and memory. Max is $167 a month billed annually and lists 10,000 monthly credits with frontier model access.

### Can ChatGPT book a restaurant?

It can check availability through OpenTable, Resy and Yelp on eligible consumer plans using GPT-5.6 Sol or GPT-5.6 Luna. OpenAI says the feature is not available in ChatGPT Work or on Business, Enterprise or Edu plans.

### Which gives better citations?

Both cite sources and both warn that citations can be incomplete or outdated. ChatGPT rewrites queries and sends them to partner search providers, while Perplexity lets Pro users pick the underlying model, so results differ by account and settings.
