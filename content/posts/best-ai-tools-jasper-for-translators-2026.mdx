## Feature Deep Dive: Prompt Templates for Localization

Prompt Templates are the secret sauce behind Jasper’s translator‑friendly workflow. 1. Preserve legal terminology (e.g., “Kündigungsfrist”) exactly as defined in the supplied glossary.
