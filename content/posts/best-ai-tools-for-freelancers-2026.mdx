## Top 5 AI Tools for Freelancers in 2026

### 1. Grammarly

Grammarly is a writing assistant that offers a range of features, including grammar and spell checking, plagiarism detection, and sentence structure suggestions. It's available as a browser extension, desktop app, and mobile app, making it easy to use on the go.

- Price: $11.66/month (basic plan)
- Features: grammar and spell checking, plagiarism detection, sentence structure suggestions
- User rating: 4.5/5

### 2. Lumen5

Lumen5 is a video creation tool that uses AI to turn blog posts and articles into engaging videos. It offers a range of templates, music tracks, and animation effects, making it easy to create professional-looking videos.

- Price: $19.99/month (basic plan)
- Features: video creation, animation effects, music tracks
- User rating: 4.5/5

### 3. Ahrefs

Ahrefs is an SEO tool that offers a range of features, including keyword research, backlink analysis, and content optimization suggestions. It's a must-have for any freelancer looking to improve their online presence.

- Price: $179/month (basic plan)
- Features: keyword research, backlink analysis, content optimization suggestions
- User rating: 4.5/5

### 4. Hemingway Editor

Hemingway Editor is a writing tool that simplifies complex sentences and offers suggestions for improvement. It's a great tool for freelancers who want to improve their writing skills and create clear, concise content.

- Price: free (web-based)
- Features: sentence simplification, grammar and spell checking
- User rating: 4.5/5

### 5. Canva

Canva is a design tool that offers a range of templates, design elements, and collaboration features. It's a great tool for freelancers who want to create professional-looking designs without needing extensive design experience.

- Price: $12.95/month (basic plan)
- Features: design templates, collaboration features
- User rating: 4.5/5
