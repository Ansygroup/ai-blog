## Top AI Writing Tools for Freelancers

Writing is a crucial aspect of freelancing, and AI tools can help you improve your writing skills and efficiency.

### 1. Grammarly Premium

Grammarly Premium is an AI-powered writing tool that offers comprehensive grammar, spell, and punctuation checks. It also provides suggestions for improving sentence structure, tone, and style.

* Price: $11.66/month
* Features: Grammar, spell, and punctuation checks, sentence structure suggestions, tone and style analysis
* Platform: Web, iOS, Android

### 2. ProWritingAid

ProWritingAid is another popular AI writing tool that offers grammar, spell, and punctuation checks, as well as suggestions for improving sentence structure, word choice, and style.

* Price: $20/month
* Features: Grammar, spell, and punctuation checks, sentence structure suggestions, word choice analysis
* Platform: Web, iOS, Android

### 3. Language Tool

Language Tool is a free AI-powered writing tool that offers grammar, spell, and punctuation checks, as well as suggestions for improving sentence structure and style.

* Price: Free
* Features: Grammar, spell, and punctuation checks, sentence structure suggestions, style analysis
* Platform: Web
