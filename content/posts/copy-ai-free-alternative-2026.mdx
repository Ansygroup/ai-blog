## Top Copy.ai Free Alternatives

### Writesonic

Writesonic offers a free [plan](/posts/cursor-pricing-breakdown-2026) with 10,000 words/month, making it an attractive Copy.ai free alternative. Key features include:
- 10,000 words/month on free plan
- 100+ AI [templates](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026)
- Support for 25+ languages
- Integration with WordPress and Google Docs

Writesonic's [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) start at $16/month for 200,000 words.

### Rytr

Rytr provides a free plan with 5,000 words/month and a user-friendly [interface](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-real-estate-agents-2026-top-picks). Key features include:
- 5,000 words/month on free plan
- 40+ AI templates
- Support for 30+ languages
- Chrome extension for easy access

Rytr's paid plans start at $9/month for 20,000 words.

### Jasper AI

Jasper AI offers a 7-day free trial, but no permanent free plan. Key features include:
- 7-day free trial
- 50+ AI templates
- Support for 30+ languages
- Integration with Surfer SEO

Jasper AI's paid plans start at $29/month for 20,000 words.
