## Top Copy.ai Free Alternatives

### [Writesonic](/posts/chatgpt-vs-writesonic)

Writesonic offers a free plan with 10,000 words/month, making it an attractive Copy.ai free alternative. Key features include:
- 10,000 words/month on free plan
- 100+ AI templates
- Support for 25+ languages
- Integration with WordPress and [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Docs

Writesonic's paid [plans](/posts/jasper-pricing-explained) start at $16/month for 200,000 words.

### [Rytr](/posts/rytr-review-2026)

Rytr provides a free plan with 5,000 words/month and a user-friendly interface. Key features include:
- 5,000 words/month on free plan
- 40+ AI templates
- Support for 30+ languages
- Chrome extension for easy access

Rytr's paid plans start at $9/month for 20,000 words.

### [Jasper](/posts/best-jasper-desktop-app-review-2026) AI

Jasper AI offers a 7-day free trial, but no permanent free plan. Key features include:
- 7-day free trial
- 50+ AI templates
- Support for 30+ languages
- [Integration](/posts/claude-api-guide) with Surfer SEO

[Jasper](/posts/best-jasper-desktop-app-review-2026) AI's [paid](/posts/jasper-vs-free-version) [plans](/posts/jasper-pricing-explained) start at $29/month for 20,000 words.
