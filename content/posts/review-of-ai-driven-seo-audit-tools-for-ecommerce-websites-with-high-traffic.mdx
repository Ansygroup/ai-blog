## Top AI-Driven SEO Audit Tools for E-commerce Websites

### Ahrefs

Ahrefs is a well-known SEO toolset that offers a comprehensive SEO audit feature. Its AI-driven [analysis](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) provides insights into technical SEO issues, [content](/posts/ai-automated-social-media-content-calendar-real-estate-agents) optimization, and backlink analysis. Ahrefs' SEO audit tool analyzes over 100 million keywords and identifies issues such as broken links, duplicate [content](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation), and slow page loading speeds.

* Price: $99-$999/month
* [Features](/posts/chatgpt-api-guide): Technical SEO audit, content optimization, backlink analysis
* Integration: Google Analytics, [Google Search](/posts/perplexity-vs-google-search-2026) Console

### SEMrush

SEMrush is another popular SEO toolset that offers a [comprehensive](/posts/chatgpt-for-beginners) SEO audit feature. Its AI-driven [analysis](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) provides [insights](/posts/claude-reddit-reviews-2026) into technical SEO issues, content [optimization](/posts/claude-for-youtube), and competitor analysis. SEMrush's SEO audit tool provides a detailed analysis of website optimization, with AI-driven recommendations for improvement.

* Price: $99.95-$449.95/month
* Features: Technical SEO audit, content optimization, competitor analysis
* Integration: [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Analytics, [Google Search](/posts/perplexity-vs-google-search-2026) Console

### SiteCrawl

SiteCrawl is an AI-driven SEO audit tool that uses machine learning to identify and prioritize SEO issues. Its analysis provides insights into technical SEO issues, content optimization, and website [performance](/posts/claude-best-settings-2026).

* Price: $49-$249/month
* Features: Technical SEO audit, content optimization, website performance analysis
* Integration: Google Analytics
