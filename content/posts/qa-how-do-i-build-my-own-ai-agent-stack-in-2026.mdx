## The Evolution of AI Agent Architecture

As foundation models become faster and more reliable, the composition of the standard agent stack is shifting. In previous years, developers spent significant engineering hours building](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/how-to-use-chatgpt-api-for-building-a-custom-chatbot) complex memory retrieval pipelines and custom routing layers to keep models on track. By 2026, much of this boilerplate is being abstracted away by native model capabilities](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/claude-honest-review-2026), such as expansive context](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/best-ai-tools-content-repurposing-2026) windows and reliable native function calling.
