Yes. Suno supports **15 languages**, including Spanish (Latin American), French (France), Mandarin (Standard), Hindi, Arabic, and Portuguese (Brazil). Each language comes with at least three regional accents, enabling teachers to match student dialects.

### What’s [the best](/posts/copy-ai-limitations-2026) workflow to combine Suno audio with Synthesia video?

2. Generate the audio in Suno (choose voice & language).

This end‑to‑end process takes **under 30 minutes** for a 10‑minute lesson.

### Are there any [hidden fees](/posts/kaggle-api-cost-comparison-2026) or usage caps?
