## What Is AI Pricing 2026?

AI pricing in 2026 refers to the cost of using AI-powered tools and software. These tools can range from simple chatbots to complex machine learning platforms. ## AI Pricing Models

There are several AI pricing models to consider:

* **Per-user pricing**: This model-review)-review)-review) charges based on the number of users. For example, HubSpot's [AI-powered CRM](https://www.hubspot.com) starts at $50 per user per month.
* **Per-feature pricing**: This model charges based on the features used. For example, [Google Cloud AI Platform](https://cloud.google.com/ai-platform) charges based on the specific features and services used.
* **Tiered pricing**: This model offers different pricing tiers with varying levels of features and support. For example, Chatfuel's AI-powered chatbot offers a free plan, as well as several paid tiers starting at $15 per month.
