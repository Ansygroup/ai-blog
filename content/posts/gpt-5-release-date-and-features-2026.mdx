While "AGI" is a debated term, GPT-5 is expected to exhibit "General Intelligence" in the sense of being able to learn and execute almost any cognitive task a human can do via a computer.

### Will GPT-5 replace programmers?
It won't replace programmers, but it will replace the *act of coding*. The role will shift from "writing syntax" to "system architecture and AI orchestration."

### How will GPT-5 handle hallucinations?
GPT-5 is expected to use "Verifiable Reasoning," where the model checks its work against external sources or internal logic gates before presenting an answer, drastically reducing errors.
