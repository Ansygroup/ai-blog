## FAQ

### How does Hugging Face pricing compare to OpenAI for token usage?
Hugging Face’s Starter tier charges **$0.0008 per 1 000 tokens**, which is roughly **20 % cheaper** than OpenAI’s `gpt‑3.5‑turbo` at **$0.0010**. The Pro tier drops to **$0.0006**, making large‑scale deployments significantly more affordable.

### Can I run Hugging Face models on my own hardware?
Yes. With the **Infinity** runtime, you can deploy any model from the Hub on-premise using Docker or Kubernetes. This gives you full control over latency, data residency, and cost.

### Is the Model Hub truly open source?
All models hosted on the Hub are released under permissive licenses (Apache 2.0, MIT, CC‑BY). You can download, modify, and re‑publish them. The underlying `transformers` library is also open source, fostering community contributions.

### Does Hugging Face support multilingual NLP out of the box?
Absolutely. The Hub contains over **3 000** multilingual models, including XLM‑R, mT5, and multilingual BERT. AutoTrain also offers a “multilingual” preset that automatically selects the best language‑aware architecture.

### What support options are available for Enterprise customers?
Enterprise plans include a dedicated Technical Account Manager, 24/7 SLA with 99.9 % uptime, and priority bug fixes. Infinity subscribers also receive on‑site training workshops and custom model optimization services.

### How secure is the Inference API for sensitive data?
Data sent to the Inference API is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. For regulated industries, the **Private Hub** and **Infinity** solutions provide isolated environments that comply with ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR
