## What Is This New Wave of AI-Driven Cybersecurity?

Traditional security tools rely on a technology called "signature-based detection." This approach has a massive flaw: it only protects you against *known* threats. If a hacker modifies a single line of [code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) to create a "zero-day" exploit, traditional antivirus remains complete](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/midjourney-for-coding)](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/midjourney-for-coding)](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/midjourney-for-coding)](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/midjourney-for-coding)ly blind to it.
