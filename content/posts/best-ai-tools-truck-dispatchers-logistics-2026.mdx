A dispatcher's day is a chain of small promises: the load will be at the dock by 2, the driver will call when he is ten miles out, the invoice will go out with the signed proof of delivery. One missed link and three people call. AI tools earn their place in dispatch by keeping those promises visible and by writing the dozens of routine messages a dispatcher sends before lunch. They do not decide which driver takes which load.

Prices below were read on vendor pages on November 27, 2026. Last tested: November 27, 2026. Important limit: the fleet vendors checked (Samsara, Motive and Trucker Path) did not publish usable prices on the pages read, so this list quotes prices only for general tools and describes the fleet products without a figure.

## Airtable: a load board you control

Airtable's Team plan was $20 and Business $45 per user per month billed annually. A base with one row per load, with pickup, delivery, driver, rate, status and document links, replaces the whiteboard and the three spreadsheets that disagree with each other. Views by driver and by day give each person their own list.

The value comes from one rule: a load is not delivered until the proof of delivery is attached. That rule makes invoicing a filter, not a hunt through inboxes.

Where it fails: a tracker is only as current as the last update. If drivers do not report, the data lies. Fix the habit before paying for more seats.

## Claude for the messages dispatchers repeat

Claude Pro was $17 per month annual or $20 monthly. A dispatcher can paste load details and ask for a short pickup confirmation to a shipper, a delay notice to a receiver with a revised time, or a rate-confirmation summary for the driver in plain language. For a team sending 40 messages a day, saving 90 seconds each is an hour.

Where it fails: never paste rate sheets or customer contracts into any tool without checking the terms of the service and your customer's confidentiality clauses. Remove names and rates when you only need wording help.

## Fleet tools: Samsara, Motive and Trucker Path

Samsara's and Motive's pages returned nothing usable when fetched, and Trucker Path's page contained customer testimonials but no price. One testimonial claimed a customer's monthly toll bill fell from roughly $84,000 to $40,000. That is a vendor-published customer quote, not an independent result, so do not plan around it.

These products matter in one place: electronic logs, driver safety and location data are often required or valuable, and AI features here usually mean alerts and summaries on that data. Ask each vendor for a written quote that lists hardware, per-vehicle subscription, contract length and the cost of leaving.

## Zapier and Make for glue

The comparison between automation platforms is covered in [n8n vs Zapier vs Make](/news/ai-workflow-automation-2026-n8n-vs-zapier-vs-make). For dispatch, the useful flow is simple: when a load's status changes to delivered with a document attached, create the invoice draft and notify billing.

## What the vendor pages do not say

Detention and deadhead are where margin leaks, and no AI feature fixes them alone. A tool that logs arrival and departure times at each stop gives you the evidence to bill detention, which many small carriers never claim because they cannot prove the wait. Start recording timestamps for every stop now, even in a spreadsheet. A month of data tells you which shippers waste your drivers' time and what to charge them.

Driver turnover is the other hidden cost. A clear, polite and timely message flow, including pay questions answered the same day, is worth more than any route optimizer. Use drafting tools to make sure no driver question waits overnight.

## Who should skip this list

A one-truck owner-operator can run on a phone, a notes app and a load board app. A large fleet with an existing transportation management system should ask that vendor for built-in drafting before buying separate tools.

## What to buy first

Build the load tracker in Airtable's free or Team plan first and enforce the proof-of-delivery rule for 30 days. Add Claude Pro when message writing is a daily cost. Request quotes from two fleet vendors on the same specification, and compare the three-year total, not the monthly figure. Only then add automation.
