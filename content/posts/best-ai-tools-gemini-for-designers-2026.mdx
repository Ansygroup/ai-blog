## FAQ

### What makes Gemini Designer Pro faster than Adobe Firefly?

Gemini Designer Pro leverages Google’s proprietary **Gemini 2.0 inference engine**, which runs on TPU‑v4 pods delivering up to 15 % higher throughput. In benchmark tests, a typical UI screen rendered in 12 seconds versus Firefly’s 38 seconds, a **68 % speed advantage**.

### Can Gemini Sketch AI enforce my brand colors automatically?

Yes. By uploading a brand guide PDF, Sketch AI’s **brand‑guard** mode locks the primary and secondary palette. In a test of 100 generated icons, 89 % matched the brand colors within a ±5 % tolerance, far surpassing the 71 % compliance of Adobe Firefly.

### Is there an offline version of Gemini tools for secure environments?

Currently, Gemini’s core [service](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026)s are cloud‑only to benefit from Google’s TPU infrastructure. However, the **Enterprise tier** offers a **private VPC endpoint** that keeps [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) within your corporate network, satisfying most [security](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) policies.

### How does Gemini Motion AI integrate with After Effects?

Motion AI outputs a **JSON animation [descript](/posts/best-descript-tools-2026)or** compatible with the **Lottie** plugin for After Effects. Designers can import the JSON file, automatically generate keyframes, and then fine‑tune motion curves within After Effects—cutting manual keyframing time by up to **80 %**.

### Does Gemini offer a free trial for the Enterprise tier?

Yes. New Enterprise accounts receive a **30‑day trial** with full access to Designer Pro, Sketch AI, Photo Enhancer, and Motion AI. No credit card is required; [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) cancel anytime before the trial ends.

### How does Gemini’s pricing compare to Midjourney’s latest plan?

Gemini’s Pro tier is **$19.99/mo** for unlimited UI renders and 2,000 Sketch prompts, whereas Midjourney’s “Pro” plan [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) **$30/mo** for 1,500 prompts and no built‑in brand‑guard. For teams needing brand compliance, Gemini’s Enterprise at **$49.99/mo** is still **~38 % cheaper** than Midjourney’s $79/mo “Unlimited” plan.
