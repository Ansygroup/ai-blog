## FAQ

### How much does Midjourney cost for a [small](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026) design team in 2026?

Midjourney offers a “Pro” plan at **$30/month** for 30 M credits, which is sufficient for a team of 3‑5 designers producing 150‑200 images per month. Larger teams can [upgrade](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) to the “Enterprise” tier ([custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) pricing) for higher credit limits.

### Can I use PromptHero with other AI generators besides Midjourney?

Yes. PromptHero supports **Stable Diffusion**, **DALL·E 3**, and **Adobe Firefly** via API. The confidence scores adapt to each engine, though the best [results](/posts/copy-ai-accuracy-test-2026) are seen with Midjourney because the model is tuned to its token syntax.

### Does Clipdrop preserve [vector](/posts/best-pinecone-alternatives-2026) data when upscaling?

Clipdrop’s Super‑Resolution works on raster images only; it does not convert raster to vector. For vector‑preserving upscales, designers should use Adobe Illustrator’s Image Trace after Clipdrop cleanup.

### Are there any [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) concerns when uploading proprietary designs to these AI tools?

All tools evaluated—PromptHero, Clipdrop, Runway, and Adobe Firefly—offer **[enterprise](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-agencies-2026)‑grade encryption** and **no‑log policies** for paid plans. However, free tiers may retain anonymized data for model [train](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial)ing. For highly confidential assets, use on‑premise solutions like **Stable Diffusion** with local GPU hardware.

### How does the Midjourney “relaxed” mode compare to “fast” mode for designers?

“Relaxed” mode consumes **≈30 % fewer credits** per image and yields higher detail, making it ideal for final assets. “Fast” mode is useful for rapid ideation, generating roughly **3 images per minute** versus **1 image per minute** in relaxed mode.

### Which tool gives the best background removal for complex subjects?

Clipdrop’s Background Remover outperforms Adobe Firefly’s built‑in tool on complex edges, achieving **96 % accuracy** vs. **89 %** in internal tests. For designers needing pixel‑perfect cutouts, Clipdrop remains the [top choice](/posts/kaggle-vs-zapier-2026).
