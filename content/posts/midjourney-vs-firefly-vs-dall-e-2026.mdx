## What Is Midjourney?

Midjourney is a cloud-based AI image generator that allows users to create stunning images with just a few clicks. Founded in 2021, Midjourney has quickly become one of the most popular AI image generators on the [market](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-market-research-2026). With a free plan available and a paid plan starting at $10/month, Midjourney is an affordable option for both personal and professional use.

## What Is Firefly?

Firefly is another top-notch AI image generator that has gained a lot of attention in recent months. With a [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026)-friendly interface and a wide range of customization [options](/posts/jasper-cheaper-alternative), Firefly is a great choice for those who want more control over their images. Firefly also offers a free plan, as well as several paid plans starting at $15/month.

## What Is DALL-E?

DALL-E is a cutting-edge AI image generator developed by the research team at Meta AI. With its advanced AI capabilities, including text-to-image and image-to-image generation, DALL-E is the most powerful tool in this comparison. However, it also comes with a higher price tag, with a paid plan starting at $20/month.
