Most online courses fail before the first lesson is recorded. The creator spends three months on slides and a platform, then discovers nobody wanted to buy it. AI tools make that failure faster if you let them: they can generate a full outline in a minute and a full course in a weekend, and neither tells you whether anyone will pay. The best use of AI for a course creator is on the validation and the boring parts, not on replacing your expertise.

Prices below were read on vendor pages on November 27, 2026. Last tested: November 27, 2026. Several vendors were running Black Friday discounts, so list prices are used here.

## The short answer

| Scenario | Pick | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First course, testing demand | Teachable Starter | $39 per month, but 7.5% transaction fee on sales |
| Selling steadily, want lower fees | Teachable higher tiers | $89 and $189 list prices, 0% fee with Teachable Payments |
| Want full commerce tools | Thinkific | Three tiers, $109, $219 and $499 monthly or $82, $164 and $374 billed annually |
| Outline, scripts and email copy | Claude Pro | $17 annual or $20 monthly |
| Recording and editing lessons | Descript | Plans at $16, $24 and $50 |

## Teachable: read the fee line

Teachable's Starter plan was $39 per month with a 7.5% transaction fee and a limit of 5 products. The higher tiers listed at $89 and $189 per month before discounts. The page states that the builder, growth and custom plans have a 0% transaction fee when you use Teachable Payments, and that the fee applies on the Starter plan only.

The arithmetic matters. At $2,000 in monthly sales, the Starter fee is $150. The $89 plan costs $50 more than Starter in subscription and removes that fee, so it is cheaper at that sales level. Do this sum with your own forecast, not the vendor's example.

Where it fails: payment processor fees are separate from the platform fee on most tools. Check the line for card processing in the checkout terms.

## Thinkific: more room, higher price

Thinkific's page showed three tiers at $109, $219 and $499 per month on monthly billing, falling to $82, $164 and $374 when billed annually. That is a 25% annual saving, and the page says so. It describes commerce tools as available from the first tier.

The tradeoff is cost against stage. A creator with no sales should not be on a $219 plan. Start low, and upgrade when a specific limit blocks you.

## Descript for recording lessons

Descript's paid tiers were $16, $24 and $50 on the page read. Editing a lesson by editing its transcript is the fastest way to remove mistakes without re-recording. For a creator with a day job, this can be the difference between shipping a module a week and abandoning at lesson six. See the full [Descript review](/posts/descript-review-2026-top-all-in-one-audio-video-editor).

## Claude for outlines, scripts and emails

Claude Pro was $17 per month annual or $20 monthly. Give it your real outline and ask it to find gaps and the questions a beginner would ask. Ask it to write the sales email sequence in your voice, then rewrite the first lines yourself.

Where it fails: it will happily produce a polished outline for a topic you know nothing about. It cannot supply your results, stories or student outcomes, and those are what buyers pay for.

## What the vendor pages do not say

Presale beats product. Before building anything, sell the idea to 10 people at a discount, as a live cohort over video calls. If you cannot sell 10 seats, a recorded course will not sell either. Use the live sessions to learn which lessons people actually asked about, then record those. This costs nothing in platform fees and gives you real material for testimonials.

Also, platforms differ on who owns the student relationship. Check whether you can export your student email list and whether the platform emails students on your behalf. Moving platforms later is far easier if you own the list.

## Who should skip this list

If you already teach through a community or membership with active members, adding a course platform may split your audience. If your audience is under a few hundred people, a simple payment link and a private video folder can validate the idea for free.

## What to buy first

Run a live paid cohort first with a payment link. Once 10 or more people have paid and finished, put the recordings on Teachable's Starter plan and check whether the 7.5% fee or the higher plan is cheaper at your actual monthly sales. Buy Claude Pro for the outline and email work, and Descript only when you start recording at volume.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Teachable's Starter plan worth it?

It costs $39 per month with a 7.5% transaction fee on the page read. At around $2,000 monthly sales, a higher plan with 0% fees can cost less overall.

### Can AI create a whole course for me?

It can draft outlines and scripts, but buyers pay for your results and stories. Presell a live cohort before recording anything.

### Which course platform is cheapest?

It depends on sales volume. Compare subscription plus transaction fees at your own forecast, not at the vendor's example.
