## What Are AI Voice Generators?

AI voice generators, also known as text-to-speech (TTS) tools, use artificial intelligence to convert written text into natural-sounding human voices. These tools have numerous applications, from audiobook narration and voice assistants to customer service chatbots and language learning platforms.
