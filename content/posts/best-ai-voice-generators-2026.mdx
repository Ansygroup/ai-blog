## What Are AI Voice Generators?

AI voice generators, also known as text-to-speech (TTS) tools, use artificial intelligence to convert written text into natural-sounding human voices. These tools have numerous applications, from audiobook narration and voice [assistants](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) to customer service chatbots and [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) learning platforms.
