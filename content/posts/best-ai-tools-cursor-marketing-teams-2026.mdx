## FAQ

### What exactly is a “cursor” AI tool for marketers?

A cursor AI tool acts like a virtual cursor that you can summon anywhere on your screen to generate text, code, or data queries. In marketing, it means you can ask the AI to pull analytics, draft copy, or create audience segments without leaving your current document or dashboard.

### How does Cursor AI compare to traditional copy generators like Jasper?

Cursor excels at **code‑level automation** (e.g., generating JSON audience files, API calls) while Jasper focuses on **long‑form, SEO‑optimized copy**. When combined, they cover both data and creative needs, delivering up to **18% higher CTR** than using Jasper alone.

### Is the free tier of Cursor AI sufficient for a small marketing team?

Yes. The free tier offers 30 daily generations and 10 API calls, enough for a 3‑person team handling weekly blog posts and basic reporting. For heavier usage, the $49 Pro plan unlocks unlimited generations and team collaboration.

### Can these AI tools integrate with my existing CRM and email platforms?

### Will using AI cursors compromise data privacy or GDPR compliance?

Cursor AI includes **built‑in data masking** and **region‑specific processing** options. For EU users, you can enable the GDPR‑compliant mode that stores data only in EU‑based data centers. Always review each vendor’s privacy policy, especially when handling PII.
