## FAQ

### How does Writer.com ensure my consulting firm’s confidential data stays secure?

Writer.com stores data in encrypted, SOC 2‑certified data centers and offers optional EU‑regional [hosting](/posts/pinecone-setup-guide-2026). All content is processed in‑memory and never written to disk without your explicit permission, [meeting](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) GDPR and CCPA standards.

### Can Jasper Chat generate data‑driven charts for proposals?

Jasper can suggest chart [descriptions](/posts/ai-tutorial-seo-optimized-amazon-product-descriptions-2026) and embed placeholder data, but it does not create actual [visual](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026)izations. You’ll still need PowerPoint or Excel to render the final chart, though Jasper’s text can be copied directly into slide notes.

### Is ProWritingAid’s style score comparable to Grammarly’s readability score?

ProWritingAid’s 30‑point style score aggregates grammar, readability, and structural metrics, providing a broader view than Grammarly’s single “Readability” score. In our tests, ProWritingAid consistently rated consulting documents 12 points higher, reflecting deeper analysis.

### Does Microsoft Editor work offline for consultants on the road?

Yes. When you have a Microsoft 365 [subscription](/posts/grammarly-pricing-breakdown-2026), the Editor engine syncs locally on Windows and macOS, allowing offline editing. Changes sync once you reconnect to the internet.

### Which tool offers the best integration with CRM platforms like Salesforce?

Jasper’s “CRM Outreach” template integrates directly with Salesforce, auto‑generating follow‑up emails based on opportunity data. Writer.com offers API hooks that can be custom‑built for CRM sync, but requires [developer](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-developers-2026) resources.

### How do I decide between a per‑user subscription and a site‑wide license?

For firms with **<25 consultants**, per‑user pricing (Writer.com, Jasper) is usually cheaper. For **>100 consultants**, negotiating a site‑wide license with volume discounts (often available from Writer.com and Microsoft) yields better ROI and simplifies admin.
