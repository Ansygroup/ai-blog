## What Is Automated Subtitle Generation?

Automated subtitle generation is the process of using artificial intelligence—specifically Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) models—to convert spoken audio from a video into written text, synchronized precisely with the video's timeline. 

In the early days of YouTube, creators had to rely on basic speech-to-text models that produced blocky, unpunctuated, and often hilariously inaccurate captions. Today, modern AI captioning tools analyze vocal inflections, recognize speaker transitions, filter out background noise, and automatically insert correct punctuation and capitalization. 

These tools typically output subtitles in two ways:
1. **Closed Captions (CC):** Generates sidecar files like `.srt` or `.vtt` that viewers can toggle on or off. These are highly valuable for YouTube SEO because search engine crawlers index the text.
2. **Open Captions (Burned-in):** Captions are rendered directly onto the video track. This format is essential for vertical formats like YouTube Shorts, where eye-catching, animated text keeps viewers hooked.
