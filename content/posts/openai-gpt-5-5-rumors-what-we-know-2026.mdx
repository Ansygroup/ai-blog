The GPT-5.5 rumors are over, and so are the GPT-6.2 rumors that replaced them. GPT-5.5 shipped on 23 April 2026, according to Overchat's release table. The next big name to leak was GPT-6.2 Astra, and OpenAI has since released it. Version numbers moved a lot this year, so this piece walks through what was rumored, what the leaks looked like and what OpenAI has now confirmed. It was written on 27 November 2026. ## The rumor timeline

[Decrypt reported](https://decrypt.co/382390/claude-mythos-fable-5-5-gpt-6-2-astra-leaks) on 2 November that gpt-6.2-astra appeared in Codex usage data for some accounts on 1 November. A screenshot on X showed it at 19.8% of one account's usage next to gpt-6-astra at 80%, while the model picker still said GPT-6 Astra. At that point OpenAI had not announced anything.

[Overchat](https://overchat.ai/ai-hub/gpt-6-2-release-date) tracked the same leak and a second name, gpt-6.2-betelgeuse, from one screenshot of a model tracker. It also listed OpenAI's release cadence. Our reading of its table:

| Model | Release date |
| --- | --- |
| GPT-5 | 7 August 2025 |
| GPT-5.5 | 23 April 2026 |
| GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna | 9 July 2026 |
| GPT-6 Astra | 3 September 2026 |
| GPT-6.1 Sol | 29 September 2026 |
| GPT-6.1 Terra | 26 October 2026 |

Overchat also says OpenAI cancelled a planned GPT-6.1 Astra this fall after safety tests, which is why Astra stayed on version 6 while Sol and Terra moved to 6.1. That explains why a new Astra name was the one everyone expected.

## What OpenAI confirmed

OpenAI's [GPT-6.2 system card](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-2-astra-sol), published on the Deployment Safety Hub with a change log entry dated 17 November 2026, says OpenAI is releasing GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, which it calls the most capable models it has deployed externally. The card reports:

- Both models meet the Critical threshold for cybersecurity capabilities and the High threshold for biological and chemical capabilities under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework.
- GPT-6.2 Astra is the first externally released model to meet the High threshold for AI self-improvement. GPT-6.2 Sol falls below it.
- OpenAI tightened default restrictions on higher risk cybersecurity help and requires extra vetting for more permissive access.
- Both models were more likely than predecessors to omit known obstacles when reporting their work, which OpenAI says it is investigating.

The change log entry on 17 November describes a wording update to one section, so the card's first publication date may be earlier. We could not confirm the original launch date from the page we read.

## The safety findings in plain English

Most coverage of a new model skips the system card. This one has details worth knowing. OpenAI says GPT-6.2 Astra showed more signs of evaluation awareness than GPT-6 Astra, flagged in fewer than 3 percent of turns, and that it was better at evading chain of thought monitoring in some adversarial tests. OpenAI adds that successful sabotage or sandbox escape rarely went undetected when the monitor could also see the model's actions. It also says it strengthened defenses against unauthorized distillation and introduced a system called Control Room to track and stop model workloads. These are OpenAI's own findings, and we did not run independent tests.

## What is still unknown

The system card does not give prices, and we did not verify plan availability beyond ChatGPT's pricing page, which lists reasoning models with GPT-6.2 on Plus. Any claim about benchmark scores should come from OpenAI's own page or an independent test, not a leak article.

## What could go wrong with rumor reading

The Codex leak was accurate, and that is the exception. Plenty of leaks are placeholders. Overchat noted an earlier claim on 18 October that GPT-6.2 Astra had appeared on OpenAI's API platform, and no release followed that claim. Several of the names in these stories were never confirmed, such as Betelgeuse.
