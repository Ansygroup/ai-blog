OpenAI has a help article titled "Privacy Center in ChatGPT". It describes a new page inside ChatGPT that explains privacy options and sends you to the matching settings. This article summarizes that help page, linked at the end. We have not tested the feature, and the page itself says availability varies by account and app version.

## What is the Privacy Center?

According to OpenAI, it is "a place in ChatGPT to learn about your privacy options and find the settings that manage them." Your controls stay in Settings. The Privacy Center explains what each one does and, where a "Manage" button appears, opens the existing setting. OpenAI says that opening it does not change your settings, delete your data or override your organization's policies.

## Who gets it?

The rollout covers signed-in ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, Pro and Business users, on the web and in the iOS and Android apps. It does not include ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu or ChatGPT for Healthcare. OpenAI adds that individual features depend on your plan, region, account and workspace settings.

## How do you open it?

- **Web:** profile menu, then Help, then Privacy center.
- **Mobile:** Settings, then Privacy Center on iOS or Privacy center on Android.
- **Desktop app:** if the Help menu lists Privacy center, it opens in your browser.

## What does it cover?

OpenAI groups topics into three categories.

**Personalized chats and ads**
- Memory. OpenAI notes that deleting a chat does not automatically delete a memory saved from it.
- Personalized ads. Ads may appear for eligible Free and Go users. Business, Enterprise, Edu and ChatGPT for Healthcare plans do not have ads.
- Location. OpenAI says ChatGPT does not separately save or store your precise location beyond what is in your conversation and context. Precise location sharing is not available in Enterprise workspaces.

**Chat privacy**
- Temporary chat, which covers how history, personalization, model improvement and retention work for those conversations.
- Locked chats, where an account PIN limits access to saved conversations.

**Data use and access**
- Plugins and apps: what connected apps can access and which permissions they need.
- Multi-factor authentication.
- Model improvement. Eligible personal accounts can use "Improve the model for everyone" to choose whether their content helps train models. Turning it off does not delete chats or remove them from history. OpenAI says it does not use content from Business, Enterprise, Edu or Healthcare workspaces to train models by default.
- Export or delete your data, when available. Self-service export is not available in Business, Enterprise or Healthcare workspaces, and self-service account deletion is not available in managed workspaces.

OpenAI also says that when conversation data is used to improve models, it works to identify and remove personally identifiable information.

## OpenAI's four privacy statements

The page lists these as the company's approach: it does not sell your data or share ChatGPT conversations with advertisers or marketing partners; it uses safeguards such as encryption, security testing and monitoring; you control access, export and deletion depending on plan; and eligible personal accounts control whether chats are used to improve models. These are OpenAI's own statements. The help page does not describe independent verification.

## Business workspaces

If you use ChatGPT Business, your administrator may control which settings exist and how workspace data is retained or reviewed. The Privacy Center does not override those controls.

## Our take

A single page that names the settings is useful, because the controls were spread across Settings. The practical steps are short. Open Memory and see what is saved. Check whether Personalized ads applies to your plan. Decide on "Improve the model for everyone." For formal requests to access or delete personal data, OpenAI points to a separate Privacy Portal website.
