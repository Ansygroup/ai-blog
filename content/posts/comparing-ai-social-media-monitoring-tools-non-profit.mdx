## What Is AI-Powered Social Media Monitoring?

AI-powered social media monitoring involves using machine learning algorithms to analyze vast amounts of social media data, providing insights into consumer behavior, sentiment, and trends. This technology enables organizations to stay on top of conversations about their brand, competitors, and industry, making data-driven decisions to improve their online presence.

## Top AI-Powered Social Media Monitoring Tools for Non-Profit Organizations

### Hootsuite Insights

Hootsuite Insights offers a comprehensive suite of social media monitoring tools, including AI-powered analytics, social media listening, and influencer identification. Its user-friendly interface makes it easy to navigate and track conversations, sentiment, and trends in real-time. Pricing starts at $19/month for up to 10 social media profiles.

### Brandwatch

Brandwatch's AI-powered social media monitoring tool can track up to 200 million social media conversations, providing real-time insights into consumer behavior and sentiment. Its advanced analytics capabilities enable organizations to identify trends, track competitors, and adjust their strategy accordingly. Pricing starts at $1,000/month for up to 10 social media profiles.

### Sprout Social

Sprout Social's social media monitoring tool integrates with over 20 social media platforms, providing comprehensive insights into consumer behavior and sentiment. Its AI-powered analytics capabilities enable organizations to track conversations, sentiment, and trends in real-time. Pricing starts at $99/month for up to 5 social media profiles.

## Who Should Use This?

Non-profit organizations with a small to medium-sized social media presence (up to 10 social media profiles) and a limited budget should consider Hootsuite Insights. Organizations with a medium to large-sized social media presence (up to 50 social media profiles) and a moderate budget should consider Sprout Social. ## Who Should Skip This?
