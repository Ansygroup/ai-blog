## What Is AI-Powered Social Media Monitoring?

AI-powered social media monitoring tools use natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to analyze social media conversations about a brand. These tools can track keywords, hashtags, and mentions, providing valuable [insights](/posts/claude-reddit-reviews-2026) into brand reputation, [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) sentiment, and social media engagement.
