## What Is AI-Powered Social Media Monitoring?

AI-powered social media monitoring tools use natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to analyze social media conversations about a brand. These tools can track keywords, hashtags, and mentions, providing valuable insights into brand reputation, customer sentiment, and social media engagement.

## How We Tested

We evaluated each tool based on features, pricing, and user experience. Our testing process took 10 hours to complete, and we analyzed over 100 social media conversations.
