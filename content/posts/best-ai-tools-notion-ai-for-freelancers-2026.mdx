The tool’s “Ask Notion” sidebar works like a chatbot, pulling context from the page you’re on, which reduces the friction of switching between a note‑taking app and a separate AI chat window.

The biggest advantage [for freelancers](/posts/best-ai-chatgpt-tools-freelancers-2026) is cost‑effectiveness.
