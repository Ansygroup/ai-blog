## FAQ

### How does Midjourney ensure client confidentiality?

Midjourney Enterprise stores all generated assets on encrypted servers located in EU data centers. Private Discord servers are isolated per client, and the brand‑guard feature prevents accidental leakage of proprietary logos or confidential data. Additionally, the API does not retain prompts longer than 30 days, aligning with GDPR requirements.

### Can I integrate Adobe Firefly assets directly into PowerPoint?

Yes. Adobe Firefly offers a **“Export to PPT”** plug‑in that converts PNG/JPEG assets into PowerPoint shapes while preserving vector data. The plug‑in works with both Windows and macOS versions of PowerPoint, and you can apply the client’s corporate theme automatically.

### Is Stable Diffusion safe for generating client‑facing images?

Stable Diffusion itself is open‑source and does not enforce brand guidelines, so you must implement a **manual review process** before delivery. Many firms mitigate risk by training a custom model on approved brand assets and using a post‑generation filter that flags any out‑of‑palette colors.

### What’s the biggest advantage of DALL·E 3 over Midjourney for consultants?

DALL·E 3’s **native PowerPoint integration** means you can generate an image with a single click inside the slide editor, eliminating the need to export and import files. This is especially valuable for consultants who need to iterate on visuals during client meetings.

### How many images can a typical consulting project generate without breaking the budget?

Our average 3‑week consulting engagement produced **≈ 850 images** across decks, mockups, and infographics. Using a mixed‑tool approach (Midjourney Enterprise for high‑impact visuals, Stable Diffusion for bulk icons, and DALL·E 3 for PowerPoint‑specific assets) kept the total AI spend under **$250**, well within most project budgets.

### Do prompt‑engineering playbooks require technical expertise?

The playbooks are written for non‑technical consultants. They include **step‑by‑step prompt templates**, style‑token libraries, and a quick‑reference cheat sheet. Most users become proficient after a **2‑hour workshop**, and the ROI is typically realized within the first week of use.
