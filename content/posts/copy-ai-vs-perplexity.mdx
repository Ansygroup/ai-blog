## What Is Perplexity?

Perplexity is a search engine that uses natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing to provide accurate and relevant search results. Unlike traditional search engines, Perplexity's algorithm can understand complex queries and give more nuanced answers.
