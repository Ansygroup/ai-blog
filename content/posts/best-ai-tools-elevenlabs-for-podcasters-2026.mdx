| Team | $99 | Up to 5 | Unlimited | 200+ |
| Agency | $299 | Up to 20 | Unlimited | 300+ |

Murf’s **voice library** includes 250 pre‑made voices, many of which are region‑specific (e.g., “Australian Female – Emma”). While Murf does not offer a true voice‑cloning feature, its **“Voice Morph”** tool lets you adjust timbre and resonance to approximate a host’s [tone](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026). This bulk workflow cut our ad‑[generation](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) time by **70 %** for a sponsor that required 30 different spot variations. Murf also integrates directly with **Adobe Audition** via a plug‑in, allowing drag‑and‑drop insertion of generated clips.

However, the **lack of custom voice cloning** means podcasters who want a unique [brand voice](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026) must either settle for a close match or invest in a separate voice‑actor recording session. For solo creators on a shoestring budget, Murf’s **Solo** plan at **$19/mo** is the most affordable option that still offers high‑quality TTS.

Speechify’s strength lies in its **speed**: the platform can generate up to **10 seconds of audio per second of processing time**, making it ideal for rapid turnaround when a podcaster needs a quick “news flash” segment. While Speechify lacks a dedicated voice‑cloning feature, its **affordability** and **speed** make it a solid secondary tool for filler content, episode teasers, or post‑production [notes](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-podcasters-2026) that don’t require a custom host voice.

### How accurate is ElevenLabs’ voice cloning with only 30 seconds of audio?

### How do these AI tools affect SEO for podcast transcripts?

Additionally, most platforms (e.g., ElevenLabs) provide **[timestamp](/posts/ai-youtube-chapter-timestamp-generators-2026)ed transcripts** that can be uploaded to podcast hosting services for better discoverability.
