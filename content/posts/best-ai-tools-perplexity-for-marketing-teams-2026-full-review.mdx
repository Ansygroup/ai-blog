### Can AI tools like Perplexity replace a human content strategist?
AI tools augment, not replace, human strategists. ### What are the privacy and data security considerations when using AI tools for marketing?
When using AI tools, marketers should verify that the platform complies with GDPR, CCPA, and industry‑specific regulations. Look for features like data encryption at rest and in transit, role‑based access controls, and the ability to opt‑out of data training. Additionally, avoid uploading personally identifiable information (PII) unless the tool explicitly supports secure handling and retention policies.
