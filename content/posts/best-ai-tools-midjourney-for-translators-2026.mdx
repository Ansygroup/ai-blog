## FAQ

### How does MidJourney handle non‑Latin characters in prompts?

MidJourney supports Uni[code](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026), but complex scripts (Arabic, Hindi) may need transliteration or a short‑English description. In our tests, adding a **language tag** (e.g., `lang:ar`) improved output fidelity by 22 %.

### Can I automate prompt generation from source text?

Yes. Using the **Prompt‑Library API**, [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) script a pipeline that extracts key terms from a source segment (via regex or a language model) and feeds them into MidJourney. The resulting image URLs can be auto‑populated in MemoQ through its REST endpoint.

### Is there a [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial)‑privacy concern when sending confidential source material to MidJourney?

MidJourney’s Enterprise tier offers **on‑premise inference** and end‑to‑end encryption, complying with GDPR and ISO 27001. For highly confidential projects, we recommend the Enterprise [plan](/posts/cursor-pricing-breakdown-2026) or an on‑premise Stable Diffusion deployment.

### How do the image generation [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) compare to [hiring](/posts/best-ai-chatgpt-tools-recruiters-2026) a graphic [design](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026)er?

A [freelance](/posts/top-ai-tools-freelancers-2026) designer in the US averages $50‑$80 per hour. MidJourney Turbo generates 1,200 images for $30, effectively costing **$0.025 per image**—a 99 % cost reduction for repetitive UI screenshots.

### Does MidJourney integrate with Google Translate?

There is no native plugin, but you can chain the APIs: generate an image with MidJourney, extract alt‑text with OCR (e.g., Tesseract), translate via Google Cloud Translation, and re‑inject. This workflow adds ~6 seconds per image, making DeepL Pro the faster option for European languages.

### What’s the best prompt structure for translating product packaging?

A proven [template](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026):  
`/imagine "high‑resolution photo of [product name] packaging, front view, label in [source language], tagline in [target language], soft‑natural lighting, 300 dpi"`  
Replace placeholders with your actual terms; the result consistently yields clear, bilingual mock‑ups.
