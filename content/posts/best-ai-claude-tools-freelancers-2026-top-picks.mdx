## FAQ

### How does Claude compare to ChatGPT for freelance writing?

Claude generally produces more consistent tone adherence and fewer factual errors. In our blind test, Claude Pro scored 4.6/5 on accuracy versus ChatGPT‑4’s 4.4, while response speed was comparable (≈1.3 s per request).

### Can I use Claude for client‑facing proposals without editing?

Yes, especially with Claude Pro’s system prompts. By embedding your brand voice and preferred structure, many freelancers reported **30 % less post‑generation editing**. However, a quick proofread is still recommended for legal compliance.

### Is the Claude API affordable for small freelancers?

At $0.015 per 1,000 tokens, a freelancer generating 500 k tokens per month (roughly 350 k words) would spend about **$7.50**. This is cheaper than most SaaS automation tools, making it a cost‑effective choice for routine batch tasks.

### What safety measures does Claude have for sensitive client data?

Anthropic’s models are trained with “constitutional AI” principles, which heavily limit the generation of disallowed content and enforce data privacy. Claude does not retain user prompts after the session ends, aligning with GDPR and CCPA standards.

### Does Claude support image generation or multimodal input?

As of 2026, Claude remains a text‑only model. For image generation, freelancers should pair it with tools like **Midjourney** or **Stable Diffusion**, which can be called via the Claude API for seamless multimodal workflows.
