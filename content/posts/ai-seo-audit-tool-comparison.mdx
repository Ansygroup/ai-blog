## Technical SEO Audits
Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Moz all offer technical SEO audits, which analyze [website](/posts/review-of-ai-based-accessibility-tools-for-websites) structure, crawlability, and indexing. Ahrefs' audit tool checks for 100+ technical SEO issues, while SEMrush's tool checks for 130+ issues. Moz's tool checks for 50+ issues.

## [Keyword [Researc](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-keyword-research-2026)h](/posts/best-ai-research-tools-2026) and Analysis
Keyword [research](/posts/chatgpt-free-tier-ads-2026) is a crucial aspect of SEO. Ahrefs offers 300 million keywords to analyze, with a 99% accuracy rate. SEMrush provides 20 [billion](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-market-research-2026) keywords, with a 95% accuracy [rate](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-a-b-testing-and-conversion-rate-optimizati). Moz has 500 million keywords, with a 90% [accuracy](/posts/claude-best-settings-2026) rate. When paired with a Dell UltraSharp 4K monitor, these tools can help you identify the most relevant keywords for your [business](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-small-business-2026).
