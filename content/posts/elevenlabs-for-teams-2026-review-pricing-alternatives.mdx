## FAQ

### How many custom voices can I create with ElevenLabs for Teams?

Each seat can create up to **5 custom voice models**, and the Enterprise tier raises the total repository limit to **200 voices** across the organization.

### Does ElevenLabs for Teams support real‑time [streaming](/posts/bahrain-grand-prix-in-malaysia-f1-free-2026-guide) audio?

Yes. The **Dynamic Prosody Engine** streams audio as soon as the text is received, with latency under **1 second** for short prompts, making it suitable for live chatbots.

### What languages are available in the enterprise plan?

All 30+ languages in the core platform are unlocked for Enterprise users, including **Mandarin Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, and Brazilian Portuguese**. Lower tiers may have a subset (typically 15 languages).

### Can I export the generated audio in lossless formats?

Audio can be downloaded as **WAV (48 kHz, 24‑bit)**, **MP3 (320 kbps)**, or **OGG**. The Enterprise tier also supports batch export via the API.

### Is there a free trial for ElevenLabs for Teams?

ElevenLabs offers a **14‑day free trial** with 2,000 voice minutes and access to the collaboration dashboard, allowing teams to evaluate the workflow before committing.

### How does ElevenLabs handle data privacy for uploaded source audio?

All source audio is encrypted at rest and deleted after **30 days** unless a custom retention policy is set. For HIPAA‑Ready [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026)s, the retention window can be extended to **90 days** under strict audit controls.
