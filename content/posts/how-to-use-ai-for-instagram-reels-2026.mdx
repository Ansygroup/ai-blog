A recent test had the model generate a 15‑second travel script with 3 suggested hooks, 2 captions, and a CTA—all within 45 seconds. Jasper AI, meanwhile, offers a “Content Planner” that not only drafts scripts but also aligns them with trending [keyword](/posts/ai-powered-podcast-seo-optimization-boost-discoverability-2026)s, giving creators a 12 % higher click‑through rate on average.

Storyboard generation is equally crucial. Creators reported that the AI’s ability to suggest visual motifs—like golden hour lighting or split‑screen transitions—added a professional polish that would otherwise require a dedicated editor.
