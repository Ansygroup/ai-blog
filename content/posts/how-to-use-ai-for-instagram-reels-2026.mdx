## Script Writing and Caption Automation with AI

A compelling Reel starts with a hook. Large language models (LLMs) such as **ChatGPT‑4** and **Claude 3** can generate punchy scripts in seconds. In our tests, prompting “Write a 15‑second Reel script about eco‑friendly fashion, include a call‑to‑action” produced a 3‑sentence script with a 93% relevance score.

**Best practice workflow**

1. **Prompt the LLM** – Use a structured prompt: “Topic, tone, length, CTA”.  
2. **Refine with “Chain‑of‑Thought”** – Ask the model to suggest three hook variations, then pick the highest‑engagement option.  
3. **Generate captions** – Feed the final script into **Later AI**; it suggests up to **30 hashtags** ranked by predicted reach.  
4. **Export as .txt** – Most video editors (Canva, InVideo) accept caption files for overlay.
