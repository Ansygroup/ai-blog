## Step‑by‑Step Workflow: From Idea to Publish

1. **Ideation with AI Prompt Assistants** – Use ChatGPT‑4 or Claude 2 to brainstorm Reel concepts. Example prompt: “Give me three 15‑second Reel ideas for a vegan snack brand, each with a hook, visual style, and music genre.”  
2. **Script Generation** – Feed the chosen idea into Jasper AI or Copy.ai to produce a concise 30‑word script that fits Instagram’s 15‑second limit.  
3. **Visual Creation** – Paste the script into Runway Gen‑2. Select “Reel” output preset; the tool returns a 15‑second video in 4K within 45 seconds.  
4. **Music Matching** – Upload the video to Beatoven, select “Upbeat Pop” mood, and let the AI adapt the beat to visual cuts.  
5. **Captioning & Subtitles** – Drop the video into Kapwing; enable auto‑captioning and choose “Instagram style” font. Export with embedded SRT for accessibility.  
6. **Branding Overlays** – Use Canva AI Video to add your logo, brand colors, and a CTA button that appears in the last 3 seconds.  
7. **Scheduling** – Connect the final MP4 to Later’s Instagram Scheduler, set the optimal posting time (identified by AI‑driven audience insights), and publish.

Following this workflow, a solo creator can produce three polished Reels per hour—a [productivity](/posts/best-cursor-tools-2026) leap that translates directly into higher follower growth.
