## What Is Gamma for Teams 2026?

Gamma for Teams 2026 is an AI‑powered add‑on that sits inside popular messaging platforms—primarily Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Discord—to turn unstructured conversation into structured knowledge. Leveraging a proprietary large‑language model (LLM) fine‑tuned on enterprise communication data, Gamma can:

1. **Generate instant meeting minutes** from live chat or voice transcriptions.  
2. **Create actionable task lists** by extracting verbs and owners from discussion threads.  
3. **Suggest context‑aware documents** from a company’s internal knowledge base, reducing “search fatigue.”  

The platform was launched in early 2025, and the 2026 version adds a “team‑wide prompt library” and a “dynamic insight engine” that surfaces trends across channels (e.g., rising mentions of “budget cut” or “product launch”). Gamma’s AI core runs on a hybrid cloud architecture: the inference layer is hosted on Azure’s confidential compute, while the data‑processing pipeline can be self‑hosted behind a corporate firewall for strict compliance environments. This flexibility makes it attractive to both remote‑first startups and regulated enterprises.

## Integration, Security, and Admin Controls
