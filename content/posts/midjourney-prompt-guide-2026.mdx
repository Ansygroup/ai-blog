## Understanding Midjourney's Prompt Structure

A well-crafted Midjourney prompt consists of several key elements, including:

* **Subject**: The main object or scene you want to generate.
* **Style**: The aesthetic or artistic [style](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026) you want to achieve (e.g., photorealistic, impressionist, abstract).
* **Color palette**: The colors you want to [feature](/posts/copy-ai-vs-chatgpt-2026) in the image.
* **Texture and lighting**: The surface [quality](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026) and illumination you want to capture.
