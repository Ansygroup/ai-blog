### 2. Grammarly - Writing Assistant

Grammarly is a writing assistant that can help freelancers improve their writing quality and productivity. With Grammarly, you can check your writing for grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors, as well as get suggestions for improvement. Grammarly also offers a range of writing tools, including a plagiarism detector and a citation generator.

* Pricing: $11.66/month (basic plan), $29.95/month (premium plan)
* Features: Grammar and spelling checker, plagiarism detector, citation generator
* Amazon Affiliate Link: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B075RXBZ7R?tag=ansy07-20](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B075RXBZ7R)

### 3. Trello - Project Management Tool

Trello is a project management tool that can help freelancers organize their projects and increase their productivity. With Trello, you can create boards, lists, and cards to track your projects and tasks. Trello also offers a range of integrations with other tools, including Google Drive and Slack.

* Pricing: Free (basic plan), $12.50/month (gold plan)
* Features: Board, list, and card creation, integrations with other tools
* Amazon Affiliate Link: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0792V4YQ9?tag=ansy07-20](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0792V4YQ9)

### 4. Google Workspace - Productivity Suite

Google Workspace is a productivity suite that can help freelancers collaborate with their teams and clients. With Google Workspace, you can create and edit documents, spreadsheets, and presentations in real-time. Google Workspace also offers a range of integrations with other tools, including Slack and Trello.

* Pricing: $6/month (basic plan), $18/month (business plan)
* Features: Document, spreadsheet, and presentation creation, real-time collaboration
* Amazon Affiliate Link: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B076MXW8T1?tag=ansy07-20](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B076MXW8T1)

### 5. Fiverr - Freelance Marketplace

Fiverr is a freelance marketplace that can help freelancers find clients and projects. With Fiverr, you can create a profile, showcase your skills and services, and bid on projects. Fiverr also offers a range of tools and resources to help freelancers succeed, including a job board and a community forum.

* Pricing: Free (basic plan), $5/month (pro plan)
* Features: Profile creation, skill and service showcase, project bidding
