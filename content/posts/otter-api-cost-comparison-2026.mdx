## FAQ

### How does Otter calculate its volume discounts?

Otter applies a tiered discount [automatic](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)ally once your monthly usage exceeds the defined thresholds (5 M, 10 M, 20 M minutes). The discount is applied to the base per‑minute rate before any add‑on fees, and it stacks with any enterprise‑negotiated custom rates.

### Is there a hidden “minimum spend” for the Enterprise tier?

No. Otter’s Enterprise tier has no minimum monthly spend; you only pay for the minutes you consume. However, contracts typically include a 12‑month commitment to lock in the discounted rate.

### Can I get a custom SLA higher than 99.9 %?

Yes. For high‑frequency trading or emergency services, Otter offers a “Platinum” SLA with 99.99 % uptime and a dedicated [engineering](/posts/fixing-undefined-errors-ai-apps) liaison. Pricing for this tier is negotiated on a case‑by‑case basis and is not listed publicly.

### How does Otter handle data [security](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) and compliance?

Otter complies with GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA (when you enable the HIPAA‑ready add‑on). All audio files are encrypted at rest (AES‑256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Enterprise [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026)s can request on‑premise deployment for maximum control.

### Does Otter charge for storage of transcripts?

Transcripts are retained for 90 days on the Standard tier and 180 days on Enterprise at no extra charge. If [you need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) indefinite archival, Otter offers a storage add‑on at $0.0002 / record per month.

### What’s the cost difference between real‑time and batch transcription?

Real‑time streaming adds $0.001 / minute on top of the base rate. For a 60‑minute live event, that’s an extra $0.06, which is negligible compared to the $0.36 base cost on the Enterprise tier. Batch transcription has no streaming surcharge.
