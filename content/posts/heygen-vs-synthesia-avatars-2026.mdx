HeyGen and Synthesia both turn a script into a presenter on screen, but their pricing pages now describe two different products. HeyGen sells monthly plans with a credit pool and a long list of avatar engines. Synthesia sells one shared credit pool across its video agent, Studio video, dubbing and surveys. As of November 2026, that difference decides the choice more than avatar quality does. Last tested: 27 November 2026, from both vendors' live pricing pages.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo creator publishing social video | HeyGen | Creator is $29 a month with 1080p export and branding removed |
| Cheapest paid start | Synthesia | Starter is $19 a month, with the first month at $9 |
| 4K export | HeyGen | 4K is listed on Pro at $49 a month |
| Team training with SCORM and SSO | HeyGen Business | SAML/SSO and SCORM export are listed at $149 a month |
| Shared workspace for up to 5 editors | Synthesia | Team plan lists 5 editors and unlimited guests |
| Language reach | HeyGen | Creator lists 175+ languages and dialects |

## HeyGen plans

| Plan | Price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 3 watermarked videos a month, up to 1 minute each, 500+ presenters |
| Creator | $29 a month ($24 on annual billing) | 600 credits, 1080p, no HeyGen branding, 175+ languages, credits roll over one month |
| Pro | $49 a month | 1,000 credits, 4K export, Avatar 6 Pro, videos up to 30 minutes, 1 professional voice clone |
| Business | $149 a month plus $20 per extra seat | 1,500 credits, videos up to 60 minutes, SAML/SSO, SCORM export, Zapier, Make and n8n integrations |
| Enterprise | Custom | No maximum video length, SCIM, dedicated manager |

HeyGen's Pro plan also lists access to Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1 and Nano Banana for video and image generation, so one subscription replaces a few others.

## Synthesia plans

| Plan | Price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $0 | 500 credits, 10 minutes of Studio video, watermark, Syren videos up to 3 minutes |
| Starter | $19 a month, $9 for the first month | Up to about 150 minutes of Studio video, 125+ avatars |
| Pro | Not captured in our fetch | Up to about 720 minutes of Studio video, 180+ avatars, no 3 minute cap on Syren |
| Team | $99 a month, $79 on the Black Friday and Cyber Week offer | Up to 5 editors, unlimited guests, 1-click translation into 140+ languages |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited Studio minutes under fair usage, 240+ avatars |

Syren is Synthesia's video agent. The page says a one minute Syren video typically uses 1,000 to 2,000 credits. On the free 500 credits, that is under one minute of finished video, so treat Basic as a trial.

## Languages, voice and avatars

HeyGen's Creator plan lists 175+ languages and dialects and a custom video avatar made with your own voice and likeness. Pro adds one professional voice clone, which the page prices at $29 a month and shows as free on Pro. HeyGen's text to speech allowance is up to 10 hours on Creator and 16+ hours on Pro.

Synthesia's Team plan lists one-click translation into 140+ languages, and its dubbing feature preserves the original speaker's voice, accent and tone. Avatar counts rise with the plan: 125+ on Starter, 180+ on Pro and Team, 240+ on Enterprise. Personal avatars are included on paid plans, and Studio avatars are the paid add-on mentioned above.

## What the free plans prove

Free plans are useful for one thing, which is checking lip sync and voice on your own script. Neither is useful for publishing, since both add a watermark. HeyGen Free caps a video at one minute and three videos a month. Synthesia Basic gives 10 minutes of Studio video and 500 credits, and it caps Syren videos at 3 minutes. Run the same script on both before you decide.

## Where each one fails

HeyGen fails on credit planning. Credits are shared across avatars, voice, translation and generation models, and the page does not give a per minute credit price in the section we read. Test a real script before you commit to a volume.

Synthesia fails on expense for custom looks. A Studio Express-1 custom avatar is a paid add-on at $1,000 a year for annual plan users only, and it can take up to 10 days to process. Each action b-roll asset costs 230 credits.

## Who should skip which

Skip Synthesia if you want 4K or a long list of third party video models in one plan. Skip HeyGen if you need a shared workspace at a flat team price without paying per seat. ## Test it yourself

1. Write a 60 second script with one product name and one number in it.
2. Run it through HeyGen Free and Synthesia Basic.
3. Check lip sync on the number and the product name, then check the watermark placement.
4. Translate the same script into one language you can read and listen for pronunciation.
5. Note credits used on each, then multiply by your monthly video count.

## Which should you pick

Pick HeyGen Creator at $29 for social and marketing clips, and Pro at $49 if you need 4K or videos longer than a few minutes. Pick Synthesia Starter at $19 for training and explainer content, and move to Team at $99 when several editors need the same workspace. Prices were read on 27 November 2026 and the promotions end soon, so check before you pay.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is cheaper, HeyGen or Synthesia?

Synthesia Starter is $19 a month with a $9 first month. HeyGen Creator is $29 a month, or $24 on annual billing.

### Does HeyGen export 4K?

Yes. The HeyGen pricing page lists 4K export on Pro at $49 a month, while Creator exports at up to 1080p.

### Is there a free plan?

Both have one. HeyGen Free gives 3 watermarked videos a month up to 1 minute. Synthesia Basic gives 500 credits and 10 minutes of Studio video with a watermark.
