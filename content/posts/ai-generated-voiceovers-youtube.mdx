## Customizing Your AI Voiceover
Customization is key to creating a unique and engaging AI voiceover. Consider the following:
- Voice selection: Choose from various voices, accents, and languages to match your brand and content
- Tone and pace: Adjust the tone and pace to convey the desired emotion and atmosphere
- Emphasis and inflection: Add emphasis and inflection to specific words or phrases for added drama and effect

Before you feed your script to an AI voice engine, clean it up for natural speech. Remove overly long sentences, keep each sentence under 20 words, and insert pauses using punctuation or SSML tags like `<break time="500ms"/>`. Highlight brand‑specific terminology and decide whether you want a conversational or formal tone; most platforms let you adjust speaking rate, pitch, and emphasis, which helps the AI sound less robotic.

Once you’ve generated a single voiceover, scale the process by batching multiple videos. Export the audio as a lossless WAV or high‑bitrate MP3, then import it directly into your video editor (e.g., Adobe Premiere, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve) and sync it with the visual timeline. Many AI tools also offer API access, so you can automate script‑to‑audio conversion with a simple webhook, saving hours when producing weekly series or multilingual versions.
