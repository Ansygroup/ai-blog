## Recommended Gear

Before you feed your script to an AI voice engine, clean it up for natural [speech](/posts/best-ai-tools-elevenlabs-for-translators-2026). Remove overly long sentences, keep each sentence under 20 words, and insert pauses using punctuation or SSML tags like `<break time="500ms"/>`. Highlight brand‑specific terminology and decide whether you want a conversational or formal tone; most [platforms](/posts/best-ai-powered-push-notification-ab-testing-platforms-2026) let you adjust speaking rate, [pitch](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026), and emphasis, which helps the AI sound less robotic.

Once you’ve generated a single voiceover, [scale](/posts/synthesia-for-teams-2026-review) the process by batching multiple videos. Export the audio as a lossless WAV or high‑bitrate MP3, then import it directly into your video [editor](/posts/cursor-editor-deep-dive-2026) (e.g., Adobe Premiere, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve) and sync it with the [visual](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) timeline. Many AI tools also offer API access, so you can automate script‑to‑audio conversion with a simple webhook, saving hours when producing weekly series or [multilingual](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools) versions.
