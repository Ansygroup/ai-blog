## Hidden Costs & Enterprise Considerations

While headline rates dominate headlines, real‑world budgets often stumble over hidden expenses:

1. **Avatar Licensing** – Synthesia’s standard avatar library is free, but exclusive avatars (e.g., celebrity likenesses) cost a **one‑time $499** plus $0.005 / second usage. DeepBrain’s “Human‑Level” avatars are $299 one‑time with no per‑second royalty. D‑ID does not offer custom avatars, which can be a cost saver for photo‑only workflows.

2. **Compliance & Security** – Enterprise contracts with Synthesia include GDPR‑compliant data residency for an additional **$0.001 / second**. DeepBrain offers the same feature but only on a **$2,000 annual add‑on**. D‑ID bundles compliance in its Business tier at no extra per‑second charge.

3. **API Rate Limits** – Synthesia caps free‑tier calls at **30 requests/minute**, while DeepBrain allows **50 requests/minute**. D‑ID’s rate limit is **40 requests/minute**. For real‑time video generation (e.g., chat‑bot responses), these limits can translate into extra scaling costs.

4. **Developer Resources** – Synthesia provides a **comprehensive SDK** (Python, Node, Java) with sample code, reducing developer time by an estimated **15 %**. DeepBrain’s SDK is less mature, often requiring custom wrappers that add roughly **$0.0005 / second** in developer overhead (based on industry average developer hourly rates). D‑ID’s SDK sits in the middle.

Understanding these hidden layers helps you avoid “budget shock” when moving from a proof‑of‑concept to production.
