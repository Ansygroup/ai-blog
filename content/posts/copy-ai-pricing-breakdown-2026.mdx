## FAQ

### How many words can I generate on the Copy AI Free plan in 2026?
You get up to **10,000 words per month** and a maximum of **5 AI‑generated images**. Exceeding these limits triggers a $0.12 per additional 1,000‑word charge.

### Does Copy AI offer a discount for annual billing?
Yes. Both the **Pro** and **Business** plans receive a **15 % discount** when you pay annually, lowering the effective monthly cost to $39.80 and $169.20 respectively.

### Can I integrate Copy AI with my own CMS via API?
Only the **Business** tier includes API access. It provides **10,000 free calls per month**; additional calls are billed at **$0.001 each**. The API supports JSON payloads for text generation, image [creation](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial), and tone adjustments.

### How does Copy AI’s SEO Optimizer differ from Jasper AI’s?
Copy AI’s 2026 SEO Optimizer integrates directly with **Surfer SEO**, delivering a full‑page “Scorecard” that compares keyword density, readability, and backlink potential against the top 10 ranking pages. Jasper AI’s optimizer offers keyword suggestions but lacks the comprehensive competitive gap analysis.

### Are there any hidden fees [for image](/posts/how-to-use-gpt-4-vision-api-for-image-analysis-and-descripti) generation?
The Business plan includes **1,000+ images** per month. Any images beyond that are billed at **$0.20 each**. Premium high‑resolution packs cost an extra **$30/mo**.

### What is the refund [policy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) if I cancel after a month?
Copy AI offers a **30‑day money‑back guarantee** for the Pro and Business plans when billed monthly. Annual [subscription](/posts/grammarly-pricing-breakdown-2026)s are non‑refundable but can be paused for up to three months.
