## Who Should Use This?

**Solo bloggers and creators** should start with **ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo)**. The combination of reasoning, image generation, and Custom GPTs covers nearly every use case. Pair it with a free Hemingway Editor for final polish.

**Marketing teams of 3+ people** should consider **Jasper AI ($49/mo)** or **Copy.ai ($49/mo)**. The Brand Voice training and workflow automation pay for themselves within the first campaign. We've seen teams reclaim 15+ hours per week.

**Authors and long-form writers** should use **Claude Pro ($20/mo)** for drafting and **Sudowrite ($19/mo)** for fiction polishing. The 200K context window means you can paste entire chapters for feedback](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-sentiment-analysis-in-customer-feedback-for-ecommerce).

**SEO publishers** running affiliate sites should choose **Writesonic ($20/mo)**. The factual citations and direct WordPress publishing save the most time per article in our test.

## Who Should Skip This?

If you're a student writing academ](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/gemini-for-students-2026)ic work, AI writing assistants can get you in trouble. Most universities now run AI detection (Turnitin's AI score, GPTZero) and even ChatGPT's "humanizer" tips](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/claude-prompt-guide-2026) leave detectable patterns. Use AI for outlining and research](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-keyword-research-2026), but write your final draft yourself.

If you write less than 500 words per week, $20/month isn't worth](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-honest-review) it. A free Grammarly account plus a human editor will serve you better. Also skip if your work requires verifiable citations — every tool we tested still fabricated sources 8-15% of the time.

If you're privacy-sensitive (lawyers, doctors, journalists with sources), avoid pasting sensitive data into any LLM. ChatGPT's "temporary chat" mode and Claude's zero-data-retention policy help, but nothing is foolproof.
