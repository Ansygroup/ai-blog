## Top AI Tools for Automated Data Analysis and Visualization

### 1. Tableau

Tableau is a popular AI [tool for data analysis and visualization. Its intuitive interface allows users to connect to various data sources, including Excel, Google](/news/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Sheets, and databases. Tableau offers a 14-day free trial and pricing starts at $35/user/month.

### 2. Power BI

Power BI, developed by [Microsoft, is another robust AI tool for data analysis and visualization. It offers a free version with limited](/posts/review-of-ai-driven-podcast-editing-tools-for-independent-podcasters-with-limited-budget) features and a paid plan starting at $9.99/user/month. Power BI integrates well with other [Microsoft products, such as Excel and Dynamics.

### 3. Looker Studio (formerly Google](/news/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Data Studio)

Looker Studio is a free AI [tool for data visualization that integrates seamlessly with Google](/news/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Analytics and Google Sheets. Its drag-and-drop interface makes it easy to create interactive dashboards and reports.
