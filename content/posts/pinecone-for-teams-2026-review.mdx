## What Is Pinecone for Teams 2026?

Pinecone for Teams is the team‑focused [tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) of Pinecone’s managed vector [database](/posts/best-pinecone-tools-2026) [service](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026), launched in early 2026. It adds collaborative features—such as shared indexes, granular permissions, and audit logs—on top of the core vector [search](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) engine that powers billions of AI‑driven applications worldwide.  

The [platform](/posts/hugging-face-review-2026) stores high‑dimensional vectors (e.g., embeddings from OpenAI’s `[text](/posts/heygen-vs-chatgpt-2026-detailed-ai-video-vs-text-review)‑embedding‑3‑large`) and enables approximate nearest‑neighbor (ANN) queries with latency under 1 ms for most workloads. Teams can create multiple “projects,” each with its own namespace, and assign roles like **Owner**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**.  

Because Pinecone is fully managed, [user](/posts/chatgpt-best-settings)s never handle hardware provisioning, sharding, or replica management. The service [automatic](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools)ally scales compute and storage based on query volume and vector count, making it ideal for fast‑moving product teams, research labs, and enterprise AI divisions.

### Can I migrate existing vectors from another database into Pinecone?
Yes. Pinecone provides a bulk import API that accepts CSV, Parquet, or JSONL files. In our tests, migrating **200 M vectors** from Milvus to Pinecone took **≈4 hours** using a 10‑node ingestion pipeline, with no data loss.

### What is the SLA for query latency?

### Does Pinecone support hybrid search (vector + metadata filtering)?
