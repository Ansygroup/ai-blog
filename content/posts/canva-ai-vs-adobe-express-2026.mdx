Canva and Adobe Express now overlap on most everyday design work. Both have a free plan with AI, both make presentations and export to PowerPoint, and both connect to the main AI assistants. The real differences are price, billing, template counts and how each company words its AI training policy. As of November 2026, Adobe Express is cheaper for one person and for small teams. Canva is bigger on templates and brand kits. Last tested: 27 November 2026.

A source note. Canva's pricing page refused our fetch with an unsupported client message, and Adobe's page loaded without its price cards. Prices and allowances below come from [NOXI's comparison](https://noxi.ai/blog/canva-vs-adobe-express), which says it opened both vendors' pages on 24 October 2026. NOXI publishes a presentation tool and discloses that. Check live prices before you buy.

## The verdict first

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest plan for one person | Adobe Express | Premium is $9.99 a month, against Canva Pro at $144 a year or $18 monthly |
| Template variety | Canva | 3.6+ million templates on Pro, against 519,000+ on Adobe Premium |
| Small team seats | Adobe Express | Teams is $4.99 per licence a month in year one, then $7.99 |
| Brand kits | Canva | 5 on Pro and 100 on Business |
| AI training statement | Adobe Express | Adobe says your content is never used to train its or partner models |
| You already pay for Creative Cloud | Adobe Express | Premium is included with Creative Cloud Standard and Pro, per Adobe |

## Pricing side by side

| Tier | Canva | Adobe Express |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0, up to 20 AI uses a month, 1.6+ million templates, 1 brand kit | $0, free daily generations, 268,000+ templates in the plan table |
| One person | Pro $144 a year ($12 a month) or $18 monthly | Premium $9.99 a month, no annual price shown |
| AI allowance | Up to 200 Premium AI uses a month | 250 generative credits a month |
| Team seat | Business $250 a year or $25 monthly per person | Teams $4.99 per licence in year one, then $7.99, 2 seat minimum |

Our arithmetic from those numbers: twelve months of Adobe Express Premium is $119.88, Canva Pro on annual billing is $144, and Canva Pro paid monthly is $216. For a team seat, Adobe Teams is $59.88 in year one and $95.88 afterward, while Canva Business is $250. Units for AI differ, so neither allowance wins on its own.

Two plan facts catch people out, per NOXI. Only one person can use a Canva Pro account, and Canva Teams is closed to new sign-ups, so a shared paid Canva plan means Business. Adobe's $4.99 team price applies to the first year only.

## AI tools

Canva's Magic Studio covers prompt to design, copy drafting and image and video generation, according to Tech Tool Crate. Adobe's page says the Firefly models were trained on licensed content such as Adobe Stock and public domain material, and describes outputs as designed to be commercially safe. Adobe's own page adds that beta features may not be cleared for commercial use, so read the label on each tool.

## Heavier AI use and add-ons

If the AI allowance on the base plan is not enough, both sell more. NOXI lists Adobe's Firefly Pro at $19.99 a month, which includes Express Premium features plus 4,000 credits. For Canva it lists an AI Pass add-on described as 40x more AI than Canva Pro, and says the add-on price is not on the plan page. Adobe also says Express Premium comes with Creative Cloud Standard and Pro, most single app plans and every Acrobat Studio subscription, so check what you already own before adding a second subscription.

## Schools, nonprofits and business plans

NOXI reports that Canva offers K-12 schools and not-for-profits most premium features for free, with nonprofits getting one team of up to 50 users. Adobe lists a free Express for K-12 Education license with Premium at no cost. On the business side, Adobe's own page describes three business options: Express for teams, Express for enterprise, and Express and Firefly for enterprise, with an admin console, 1 TB of cloud storage per user and 24/7 support. Custom Firefly models trained on brand assets are listed for enterprise plans only.

## Where each one fails

Canva fails on cost for teams and on flexibility, since a single Pro account is for one person only. Its AI training default is not stated on the current pages, according to NOXI, so check your Privacy Settings. Adobe Express fails on template depth, and its monthly only price means no annual discount is shown. Premium editing features such as remove background need a paid plan.

## Who should skip which

Skip Canva if you want the lowest price per person or per team seat. Skip Adobe Express if you rely on the template library or on many brand kits. ## Test it yourself

1. Pick one real task, such as a social post and a five slide deck.
2. Build both in the free plans using each tool's AI.
3. Count how many AI uses you spent against the free limits.
4. Export the deck to PowerPoint and check the fonts.
5. Open your privacy settings in each and note what they say about AI training.

## Which should you pick

Pick Adobe Express Premium at $9.99 if price matters and you do not need huge template libraries. Pick Canva Pro if you make many designs for different brands and want the templates. If you already have Creative Cloud, check first, because Express Premium may already be included. Prices were last verified through NOXI on 24 October 2026, so confirm them live.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Adobe Express cheaper than Canva?

For one person, yes. A 24 October 2026 review lists Adobe Express Premium at $9.99 a month and Canva Pro at $144 a year or $18 monthly.

### Does Canva or Adobe train AI on my content?

Adobe says your content is never used to train its models or partner models. Canva's current pages leave the default unstated, so check your Privacy Settings.

### Can a team share one Canva Pro account?

No. Only one person can use a Canva Pro account, and a shared paid plan means Canva Business.
