Grammarly and ProWritingAid both fix [grammar](/posts/grammarly-review-2026-test-pricing-alternatives), but they are built for different [writing](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-journalists-2026). Grammarly targets work [email](/posts/best-ai-powered-influencer-outreach-email-templates-2026), documents and [teams](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-marketing-teams-2026). ProWritingAid targets fiction, manuscripts and long reports, with deep style reports and a lifetime license. As of November 2026, the price gap is small on annual billing and the feature gap is large. Last tested: 27 November 2026.

Where [prices](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) come from: both vendor pages loaded, but they [show](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-podcasters-2026) monthly rates only for annual billing or do not show the billing period. ## What each free plan does

Grammarly Free covers spelling and grammar and gives 100 AI prompts a month. ProWritingAid Free allows 2 runs of each [line](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026) [editing](/posts/ai-youtube-chapter-timestamp-generators-2026) report per day on 500 words of text, plus 10 Rephrases a day. Grammarly's free plan is the better daily checker. ProWritingAid's free plan is a trial of its reports.

## What [the paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) plans add

Grammarly Pro raises AI prompts to 2,000 per member per month and adds tone adjustment, full sentence rewrites, citation [consistency](/posts/review-best-ai-powered-brand-voice-checkers-2026), accidental plagiarism checks and AI text detection. Enterprise lists unlimited prompts, SAML single sign-on, [data](/posts/ai-for-generating-schema-markup-for-seo-2026-tutorial) loss [prevention](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) and BYOK encryption.

ProWritingAid Premium lists unlimited line editing runs and rephrases, 25+ analysis reports, a [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) style guide, customizable suggestions, author comparison and unlimited document storage. Premium Pro adds 30 [chapter](/posts/ai-youtube-chapter-timestamp-generators-2026) critiques a day, 50 Sparks a day and live workshops with authors and editors.

## Teams, [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) and admin

Grammarly's plan table lists account roles and permissions, SAML single sign-on, SCIM, managed mode, user groups, custom roles, audit logs and [enterprise](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-agencies-2026) key management, with the heaviest controls on Enterprise. Free allows 1 style guide and 1 brand tone, while the paid columns list unlimited. Grammarly cites a SOC 2 Type 2 report on its plans page.

ProWritingAid lists 100% data [security](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) and privacy as a feature and offers comment and share on Premium. For a team of one or two writers that is enough. For a company that needs single sign-on and audit logs, the Grammarly page is where those controls appear.

## How to decide in one afternoon

Take three real documents: an email you sent this week, a report or article, and a piece of [creative](/posts/best-ai-runway-tools-for-designers-2026-top-picks) writing if you write any. Run all three through both free plans. Grammarly should win the first. ProWritingAid should surface more on the third. If one tool wins all three for you, buy that one on annual billing. If they split, match the tool to the type of writing you do most hours a week, since that is where a yearly plan pays for itself.

## Where each one fails

Grammarly fails on [cost](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) if you pay monthly, since $30 a month is two and a half times the annual rate. ProWritingAid's pricing page lists no team security controls in the sections we read, and its refund [terms](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) are strict: yearly and lifetime plans can be refunded within three days, and monthly plans cannot be refunded.

## Who should skip which

Skip Grammarly if you write novels and want reports on pacing, repetition and sensory detail. Skip ProWritingAid if you mostly write [short](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-instagram-reels-2026) messages and want suggestions inside every app. For more options, read [Grammarly alternatives for teachers](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-teachers-2026-grammarly-alternatives) and [Grammarly tools for agencies](/posts/best-ai-tools-grammarly-for-agencies-2026).

## Test it yourself

1. Paste a 500 word page of your own writing into both free plans.
2. Count the suggestions you accept in five [minutes](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026).
3. Note which flags are real [errors](/posts/fixing-undefined-errors-ai-apps) and which are style choices you reject.
4. Run a work email through both and see which suggestion fits your tone.
5. If you write fiction, run the repetition and sensory reports on ProWritingAid and see if you learn something.

## Which should you pick

Pick Grammarly Pro on annual billing at $144 if you write for work. Pick ProWritingAid Premium at $120 a year, or $399 once, if you write long form and want reports. Never pay monthly for either unless [you need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) it for one month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ProWritingAid cheaper than Grammarly?

On annual billing, yes. ProWritingAid Premium is $120 a year and Grammarly Pro is $144 a year, per a 16 November 2026 third party check.

### Does ProWritingAid have a lifetime plan?

Yes. Premium is $399 once and Premium Pro is $699 once, according to the same comparison. Grammarly has no lifetime option.

### Can I get a refund on ProWritingAid?

Yearly and lifetime plans can be refunded if you ask within three days of purchase. Monthly plans cannot be refunded.

### Which is better for fiction?

ProWritingAid. It lists 25+ analysis reports, story critique tools and author comparison.
