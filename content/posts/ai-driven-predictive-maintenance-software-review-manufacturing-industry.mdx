## What Is AI-Driven Predictive Maintenance Software?

AI-driven predictive maintenance software uses machine learning algorithms, sensor [data](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial), and other sources to predict when equipment will fail, allowing for proactive maintenance and reducing downtime. This approach is more effective than traditional reactive maintenance, which relies on scheduled maintenance or symptom-based repairs.
