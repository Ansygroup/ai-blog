### Can I import my own custom avatar into HeyGen for Teams?
Yes, the Professional and Enterprise plans include a “Custom Avatar Builder” that lets you upload a 3D scan or a set of reference images. HeyGen’s design partner network will then create a rigged avatar for a one‑time fee of $1,200.

### What is the maximum video length HeyGen can generate?
The platform supports videos up to **30 minutes** in a single render. For longer productions, you can chain multiple renders and stitch them together using any standard video editor.

### Does HeyGen integrate with my existing LMS (e.g., Moodle or Canvas)?
HeyGen offers RESTful APIs and pre‑built connectors for major LMS platforms. You can push generated videos directly into a course module via a simple POST request, and the API returns a secure, expiring URL for playback.

### How does HeyGen’s pricing compare to per‑video pricing models?
HeyGen’s subscription model (per‑user) typically results in a **lower cost per video** when you produce more than 30 videos per month per user. By contrast, Synthesia charges $30 per video after a limited free quota, which can quickly outpace HeyGen’s flat monthly fee for high‑volume teams.

### Is there a free trial available for HeyGen for Teams?
