## What Is Website Accessibility Auditing?

Website accessibility auditing involves testing a website to ensure it meets the Web [Content](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) and the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). This includes checking for [features](/posts/gemini-api-guide) such as keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and high contrast mode. AI-powered website accessibility auditing tools can help automate this process, saving time and reducing the risk of human error.
