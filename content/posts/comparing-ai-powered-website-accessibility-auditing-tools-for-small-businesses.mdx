## What Is Website Accessibility Auditing?

Website accessibility auditing involves [testing](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026) a website to ensure it meets the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) and the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). This includes checking for features such as keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and high contrast mode. AI-powered website accessibility auditing tools can help automate this process, saving time and reducing the risk of human error.

No, while AI-powered website accessibility auditing tools can detect many accessibility issues, they may not catch all problems.
