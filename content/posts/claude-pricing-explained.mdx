## FAQ

### What is the difference between Claude's Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus plans?
The main difference lies in the AI capabilities and pricing. Haiku is ideal for basic tasks, Sonnet for advanced tasks, and Opus for large-scale applications.

### Can I try Claude for free?
Yes, Claude offers a free trial with 100,000 tokens, perfect for testing the AI.

### How does Claude compare to ChatGPT in [terms](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) of pricing?
Claude's pricing is competitive with ChatGPT, offering a more affordable entry point with its Haiku plan.

### What are the main advantages of Claude's Team plan?
The Team plan offers additional features, including more tokens, priority support, and enhanced [security](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide).

### Is Claude suitable for large-scale applications?
Yes, Claude's Opus plan is designed for large-scale applications, offering premium AI capabilities and priority support.

## Final Verdict

In conclusion, Claude offers a competitive pricing structure with plans starting at $0.15 per million tokens. **The Haiku plan is a great starting point for individuals and [small](/posts/best-ai-cursor-tools-small-business-owners-2026) businesses**. For more advanced features, consider upgrading to the Sonnet or Opus plans. When compared to ChatGPT and Google Gemini, Claude provides a robust set of features at an affordable price.
