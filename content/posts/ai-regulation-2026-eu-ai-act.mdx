## What Is the EU AI Act 2026?

The EU AI Act 2026 is a proposed regulation aimed at ensuring AI systems are safe, trustworthy, and respect human rights. The regulation will apply to a wide range of AI systems, from chatbots and virtual assistants to more complex systems used in healthcare and finance. For example, AI-powered medical diagnosis tools like **MedWhat** and **DeepMind** will need to comply with the regulation.

## How Will the EU AI Act 2026 Regulate AI Systems?

The EU AI Act 2026 will categorize AI systems based on their risk level, with high-risk systems facing stricter requirements. The regulation will consider factors such as the system's impact on human rights, safety, and transparency. AI systems will be classified into three categories:

* **Unrestricted**: AI systems with minimal risk, such as chatbots and virtual assistants.
* **High-risk**: AI systems with significant risk, such as those used in healthcare, finance, and education.
* **Very high-risk**: AI systems with extreme risk, such as those used in critical infrastructure and law enforcement.

## Requirements for High-Risk AI Systems

High-risk AI systems will need to meet strict requirements, including:

* **Transparency**: AI systems must provide clear information about their decision-making processes.
* **Explainability**: AI systems must be able to explain their decisions and actions.
* **Human oversight**: AI systems must have human oversight and control mechanisms in place.
