## What Are AI Chatbots?

AI [chatbots](/posts/best-claude-free-alternative-2026) are computer programs designed to simulate human-like conversations. They use natural [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing ([NLP](/posts/ai-content-moderation-system-user-generated-content)) and machine learning algorithms to understand and respond to [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026) input. ChatGPT and Claude are two popular AI chatbots that have gained significant attention in recent years.

## ChatGPT: A Leader in AI Chatbots

ChatGPT is an AI chatbot developed by [OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026). It was launched in November 2022 and has since become one of the most popular AI chatbots on the [market](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-market-research-2026). ChatGPT uses a transformer-based architecture to understand and respond to [user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026) input. It's known for its ability to generate human-like text and engage in conversation.

## Claude: A New Challenger

Claude is an AI chatbot developed by [Anthropic](/posts/claude-mythos-2026-anthropic-most-capable-model-review). It was launched in 2023 and has quickly gained popularity due to its transparent pricing [model](/posts/claude-4-opus-vs-sonnet-comparison-2026) [and user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026)-friendly interface. Claude uses a similar transformer-based architecture to ChatGPT but has some key differences in its approach to AI.

## Performance [Comparison](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison)

In terms of performance, both ChatGPT and Claude deliver impressive results. However, ChatGPT has a slight edge in terms of accuracy and [response](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-cold-email-outreach-2026) time. According to a review by TechRadar, ChatGPT scored 4.5/5 in terms of accuracy, while Claude scored 4/5.
