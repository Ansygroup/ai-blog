## What Are AI Chatbots?

AI [chatbots are computer programs designed to simulate human-like conversations. They use natural language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) processing ([NLP) and machine learning algorithms to understand and respond to user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026) input. ChatGPT and Claude are two popular AI chatbots that have gained significant attention in recent years.

## ChatGPT: A Leader in AI Chatbots

ChatGPT is an AI chatbot developed by OpenAI](/posts/openai-sora-shutdown-2026). It was launched in November 2022 and has since become one of the most popular AI chatbots on the [market. ChatGPT uses a transformer-based architecture to understand and respond to user](/posts/chatgpt-reddit-reviews-2026) input. It's known for its ability to generate human-like text and engage in conversation.

## Claude: A New Challenger

Claude is an AI chatbot developed by [Anthropic. It was launched in 2023 and has quickly gained popularity due to its transparent pricing model](/posts/claude-4-opus-vs-sonnet-comparison-2026) [and user-friendly interface. Claude uses a similar transformer-based architecture to ChatGPT but has some key differences in its approach to AI.
