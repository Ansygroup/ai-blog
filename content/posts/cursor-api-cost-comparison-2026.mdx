## FAQ

### What is the difference between token and character pricing in Cursor API?

Cursor charges per token, where a token roughly equals 4 characters of English text. This aligns with most GPT‑style models, allowing developers to predict costs based on prompt length.

### Does Cursor offer a free trial beyond the free tier?

Cursor’s free tier is effectively a trial, providing 5 million tokens/month with no credit card required. For larger workloads, a 30‑day trial of the Pro tier is [available](/posts/qa-what-is-the-best-ai-image-generator-available-today) upon request.

### Are there any usage limits on the free tier?

Yes, the free tier caps each request at 50 k tokens and limits the context window to 30 k tokens. Exceeding these limits will result in request rejection.

### How does Cursor’s “context‑aware pruning” work?

The pruning algorithm analyzes the prompt for redundancy and removes non‑essential tokens before sending them to the model, reducing token count without compromising output quality.
