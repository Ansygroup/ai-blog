## What Is Automated YouTube [Video](/posts/ai-video-editing-software-comparison) Captioning and Subtitling?

Automated YouTube video closed captioning and subtitling use artificial intelligence to generate text transcripts of your video [content](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation). This text is then synced with the video timeline, creating captions and subtitles that appear during playback. This process makes your videos more accessible to a wider audience, including those who are deaf or hard of hearing, and viewers who prefer to watch content in their [native](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026).
