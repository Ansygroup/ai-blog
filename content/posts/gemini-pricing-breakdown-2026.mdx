## Hidden Costs, Rate Limits, and Billing Nuances

While the headline rates look attractive, a few hidden factors can affect the bottom line:

1. **Over‑age Penalties** – Once you exceed the allocated token quota, Gemini charges **$0.025 per 1 M tokens**, a 39 % jump from the base rate.  
2. **Rate Limits** – Free tier is capped at **30 RPS** (requests per second). Pro tier allows **200 RPS** by default; higher limits](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-api-cost-comparison-2026) require a $2,000/month add‑on.  
3. **Data Egress** – Exporting large dataset](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/best-ai-data-labeling-platforms-ml-2026-review)s (e.g., embeddings) incurs standard Google Cloud egress fees ($0.08 per GB for North America).  
4. **Fine‑Tuning Credits** – While fine‑tuning is optional, each train](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/auto-ml-tutorial)ing hour costs $0.12, plus a one‑time $500 model‑hosting fee for custom checkpoints.  
5. **Currency Conversion](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-vs-free-version-2026)** – Prices are listed in USD; European customers may see a 1–3 % variance due to FX rates.

Understanding these nuances helps prevent surprise invoices, especially for startups that may unintentionally exceed free‑tier limits](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/chatgpt-free-tier-limits-2026) during a product](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/ai-automated-ecommerce-product-bundle](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/ai-automated-ecommerce-product-bundle-pricing)-pricing) launch.
