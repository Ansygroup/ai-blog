## FAQ

### How much does Loom cost for a 50‑person team?

A 50‑person Business plan would cost **$12 × 50 = $600 per month** (billed annually) plus any add‑ons like extra storage or HIPAA compliance. Loom also offers volume discounts for teams over 200 users.

### Does Loom support automatic [transcription](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) in languages other than English?

Yes. Loom’s AI captions currently support **English, Spanish, French, German, and Japanese** with accuracy rates ranging from 92 % to 96 % depending on language. Additional languages are slated for Q2 2027.

### Can I embed Loom videos in a public [website](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) without a Loom account?

Absolutely. Loom provides an **iframe embed code** that works without requiring viewers to log in. You can also enable password protection or domain whitelisting for extra security.

### How does Loom’s video analytics compare to Vidyard’s?

Loom’s Business dashboard offers **heatmaps, viewer drop‑off points, and average watch time** per segment, whereas Vidyard’s Pro tier only shows total views and basic engagement metrics. In our tests, Loom’s granular data helped a [marketing](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial) team improve video completion rates by **23 %**.

### Is there a way to bulk delete videos in Loom?

Yes. The Enterprise admin console includes a **bulk actions panel** where you can select multiple videos and delete, archive, or move them to a collection in one click.

### Does Loom integrate with CRM platforms like Salesforce?

While Loom does not have a native Salesforce connector, you can use **Zapier** or **Workato** to automatically [push](/posts/best-ai-powered-push-notification-ab-testing-platforms-2026) new Loom video links to Salesforce records, creating a seamless sales workflow.
