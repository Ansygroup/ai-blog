### Are there any [hidden](/posts/kaggle-api-cost-comparison-2026) costs in the pricing tables above?

All listed [prices](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) are **base subscription fees**. Additional costs may arise from **custom avatar creation**, **extra rendering minutes**, or **premium voice‑cloning**. Agencies should factor in these variables when budgeting for large‑scale campaigns.
