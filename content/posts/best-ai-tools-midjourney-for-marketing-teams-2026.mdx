## FAQ

### How does Midjourney Enterprise enforce brand guidelines?

Midjourney Enterprise includes a Brand Guardrails module where you upload HEX color palettes, logo masks, and style references. The engine automatically respects these cons[train](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial)ts, rejecting prompts that violate them. Teams can also lock specific prompt parameters to ensure consistency across campaigns.

### Can PromptBase prompts be customized for my brand’s voice?

Yes. PromptBase Pro users can request “prompt [tailoring](/posts/best-ai-powered-resume-tailoring-ats-optimization-2026)” where the [seller](/posts/best-ai-perplexity-tools-for-ecommerce-sellers-2026) adjusts wording, tone, and style cues to match your brand guide. The marketplace shows conversion lift percentages for each prompt, letting you pick the most effective starting point.

### Is there a free trial for Lexica’s Asset Hub?

Lexica offers a 14‑day free trial with 500 asset uploads and full AI‑tagging [feature](/posts/copy-ai-vs-chatgpt-2026)s. After the trial, you can downgrade to the [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) (limited to 1,000 searchable tags) or upgrade to the Pro plan.

### How do I integrate Midjourney images into Meta’s Dynamic Creative?

Export your Midjourney assets to a [cloud](/posts/pinecone-setup-guide-2026) storage bucket (e.g., AWS S3) and use Lexica’s API to pull the AI‑generated tags. Then map those tags to Meta’s Dynamic Creative fields via the Ads Manager UI or the Marketing API. This workflow reduces manual asset tagging by **80%**.

### What’s the biggest limitation of using Midjourney for video ads?

Midjourney generates still images only. To create motion, [you need](/posts/copy-ai-free-tier-limits-2026) a secondary tool like Runway Gen‑2 or Adobe After Effects. The extra step adds cost and time, so teams focused on video‑first strategies may prefer native video [generators](/posts/ai-code-doc-generators-python-javascript-2026).

### How does Adobe Firefly’s pricing compare to Midjourney for a 15‑person team?

Adobe Firefly is bundled with the Creative Cloud All Apps plan at $54.99 per seat, totaling **$824.85/month** for 15 users. Midjourney Enterprise for the same headcount would be roughly **$1,199/month** (includes 25 seats). While Firefly is cheaper per seat, Midjourney offers higher image fidelity and brand‑guardrails that can offset the price difference for visual‑intensive campaigns.
