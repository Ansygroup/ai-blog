Key features:

* **Live Captions & Highlights:** While you speak, Otter adds speaker labels and timestamps, letting you highlight key moments with a single click.
* **Searchable Archive:** Every transcript is indexed, enabling keyword search across all past recordings—a lifesaver when you need to locate a specific contract clause.
* **Collaboration:** Up to 5 collaborators can comment, edit, and export simultaneously, making it easy for a writer‑designer duo to co‑author a blog post from a recorded interview.
* **Export Options:** Export to DOCX, PDF, SRT, and even CSV for data‑driven analysis.

## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai for noisy environments?

Otter.ai’s noise‑cancellation algorithm yields about **94 % accuracy** in coffee‑shop or co‑working spaces, compared to 99.5 % in quiet rooms. Using a good microphone can push accuracy back above 97 %.

### Can I export Otter.ai transcripts directly to WordPress?

Yes. Otter offers a one‑click export to **DOCX**, **PDF**, and **HTML**. The HTML version can be pasted into the WordPress Gutenberg editor, preserving timestamps and speaker labels.

### Does Fireflies.ai integrate with project‑management tools?

Fireflies.ai integrates natively with **Asana**, **Trello**, **Monday.com**, and **ClickUp**. After a meeting, the AI‑generated summary can be sent as a task or comment, automating follow‑up.

### What is the best tool for generating subtitles for YouTube videos?

For YouTube creators, **Descript** is the most efficient because it combines transcription, subtitle export (SRT), and audio editing in one platform. Its “Studio Sound” AI also cleans up background noise, improving subtitle timing.

### Is there a free way to get multilingual transcription?

Sonix.ai offers a **$10 /hr** pay‑as‑you‑go model, which is cheaper than most subscription services for occasional multilingual work. Alternatively, **Google Cloud Speech‑to‑Text** provides a free tier of 60 minutes per month, though it requires technical setup.

### How does Rev.ai handle industry‑specific terminology?

Rev.ai allows you to upload a **custom vocabulary list** (up to 10,000 terms). In our tests with legal terminology, accuracy rose from 93 % to **98 %**, making it ideal for freelancers in niche sectors.
