## What Is Automated E-commerce Product Bundle Pricing?

Automated e-commerce product bundle pricing is a pricing [strategy](/posts/ai-voice-search-seo-keyword-clustering-boost-rankings-2026) that uses AI to create bundles of products that appeal to customers and drive sales. This approach involves analyzing customer behavior, preferences, and purchasing history to create personalized bundles that are likely to be of interest to them.

By using AI-driven product bundle pricing, businesses can increase customer satisfaction, reduce cart abandonment [rates, and drive revenue growth. In fact, a study by RetailMeNot found that product bundling can increase sales by up to 15% (Source: RetailMeNot](https://www.retailmenot.com/blog/product-bundling-statistics/)).
