## What Is [Automated](/posts/ai-data-cleaning-tutorial) E-commerce Product Bundle Pricing?

Automated e-commerce product bundle pricing is a pricing strategy that uses AI to create bundles of products that appeal to customers and drive sales. This approach involves analyzing [customer](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-automated-customer-feedback-analysis) behavior, preferences, and purchasing history to create personalized bundles that are likely to be of interest to them.

By using AI-driven product bundle pricing, businesses can increase customer satisfaction, reduce cart abandonment [rates](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-cold-email-outreach-2026), and drive revenue growth. In fact, a study by RetailMeNot found that product bundling can increase sales by up to 15% (Source: [RetailMeNot](https://www.retailmenot.com/blog/product-bundling-statistics/)).
