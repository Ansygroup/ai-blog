## The Shift Toward Deterministic Verification and Multi-Agent Audits

Early AI research relied on probabilistic text generation, which caused models to guess citations that sounded plausible but did not exist. By late 2026, the industry standard has shifted toward deterministic citation architectures.

2.  **Auditing Agents:** Cross-reference the model’s draft against the extracted excerpts to flag unsupport](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-automating-customer-support-on-facebook](https://ai-blog-ten-steel.vercel.app/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-automating-customer-support-on-facebook-me)-me)ed claims before displaying the final output.
