## FAQ

### How accurate is ElevenLabs Speech‑to‑Text on noisy audio?

### Can I use ElevenLabs Voice Cloning for commercial products?

Yes. The Pro and Enterprise plans grant commercial usage rights. The licensing terms allow unlimited distribution of cloned audio, provided you retain the original speaker’s consent and comply with ElevenLabs’ ethical use policy.

### What is the latency for the Real‑Time Streaming SDK in a global deployment?

The SDK delivers sub‑50 ms end‑to‑end latency in North America and Europe when using regional endpoints. For Asia‑Pacific, latency rises to ~62 ms, still well below the 100 ms threshold for most interactive applications.

### Does ElevenLabs support SSML for advanced speech markup?

ElevenLabs uses its own JSON‑based markup, but it also accepts SSML (Speech Synthesis Markup Language) for compatibility. The platform maps SSML tags like `<prosody>` and `<break>` to its internal “emotion tags,” preserving the intended effect.

### How does ElevenLabs handle data privacy for speech recordings?

All recordings are encrypted at rest (AES‑256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). ElevenLabs offers a “data‑no‑retain” option where raw audio is automatically deleted after processing, satisfying GDPR and CCPA requirements.

### Is there a free tier for developers to experiment?

Yes. The free tier includes 500 k characters of TTS and 60 minutes of STT each month, plus access to the Starter clone (30‑second sample). No credit card is required to activate the free tier.
