### What to Look For
When it comes to buying noise-cancelling headphones, there are several factors to consider. First and foremost, you need to look at the type of noise cancellation technology used. There are two main types: active noise cancellation (ANC) and passive noise cancellation. ANC uses microphones to pick up ambient noise and produces a "negative" sound wave to cancel it out, while passive noise cancellation uses physical barriers to block out sound. You should also consider the battery life, comfort, and sound quality of the headphones.

Another crucial factor to consider is the price. Noise-cancelling headphones can range from under $100 to over $300, so it's essential to set a budget before making a purchase. Additionally, you should think about the features that are important to you, such as wireless connectivity, microphone quality, and durability. Some headphones also come with additional features like transparency mode, which allows you to hear your surroundings while still listening to music.

Finally, you should read reviews and do your research before making a purchase. Look for headphones with good sound quality, comfortable design, and reliable noise cancellation. You should also check the warranty and customer support offered by the manufacturer. By considering these factors, you can find the perfect pair of noise-cancelling headphones for your needs.
