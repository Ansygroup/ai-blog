### What to Look For
When shopping for noise-cancelling headphones, there are several factors to consider to ensure you find the right pair for your needs. First, consider the type of noise cancellation technology used, such as active noise cancellation (ANC) or passive noise isolation. ANC uses electronic noise cancellation to eliminate ambient sounds, while passive noise isolation relies on physical barriers to block out noise. Additionally, think about the headphone's sound quality, comfort, and battery life, as these will impact your overall listening experience.

Another important factor to consider is the headphone's connectivity options and compatibility with your devices. Look for headphones with Bluetooth connectivity, USB-C charging, and possibly even a 3.5mm audio jack for wired connections. Some headphones may also offer additional features like transparency mode, which allows you to hear your surroundings while listening to music, or customizable EQ settings to tailor the sound to your preferences.

Lastly, consider the headphone's design and build quality, as well as any additional features that may be important to you, such as foldability, water resistance, or a built-in microphone. With so many options available, it's essential to weigh your priorities and find a pair that meets your specific needs and budget.
