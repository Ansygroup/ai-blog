### What to Look For
When shopping for noise-cancelling headphones, there are several key factors to consider. First and foremost, look for headphones with advanced noise-cancelling technology, such as active noise cancellation (ANC) or hybrid noise cancellation. This technology uses microphones to capture ambient noise and produce a "negative" sound wave to cancel it out, resulting in a quieter and more immersive listening experience.

Another important factor to consider is sound quality. Look for headphones with high-fidelity audio, deep bass, and clear treble. Additionally, consider the battery life of the headphones, as well as their comfort and durability. If you plan to use your headphones for long periods of time, look for ones with comfortable ear cups and a sturdy headband. Finally, consider the price point and whether the headphones offer good value for the cost.

It's also important to consider the additional features that come with noise-cancelling headphones. Some popular features include transparency mode, which allows you to hear your surroundings while still listening to music, and customizable EQ settings, which allow you to tailor the sound to your preferences. Others may include features like voice assistant integration, wireless charging, and foldable designs for easy storage.
