### What to Look For
When shopping for noise-cancelling headphones, there are several key factors to consider. Firstly, the type of noise cancellation is crucial - active noise cancellation (ANC) is generally more effective than passive noise isolation. Additionally, consider the headphones' frequency response, driver size, and impedance to ensure they can produce high-quality sound. The battery life and charging method are also important, as you'll want headphones that can last throughout your workday.

Another critical aspect is comfort and ergonomics. Look for headphones with adjustable headbands, soft ear cushions, and breathable materials to prevent discomfort during extended wear. The weight and foldability of the headphones are also important if you plan to travel or commute with them. Finally, consider the additional features such as microphone quality, voice assistant integration, and app support to enhance your overall listening experience.

In terms of specific features, transparency mode, which allows you to hear your surroundings while listening to music, can be useful for commuters or those who need to be aware of their environment. Hi-res audio and deep bass can also enhance your listening experience, making it more immersive and engaging. By considering these factors, you can find the perfect pair of noise-cancelling headphones for your deep work needs.
