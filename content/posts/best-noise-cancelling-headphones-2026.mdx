### What to Look For
When shopping for noise-cancelling headphones, there are several key factors to consider. First and foremost, the quality of noise cancellation is crucial - look for headphones with advanced noise-cancelling technology, such as active noise control or hybrid noise cancellation. Additionally, consider the battery life, comfort, and sound quality of the headphones, as these will all impact your overall listening experience.

Another important consideration is the type of audio codec used by the headphones. Hi-res audio codecs, such as aptX or LDAC, can provide a more detailed and immersive listening experience. Furthermore, consider the connectivity options - Bluetooth 5.0 or higher is recommended for stable and reliable connections. Some headphones also offer additional features, such as transparency mode, which allows you to hear your surroundings while still listening to music.

The design and build quality of the headphones are also important factors to consider. Look for headphones with durable materials, such as metal or high-quality plastic, and a comfortable fit. Some headphones also offer foldable designs, which can be convenient for travel. Finally, consider the price - while high-end headphones can offer advanced features and superior sound quality, there are also many affordable options available that can provide excellent value for money.
