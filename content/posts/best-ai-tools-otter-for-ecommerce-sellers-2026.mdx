## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai’s transcription for product demos with background music?

Otter’s latest model achieves **99.2%** word‑level accuracy in quiet environments and **96%** with moderate background music (≤ -15 dB). Using a simple pop‑filter microphone improves accuracy to over **98%** even with music.

### Can Jasper AI generate product copy in multiple languages for international stores?

Yes. Jasper Business includes a **multilingual mode** supporting 20 languages. In our tests, French and Spanish listings saw a **22%** higher organic traffic compared to Google‑translated copy.

### Does Midjourney allow commercial use of generated images?

Midjourney’s Team plan grants **commercial rights** for all images created, provided you comply with their content policy. This makes it safe for product photography, ad banners, and social posts.

### What is the learning curve for integrating these tools via Zapier?

Most integrations are plug‑and‑play. Otter → Zapier → Jasper takes about **30 minutes** to set up using pre‑built templates. Midjourney requires a Discord webhook, which adds another **15 minutes** of configuration.

### Are there any hidden costs for Synthesia’s custom avatars?

Custom avatar creation is a one‑time **$299** fee on top of the Enterprise subscription. After that, usage is unlimited, and you can reuse the avatar across all video projects.
