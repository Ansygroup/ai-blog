## Output Flexibility: Formats, Resolutions, and Commercial Rights

All three platforms now support PNG, JPEG, and WebP exports, but each has quirks:

- **Midjourney**: Native PNG with optional lossless compression. Upscaling to 8 k retains vector‑like sharpness, but the file size can exceed 12 MB. Commercial rights are granted globally, but you must retain the “Generated by Midjourney” attribution for the [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026).
- **Firefly**: Offers a “Brand‑Safe PNG” that strips any AI‑watermark automatically. You can export directly to PSD, preserving layers when using Generative Fill. Adobe’s licensing guarantees royalty‑free commercial [use for](/posts/how-to-use-ai-for-creating-interactive-ebooks-and-digital-ma) any asset created after July 2025.
- **DALL·E 3**: Supports PNG, JPEG, and SVG (vector conversion via API). The new “Transparent Background” flag lets you generate PNGs with alpha channels—a boon for UI designers. Commercial rights are unconditional, and OpenAI provides a “No‑Attribution” clause for all [paid](/posts/copy-ai-vs-free-version) outputs.

Stable Diffusion XL 1.0 (self‑hosted) still offers the most format flexibility (including TIFF and EXR), but you must manage your own licensing and hardware costs.

## FAQ

### What are the main differences between Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, and DALL·E 2026?
Midjourney focuses on artistic, stylized outputs with strong community prompts, Adobe Firefly emphasizes safe, commercial‑grade images with built‑in content filters and integration into Adobe apps, while DALL·E 2026 offers versatile, high‑fidelity generation with advanced text‑to‑image capabilities, multimodal extensions, and a broader range of licensing options.

### Which AI image generator produces the highest resolution images in 2026?
DALL·E 2026 currently leads in maximum resolution, supporting up to 12K × 12K pixels for premium users, whereas Midjourney tops out around 8K and Adobe Firefly offers up to 6K with its latest Pro plan.

### Are the images created by Midjourney, Firefly, and DALL·E safe for commercial use?
Adobe Firefly is designed specifically for commercial use with clear licensing and no copyright concerns. DALL·E 2026 provides commercial rights for generated content under its standard license, but users must avoid restricted [subject](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026)s. Midjourney’s commercial policy allows usage for paid plans, but users should review the latest [terms](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) to ensure compliance with any attribution or usage limits.

### How do the pricing models of Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, and DALL·E 2026 compare?
Midjourney uses a subscription model starting at $10/month for basic access, with higher tiers for faster GPU time. Adobe Firefly is bundled with Creative Cloud subscriptions (e.g., $52.99/month for the full suite) and offers a pay‑as‑you‑go credit system for extra renders. DALL·E 2026 provides a free tier with limited credits and a pay‑per‑image or subscription plan (e.g., $15/month for 115 credits) for higher‑volume users.
