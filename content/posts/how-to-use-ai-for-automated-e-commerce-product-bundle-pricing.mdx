## Introduction to AI-Powered Pricing
AI is revolutionizing e-commerce pricing strategies, enabling businesses to make data-driven decisions and stay competitive. ## Understanding Product Bundle Pricing
Product bundle pricing involves offering multiple products together at a discounted price. This strategy can increase average order value, enhance customer satisfaction, and reduce inventory costs. However, determining the optimal bundle price can be challenging, especially in dynamic markets. AI-powered pricing tools can help e-commerce businesses overcome this challenge by analyzing customer purchase history, browsing behavior, and market trends to identify profitable bundle pricing opportunities.
