Midjourney Strengths:
├── Hyper-realistic textures and skin tones
├── Unmatched artistic flexibility (oil painting, 3D render, vector)

Photosonic is built directly into the Writesonic dashboard. While it has improved significantly with its 3.0 update, it is designed for speed and convenience rather than high-end digital art.
