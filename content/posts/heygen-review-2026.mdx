### What is HeyGen and how does it differ from other AI video generators?
HeyGen is an AI-powered video creation platform that lets users generate short videos from text, images, or scripts. ### Can I use HeyGen for free and what are the [limitations](/posts/copy-ai-limitations-2026) of the free plan?
Yes, HeyGen offers a [free tier](/posts/copilot-free-tier-limits-2026) that includes up to 10 video exports per month, a watermark on each video, and access to a limited set of avatars and voice styles. The free plan also caps video length at 30 seconds and restricts resolution to 720p. To unlock higher resolutions, longer videos, and watermark‑free exports, you need to upgrade to a paid subscription.

### How accurate is HeyGen’s lip‑sync and facial expression technology?
HeyGen uses a proprietary deep‑learning [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) that aligns phonemes with facial movements in real time. In independent tests, the lip‑sync accuracy scores around 92 % on the LSE (Lip‑Sync Error) benchmark, which is comparable to top‑tier competitors like Synthesia. The platform also adds subtle facial expressions based on the tone of the script, though extremely nuanced emotions may still look slightly generic.

### Is HeyGen suitable for commercial use and does it provide licensing for generated content?
Yes, HeyGen’s commercial license covers all videos created on paid [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026), allowing you to use the content for marketing, [advertising](/posts/2026-ai-powered-podcast-ad-creation-placement-tutorial), e‑learning, and resale without additional royalties.
