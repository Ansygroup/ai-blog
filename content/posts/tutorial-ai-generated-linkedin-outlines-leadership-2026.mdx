## Prompt Engineering: The 5‑Step Blueprint That Works Every Time

1. **Define the Persona** – Start with a clear role. Example: “You are a C‑level fintech strategist.”  
2. **Specify the Goal** – “Create a LinkedIn article that positions the reader as a thought leader on decentralized [finance](/posts/top-ai-powered-compliance-platforms-finance-2026).”  
3. **Insert Core Keywords** – Pull the top three LinkedIn‑[ranked](/posts/best-ai-code-generators) keywords from Frase (e.g., “DeFi adoption,” “regulatory sandbox,” “tokenized [asset](/posts/ai-generated-3d-asset-tools-2026)s”).  
4. **Set the Structure** – “Outline with a hook, three main points, and a CTA.”  
5. **Add Contextual Data** – Provide a recent statistic (e.g., “According to a June 2026 CB Insights report, DeFi transaction volume grew 42% YoY”).

When this prompt is fed into Jasper’s “Boss Mode,” the output includes a headline, a 150‑word hook, three sub‑headings with bullet points, and a CTA that references a downloadable whitepaper. The same prompt in Copy.ai yields a similar skeleton but often omits the CTA, requiring manual addition.
