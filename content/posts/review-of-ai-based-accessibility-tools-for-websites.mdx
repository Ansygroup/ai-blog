## What Are AI-Based Accessibility Tools?

AI-based accessibility tools use artificial intelligence to improve website accessibility for people with disabilities. These tools can help with tasks such as [image](/posts/midjourney-vs-firefly-vs-dalle-2026) description, text-to-speech functionality, and keyboard navigation.
