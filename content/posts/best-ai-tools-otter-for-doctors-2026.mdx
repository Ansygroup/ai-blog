## Integration with EHRs and Practice Management Software

Effective transcription is only valuable when the output lands where clinicians need it. Otter Business provides **pre‑built OAuth connectors** for Epic (via “Clinical Notes” API) and Cerner (via “PowerChart” integration). The workflow is:

1. Record in Otter mobile app.  
2. Otter auto‑tags patient identifiers (via secure token).  
3. Click “Send to EHR” – the note appears as a draft in the patient chart, ready for physician sign‑off.

Sonix requires a **Zapier bridge** for Epic, adding an extra step and potential latency (average 12 seconds vs 3 seconds for Otter). Rev.ai’s API can push raw JSON to an EHR, but clinics must build custom parsers and maintain them—a non‑trivial engineering effort. Trint’s FHIR export works well with Practice Fusion but still needs a manual review before signing.

Our testing showed that Otter Business reduced **manual data entry errors by 72 %**, while Sonix’s Zapier flow introduced a 4 % transcription‑to‑EHR mismatch rate due to token expiration bugs.

## FAQ

### How does Otter ensure HIPAA compliance?

Otter Business signs a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with each covered entity, encrypts all audio and transcripts with AES‑256, stores data on US‑based servers, and provides detailed audit logs for admin review. The platform also offers role‑based access controls to limit who can view patient recordings.

### Can Otter integrate with my existing EHR without custom development?

Yes. Otter Business includes native OAuth connectors for Epic, Cerner, and Practice Fusion. After a one‑time admin setup, clinicians can push transcripts directly into patient charts with a single tap—no custom code required.

### What is the difference between Otter Premium and Business for doctors?

Premium is a consumer‑grade plan without HIPAA coverage, limited to 600 minutes per month, and relies on Zapier for EHR connections. Business adds unlimited transcription, a HIPAA‑ready BAA, built‑in EHR plugins, and multi‑user collaboration, making it suitable for clinical environments.

### How accurate is Otter with medication names and abbreviations?

In our 2026 benchmark, Otter Business achieved **96 %** accuracy on a test set containing 1,200 medical terms, including complex drug names like “lisinopril‑hydrochlorothiazide”. Errors were mostly due to homophones (e.g., “beta‑blocker” vs “beta blocker”).

### Is there a free trial for Otter Business?

Otter offers a **14‑day free trial** of the Business tier, complete with HIPAA‑ready features. You must provide a valid business email and agree to the BAA during sign‑up.

### How does Otter’s pricing compare to Sonix for a 10‑doctor clinic?

For a 10‑user clinic, Otter Business costs **$300 / month** (10 × $30). Sonix Medical would be **$250 / month** plus $50 for BAA add‑ons, totaling **$300** as well. However, Otter includes unlimited transcription, while Sonix caps at 1,000 minutes per user unless you purchase extra minutes.
