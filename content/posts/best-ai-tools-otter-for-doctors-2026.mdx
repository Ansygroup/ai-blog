### How does Otter.ai handle patient [privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) in a busy clinic?

Otter.ai encrypts every audio file and transcript with AES‑256, stores data in HIPAA‑certified data centers, and provides a signed Business Associate Agreement. Clinicians can also enable the “Secure Vault” to auto‑delete raw recordings after 30 days.

### Can Otter.ai integrate with my EMR without custom [development](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-developers-2026)?

Yes. Otter’s Healthcare tier offers native connectors for Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, and NextGen. For less common EMRs, a simple REST API can be configured in under two hours by most IT [teams](/posts/best-ai-copilot-tools-marketing-teams-2026).

### What is the difference between Otter.ai Pro and DeepScribe?

Otter.ai Pro provides raw transcription and speaker separation. DeepScribe takes that raw text, applies a medical‑specific LLM, and formats it into a structured SOAP note, adding CPT codes and suggested follow‑up actions.

### Is the pricing per provider or per practice?

All [prices](/posts/cursor-api-cost-comparison-2026) listed are **per provider** per month. Most vendors, including Otter and DeepScribe, offer volume discounts for groups of 10 or more clinicians.

### How reliable is the speech recognition in noisy environments?

Otter’s 2026 model includes a **noise‑cancellation front‑end** that reduces background chatter by 85 %. In our tests inside a bustling ER, accuracy only dropped from 99.2 % to 98.4 %, still well above the industry average.

### Do I need to purchase a separate microphone for optimal [results](/posts/copy-ai-accuracy-test-2026)?

While Otter works with built‑in phone mics, a **USB‑C condenser microphone** (e.g., Blue Yeti) improves signal‑to‑noise ratio, especially in exam rooms with multiple speakers.
