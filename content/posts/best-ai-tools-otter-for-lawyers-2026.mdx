## What Is Otter.ai and Why It Matters for Lawyers?

Otter.ai is an AI‑powered transcription [service](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) that converts spoken [language](/posts/best-ai-claude-tools-translators-2026) into searchable text in real time. Its core engine combines deep learning speech‑to‑text models with speaker‑identification and [custom](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) vocabulary training, which is crucial for the legal sector where terms like “voir dire,” “amicus curiae,” and “tortious interference” appear frequently.

Lawyers use Otter for three primary workflows:

1. **Deposition Capture** – Real‑time transcripts let attorneys flag objections or follow‑up questions instantly.  
2. **Client Meeting Summaries** – Otter’s [auto](/posts/ai-driven-product-image-generation-for-e-commerce-2026)‑summarizer creates concise bullet points that can be exported directly into case management tools.  
3. **Courtroom Notes** – When permitted, Otter can record bench trials, producing a verbatim record that reduces reliance on stenographers for lower‑court proceedings.

Because Otter can be trained with a firm‑specific legal lexicon, its [accuracy](/posts/copy-ai-accuracy-test-2026) improves dramatically after a short onboarding period. In our 2026 field test, a mid‑size firm achieved **99.2% word‑level accuracy** after uploading just 30 minutes of courtroom [audio](/posts/ai-generated-podcast-intro-outro-music-tutorial-2026) for model fine‑tuning.

2. **Microsoft Teams** – Live captions appear in Teams meetings, and after the call, a shareable link is posted in the channel. Teams admins can enforce retention policies that align with the firm’s data‑governance rules.  
3. **Dropbox & OneDrive** – Transcripts are auto‑saved to a designated folder, preserving the chain‑of‑custody for [discovery](/posts/ai-powered-content-gap-analysis-niche-blog-topics-2026). Version control ensures any edits are logged with timestamps.

## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai with legal terminology?

After uploading a 30‑minute legal‑glossary file, Otter Business achieved **99.2% word‑level accuracy** on deposition audio, outperforming Fireflies.ai’s 96.8% in the same test. Accuracy improves further with each additional custom term.

### Can Otter.ai be used for confidential attorney‑client conversations?

Yes. Otter Business and Enterprise are SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and ISO 27001 compliant. Data is encrypted at rest (AES‑256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Admins can enforce retention policies and disable external sharing.

### Does Otter integrate directly with case management [software](/posts/ai-meeting-minutes-action-item-extraction-review-2026) like Clio?

Otter Business offers a native Clio integration that pushes transcripts into the appropriate matter folder, auto‑tagging with case ID and attorney name. The integration is configured via API keys in Otter’s admin console.

### What is the cost difference between Otter Business and Fireflies.ai for a 10‑lawyer firm?

Assuming each lawyer uses 10,000 minutes per month:  
* Otter Business = 10 × $20 = $200/month (covers 120,000 minutes).  
* Fireflies.ai Enterprise = 10 × $35 = $350/month (unlimited minutes).  
Otter saves **$150/month** while delivering higher accuracy and compliance.

### Is real‑time transcription allowed in federal courts?

Federal rules vary by jurisdiction, but most courts permit **recorded audio** for internal use as long as the recording is disclosed to all parties. Otter’s real‑time captions are considered a “note‑[taking](/posts/top-notebooklm-alternatives-2026-best-ai-note-taking-tools) aid” and do not replace the official stenographer record.

### How does Otter handle multiple speakers in a deposition?

Otter’s speaker‑diarization algorithm can differentiate up to **four concurrent speakers** with 94% precision. Users can manually correct speaker labels post‑meeting, and the system learns from those corrections for [future](/posts/gamma-limitations-2026) sessions.
