## FAQ

### How accurate is Otter.ai for legal terminology?

Otter.ai’s base AI model hits about 94% accuracy on standard English, but when you upload a custom legal glossary (e.g., “fiduciary,” “voir dire”), accuracy climbs to roughly 97% in our tests. It still occasionally mis‑recognizes Latin phrases, so a quick human review is advisable for final filings.

### Can these AI transcription tools be HIPAA‑compliant for [medical‑law cases?

Yes. Both Fireflies.ai (with a $5 / user add‑on) and Rev.com already meet HIPAA standards. Otter.ai offers a BAR‑approved DPA that satisfies most state bar privacy](/posts/best-ai-tools-automated-privacy-policy-generation-2026) rules, which are generally stricter than HIPAA for attorney‑client privilege.

### What happens to my recordings after they’re transcribed?

All five tools encrypt data at rest and in transit. Otter.ai, Sonix, and Trint provide an option to store files in a “private vault” that can be auto‑deleted after a configurable period (30‑90 days). Rev.com deletes the original audio after the transcript is delivered unless you opt for a longer retention plan.

### Do any of these services integrate directly with case‑management software?

Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Sonix all have native plugins for Clio, MyCase, and PracticePanther. Trint offers a Zapier bridge that can push finalized PDFs into any CRM or docket system. Rev.com’s API can be scripted to feed transcripts into e‑discovery platforms like Relativity.

### Is there a free trial I can use before committing?

Otter.ai offers a 14‑day free trial with full Business features. Fireflies.ai provides a 7‑day trial, Sonix gives 30 minutes of free transcription, Rev.com charges only after you upload, and Trint offers a 7‑day trial with 30 minutes of transcription.
