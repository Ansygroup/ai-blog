## FAQ

### How does Jasper’s credit system work for small businesses?

Credits are allocated monthly based on your plan. One word credit equals one generated word, while one image credit equals a single 1024×1024 pixel image. Unused credits roll over for up to three months, giving flexibility during slower periods.

### Can Jasper integrate with my existing CRM (e.g., HubSpot)?

Yes. Jasper offers native Zapier connectors and a REST API. You can automatically push generated leads, email drafts, or support replies into HubSpot, Salesforce, or Zoho without writing code.

### Is Jasper’s data secure for handling customer information?

Jasper is ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certified, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and provides GDPR‑compliant data processing agreements—critical for small businesses that store personal data.

### What’s the difference between Jasper Business and Enterprise plans?

The Business plan provides unlimited word and image credits, full workflow automation, and priority support. Enterprise adds a dedicated account manager, custom SLA, on‑premise deployment options, and volume‑based discounts for organizations exceeding 5 M words per month.

### How does Jasper compare to Copy.ai for social media content?

Jasper’s Social Scheduler generates captions, hashtags, and matching visuals in one step, whereas Copy.ai only creates text. In our side‑by‑side test, Jasper achieved a 15% higher engagement rate due to cohesive visual‑text pairing.
