## What Is Claude](/news/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-cursor-windsurf-claude-code-codex)?

Claude is an AI assistant developed by Anthropic, designed to assist with a wide range of tasks, from [writing and analysis to coding and conversation. It's built on a large language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) [model (LLM) that allows it to understand and respond to complex prompts.

!Getting Started with [Claude](/news/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-cursor-windsurf-claude-code-codex)
## Getting Started with Claude](/news/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-cursor-windsurf-claude-code-codex)

To start using Claude, simply visit the Anthropic [website and sign up for an account. You can choose between a free plan and a paid](/posts/jasper-vs-free-version) plan, Claude Pro. The free plan offers 100,000 tokens per month, while the paid plan costs $20/month and offers 5x more usage.
