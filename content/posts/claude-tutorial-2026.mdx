## What Is [Claude](/posts/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-cursor-windsurf-claude-code-codex)?

Claude is an AI assistant developed by Anthropic, designed to assist with a wide range of tasks, from [writing](/posts/chatgpt-vs-copy-ai) and analysis to coding and conversation. It's built on a large [language](/posts/replit-ai-agent-review-2026) [model](/posts/claude-4-opus-vs-sonnet-comparison-2026) (LLM) that allows it to understand and respond to complex prompts.

## Getting Started with [Claude](/posts/best-ai-coding-tools-2026-cursor-windsurf-claude-code-codex)

To start using Claude, simply visit the Anthropic website and sign up for an account. You can choose between a free plan and a [paid](/posts/jasper-vs-free-version) plan, Claude Pro. The free plan offers 100,000 tokens per month, while the paid plan costs $20/month and offers 5x more usage.

## Features and [Capabilities](/posts/claude-honest-review-2026)

Claude excels in tasks requiring complex reasoning, nuanced understanding, and generating high-quality [content](/posts/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation). It's particularly useful for:

* Writing and editing: Claude can help with [[content](/posts](/posts/claude-for-youtube)/best-ai-tools-for-social-media-content-calendar-creation) creation, proofreading, and summarization.
* Analysis: Claude can analyze large datasets, identify patterns, and provide [insights](/posts/claude-reddit-reviews-2026).
* Coding: Claude can assist with [code](/posts/best-ai-code-assistants-2026) writing, debugging, and [optimization](/posts/claude-for-youtube).
