## FAQ

### What is the main difference between Copy.ai and Midjourney?
The primary difference between Copy.ai and Midjourney is their focus](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026): Copy.ai specializes in AI writing, while Midjourney excels in AI image generation.

### Can I use [Copy.ai for image generation?
While Copy.ai offers some basic image generation capabilities, it is not its primary focus](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026). For advanced image generation, Midjourney is a [better choice.

### Is Midjourney suitable for non-designers?
Midjourney has a steeper learning curve, making it more suitable for designers](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-designers-2026) and visual artists. However, users can still achieve high-quality [results with practice and patience.

### Can I integrate Copy.ai and Midjourney with other tools?
Both Copy.ai and Midjourney offer integration](/posts/writesonic-api-guide) capabilities with popular [platforms. Copy.ai integrates with Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Drive, Slack, and WordPress, while [Midjourney integrates with Discord and Figma.

### Which tool is more affordable?
Midjourney](/posts/flux-vs-midjourney-2026-photorealism-comparison)'s pricing starts at $10/month (billed annually), making it more affordable for individual users. Copy.ai's pricing begins at $49/month (billed annually).
