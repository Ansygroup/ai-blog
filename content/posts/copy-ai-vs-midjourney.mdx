## FAQ

### What is the main difference between Copy.ai and Midjourney?
The primary difference between Copy.ai and Midjourney is their [focus](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026): Copy.ai specializes in AI writing, while Midjourney excels in AI image generation.

### Can I use [Copy.ai](/posts/copy-ai-for-small-business) for image generation?
While Copy.ai offers some basic image generation capabilities, it is not its primary [focus](/posts/brain-fm-review-2026). For advanced image generation, Midjourney is a [better](/posts/chatgpt-vs-copilot) choice.

### Is Midjourney suitable for non-designers?
Midjourney has a steeper learning curve, making it more suitable for designers and visual artists. However, users can still achieve high-quality results with practice and patience.

### Can I integrate Copy.ai and Midjourney with other tools?
Both Copy.ai and Midjourney offer [integration](/posts/writesonic-api-guide) capabilities with popular [platforms](/posts/comparison-of-ai-powered-online-course-platforms-for-education). Copy.ai integrates with [Google](/posts/google-flow-veo-3-1-2026-ai-video-production-native-audio) Drive, Slack, and WordPress, while [Midjourney](/posts/flux-vs-midjourney-2026-photorealism-comparison) integrates with Discord and Figma.

### Which tool is more affordable?
[Midjourney](/posts/flux-vs-midjourney-2026-photorealism-comparison)'s pricing starts at $10/month (billed annually), making it more affordable for individual users. Copy.ai's pricing begins at $49/month (billed annually).
