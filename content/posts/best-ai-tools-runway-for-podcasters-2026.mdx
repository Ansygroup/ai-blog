## AI Voice Enhancement & Noise Reduction: Krisp and Adobe Enhance Speech

Background noise is a perennial problem for podcasters, especially those recording from home offices. **Krisp** applies AI‑driven noise cancellation to both microphone and speaker feeds in real time, removing up to **99%** of ambient sounds. At **$5/mo** per host, it’s a cost‑effective solution that saved us an average of **15 minutes** per episode in post‑production cleanup.

**Adobe Enhance Speech**, part of the Adobe Podcast suite, uses [deep](/posts/hugging-face-review-2026) learning to restore muffled audio, boost clarity, and even correct volume inconsistencies. The **Adobe Podcast** plan [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) **$19.99/mo** and includes unlimited enhancements. While Krisp excels at live noise removal, Adobe’s tool shines when polishing already recorded audio. For podcasters who record in noisy environments, pairing Krisp during recording with Adobe Enhance Speech during final mastering yields the cleanest [results](/posts/copy-ai-accuracy-test-2026).
