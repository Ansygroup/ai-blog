Last checked: November 2026. Two kinds of source here: the 2026 State of Voice in CX report from Krisp, based on a survey of 815 contact centre executives in 12 countries run with Ryan Strategic Advisory ([report PDF](https://resources.krisp.ai/hubfs/WhitePaper/2026%20State%20of%20Voice-Krisp.pdf)), and the public pricing pages of Retell AI ([retellai.com/pricing](https://www.retellai.com/pricing)) and Vapi. Krisp sells voice AI products, so its survey is a vendor survey. We say so wherever we quote it.

"Mainstream" is a claim that needs a definition. Here we mean that most large contact centres are using or planning specific voice AI tools, and that anyone can buy a phone agent by the minute. The first half is supported by one vendor survey. The second half [you can](/posts/n8n-free-tier-limits-2026) check yourself on a pricing page.

## What the Krisp survey found

| Finding | Figure |
|---|---|
| Executives surveyed | 815 across 12 countries |
| Name [hiring](/posts/best-ai-chatgpt-tools-recruiters-2026) and staffing by region and language as the top barrier to scaling voice support | 40% |
| Speech [analytics](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-marketing-teams-2026), current or planned adoption | 80% |
| Agent assist, current or planned adoption | 72% |
| AI voice [translation](/posts/2026-review-best-ai-multilingual-video-caption-tools), current or planned adoption | 88% |
| Allocate more than half of their voice budget to onshore [live](/posts/best-ai-tools-real-time-chat-sentiment-analysis-2026) [agents](/posts/ai-agents-aggressively-hack-us-canada-2026-guide) | 56% |
| Spend under 10% of budget on AI agents | 65% |
| Spend under 10% of budget on AI translation services | 55% |

Two points stand out. First, "current or planned" mixes people who already use a tool with people who intend to. The report itself describes a market "in mid-transition," running legacy and AI tools in parallel. Second, budgets lag intent. Even among these executives, most still spend the large majority of their voice budget on human agents and under a tenth on AI agents.

The respondents rated agent assist and [speech](/posts/best-ai-tools-elevenlabs-for-translators-2026) analytics highest for impact, both 5.0 on a 6-point scale. Those tools help human agents. They are not the same thing as an AI answering the phone alone. That distinction is the main caveat on "voice AI goes mainstream": in this survey, mainstream mostly means AI assisting people on calls.

## What a phone agent [costs](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026)

For smaller businesses, the practical question is the per-minute [price](/posts/suno-vs-chatgpt-2026). From the vendors' own pages, as of November 2026:

- **Retell AI** advertises $0.07 to $0.31 per minute for voice agents, depending on the voice and [model](/posts/auto-ml-tutorial) choices. Its listed components include voice infrastructure at $0.055 per minute, and platform voices at $0.015 per minute, with ElevenLabs voices priced from $0.04 to $0.10 per minute.
- **Vapi** lists [hosting](/posts/pinecone-setup-guide-2026) at $0.05 per minute, before the speech, language model and telephony providers you attach.

Those are the component costs, not a total bill. The cost of a call is the sum of the voice platform, the language model, the voice and the phone [line](/posts/tutorial-ai-driven-ab-testing-email-subject-lines-2026), so build a spreadsheet with all four before you promise a number to a client.

## What you will not find on a vendor page

The survey's biggest barrier is not [technology](/posts/best-ai-claude-tools-translators-2026). It is staffing across languages and regions, with 40% naming it first. Krisp's own report notes the staffing barrier is cited far more often than integration complexity. For a small business that means the real use case for a phone agent is covering the hours and languages you cannot staff, not replacing the staff you already have. A voice agent that answers at 2 a.m. in a second language is easier to justify than one that replaces a daytime receptionist.

It is also [worth](/posts/grammarly-for-teams-2026-review) reading the survey with its commercial interest in mind. A company that sells accent conversion and noise cancellation has reason to publish a report in which those tools [show](/posts/best-ai-tools-copilot-for-podcasters-2026) strong adoption.

## Checks before you put one on your phone line

1. **Disclosure.** Tell callers they are talking to an AI. Rules vary by place, so check local law.
2. **Handoff.** Decide when and how the call transfers to a person, and test it.
3. **Recording consent.** If you record or transcribe, check the consent rules where your callers are.
4. **Cost ceiling.** Set a spending limit and a maximum call length.
5. **Review.** Listen to a sample of real calls every week for the first month.

As of November 2026, these are [open](/posts/top-hugging-face-alternatives-2026) questions, not forecasts: whether "planned" adoption turns into spending, whether budgets shift from onshore agents toward AI, and whether independent surveys, not vendor-run ones, report similar adoption.

## Our judgment

Voice AI is mainstream as a tool that helps human agents, and early as a tool that replaces them. If you run a small business, test a phone agent on after-hours calls first, price the [full](/posts/best-ai-tools-claude-for-marketing-teams-2026) per-minute stack, and keep a person one transfer away. Treat any single survey from a voice AI vendor as a direction, not a measurement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does an AI phone agent cost per minute?

Retell advertises $0.07 to $0.31 per minute depending on voice and model, and Vapi lists hosting at $0.05 per minute before provider costs. Add the language model, voice and phone line for a full total.

### Are most contact centres using voice AI?

In Krisp's survey of 815 executives, 80% were using or planning speech analytics and 72% agent assist, but 65% spent under 10% of budget on AI agents. It is a vendor survey.

### Do I have to tell callers it is an AI?

Rules vary by location, so check local law. Disclosing is the safe default.
