## Pricing Overview

Writesonic offers a simple, tiered pricing structure that caters to businesses and marketers of all sizes. The platform's pricing [plans](/posts/copy-ai-pricing-breakdown-2026) include:

- **Basic Plan:** $25/month (billed annually) or $35/month (billed monthly)
- **Pro Plan:** $50/month (billed annually) or $65/month (billed monthly)
- **Business Plan:** $100/month (billed annually) or $125/month (billed monthly)

Each plan offers a different number of credits, which can be redeemed [for content](/posts/jasper-for-content-marketing) generation. We found that Writesonic's pricing is competitive, considering the quality and quantity of content generated.
